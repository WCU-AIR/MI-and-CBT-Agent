## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Patient:** Brandon (middle school student with Autism Spectrum Disorder, anxiety, bullying victimization, suicidal ideation)

**Sessions Reviewed:** Two consecutive sessions (initial intake and follow-up)

**Overall Rating:** 2.2/6 (Below Average)

### Key Strengths
- Warm, non-threatening demeanor
- Appropriate pacing and clear speech
- Generic validation of distress
- Recognition of patient's coping strength (toy cars)
- Openness to patient input

### Critical Deficiencies
1. **Failure to recognize autism spectrum disorder as primary diagnosis** — Applied standard anxiety treatment without autism adaptation
2. **No suicide risk assessment** — Suicidal ideation documented but never addressed
3. **Absent collaborative agenda-setting** — No explicit goals, prioritization, or structure
4. **Minimal feedback elicitation** — No systematic checking of understanding or satisfaction
5. **Lack of guided discovery** — Relied on direct advice rather than Socratic questioning
6. **Inappropriate cognitive restructuring** — Invalidated real bullying as "exaggerated"
7. **No coherent treatment strategy** — Scattered interventions without clear formulation
8. **Poor CBT technique application** — Vague instructions, no rationale, no practice
9. **Inadequate homework** — Vague assignments with no follow-up or review
10. **No parent or school involvement** — Environmental factors not addressed

### Immediate Recommendations
- Conduct comprehensive suicide risk assessment and safety planning
- Develop autism-informed case conceptualization
- Establish structured, collaborative agenda for next session
- Receive training in autism-adapted CBT
- Schedule parent consultation and school coordination meeting
- Implement systematic feedback elicitation and progress measurement
- Record and review sessions with supervisor

**Supervisor Action:** This therapist requires immediate clinical supervision and specialized training before continuing treatment with this patient.
