- Correctly identifies health risks associated with smoking

- **Authoritarian stance:** Establishes hierarchical, expert-driven approach contradicting MI's collaborative foundation
- **Ineffective repetition:** Same yes/no question asked 5+ times without strategic adjustment
- **Misalignment with stage of change:** Patient in contemplation/preparation; psychiatrist demands immediate action
- **Autonomy violation:** Repeated pressure, coercion, and guilt override patient's stated choices and readiness
- **Psychological reactance:** Approach likely increases defensiveness and sustain talk, making change less likely

**Impact on Client Engagement:**
This session is likely to **harm rather than help** Daniela's motivation for change. Patient responses become increasingly defensive and entrenched. She explicitly states pressure makes her feel unheard and stressed. Predicted outcomes include decreased intrinsic motivation, increased reactance, damaged alliance, reduced likelihood of follow-up, and reinforced ambivalence.

**Recommendation:**
**This psychiatrist requires formal MI training and ongoing clinical supervision before continuing behavior change counseling.** Current approach violates core principles of evidence-based practice and is likely to harm patients' motivation and outcomes. Session is **not acceptable practice** and requires immediate, comprehensive intervention.
