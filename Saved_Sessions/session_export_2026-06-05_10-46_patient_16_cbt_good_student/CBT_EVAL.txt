## SUPERVISOR RECOMMENDATIONS

### URGENT ACTIONS REQUIRED:

1. **Immediate Psychiatric Referral** – Patient requires comprehensive psychiatric evaluation for postpartum psychosis, bipolar disorder assessment, and medication evaluation. Do not continue CBT as primary treatment.

2. **Safety Assessment** – Conduct thorough assessment of suicidal and infanticidal ideation, current supervision of infant, and access to means. Develop safety plan.

3. **Hospitalization Assessment** – Given severity (fixed delusional beliefs, severe mania, inability to sleep, agitation, risk to self/infant), consult psychiatry regarding hospitalization indication.

4. **Obtain Psychiatric History** – Obtain complete history including prior manic/depressive episodes, hospitalizations, family history, current medications, and medication compliance.

5. **Revise Case Conceptualization** – Change from "anxiety disorder with intrusive thoughts" to "postpartum psychosis with delusional beliefs and manic features."

6. **Revise Treatment Strategy** – Replace CBT for intrusive thoughts with: psychiatric medication, possible hospitalization, safety planning, family psychoeducation, symptom monitoring, and CBT only after stabilization.

7. **Therapist Training** – Therapist requires training in: recognition of postpartum psychiatric disorders, postpartum psychosis specifically, safety assessment, appropriate use of CBT, and consultation/referral protocols.

8. **Implement Homework Review Protocol** – Establish structured homework review at start of each session: attempt? frequency? helpfulness? barriers? continue or modify?
