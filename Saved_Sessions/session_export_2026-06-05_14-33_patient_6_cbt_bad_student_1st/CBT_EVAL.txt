## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Identified:**
- Genuine warmth, concern, and non-judgmental stance
- Patient felt heard and supported; maintained engagement across two sessions
- Appropriate acknowledgment of sobriety milestones (3 days, 10 days)
- Consistent availability and commitment to patient

**Priority Areas for Improvement (in order of urgency):**

1. **Establish CBT structure:** Implement explicit agenda-setting, case conceptualization, and coherent treatment strategy in next session
2. **Eliminate excessive self-disclosure:** Remove personal anecdotes; replace with Socratic questioning
3. **Implement relapse prevention planning:** Conduct functional analysis of drinking triggers; develop coping strategies
4. **Apply specific CBT techniques:** Introduce thought records, behavioral activation, cognitive restructuring, sleep-focused interventions
5. **Use guided discovery:** Replace advice-giving with Socratic questions
6. **Structure homework systematically:** Assign targeted tasks with clear rationale and planned review

**Recommended Actions:**
- Immediate supervision with CBT-trained clinician
- Attendance at CBT fundamentals training
- Review of CBT manuals and case conceptualization frameworks
- Recording and review of sessions with supervisor
- Role-play practice of Socratic questioning and CBT techniques
