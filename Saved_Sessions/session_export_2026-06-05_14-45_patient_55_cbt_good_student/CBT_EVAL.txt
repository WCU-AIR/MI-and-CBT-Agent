## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Actions Required:

1. **Safety Planning (URGENT):** Implement explicit suicide risk assessment and written safety plan in next session. Non-negotiable for patients with recent suicidal behavior.

2. **Behavioral Interventions:** Introduce behavioral activation, sleep intervention, and rumination management alongside cognitive work. These are critical for addressing maintaining factors.

3. **Structured Homework:** Develop written thought record templates and detailed homework instructions with troubleshooting strategies.

4. **Case Conceptualization:** Before next session, write comprehensive case formulation linking symptoms to maintaining factors to guide treatment planning.

### Supervision Focus:

- CBT-specific techniques and their systematic application
- Risk assessment and safety planning protocols
- Behavioral interventions for depression (activation, sleep, rumination management)
- Case conceptualization development
- Comprehensive treatment planning with intervention sequencing

### Positive Feedback:

- Excellent rapport and empathic attunement
- Strong collaborative style and Socratic questioning
- Patient felt heard and expressed increased hope
- Solid foundational therapeutic skills
