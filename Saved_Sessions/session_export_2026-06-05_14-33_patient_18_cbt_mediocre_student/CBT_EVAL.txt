## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CLINICAL SUPERVISION

**Priority 1: Psychiatric Assessment Competency**
- Trainee must develop competency in recognizing acute manic episodes and other psychiatric emergencies
- Review DSM-5 criteria for manic episode; practice structured mood disorder assessment
- Consult with psychiatrist or bipolar disorder specialist about appropriate assessment protocols

**Priority 2: Safety and Risk Assessment**
- Trainee must conduct comprehensive safety assessment in all sessions, especially with acute psychiatric presentations
- Develop structured suicide/homicide risk assessment protocol
- Assess medication compliance and substance use barriers

**Priority 3: Appropriate Treatment Modality Selection**
- Trainee must understand that CBT cognitive restructuring is not primary intervention for acute mania with poor insight
- CBT is most effective for maintenance phase and relapse prevention after psychiatric stabilization
- Primary interventions for acute mania: psychiatric evaluation, medication management, possible hospitalization, psychoeducation, family involvement

**Priority 4: Clinical Authority and Boundaries**
- Trainee must learn to balance warmth with appropriate clinical authority necessary for managing acute psychiatric illness
- Practice delivering clinical concerns with both compassion and firmness
- Study motivational interviewing for addressing ambivalence about treatment in patients with poor insight

**Priority 5: Collaborative Goal-Setting with Poor Insight**
- Trainee must develop skills in establishing shared problem definition even when patient denies having problem
- Practice acknowledging patient autonomy while maintaining clinical authority
- Learn to respectfully but firmly address poor insight as barrier to treatment
