### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Session demonstrates direct confrontation of Marisol's sustain talk, which predictably strengthens it. Marisol's core sustain talk includes: "I'm handling it my way," "I'm confident I can handle it," "I'm responsible enough to do what I think is best for me," "I won't just give in because someone insists," and "It's my call." Psychiatrist responds to this sustain talk with direct argument and pressure: "you really need to listen," "that's reckless," "your attitude is exactly why people end up with infections," "you have to stop resisting this," "you're just asking for trouble." These confrontational responses are precisely what MI research shows strengthens client resistance and sustain talk. Each time psychiatrist argues against Marisol's position, Marisol becomes more entrenched in it. MI approach to softening sustain talk would use techniques like double-sided reflection ("On one hand, you want to make your own decisions about your body, and on the other hand, you don't want to end up with an infection"), reframing ("It sounds like you care about your health, which is why you're thinking about this"), or strategic redirection ("What would it look like if you found a way to care for your piercing that felt like your choice?"). Instead, psychiatrist argues directly, which is least effective approach and strengthens defensive positioning.
