## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL CONFERENCE

**Therapist Strengths:**
- Maintains warm, non-judgmental demeanor
- Demonstrates genuine concern and patience
- Establishes adequate rapport
- Identifies relevant surface-level content
- Attempts to apply CBT techniques

**Priority Areas for Immediate Intervention:**

1. **Session Structure (URGENT):** Implement collaborative agenda-setting and homework review in every session. Session 2 must include systematic review of Session 1 homework before proceeding.

2. **Guided Discovery:** Replace leading questions with genuine Socratic inquiry. Practice asking open-ended questions that enable patient self-discovery rather than confirming therapist suggestions.

3. **Cognitive Restructuring:** Teach collaborative, evidence-based approach: identify thought → examine evidence for/against → develop balanced thought with patient input → test through behavioral experiment.

4. **Individualized Interventions:** Move beyond generic suggestions. Tailor behavioral activation to patient's values, barriers, and preferences. Explore identity, meaning, and cultural dimensions.

5. **Homework Assignment and Review:** Provide specific instructions with clear rationale, prioritize tasks, anticipate barriers, confirm commitment, and systematically review in next session.

**Recommended Supervision Plan:**
- Weekly individual supervision focusing on CBT structure and technique
- Review session recordings to identify leading questions and directive language
- Practice Socratic questioning through role-play
- Develop individualized case conceptualization before next patient session
- Establish measurement and progress tracking system

**Training Resources:**
- Beck Institute CBT Core Competencies training
- Cognitive Therapy Scale (CTS) self-assessment
- Recorded examples of skilled Socratic questioning
- Case conceptualization worksheets
