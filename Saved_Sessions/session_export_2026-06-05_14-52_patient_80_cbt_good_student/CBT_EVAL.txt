## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Action Items:

1. **Develop comprehensive case formulation** integrating both separation anxiety disorder and provisional tic disorder with visual diagram showing maintaining factors
2. **Systematically address safety behaviors** through graduated reduction plan (door jamb ritual, staying close to parents)
3. **Create avoidance hierarchy** prioritizing situations from least to most anxiety-provoking (alone in room → alone in house → school → sleepovers)
4. **Identify core beliefs** through guided discovery (likely "I'm responsible for parents' safety")
5. **Involve parents in next session** to address accommodation and support independence
6. **Apply tic-specific interventions** (habit reversal training: awareness training, competing response training)
7. **Provide psychoeducation** about anxiety cycle and CBT model using age-appropriate language and visual aids
8. **Assess somatic symptoms** systematically (stomach pain frequency, triggers, severity, mind-body connection)

### Strengths to Build Upon:

- Excellent collaborative empiricism and Socratic questioning
- Warm, genuine interpersonal style with strong rapport-building
- Logical session flow and appropriate pacing
- Ability to identify key automatic thoughts and secondary cognitions
- Patient engagement and motivation for change

### Areas for Development:

- Comprehensive case formulation for dual/complex diagnoses
- Systematic application of CBT techniques (thought records, behavioral experiments, exposure hierarchies)
- Tic-specific interventions (habit reversal training)
- Parental involvement and accommodation assessment
- Safety behavior reduction planning
- Core belief identification and modification
- Psychoeducation and patient education
- In-session behavioral experiments and exposures

### Supervision Plan:

- **Session 1 (This session):** Review evaluation, discuss gaps in case formulation
- **Session 2:** Role-play developing comprehensive case formulation with supervisor
- **Session 3:** Practice systematic thought records and behavioral experiments
- **Session 4:** Training in habit reversal training for tics
- **Session 5:** Practice parental involvement sessions and accommodation assessment
- **Ongoing:** Review recordings of sessions, practice with other patients, discuss complex cases
