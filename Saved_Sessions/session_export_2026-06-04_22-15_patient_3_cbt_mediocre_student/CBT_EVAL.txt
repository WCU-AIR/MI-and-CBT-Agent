## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

- **No safety plan established** despite recent high-lethality suicide attempt (overdose requiring intubation)
- **No crisis protocols** or emergency procedures discussed
- **No assessment of current suicidal ideation** or warning signs
- **No crisis contact numbers** or between-session safety parameters
- **Absence of family involvement** in safety planning
- **No distress tolerance or crisis coping skills** taught

### Evidence-Based Practice Gaps
- Minimal use of Socratic questioning; heavy reliance on direct advice
- Vague, non-specific homework without tracking or review
- No homework review in Sessions 2-3; treatment lacks continuity
- CBT techniques mentioned but not systematically taught or practiced
- Focus on rumination (secondary) rather than suicide risk (primary)
- No explicit case conceptualization or treatment strategy

### Strengths to Build Upon
- Warm, validating interpersonal style
- Appropriate use of collaborative language
- Accurate reflection of surface-level concerns
- Reasonable session pacing and structure
- Age-appropriate communication

### Recommendations for Supervision
1. **Mandatory training** in suicide risk assessment and safety planning
2. **Specialized training** in CBT for suicidal behavior disorder
3. **Close supervision** of all high-risk cases (weekly case review minimum)
4. **Structured protocols** for safety planning and crisis management
5. **Systematic homework review** in every session with tracking
6. **Skill-building** in Socratic questioning and guided discovery
7. **Case conceptualization** development with clear treatment strategy
8. **Increased treatment intensity** for Hannah (weekly sessions, phone check-ins)
