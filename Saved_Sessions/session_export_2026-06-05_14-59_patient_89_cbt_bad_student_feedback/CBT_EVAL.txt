## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment:** Unacceptable for independent practice.

**Strengths Identified:**
- Therapist created non-threatening environment where patient felt comfortable sharing
- Demonstrated basic empathy and validation
- Patient remained engaged throughout session
- Therapist was personable and approachable

**Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Remediation:**
1. Complete absence of CBT structure and technique application
2. Failure to identify and target maintaining factors of anxiety disorder
3. Inappropriate therapeutic approach (excessive self-disclosure, boundary violations)
4. Superficial understanding of clinical presentation and anxiety mechanisms
5. Lack of collaboration and patient engagement in treatment planning

**Required Training and Supervision:**
- Weekly individual supervision with CBT-trained supervisor
- Review of session recordings with focus on Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Training in evidence-based protocols for child anxiety disorders
- Specific skill development: agenda-setting, thought records, behavioral experiments, exposure therapy
- Competency assessment before independent treatment continuation

**Timeline for Reassessment:** 8 weeks with weekly supervision and skill-building focus.
