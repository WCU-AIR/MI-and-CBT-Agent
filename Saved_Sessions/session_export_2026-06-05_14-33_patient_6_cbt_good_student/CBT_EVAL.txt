## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality:** Competent evidence-based CBT with strong foundational skills but gaps in clinical depth and comprehensiveness.

**Immediate Priorities for Therapist Development:**
1. Conduct functional analysis to identify what drinking does for patient (core maintaining factors)
2. Integrate psychoeducation about alcohol-induced depression and expected mood improvement with abstinence
3. Expand cognitive work to target automatic thoughts most directly linked to drinking behavior
4. Develop relapse prevention planning given patient's history of abstinence followed by relapse

**Strengths to Reinforce:** Guided discovery, feedback elicitation, collaboration, interpersonal effectiveness, agenda-setting.

**Areas for Continued Development:** Comprehensive case conceptualization, functional analysis, systematic application of multiple CBT techniques, psychoeducation integration, relapse prevention planning.
