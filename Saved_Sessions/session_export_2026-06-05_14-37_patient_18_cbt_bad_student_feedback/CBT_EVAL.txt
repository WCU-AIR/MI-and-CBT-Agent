## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR REMEDIATION

1. **Develop competence in systematic assessment of manic episodes** using structured protocols
2. **Eliminate excessive self-disclosure** and maintain professional boundaries
3. **Master Socratic questioning and guided discovery** techniques
4. **Establish clear agenda-setting and treatment planning** procedures
5. **Learn to apply specific CBT techniques:** cognitive restructuring, thought records, behavioral experiments, reality-testing
6. **Develop skill in managing resistance and poor insight** through collaborative exploration
7. **Implement family involvement and psychoeducation** protocols
8. **Establish structured homework assignment procedures** with clear rationale
9. **Conduct intensive supervision** with CBT-trained supervisor before independent practice
10. **Participate in specialized training** on bipolar disorder assessment and treatment

**Supervisor Review Required:** This therapist requires immediate feedback, intensive supervision, and skills remediation before conducting independent psychiatric evaluations of acute manic episodes.
