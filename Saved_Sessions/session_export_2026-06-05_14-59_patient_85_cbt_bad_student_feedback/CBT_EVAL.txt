## SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Overall Clinical Impression:**

This session does not constitute evidence-based CBT for schizophrenia. It represents generic supportive counseling with CBT-adjacent language but without CBT structure, methodology, or efficacy. The therapist demonstrated adequate interpersonal warmth but failed to deliver the clinical acuity, systematic approach, and specialized interventions required for treating active psychosis.

**Key Concerns:**

1. **Misdiagnosis of symptom presentation:** Hallucinations and paranoia treated as anxiety rather than psychosis
2. **Absence of CBT fundamentals:** No agenda, no case conceptualization, no systematic technique application, no homework
3. **Cognitive mismatch:** Abstract interventions suggested despite patient's stated concrete thinking limitations
4. **Safety gaps:** No assessment of command hallucinations or suicidality
5. **Inadequate specialization:** Therapist lacks CBT-for-psychosis training

**Recommendation:**

Immediate specialized training in CBT-for-psychosis is required before this therapist continues treating patients with schizophrenia. Close clinical supervision recommended during retraining period.
