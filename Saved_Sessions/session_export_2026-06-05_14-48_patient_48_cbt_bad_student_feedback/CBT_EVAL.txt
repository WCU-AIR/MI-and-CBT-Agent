## IMPACT ON PATIENT CARE

**Likelihood of Therapeutic Change:** LOW

This session is unlikely to produce meaningful symptom reduction. While Oscar may feel temporarily supported and encouraged, he will likely return to baseline anxiety patterns without specific intervention targeting the mechanisms maintaining his disorders.

- **Health anxiety will persist** because catastrophic interpretations were not examined or challenged
- **Avoidance will persist** because it was not systematically addressed through exposure
- **Reassurance-seeking will persist** because the cycle maintaining it was not explored
- **Panic symptoms will persist** because fear of panic and avoidance were not targeted

**Risk of Harm:** Generic stress-management suggestions may inadvertently reinforce maladaptive patterns. Encouragement to "think positive" may increase self-blame when Oscar experiences anxiety despite positive thinking, and does not address the actual problem.

**Prognosis Without Intervention:** If this approach continues, Oscar may become discouraged that therapy is not helping, potentially leading to dropout or loss of faith in psychological treatment. He may return to seeking medical reassurance, reinforcing his health anxiety and care-seeking behavior.
