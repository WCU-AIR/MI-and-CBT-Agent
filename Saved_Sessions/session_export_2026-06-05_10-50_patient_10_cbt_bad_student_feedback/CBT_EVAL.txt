## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Competency Assessment:** The therapist has **not yet developed competence in CBT delivery**. Foundational interpersonal skills (warmth, rapport, safety) are present but insufficient. Critical deficiencies in agenda-setting, guided discovery, CBT technique application, and homework assignment prevent this from qualifying as evidence-based treatment.

**Most Critical Changes Needed (Immediate Priority):**

1. **Establish structured agenda** at beginning of each session with clear goals and time allocation
2. **Eliminate excessive self-disclosure** and maintain professional boundaries
3. **Apply specific CBT techniques** (cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, exposure, problem-solving) instead of generic reassurance
4. **Use Socratic questioning** to facilitate guided discovery instead of giving advice
5. **Assign specific homework** with clear rationale and follow-up

**Recommendation:** Intensive supervision required. Do not continue treating patients without demonstrated competence in CBT fundamentals.
