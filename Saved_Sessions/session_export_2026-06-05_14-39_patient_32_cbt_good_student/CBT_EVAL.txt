**EVALUATOR NOTES:**

- **Strengths:** Excellent interpersonal effectiveness and rapport-building; strong agenda-setting and collaboration; clear cognitive model application; specific, well-structured homework; consistent validation and empathy; good feedback-seeking at session end.

- **Critical Gaps:** Fundamental diagnostic misunderstanding; inappropriate treatment strategy for delusional disorder; missed behavioral targets; incomplete symptom assessment; limited Socratic questioning; superficial evidence examination; low homework confidence not adequately addressed.

- **Evidence-Based Status:** This session does NOT represent evidence-based CBT for delusional disorder, mixed type. While techniques are evidence-based for anxiety disorders, they are inappropriate for this patient's actual diagnosis. True evidence-based treatment would involve behavioral activation, coping strategies, medication consultation, and functional goal-setting.

- **Risk Level:** Moderate risk of treatment failure and patient discouragement. Strong therapeutic alliance provides foundation for improvement if approach is modified.

- **Recommendation:** Urgent reassessment of diagnosis and treatment approach required before next session.
