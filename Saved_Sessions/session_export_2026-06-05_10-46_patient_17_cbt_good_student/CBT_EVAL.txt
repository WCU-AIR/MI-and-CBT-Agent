**SUPERVISOR REVIEW NOTES:**

This session demonstrates a **fundamental misalignment between treatment approach and patient diagnosis**. While the therapist exhibits good collaborative skills, guided discovery, and interpersonal effectiveness, the application of standard cognitive restructuring to a patient with **acute Bipolar I Disorder with psychotic features** represents a critical clinical gap.

**Most Urgent Actions Required:**
1. Immediate consultation with bipolar disorder specialist or supervisor
2. Integration of pharmacological management into conceptualization
3. Direct assessment and intervention for psychotic features
4. Implementation of mandatory homework review protocol
5. Pursuit of specialized training in bipolar disorder treatment

**Current Likelihood of Therapeutic Change:** Modest short-term benefit from behavioral experiment (pausing/breathing) possible, but underlying manic episode and psychotic symptoms unlikely to resolve through cognitive work alone. Without pharmacological management and behavioral interventions, risk of symptom escalation or relapse is significant.
