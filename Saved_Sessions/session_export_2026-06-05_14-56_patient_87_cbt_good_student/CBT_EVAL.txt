## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths to reinforce:**
- Excellent interpersonal effectiveness and rapport-building
- Accurate empathic understanding of patient's experience
- Skillful use of Socratic questioning for cognitive restructuring
- Strong collaborative agenda-setting and homework planning
- Appropriate pacing and respect for patient's processing needs

**Critical areas requiring immediate development:**
1. **Psychosis-specific assessment**: Learn to assess hallucinations' nature, content, frequency, and impact. Assess ideas of reference, delusions, and command hallucinations.
2. **Medication competency**: Understand pharmacological treatment of schizophreniform disorder. Always assess medication status and coordinate with prescriber.
3. **Safety assessment**: Develop systematic approach to assessing suicidality, homicidality, command hallucinations, and risk of acting on delusional beliefs.
4. **Reality testing**: Learn evidence-based techniques for helping patients reality-test hallucinations and develop alternative explanations.
5. **Coping strategies for voices**: Teach grounding techniques, distraction strategies, and voice management skills.
6. **Psychoeducation**: Develop ability to explain schizophreniform disorder, stress-vulnerability model, and symptom triggers in accessible language.
7. **Behavioral activation**: Learn to assess and address negative symptoms with activity scheduling and social reengagement.

**Recommended training:**
- Specialized CBT for psychosis (CBTp) certification program
- Morrison, A. P. (2002). *Working with Voices: Cognitive Therapy for Auditory Hallucinations*
- Kingdon, D., & Turkington, D. (2005). *Cognitive Therapy of Schizophrenia*
- Supervision with CBTp-trained clinician

**Next steps:**
- Schedule follow-up supervision to review next session with this patient
- Provide written feedback emphasizing strengths while clearly identifying gaps
- Develop individualized learning plan focused on psychosis-specific interventions
