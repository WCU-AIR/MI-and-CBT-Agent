## CRITICAL CLINICAL SUMMARY

**Strengths:**
- Warm, supportive demeanor; patient felt safe and expressed feeling "more hopeful"
- Non-threatening environment; patient remained engaged
- Basic empathic listening

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- **Complete absence of eating disorder assessment and treatment** (0/6 on key items)
- **No CBT structure, conceptualization, or techniques applied** (0/6 on Items 7-11)
- **Violation of professional boundaries** through excessive self-disclosure
- **Minimal collaboration and feedback elicitation**
- **Inefficient use of time** on tangential topics
- **No homework assigned**

**Clinical Risk:**
Patient with serious eating disorder (BMI 14.7, amenorrhea, malnutrition) received generic supportive therapy rather than evidence-based eating disorder treatment. Without intervention targeting eating patterns, patient will likely continue restricting and experiencing medical deterioration.

**Recommendation:**
**URGENT REFERRAL TO EATING DISORDERS SPECIALIST.** This therapist requires immediate training in CBT for eating disorders and close supervision. Current approach is insufficient for treating a potentially life-threatening condition.
