## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Observed:**
- Genuine warmth, empathy, and non-judgmental stance
- Patient remains engaged and does not become defensive
- Attempts to normalize patient experience
- Recognizes patient's family motivation

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Intervention:**
1. **Absence of CBT structure and technique** – This is supportive counseling, not CBT
2. **No treatment conceptualization or strategy** – No coherent plan for change
3. **Failure to target maintaining factors** – Key cognitions and behaviors not addressed
4. **Inappropriate self-disclosure** – Violates CBT boundaries; shifts focus from patient
5. **Excessive reassurance without cognitive work** – May paradoxically maintain anxiety
6. **No agenda, feedback, or homework** – Foundational CBT elements absent
7. **Failure to adapt for cognitive impairment** – No modification for patient's executive dysfunction
8. **Missed guided discovery opportunities** – Tells rather than asks

**Recommended Actions:**
- Enroll in structured CBT training program (ABCT, IACP, or university-based)
- Obtain regular supervision from experienced CBT therapist with session recordings
- Study core CBT texts (Beck, Westbrook et al., Padesky & Mooney)
- Record and self-monitor sessions; track progress on CTRS
- Join CBT consultation group for peer feedback
- Timeline: 6-12 months intensive training to reach competency threshold (4-5 on CTRS)
