## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

### Priority 1: Learn and Apply Specific CBT Techniques
- Master thought records, behavioral activation, exposure, sleep protocols, activity scheduling
- Stop relying on generic advice and personal anecdotes
- Practice techniques in role-plays before applying with patients

### Priority 2: Establish Clear Agenda and Case Conceptualization
- Collaboratively set agenda at beginning of each session
- Develop written case conceptualization before each session
- Use conceptualization to guide all interventions

### Priority 3: Use Guided Discovery Instead of Advice-Giving
- Replace advice with Socratic questioning
- Ask questions that help patient discover insights
- Goal: 80% questions, 20% advice

### Priority 4: Elicit and Respond to Patient Feedback
- Ask for feedback at least 3 times per session
- Check for understanding regularly
- Adjust approach based on patient responses

### Priority 5: Assign Specific, Detailed, Rationale-Explained Homework
- End every session with specific homework
- Provide clear rationale for each assignment
- Ensure understanding and plan review

### Priority 6: Focus on Key Cognitions and Behaviors
- Identify specific thoughts and behaviors maintaining symptoms
- Target these directly rather than generic issues
- Use functional analysis to understand patterns

### Priority 7: Eliminate Personal Anecdotes; Maintain Professional Boundaries
- Stop sharing personal stories
- Maintain clear professional boundaries
- Use examples from other patients (anonymously) or hypotheticals instead

### Priority 8: Develop Cultural Competence
- Acknowledge and explore Carlos's cultural background
- Address identity questions and acculturation stress
- Validate impact of contextual stressors

### Priority 9: Establish Baseline Measurements and Monitor Progress
- Measure symptom severity at baseline
- Track progress every 2-3 sessions
- Adjust treatment if progress stalls

### Priority 10: Develop Coherent Treatment Strategy
- Create comprehensive plan addressing all diagnoses
- Establish logical sequence of interventions
- Connect all work to clear treatment goals

### Priority 11: Seek Structured Supervision and Training
- Work with experienced CBT supervisor
- Record sessions for review
- Complete assigned readings and practice
