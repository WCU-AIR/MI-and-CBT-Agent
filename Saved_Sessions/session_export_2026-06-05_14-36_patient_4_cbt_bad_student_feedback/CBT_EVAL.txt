## SUPERVISOR RECOMMENDATIONS

**Priority Actions:**

1. **Implement structured agenda-setting** in every session with collaborative prioritization
2. **Eliminate self-disclosure** and maintain professional boundaries
3. **Replace advice-giving with Socratic questioning** to enable guided discovery
4. **Develop clear problem formulation** identifying maintaining factors and treatment targets
5. **Implement specific CBT techniques** (behavioral activation, cognitive restructuring, exposure)
6. **Assign structured homework** with clear rationale and review protocol
7. **Systematically elicit feedback** throughout sessions
8. **Seek formal CBT training and supervision** to address fundamental competency gaps

**Clinical Risk:** Without evidence-based intervention, patient's adjustment disorder may worsen or become chronic. Patient may develop more severe anxiety or depressive disorder. Patient may lose hope that treatment can help.
