## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Immediate Actions Required:**

1. **URGENT:** Implement comprehensive suicide risk assessment and safety planning in next session. Use structured tool (e.g., Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale). Develop written safety plan with crisis resources.

2. **Supervision:** Weekly individual supervision required. Focus on: suicide risk assessment, CBT techniques (cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, thought records), bipolar II pharmacology, and session structure.

3. **Training:** Attend training on suicide risk assessment and safety planning. Complete CBT skills training with emphasis on Socratic questioning and guided discovery.

4. **Case Reassignment:** Consider reassigning high-risk cases to more experienced clinician until competencies are demonstrated.

5. **Recording Review:** Therapist should record sessions and review with supervisor to identify patterns of advice-giving versus discovery-based questioning.

**Strengths to Build Upon:**

- Demonstrated warmth and non-hostile demeanor
- Patient felt heard and safe (basic rapport established)
- Genuine desire to help patient

**Critical Gaps to Address:**

- Suicide risk assessment and safety planning
- Application of CBT techniques
- Clinical conceptualization and treatment strategy
- Responsiveness to patient feedback
- Understanding of bipolar II disorder
- Session structure and agenda-setting
