## SUMMARY OF CLINICAL FINDINGS

### Strengths Demonstrated:
- **Appropriate therapeutic tone**: Gentle, non-threatening demeanor suitable for anxious child
- **Basic empathic validation**: Used validating language normalizing patient's experience
- **Patient autonomy support**: Offered choices and asked "What do you think?"
- **Problem identification**: Correctly identified school anxiety, peer bullying, and negative self-beliefs
- **Multiple intervention attempts**: Introduced several CBT-relevant strategies

### Critical Deficiencies:
- **Unstructured agenda-setting**: No explicit session goals, priorities, or time allocation; vague and reactive direction
- **Absent feedback elicitation**: Failed to systematically check understanding, credibility, or satisfaction; resistance unexplored
- **Superficial patient understanding**: Missed sensory sensitivities, social misinterpretation, shame about masking, suicidal ideation, and role of special interests
- **Pseudo-collaboration**: Used collaborative language but maintained therapist-driven, directive approach; patient compliant but not genuinely collaborative
- **Minimal guided discovery**: Relied on direct suggestion and persuasion rather than Socratic questioning
- **Unfocused intervention**: Addressed general anxiety rather than Brandon's specific maintaining factors (avoidance, social withdrawal, identity beliefs, sensory overwhelm)
- **Incoherent treatment strategy**: No clear case conceptualization or logical progression; generic interventions not tailored to ASD presentation
- **Poorly executed CBT techniques**: Techniques introduced without adequate explanation, practice, or monitoring; several important techniques absent
- **Inadequate homework**: Assignments lacked rationale, specificity, and practice; no systematic follow-up in subsequent session
- ****CRITICAL SAFETY FAILURE**: No assessment or management of suicidal ideation despite documented history of "wishing he had never been born"**
- **No progress monitoring**: Second session repeated first without checking outcomes or adjusting approach

### Clinical Impact:
Brandon remains at significant risk due to bullying, social isolation, suicidal ideation, and unmet mental health needs. Current therapeutic approach is unlikely to produce meaningful improvement because interventions are not tailored to his ASD diagnosis, core maintaining factors are not targeted, patient does not understand treatment rationale, skills are not practiced or mastered, and safety is not addressed.

### Immediate Recommendations:
1. **URGENT: Conduct comprehensive suicide risk assessment and develop safety plan**
2. Develop clear, collaborative case conceptualization tailored to Brandon's ASD
3. Establish structured agenda-setting with explicit goals and time allocation
4. Shift from directive suggestion to guided discovery through Socratic questioning
5. Focus on Brandon's most central maintaining factors
6. Teach and practice all CBT skills in-session before assigning as homework
7. Systematically review and follow up on homework at each session
8. Involve Brandon's mother and coordinate with school
9. Address school avoidance through behavioral activation and graduated exposure
10. Provide psychoeducation about ASD, sensory sensitivities, and social communication deficits

**This evaluation indicates significant gaps in CBT competence requiring immediate supervision, training, and case consultation to ensure patient safety and treatment effectiveness.**
