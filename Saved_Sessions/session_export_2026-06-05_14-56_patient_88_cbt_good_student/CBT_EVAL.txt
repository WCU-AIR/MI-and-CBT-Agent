## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Immediate Priority Actions:**

1. **Develop explicit personality disorder conceptualization** – Therapist must articulate how schizotypal and paranoid features drive Henry's difficulties before next session.

2. **Establish multi-session treatment plan** – Collaboratively develop 12-16 session plan addressing core beliefs, behavioral avoidance, and social withdrawal.

3. **Assess magical thinking and ideas of reference directly** – Use guided discovery to examine these core distortions.

4. **Expand cognitive work to core beliefs** – Implement downward arrow technique to access beliefs underlying surface thoughts.

5. **Incorporate behavioral activation and graded exposure** – Address social withdrawal with structured behavioral interventions.

6. **Assess for emerging psychotic symptoms** – Establish safety protocols and monitoring procedures given risk for psychotic progression.

7. **Strengthen homework structure** – Assign formal thought records with explicit rationale and follow-up plan.

**Supervision Focus:** Personality disorder-specific CBT conceptualization and intervention. Recommend training in schema therapy or mentalization-based approaches.
