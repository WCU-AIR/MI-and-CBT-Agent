## SUMMARY OF CLINICAL CONCERNS

1. **No Application of CBT Techniques (Score: 0/6)**
   - Zero use of cognitive restructuring, thought records, behavioral activation, or behavioral experiments
   - Session consisted of supportive counseling, not evidence-based CBT
   - Core cognitions (guilt, self-blame, belief about being bad mother) completely unaddressed

2. **No Coherent Treatment Strategy (Score: 0/6)**
   - Disconnected suggestions offered without rationale or connection to understanding of depression
   - No conceptualization of how depression is maintained
   - No measurement plan or way to evaluate progress

3. **Failure to Identify and Work with Central Cognitions (Score: 1/6)**
   - Yvonne's core beliefs ("I'm a bad mom," "I don't deserve this," "Baby is rejecting me") never named or explored
   - No systematic examination of evidence for these thoughts
   - No development of alternative perspectives

4. **No Homework or Between-Session Work (Score: 0/6)**
   - No concrete tasks assigned
   - No opportunity for patient to practice new skills
   - Work remains confined to therapy room

5. **Minimal Feedback-Seeking (Score: 1/6)**
   - Patient's expressed doubts never explored
   - No checks for understanding or agreement
   - No collaborative problem-solving when patient expressed resistance

6. **Abandoned Agenda and Structure (Score: 3/6)**
   - Initial agenda never operationalized into session plan
   - Session drifted into repetitive reassurance
   - No return to agenda or checking whether session addressed priorities

### Strengths (Limited)

- Initial collaborative agenda-setting question
- Non-hostile, warm demeanor
- Attempt to normalize experience
- No overt boundary violations

### Recommendations for Immediate Improvement

1. **Enroll in structured CBT training program** (e.g., Academy of Cognitive Therapy, university-based program)
2. **Seek weekly supervision** from experienced CBT therapist
3. **Learn core techniques:** cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, thought records
4. **Develop coherent conceptualization** for each patient before session
5. **Eliminate personal anecdotes** and maintain professional boundaries
6. **Systematically elicit feedback** at multiple points in session
7. **Assign specific homework** with clear rationale and follow-up plan
8. **Conduct thorough suicide risk assessment** for all depressed patients
9. **Use guided discovery** instead of direct advice
10. **Review session recordings** with supervisor for specific feedback

**Overall Clinical Impression:** This therapist demonstrates significant gaps in CBT competence and requires immediate, intensive training and supervision. Without intervention, patient care will continue to suffer. With appropriate support, improvement is possible.
