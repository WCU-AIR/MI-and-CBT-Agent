## SUPERVISOR RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE ACTION

**Priority 1 (Before Next Session):**
- Develop explicit case conceptualization and treatment strategy in writing
- Conduct thorough functional analysis of anger episodes (specific triggers, thoughts, emotions, behaviors, consequences)
- Create written treatment plan with clear, measurable goals and intervention sequence

**Priority 2 (Next Session):**
- Establish structured, collaborative agenda with explicit time allocation
- Teach and practice one coping skill with explicit instruction and in-session rehearsal (not just suggestion)
- Assign specific, measurable, tracked homework with clear rationale and written materials

**Priority 3 (Ongoing):**
- Implement systematic feedback elicitation at three points per session
- Use Socratic questioning and guided discovery instead of direct suggestion
- Conduct parent sessions to teach contingency management and environmental modification
- Address ADHD medication compliance and optimization with prescribing provider
- Review homework at start of each session and adjust interventions based on effectiveness

**Supervision Frequency:** Weekly individual supervision with CBT expert recommended until competency improves to 4/6 or higher on core items (7, 9, 10, 11).
