### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Significant weakness. Ahmed never articulates desire, reasons, ability, or commitment to reduce mini-cigar use. Statements are primarily sustain talk or neutral ("I don't see why," "I don't worry," "I'm not promising I'll change"). Clinician validates and reflects rather than strategically evoking change talk. Only change talk that emerges is Ahmed's agreement to "pay attention"—a behavioral compromise, not genuine motivation. Misses multiple opportunities to ask: "What would be the benefits?" "What concerns you?" "How would this help you?" "What would it take to convince you?" This is a critical gap in MI practice.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and validated but not strategically softened or redirected. Ahmed's minimization ("just little cigars," "not a big deal," "overreaction") is reflected empathically but not reframed or challenged. Clinician uses some double-sided reflections but could more actively employ reframing, exploration of ambivalence, or normalization of change. Approach is generally to accept sustain talk and move forward rather than strategically challenge it. While collaborative, this may inadvertently reinforce Ahmed's resistance.

## Overall Clinical Summary

**Session Score: 3.6/5 – Adequate to Good MI Competence**

**Strengths:**
- Strong collaborative alliance with genuine power-sharing and respect for autonomy
- Consistent acceptance and empathy with warm, nonjudgmental tone
- Skillful reflective listening that accurately captures Ahmed's perspective
- Excellent validation of lifestyle constraints and practical concerns
- Good use of double-sided reflections acknowledging ambivalence
- Strategic confidence scaling without pressure
- Avoidance of confrontation and maintenance of alliance despite resistance

**Critical Gaps:**
- **Minimal change talk evocation** — Ahmed never articulates own reasons, desire, ability, or commitment to reduce mini-cigars
- **Sustain talk not actively softened** — Minimization is validated rather than strategically reframed
- **Limited emotional exploration** — Doesn't deeply explore why mini-cigars matter to Ahmed or what he would be losing
- **Subtle agenda-setting** — "At the same time" pattern gently redirects rather than inviting co-construction
- **Direction not maintained** — Early tangents dilute focus; direction emerges gradually rather than being established early

**Impact on Client Engagement:**
Session demonstrates strong alliance-building but is unlikely to generate intrinsic motivation for change. Ahmed agrees to behavioral compromise (symptom awareness) but does not articulate own reasons for change. Any resulting behavior change will likely be externally motivated (clinician concern, wife's pressure) rather than internally motivated, which research shows is less sustainable. Ahmed remains in precontemplation—has not moved toward considering mini-cigar reduction as something he wants to do.

**Most Critical Improvements Needed:**
1. Strategically evoke change talk through open questions designed to draw out Ahmed's own reasons and concerns
2. Actively soften sustain talk through reframing and strategic questioning rather than simple validation
3. Connect mini-cigar reduction to Ahmed's stated values (control, preventing work disruption, family health)
