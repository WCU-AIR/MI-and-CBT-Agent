## Overall Assessment

**Total Score: 1.4/5**

**Summary:** This session represents a **fundamental departure from Motivational Interviewing principles**. The psychiatrist adopts a directive, expert-driven, problem-focused approach inconsistent with MI spirit. While basic supportive counseling skills are present (politeness, eventual focus on target behavior), core MI techniques—evocation, collaboration, partnership, autonomy support, and change talk cultivation—are largely absent or poorly executed.

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- Expert stance dominates; collaborative partnership absent
- Minimal genuine empathy; reflections are factual rather than emotional
- No genuine evocation of Viktor's own motivations or values
- Sustain talk not addressed strategically; often accepted or reinforced
- Premature action planning before understanding Viktor's perspective or readiness
- Leading questions designed to move Viktor toward clinician's conclusion
- Superficial acceptance without authentic unconditional positive regard

**Impact on Client Engagement:** This session is likely to **decrease intrinsic motivation for change and increase resistance**. Viktor enters in precontemplation; psychiatrist's directive approach triggers psychological reactance. External motivation (pleasing wife) is reinforced rather than developing intrinsic motivation. Viktor's final statement ("I'm doing this my way") suggests defensiveness and low likelihood of sustained behavioral change.

**MI Competency Assessment:** Psychiatrist demonstrates **minimal competence in MI**. Immediate training and supervision in Motivational Interviewing is essential before continuing to work with clients using this approach.

**Highest Priority Recommendations:**
1. Shift from expert to collaborative partner stance
2. Develop genuine empathic understanding before suggesting changes
3. Evoke Viktor's own reasons for change rather than imposing clinician's agenda
4. Avoid premature action planning until intrinsic motivation is developed
5. Address sustain talk strategically using double-sided reflections and reframing
