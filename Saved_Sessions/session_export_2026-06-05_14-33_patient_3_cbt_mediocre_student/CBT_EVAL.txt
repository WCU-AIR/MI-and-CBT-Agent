## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Observed:**
- Warm, genuine, non-judgmental interpersonal style
- Basic empathic attunement and accurate reflection of explicit content
- Identifies some relevant cognitions (shame, rumination)
- Attempts collaborative language in opening
- Maintains appropriate professional boundaries
- Accessible communication for adolescent

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Remediation:**
1. **Safety Planning (HIGHEST PRIORITY):** No safety plan developed despite recent high-lethality suicide attempt. No assessment of current suicidal ideation, warning signs, or crisis resources. This is a serious clinical and ethical gap.
2. **Risk Assessment:** No systematic assessment of current suicide risk, passive death wishes, or ongoing safety concerns.
3. **Collaborative Approach:** Interventions are therapist-suggested rather than collaboratively developed. Hannah positioned as advice-recipient rather than active agent.
4. **Guided Discovery:** Minimal Socratic questioning. Relies on direct suggestion and advice-giving.
5. **Treatment Conceptualization:** No coherent strategy. Missing critical components for suicide prevention (impulsivity management, emotion regulation, relapse prevention).
6. **Technique Application:** Superficial implementation of cognitive restructuring and behavioral activation. Lacks structure, rationale, and systematic follow-through.
7. **Homework Review:** Session 2 omits homework review—fundamental CBT component.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- Suicide risk assessment and safety planning protocols
- Guided discovery and Socratic questioning practice
- Case conceptualization and treatment planning
- Systematic CBT technique application
- Session structure and agenda-setting
- Recorded session review with direct feedback

**Training Recommendations:**
- Formal training in CBT for suicide prevention (CAMS, SPI, or equivalent)
- Intensive supervision (weekly minimum)
- Role-play practice of safety planning and risk assessment
- Review of evidence-based CBT protocols for adolescent suicide
