## Summary Assessment

### Strengths
- Clear focus maintained on vaccine decision-making throughout session
- Basic warmth and respect demonstrated in opening and closing
- Accurate factual reflections of Elena's stated concerns
- Acknowledgment of barriers Elena identifies
- Appropriate summary capturing key content
- Non-confrontational tone; no arguing or judgment

### Critical Areas for Improvement
- **Directive language dominates** creating subtle pressure rather than supporting autonomy
- **Expert stance throughout**; clinician positions self as authority with solutions
- **Minimal evocation** of Elena's values, motivations, ambivalence, and ideas
- **Superficial empathy** reflecting content but missing emotional nuance
- **No strategic cultivation of change talk** despite opportunities
- **Sustain talk not skillfully addressed**; either ignored or directly challenged
- **Advice-giving replaces collaborative problem-solving**
- **Autonomy undermined** through prescriptive language and implicit pressure
- **Partnership absent**; Elena is recipient of advice, not co-creator
- **Ambivalence not engaged** as meaningful clinical phenomenon

### Clinical Impact
This session represents supportive counseling but not motivational interviewing. Likely to produce compliance rather than genuine motivation for change. Elena agrees to clinician's suggestions but does not express authentic commitment to vaccination. Final statement ("I'm still a little worried, but at least now I've got a plan") suggests compliance rather than autonomous decision-making. Compliance-based approach risks long-term ambivalence and potential vaccine hesitancy if Elena does not develop intrinsic motivation and deep understanding of reasons for vaccination.

### Primary Recommendation
**Shift from directive to evocative stance.** Replace "should" and "probably" language with genuine questions that evoke Elena's own thinking. Stop offering solutions; start asking what Elena thinks would help. This is the highest priority for developing authentic MI competence.
