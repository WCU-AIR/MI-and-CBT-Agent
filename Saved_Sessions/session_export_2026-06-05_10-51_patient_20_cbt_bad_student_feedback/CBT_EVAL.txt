## SUMMARY OF CLINICAL CONCERNS

**Strengths Identified:**

- Allows patient adequate space to speak
- Attempts to offer encouragement and hope

**Critical Deficiencies:**
1. **Complete absence of CBT structure and technique** – No agenda, case conceptualization, or treatment strategy; no cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, or other CBT interventions
2. **Failure to address suicidal ideation** – Patient explicitly mentions thinking of death; no assessment, safety planning, or risk management conducted
3. **Inappropriate therapist self-disclosure** – Repeated personal anecdotes violate professional boundaries and consume session time
4. **Lack of collaboration and guided discovery** – Therapist tells rather than asks; patient is passive rather than active partner
5. **Failure to identify and target core cognitions and behaviors** – Central beliefs and behavioral patterns not explored; peripheral behaviors targeted instead
6. **No systematic assessment or monitoring** – No baseline assessment, mood tracking, or systematic progress evaluation
7. **Inadequate homework implementation** – Vague assignment, no rationale, superficial review, no adjustment
