## Summary Assessment

### **Strengths:**
- ✓ Strong opening and collaborative stance establishing warm, respectful tone
- ✓ Consistent validation and genuine acceptance without judgment
- ✓ Accurate reflective listening capturing emotional and relational nuance
- ✓ Affirmation of resilience and compliance history
- ✓ Successful evocation of intrinsic motivation around family connection
- ✓ Consistent autonomy support and non-prescriptive language
- ✓ Non-confrontational approach maintaining MI spirit throughout

### **Areas for Improvement:**
- ✗ Limited strategic amplification of change talk through deepening questions and scaling
- ✗ Insufficient softening of sustain talk using advanced MI techniques (double-sided reflections, reframing)
- ✗ Lack of clear, strategic direction progressively narrowing toward specific change goals
- ✗ Limited evocation of Tariq's own insight into his role and contribution to dynamics
- ✗ Occasional subtle expert framing rather than inviting client meaning-making
- ✗ Inconsistent deepening of reflections—some sophisticated, others surface-level
- ✗ Limited use of scaling or other quantitative amplification techniques
- ✗ Sustain talk allowed to occupy significant space without strategic pivoting

---

## **Clinical Recommendations (Priority Order)**

**1. Strategically Amplify Change Talk:** When Tariq produces change talk, respond with follow-up questions that deepen rather than simply validate. Use scaling questions and evocative reflections to draw out more elaboration.

**2. Use Double-Sided Reflections:** When Tariq expresses sustain talk, acknowledge both the sustain talk and underlying change talk/capacity simultaneously to gently highlight his own agency.

**3. Evoke Tariq's Own Insight:** When he externalizes blame, use gentle, curious questioning to help him reflect on his own contribution: "What do you think they might be noticing about your attitude?"

**4. Establish Clear Strategic Direction:** Early in session, clarify the specific change target with Tariq and strategically link all exploration back to this target throughout.

**5. Develop Complex, Multi-Layered Reflections:** Move beyond simple content restatement to capture multiple dimensions—emotion, values, ambivalence, resilience—in single reflections.

---

**Overall Competency Level:** Adequate foundational MI with solid collaboration and acceptance, but lacking strategic execution needed to maximize intrinsic motivation for change. With focused practice on amplifying change talk, softening sustain talk, and maintaining strategic direction, clinician could advance from 3.6/5 to 4+/5 competency.
