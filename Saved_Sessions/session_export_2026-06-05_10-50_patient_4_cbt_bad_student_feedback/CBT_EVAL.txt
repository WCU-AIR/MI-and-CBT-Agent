## CLINICAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT

**Risk Level:** HIGH

This session represents a **fundamental departure from evidence-based CBT**. While interpersonal warmth is present, the session lacks core structural and technical elements defining cognitive-behavioral therapy. 

**Specific Concerns:**
1. **Reinforcement of maladaptive patterns:** Repeated reassurance reinforces the patient's reassurance-seeking cycle, which maintains her anxiety
2. **Increased emotional dependency:** Directive approach without collaborative problem-solving increases patient's reliance on therapist for validation
3. **Missed intervention opportunities:** Catastrophic thoughts, safety behaviors, and activity avoidance—core maintaining factors—remain unaddressed
4. **Unlikely to produce change:** Generic wellness advice is not evidence-based for adjustment disorder with anxiety
5. **Potential harm:** Patient may experience increased frustration, dependency, and sense of failure when suggestions do not address underlying anxiety mechanisms
