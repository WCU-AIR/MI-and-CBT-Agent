## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Quality Summary:** This session represents inadequate CBT practice with significant clinical risks. The therapist demonstrated foundational interpersonal warmth but failed to implement core CBT components essential for evidence-based treatment.

**Most Critical Issues (Rank Order):**

1. **SAFETY ASSESSMENT FAILURE (URGENT)** — No assessment of active suicidal ideation or recent self-injury despite documented symptoms. No safety plan. This is a serious clinical and liability concern.

2. **No Case Conceptualization** — Therapist lacks explicit understanding of trauma-anxiety-avoidance cycle maintaining patient's three diagnoses.

3. **No Treatment Strategy** — Interventions are disconnected and ad hoc. No multi-phase treatment plan or trauma-focused work.

4. **Superficial Cognitive and Behavioral Work** — No systematic thought records, evidence examination, or structured exposure hierarchy.

5. **Minimal Collaboration and Guided Discovery** — Patient passive; therapist directive. Limited Socratic questioning.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- Safety assessment and planning protocols
- Case conceptualization development
- Trauma-informed CBT for adolescents
- Thought records and cognitive restructuring
- Exposure hierarchy and behavioral activation
- Collaborative agenda-setting and feedback elicitation

**Training Needs:** Comprehensive CBT skills training with emphasis on complex cases (trauma + anxiety + suicidality). Consider structured CBT certification program.
