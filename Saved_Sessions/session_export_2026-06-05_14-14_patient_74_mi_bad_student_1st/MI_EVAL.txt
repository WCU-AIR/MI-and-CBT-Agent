### Critical Findings

This session represents a **complete failure of Motivational Interviewing practice**. The psychiatrist violates virtually every core MI principle and demonstrates zero competence in MI methodology.

**Fundamental Violations:**
- Authoritarian, coercive approach antithetical to MI spirit
- Explicit rejection of collaboration and client perspective
- Systematic use of shame and threat as motivational tools
- Active invalidation of client's emotional experience
- Zero reflective listening or empathic attunement
- Suppression rather than evocation of change talk

**Impact on Client:**
This session will **significantly decrease** Linda's likelihood of engaging in change. Research demonstrates that confrontational, coercive approaches trigger psychological reactance and increase sustain talk. Client will likely feel judged, misunderstood, and more entrenched in ambivalence. Therapeutic alliance has been damaged.

### Immediate Recommendations

1. **Cease MI practice** until formal training completed
2. **Seek formal MI training** (minimum 20 hours with supervision)
3. **Develop reflective listening skills** through deliberate practice
4. **Replace ultimatums** with open-ended, curious questions
5. **Eliminate judgmental language** and shame-based motivation
6. **Validate ambivalence** as normal stage of change
7. **Amplify client's own change talk** rather than imposing external reasons
8. **Establish true collaboration** through explicit partnership language
9. **Support autonomy** through affirmation of client's right to choose
10. **Seek individual supervision** with MI-trained clinician
