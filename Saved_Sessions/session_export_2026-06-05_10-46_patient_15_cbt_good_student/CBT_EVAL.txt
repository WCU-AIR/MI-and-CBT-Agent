Session would be appropriate for adult with pure anxiety disorder but is incomplete for child with physiological condition complicated by secondary anxiety. **Recommendation:** Implement all 11 recommendations from detailed evaluation, with highest priority on: (1) comprehensive enuresis assessment and intervention, (2) contingency planning, and (3) parental engagement. |
