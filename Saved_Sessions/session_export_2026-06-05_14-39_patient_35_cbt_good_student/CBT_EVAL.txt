**SUPERVISOR NOTES:**

This therapist demonstrates **competent but not yet expert-level CBT practice**. The session shows good foundational skills in collaboration, guided discovery, and interpersonal effectiveness. However, the narrow clinical focus, incomplete attention to the medical-psychiatric interface, and lack of rigorous technique application for this complex older adult patient represent significant limitations. 

**Immediate Priority:** Develop a comprehensive, individualized treatment plan that addresses all of this patient's symptoms (anhedonia, fatigue, insomnia, social withdrawal, concentration difficulties) within the context of his Parkinson's disease and secondary depression. This requires medical-psychiatric integration and coordination with neurology.

**Supervision Focus:** (1) Case formulation and treatment planning for complex medical-psychiatric presentations; (2) Use of written CBT tools for older adults with cognitive deficits; (3) Behavioral activation and activity scheduling; (4) Family engagement and systems perspective; (5) Specialized knowledge of depression in medical conditions.

**Likelihood of Therapeutic Success:** Moderate. This session provides a reasonable foundation for behavioral activation work on anhedonia, but without addressing the broader clinical picture and medical-psychiatric interface, outcomes may be limited. With recommended modifications, this therapist could provide excellent, evidence-based CBT for this patient.
