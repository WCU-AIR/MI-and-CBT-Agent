**Session Quality Assessment:** This session represents a fundamental departure from evidence-based CBT practice. **Key Concerns:**
- No agenda established; no treatment targets identified
- No CBT techniques applied; no homework assigned
- Excessive self-disclosure and boundary violations

**Recommendation:** Immediate remedial training and close supervision required before continuing patient care.
