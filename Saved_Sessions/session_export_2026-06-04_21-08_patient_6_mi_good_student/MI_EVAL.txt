### Overall Clinical Summary

**Session Quality: Exemplary (4.6/5)**

This session demonstrates exceptional Motivational Interviewing practice with sophisticated application of MI principles across all domains. The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates acceptance, empathy, collaboration, autonomy support, and evocation through strategic questioning, complex reflections, and genuine partnership.

**Key Strengths:**
- Exemplary opening and agenda-setting prioritizing client perspective
- Sophisticated, multi-layered reflections capturing emotional nuance and underlying motivations
- Strategic evocation of past successes building client's own resources and competence
- Permission-seeking before providing information demonstrating respect for autonomy
- Skillful handling of legitimate concerns through validation and reframing rather than dismissal
- Consistent autonomy support normalizing ambivalence and positioning client as ultimate decision-maker
- Inclusive language and partnership conveying shared responsibility and equal power-sharing

**Areas for Enhancement:**
- More explicit cultivation of commitment language after providing medical information (e.g., "What would help you move from considering to committing?")
- Deeper exploration of remaining caution when client expresses hesitation (e.g., "What specifically are you cautious about?")
- More direct softening of specific sustain talk barriers
- Explicit co-construction of concrete monitoring and support plan with specific timelines
- Clarification of client's decision and next steps before session conclusion
- More direct exploration of whether phenelzine continuation is flexible or necessary

**Impact:**
This session creates psychological safety allowing client to express concerns openly without fear of judgment or pressure. By evoking past successes and positioning client as expert on own experience, clinician builds self-efficacy and intrinsic motivation. Skillful handling of legitimate safety concerns demonstrates respect for client's intelligence and agency, more likely to build trust and commitment than reassurance-seeking would achieve. Client's confidence increased and she is "considering it more seriously," though explicit decision clarification would strengthen conclusion.

**Recommendation:**
This represents exemplary MI practice that should serve as a model for trainees. Minor enhancements in explicit commitment cultivation and decision clarification would elevate this to perfect practice.
