## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **HIGHEST PRIORITY (Address Before Next Session)**

1. **Conduct Comprehensive Safety Assessment and Develop Written Safety Plan**
   - Explicitly assess current suicidal ideation, intent, and plan
   - Identify warning signs, triggers, and coping strategies
   - Create written safety plan with crisis resources
   - Review at start of each session

2. **Establish Structured CBT Treatment Plan**
   - Collaboratively identify specific target problems: (a) trauma/intrusive thoughts, (b) emotion dysregulation, (c) suicidal behavior prevention
   - Set measurable treatment goals
   - Outline specific CBT techniques to be used
   - Document plan and review with patient

3. **Eliminate Excessive Therapist Self-Disclosure**
   - Significantly reduce personal anecdotes
   - Redirect focus to patient's experience and needs
   - Establish clear professional boundaries
   - Use self-disclosure only when clinically indicated and keep brief

### **CRITICAL (Implement in Next 2-3 Sessions)**

4. **Implement Trauma-Focused Cognitive Processing**
   - Use thought records to identify intrusive thoughts about overdose
   - Examine evidence for/against trauma-related thoughts
   - Gradually expose Hannah to memories while teaching distress tolerance
   - Address core beliefs about the event

5. **Teach Specific Emotion Regulation and Distress Tolerance Skills**
   - Move beyond generic advice to evidence-based skills (DBT emotion regulation module)
   - Teach opposite action, TIPP skills, emotion identification
   - Practice skills in session
   - Assign homework to practice between sessions

6. **Use Socratic Questioning and Guided Discovery**
   - Replace directive advice with questions that guide Hannah to insights
   - Ask: "What do you think...?" "How did that feel...?" "What would happen if...?"
   - Let Hannah generate solutions and test them out
   - Reinforce her problem-solving

### **IMPORTANT (Implement Ongoing)**

7. **Assign Specific, Structured Homework with Clear Rationale**
   - Replace informal suggestions with formal assignments
   - Provide written instructions and forms
   - Explain rationale for each assignment
   - Check understanding and commitment
   - Review homework at start of next session

8. **Implement Behavioral Activation for Low Mood**
   - Conduct behavioral analysis of meaningful activities
   - Create activity schedule with specific times
   - Have Hannah rate mood before/after activities
   - Problem-solve barriers to engagement

9. **Develop Cognitive Restructuring Skills for Self-Blame**
   - Identify self-critical thoughts ("I'm hard on myself for what happened")
   - Examine evidence for/against thoughts
   - Develop more balanced, realistic thoughts
   - Practice new thoughts until automatic

10. **Establish Clear Session Structure**
    - Begin each session with brief agenda
    - Allocate specific time to each topic
    - Maintain focus and redirect tangential discussion
    - End with summary and homework assignment

11. **Elicit and Respond to Patient Feedback**
    - Ask for feedback at multiple points in session
    - Adjust approach based on Hannah's responses
    - Explicitly ask: "Does this make sense?" "Is this helpful?" "What would be more useful?"

12. **Seek Supervision and Training**
    - Attend CBT training workshops, particularly trauma-focused CBT (TF-CBT) and DBT
    - Participate in regular supervision with experienced CBT clinician
    - Record and review own sessions
    - Read evidence-based literature on adolescent trauma and suicide

---

**OVERALL CLINICAL IMPRESSION:**

This session represents a significant departure from evidence-based CBT. While the therapist demonstrated adequate interpersonal warmth, the absence of core CBT components—particularly trauma-focused intervention, safety planning, guided discovery, and structured technique application—makes this approach clinically inadequate for a patient with recent high-lethality suicidal behavior and ongoing trauma symptoms. Hannah left the session with generic suggestions without developing specific skills for processing trauma, managing intrusive thoughts, regulating emotions, or preventing future crises. **Immediate corrective action is required.**
