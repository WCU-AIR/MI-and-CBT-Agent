## SUPERVISOR NOTES

### Strengths Identified
- Genuine warmth, concern, and non-judgmental acceptance
- Supportive, encouraging tone without criticism or dismissal
- Willingness to listen; adequate space for patient expression
- Expressed availability and support ("I'm here for you")

### Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention

**Structural/Process Deficiencies:**
- No agenda established; session lacked focus and direction
- No systematic feedback-seeking; patient understanding not verified
- No homework assigned; no between-session tasks
- No CBT techniques applied; session was advice-giving, not therapy
- No coherent treatment strategy; interventions were disconnected

**Clinical/Conceptual Deficiencies:**
- Missed critical cognitive patterns (perfectionism, shame, catastrophizing, hopelessness)
- Did not explore maintaining factors (avoidance, rumination, guilt)
- Minimized serious concerns (suicidal ideation risk, family pressure)
- Did not assess or address cultural context (Dominican identity, acculturation stress, possible sexual orientation concerns)
- No psychoeducation about ADHD, depression, anxiety, or their interaction
- Did not explore function of current behaviors (video game use, avoidance, withdrawal)

**Interpersonal/Collaborative Deficiencies:**
- Therapist-centered responses (repeatedly discussed own experience)
- Expert-advice-giver role rather than collaborative partnership
- Patient passive and compliant rather than actively engaged
- No collaborative empiricism or shared problem-solving
- Barriers to change were dismissed rather than problem-solved

**Technique Deficiencies:**
- Zero thought records, behavioral experiments, or structured cognitive work
- No behavioral activation or activity scheduling
- No exposure or systematic desensitization
- No worry time or sleep hygiene protocol
- No problem-solving or skills training
- Generic advice instead of evidence-based intervention

### Recommended Training and Supervision Plan

1. **Immediate:** Review CBT fundamentals (agenda-setting, collaboration, guided discovery)
2. **Week 1-2:** Observe experienced CBT therapist conducting session with similar patient
3. **Week 3+:** Direct supervision with session recordings; focus on agenda-setting and homework assignment
4. **Ongoing:** Complete structured CBT training program (e.g., Beck Institute, ABCT)
5. **Monthly:** Supervision meetings to review case formulations and technique application

### Patient Safety Considerations

**URGENT:** Conduct proper suicide risk assessment at next session. Patient expressed suicidal ideation risk ("I thought I should quit college") which was not properly assessed. Assess intent, plan, protective factors. Document thoroughly. Consider psychiatry consultation if risk is moderate-to-high.
