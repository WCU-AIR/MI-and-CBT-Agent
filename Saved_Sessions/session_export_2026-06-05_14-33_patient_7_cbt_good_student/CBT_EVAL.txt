## DETAILED COMMENTS BY DOMAIN

### Strengths Demonstrated:
- Strong therapeutic alliance and empathic attunement
- Collaborative agenda-setting and homework design
- Appropriate pacing and adaptation to patient's presentation
- Systematic cognitive formulation
- Use of Socratic questioning for guided discovery
- Multiple feedback elicitation points

### Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Remediation:
- **Fundamental diagnostic error:** Treated secondary psychosis as primary psychiatric illness
- **Inappropriate treatment approach:** Cognitive restructuring for hallucinations secondary to neurodegeneration
- **Failure to assess neurocognitive capacity:** Did not evaluate whether patient can implement cognitive techniques given memory/executive function deficits
- **Absence of medical coordination:** No discussion of medication, neurology consultation, or medical management
- **Inadequate behavioral technique:** Grounding mentioned but not taught or practiced
- **Unrealistic expectations:** Did not set realistic goals for progressive neurological condition
- **No safety planning:** Given history of aggression and paranoid delusions

### Recommended Next Steps:
1. Immediately revise case conceptualization to acknowledge Huntington's disease etiology
2. Coordinate with neurology and psychiatry for medication optimization
3. Conduct neurocognitive assessment and adapt interventions accordingly
4. Involve family in treatment and provide psychoeducation
5. Develop realistic, collaborative goals focused on coping and quality of life
6. Replace or supplement cognitive restructuring with behavioral coping strategies for hallucinations
7. Simplify homework to match neurocognitive capacity
8. Develop safety plan addressing risk of aggression
9. Increase frequency of feedback and collaborative decision-making
10. Develop long-term treatment plan accounting for progressive nature of condition
