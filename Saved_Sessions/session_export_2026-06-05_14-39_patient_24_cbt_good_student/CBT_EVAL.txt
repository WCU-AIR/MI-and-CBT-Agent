## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Action Items:

1. **Integrate Behavioral Interventions:** In next session, explicitly introduce exposure and response prevention (ERP) as core treatment component. Conduct in-session behavioral experiment to test catastrophic predictions.

2. **Develop Comprehensive Conceptualization:** Explore relationship between recent psychotic episode, historical contamination concerns, current stressors, and present anxiety. Integrate into treatment planning.

3. **Enhance Homework Specificity:** Revise homework to include: (a) specific time durations (3-5 minutes, not "a few minutes"), (b) structured tracking tool (thought record or homework log), (c) explicit behavioral experiment framing, (d) contingency planning for barriers.

4. **Provide Psychoeducation:** Develop brief handout explaining anxiety cycle, why compulsive behaviors maintain anxiety, and how exposure/response prevention breaks the cycle.

5. **Strengthen Feedback-Seeking:** Increase frequency of understanding checks throughout session. After each new concept or technique, explicitly ask: "Does this make sense? How does this land for you? What questions do you have?"

### Strengths to Reinforce:

- Excellent collaborative stance and therapeutic alliance
- Strong empathic attunement and accurate reflections
- Skillful use of Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Clear, coherent presentation of cognitive model
- Appropriate pacing and session structure

### Areas for Development:

- Comprehensive case conceptualization integrating full clinical picture
- Behavioral interventions (exposure, response prevention, behavioral experiments)
- Specificity and structure in homework design
- Depth of cognitive work (moving beyond surface-level evidence examination)
- Consistency in feedback-seeking throughout session
- Psychoeducation and skills training

### Recommended Training:

- Advanced training in exposure and response prevention (ERP) for anxiety and OCD-spectrum disorders
- Case conceptualization workshop focusing on integrating multiple clinical factors
- Behavioral experimentation techniques and in-session practice
- Homework design and tracking tools
