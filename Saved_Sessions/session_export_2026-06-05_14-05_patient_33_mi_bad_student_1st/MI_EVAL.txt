Client offers change talk ("put money in wallet for cab," "focus on one thing at a time"), but clinician provides no response or explicitly rejects it. No genuine curiosity about client's reasons for change, values, or ideas. Active suppression of client motivation rather than evoking and amplifying it.

## Overall Clinical Summary

**Overall Session Score: 1.1/5**

**MI Competency Level:** Pre-MI / Fundamental Misalignment with MI Principles

**Session Characteristics:**
- Authoritarian, judgmental, and dismissive throughout
- Mechanical, scripted delivery with repeated identical questions (5+ times)
- Active undermining of client autonomy and motivation
- Confrontational approach to client resistance
- Dismissal of client's stated priorities in favor of clinician agenda
- Complete absence of collaborative partnership

**Impact on Client Engagement:**
This session is **highly likely to decrease client motivation for change**. Client enters with genuine ambivalence and some openness ("I do want to figure out how to keep from drinking and driving again") and offers concrete ideas. By session end, client is politely disengaging ("Maybe once I get through that mess, I can think about the rest"), indicating withdrawal and reduced likelihood of implementing recommendations.

**Critical Deficiencies:**
1. No unconditional positive regard or acceptance
2. No empathic understanding or reflective listening
3. No evocation of client motivation or ideas
4. No respect for client autonomy or readiness
5. No recognition or amplification of change talk
6. Confrontational rather than collaborative approach

**Immediate Recommendations:**
- **Urgent:** Formal MI training and supervision required before continuing practice
- Fundamental philosophical shift needed: from expert prescription to collaborative exploration
- Development of reflective listening skills as cornerstone of practice
- Training in identifying and amplifying change talk
- Adoption of collaborative, invitational language

**Whether Session Embodies MI Spirit:**
**No.** This session actively violates MI spirit and represents the opposite of MI practice. Clinician operates from traditional medical model (expert diagnosis/prescription) rather than MI model (collaborative exploration/evocation).
