## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Quality Assessment: INADEQUATE**

This session does not meet standards for evidence-based CBT. While the therapist demonstrated interpersonal warmth, critical gaps in structure, conceptualization, and technique represent a fundamental failure to deliver appropriate care for a patient with a serious progressive neurological condition.

**Key Concerns:**

1. **Misdiagnosis of Clinical Presentation:** Therapist treated progressive neurocognitive disorder as if it were a primary mood/motivational problem, leading to inappropriate interventions.

2. **Safety Issues:** REM sleep behavior disorder with violent dream enactment was treated superficially; no discussion of safety planning, medical management, or specialist referral.

3. **Unrealistic Expectations:** Repeated reassurance ("It'll get better, I promise") sets false expectations for progressive disease and may undermine trust.

4. **Lack of Collaboration:** Patient positioned as passive recipient rather than active partner in treatment.

5. **Absence of CBT Structure:** No agenda, no homework, no coherent strategy, minimal guided discovery.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**

- Psychoeducation about NCDLB, RSBD, and age-related neurocognitive conditions
- CBT competency training (agenda-setting, guided discovery, homework, collaboration)
- Case conceptualization and treatment planning for medical/neurological conditions
- Ethical considerations in working with progressive diseases
- Professional boundaries and appropriate self-disclosure

**Next Steps:**

Schedule immediate supervision session. Review session recording with focus on agenda-setting, guided discovery, and collaboration. Develop corrective action plan before next patient session.
