## Overall Session Summary

**Current MI Competency Level:** Basic-to-Intermediate

**Overall Strengths:**
- Strong collaborative opening that invites Marco to set agenda
- Consistent, genuine affirmation without generic praise
- Good reflective listening capturing emotional nuance
- Effective evocation of Marco's past successes and resources
- Solid cultivation of change talk and reinforcement of commitment
- Maintains nonjudgmental acceptance and warmth throughout
- Progressive, logical session structure

**Overall Areas for Improvement:**
- Limited depth in exploring ambivalence and underlying concerns
- Psychiatrist-led solution development rather than evoking Marco's own ideas
- Insufficient strategic softening of sustain talk
- Power imbalance: solutions feel psychiatrist-guided rather than co-constructed
- Limited exploration of deeper values, motivation, and meaning
- Confidence accepted without stress-testing against real barriers
- Lacks specificity in establishing measurable goals

**Likelihood of Sustained Change:** Moderate. Marco leaves with strong stated commitment (10/10 confidence) and concrete action plan, which is positive for short-term motivation. However, without deeper exploration of values, potential barriers, and genuine resolution of ambivalence about juice, long-term adherence may be fragile. Psychiatrist-guided nature of solutions may reduce Marco's sense of ownership and self-efficacy.

**Recommendation:** With focused practice on deepening exploration of ambivalence, evoking rather than affirming solutions, and strengthening genuine partnership, this clinician could advance toward excellent MI practice.
