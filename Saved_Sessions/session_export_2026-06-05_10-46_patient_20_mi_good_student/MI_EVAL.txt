### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Demonstrates some evocation of Anya's own motivations and resources but misses significant opportunities for systematic, strategic evocation. Asks some good open questions ("What's worked for you in the past?") that draw out her resources and confidence. However, relies heavily on affirming and validating her statements rather than evoking deeper exploration. When Anya mentions wanting to travel to Egypt, clinician validates but doesn't evoke: "Tell me more about Egypt—what draws you there? What would it mean for you?" Doesn't systematically evoke the four pillars of change talk (desire, ability, reasons, need). Misses opportunities to amplify commitment language ("I wanna really try this time") through strategic questioning like "What's different this time?"

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Demonstrates basic recognition of Anya's change talk but fails to systematically evoke and amplify it through strategic questioning. Anya produces substantial change talk throughout ("I really do wanna travel," "I'm not gonna make a decent mom the way I am," "I wanna really try this time"), but clinician primarily affirms rather than amplifies. Misses opportunities to ask evocative questions like "Tell me more about what traveling would mean for you?" or "What's different this time that makes you feel like you really want to try?" Doesn't use techniques like asking for elaboration, examples, or extremes to deepen change talk. Doesn't systematically draw out all four dimensions of change talk (desire, ability, reasons, need). Opportunity to strengthen intrinsic motivation through deeper exploration of her own reasons for change.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Demonstrates some skill in handling sustain talk but misses opportunities to strategically redirect it. Anya produces several instances of sustain talk (minimization: "just a bit of cash"; fear: "scared I'll feel left out"; status quo maintenance: "it's just what we do"). Clinician validates these concerns empathically but doesn't strategically reframe or explore discrepancies. When Anya expresses fear of missing out, clinician affirms but doesn't gently challenge: "Do you think your true friends would abandon you if you suggested something different?" Doesn't use double-sided reflections to acknowledge both sides while emphasizing change talk. When Anya minimizes gambling, clinician doesn't explore: "You say it's just a bit of cash, but earlier you mentioned you could have traveled several times with that money. Help me understand the real cost?" Sustain talk is acknowledged but not actively redirected toward change.

## Summary Assessment

### **Strengths:**
- ✓ Strong opening and collaborative stance establishing psychological safety
- ✓ Consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental approach
- ✓ Effective affirmation of client strengths and past successes
- ✓ Good complex reflections capturing emotional nuance and ambivalence
- ✓ Appropriate use of readiness scaling without pressure
- ✓ Validation of competing values (social connection vs. future goals)
- ✓ Practical, achievable next steps identified collaboratively

### **Areas for Improvement:**
- ✗ Insufficient systematic evocation of change talk; relies too heavily on validation
- ✗ Limited exploration of values-behavior connection to strengthen intrinsic motivation
- ✗ Weak strategic handling of sustain talk; validates but doesn't reframe or explore discrepancies
- ✗ Insufficient depth in exploring emotional experience (self-criticism, fear)
- ✗ No explicit accountability structure, written plan, or follow-up mechanism established
- ✗ Limited exploration of barriers, obstacles, and high-risk situations
- ✗ Missed opportunities for strategic questioning ("What's different this time?" "What concerns you most about staying the way you are?")

### **Clinical Competency Level:**
**Basic to Intermediate MI Competence** – Psychiatrist demonstrates foundational MI skills with strong acceptance, empathy, collaboration, and autonomy support. However, significant gaps exist in evocation, cultivation of change talk, and strategic handling of ambivalence. Clinician has MI spirit but needs to develop more sophisticated techniques for drawing out and amplifying client motivation.

### **Impact on Client Engagement:**
Anya leaves session with identified steps and collaborative relationship (positive). However, reliance on external validation rather than evoking internal motivation may limit sustained commitment when social pressure increases or change becomes difficult. Session maintains engagement but may not maximize intrinsic motivation for lasting change.

### **Most Critical Recommendations:**
1. **Shift from validation to systematic evocation** through strategic questioning about values, reasons, and commitment
2. **Strategically soften sustain talk** through reframing and exploration rather than empathic acknowledgment alone
3. **Establish explicit accountability structures** with written plans and follow-up mechanisms
4. **Deepen values-behavior connection** to strengthen link between dreams and behavioral changes needed
5. **Use double-sided reflections** to acknowledge ambivalence while gently emphasizing change talk
