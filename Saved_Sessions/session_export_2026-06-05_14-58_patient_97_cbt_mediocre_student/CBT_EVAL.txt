## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**URGENT CONCERNS:**

1. **Psychiatric Emergency Not Recognized:** Patient presents with steroid-induced bipolar disorder, severe mania, mood-congruent psychotic features, and recent catatonia. Therapist treated as mild anxiety disorder. This represents catastrophic failure in clinical assessment.

2. **Safety Assessment Absent:** No suicidal/homicidal ideation assessment. No psychosis monitoring. No hospitalization status reviewed. No medication compliance checked.

3. **Psychotic Content Ignored:** Patient stated "I'm invincible now—that I beat the cancer for good." Therapist normalized as "conflicting feelings" and did not explore, assess conviction, or address unreality. Delusions require direct assessment and antipsychotic medication, not cognitive restructuring.

4. **Inappropriate Treatment Level:** Outpatient psychotherapy with cognitive restructuring is not indicated for acute severe mania with psychosis. Patient requires psychiatric hospitalization, mood stabilizer initiation, antipsychotic medication, and medical evaluation.

5. **Lack of Structured CBT:** No agenda, no collaborative goal-setting, no systematic technique application, no homework follow-up, no session summary. Sessions were unstructured conversations with generic advice-giving.

6. **Session Repetition:** Session 2 identical to Session 1. No homework review. No treatment adjustment. Suggests lack of clinical reflection or memory of previous session.
