## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Strengths Demonstrated
- **Excellent Interpersonal Effectiveness (6/6):** Consistent warmth, validation, appropriate boundaries, genuine engagement
- **Strong Collaborative Practice (5/6):** Patient positioned as active partner; collaborative language throughout; patient suggestions incorporated
- **Competent Cognitive Restructuring (5/6):** Skillfully guided thought examination and alternative thought development using Socratic method
- **Effective Guided Discovery (5/6):** Used questioning to facilitate patient insights rather than imposing interpretations
- **Well-Structured Homework (5/6):** SMART assignment with obstacle planning and confidence assessment
- **Good Pacing and Session Flow (5/6):** Logical progression without tangential discussion
- **Adequate Feedback-Seeking (4/6):** Multiple instances of checking understanding

### Critical Deficiencies
- **Fundamental Misalignment with Eating Disorder Pathology (Score 2-3):** Session focused on fatigue cognitions rather than core eating disorder mechanisms
- **Inadequate Treatment Strategy (Score 2/6):** No comprehensive plan for medical monitoring, nutritional rehabilitation, weight restoration, or core cognition work
- **Insufficient Behavioral Intervention (Score 3/6):** Single snack addition inadequate for patient with BMI 14.7 and 600-calorie daily intake
- **Failure to Assess Core Cognitions (Score 3/6):** Did not explore beliefs about weight gain, body image, control, or necessity of restriction
- **No Ambivalence Assessment (Score 2/6):** Did not explore potential fear of weight gain or discrepancy between stated desire to gain weight and restrictive behavior
- **Inadequate Focus on Primary Behaviors (Score 3/6):** High activity level (3-4 hours walking) and restriction not made primary intervention targets
- **Limited Cultural Integration (Score 3/6):** Acknowledged but did not systematically explore how immigration, finances, and religion interact with eating disorder
- **No Medical Coordination (Score 0/6):** Did not establish plan for medical monitoring or coordination with medical providers

### Immediate Actions Required
1. **Reconceptualize case as anorexia nervosa** requiring comprehensive treatment, not fatigue requiring cognitive restructuring
2. **Establish medical monitoring and coordination** for patient safety
3. **Develop comprehensive nutritional rehabilitation plan** with specific caloric targets
4. **Assess and address ambivalence about weight gain**
5. **Identify and intervene on core eating disorder cognitions**

### Recommendation
Therapist should pursue specialized training in eating disorder-specific CBT through formal training programs, eating disorder specialist supervision, and consultation with eating disorder dietitians and medical colleagues.
