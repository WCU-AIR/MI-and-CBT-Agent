## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE ACTION

### 1. **URGENT: Recognize CBT is Inappropriate as Primary Intervention**
- Refer Dorothy to psychiatrist for medication optimization
- Refer to Huntington's disease specialist for neurological management
- These are evidence-based primary interventions for psychotic disorder due to neurocognitive disorder

### 2. **Conduct Comprehensive Safety Assessment**
- Assess for suicidal and homicidal ideation (patient reports judge "threatening to kill me")
- Develop safety plan
- Consider psychiatric hospitalization if risk is high

### 3. **Obtain Medical and Neurological History**
- Inquire about medical history, current medications, medication changes
- Assess for neurological symptoms (tremor, involuntary movements, balance problems, speech changes)
- Explore family history of neurological disease
- Determine if prior neurological workup completed

### 4. **Shift from Cognitive Restructuring to Reality Orientation and Coping Skills**
- Stop suggesting patient "remind herself" hallucinations aren't real
- Focus on: reality orientation, grounding techniques, coping strategies, acceptance-based approaches
- Teach and practice coping skills in session

### 5. **Establish Clear Collaborative Agenda with Realistic Goals**
- Explicitly establish prioritized agenda in writing
- Allocate specific time to each item
- Adjust for cognitive impairment with clear structure

### 6. **Simplify and Practice Homework In-Session**
- Assign only one or two tasks at a time
- Teach and practice each task in session
- Provide written instructions
- Check understanding before session ends

### 7. **Elicit Feedback Throughout Session**
- Regularly ask: "Does this make sense?" "How does this feel?" "Is this helpful?"
- Make feedback elicitation routine part of every session
- Adjust approach based on patient input

### 8. **Involve Husband as Collaborative Partner**
- Invite to sessions (with patient permission)
- Educate about Huntington's disease and symptoms
- Teach coping strategies and grounding techniques
- Discuss how to support Dorothy during hallucinations

### 9. **Recognize Limits of Competence**
- If not trained in treating psychotic disorders or neurocognitive disorders, refer to specialist
- Do not continue providing CBT as primary intervention for psychotic symptoms due to Huntington's disease
- Consult with supervisors or colleagues on complex cases
