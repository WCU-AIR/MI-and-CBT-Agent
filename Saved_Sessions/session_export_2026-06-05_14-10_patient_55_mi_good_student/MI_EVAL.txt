## Overall MI Competency Assessment

**Overall Session Score: 3.7/5 – Intermediate MI Competence**

### Summary of Strengths:
- **Exemplary autonomy support:** Consistently reinforces Isabella's right to choose; she explicitly states this enables her to consider change
- **Strong early empathy:** Opening reflections accurately capture ambivalence and cultural context with notable sophistication
- **Collaborative agenda-setting:** Opens with genuine question about Isabella's priorities
- **Avoidance of confrontation:** Skillfully avoids arguing with resistance; validates perspective and pivots toward values
- **Cultural sensitivity:** Explicitly acknowledges and respects Italian family traditions
- **Practical next steps:** Identifies concrete, achievable action (brochure review) that respects autonomy while moving toward change

### Summary of Areas for Improvement:
- **Inconsistent evocation:** Does not systematically amplify and reinforce Isabella's own motivations and reasons for change
- **Passive approach to sustain talk:** Acknowledges skepticism but does not actively redirect through double-sided reflections or gentle evidence introduction
- **Limited exploration of underlying concerns:** Does not deeply explore fears, anxieties, or what change would mean emotionally
- **Formulaic reflections in later exchanges:** Responses become more surface-level and predictable as session progresses
- **Missed self-efficacy building:** Confidence scale question lacks follow-up to build sense of agency
- **Implicit goal-setting:** Ultimate goal of reducing alcohol use is implicit rather than explicitly collaborated upon

### Impact on Client Engagement and Change:
**Engagement: Strong** – Isabella is engaged, willing to take a step, and feels respected. Her final statement demonstrates satisfaction with autonomy-supportive approach.

**Likelihood of Sustained Change: Moderate** – While Isabella has agreed to review information and discuss with partner, underlying ambivalence remains largely unresolved. She still states: "I'm still not completely convinced I have to give it up completely." Without more systematic evocation of change talk and deeper exploration of values, likelihood of meaningful behavior change is uncertain.

### Critical Recommendations for Development:
1. Systematically evoke and amplify change talk using DARN-CAT framework
2. Strategically address sustain talk through double-sided reflections and gentle evidence introduction
3. Deepen exploration of values and vision of motherhood
4. Build self-efficacy through exploration of past successes and strengths
5. Maintain depth of reflective listening throughout session
6. Explicitly collaborate on ultimate change goal, not just immediate steps
7. Develop stronger follow-up plan with clear accountability and check-in points

---

**Clinician Competency Level:** Intermediate (3.7/5) – Demonstrates solid foundational MI practice with particular strength in autonomy support and collaboration. With focused practice on evocation, sustain talk management, and values exploration, clinician could advance toward advanced competence (4.5–5/5).
