## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **Priority 1: Establish Session Agenda (HIGHEST URGENCY)**
- Begin every session with collaborative agenda-setting identifying 2-3 specific target problems
- Allocate time explicitly and follow the agenda throughout
- Example: "Ian, let's focus on your morning slowness and work concentration today—are those your priorities?"

### **Priority 2: Eliminate Excessive Therapist Self-Disclosure (CRITICAL)**
- Limit personal anecdotes to maximum 1 per session, 1-2 sentences
- Redirect focus immediately back to patient
- Maintain professional boundaries; avoid meeting therapist's own needs for connection

### **Priority 3: Implement Guided Discovery Through Socratic Questioning (CRITICAL)**
- Replace all advice-giving with skillful questions facilitating patient insight
- Example: Instead of "Think positive," ask: "When you wake up feeling slow, what thoughts go through your mind?"
- Practice writing 5-10 Socratic questions before each session

### **Priority 4: Apply Specific CBT Techniques**
- **Behavioral Activation:** Systematically schedule valued activities despite low motivation
- **Thought Records:** Identify automatic thoughts accompanying morning slowness and concentration difficulties
- **Cognitive Restructuring:** Challenge depressive thoughts like "I'm just going through the motions"
- **Activity Monitoring:** Track mood-activity relationships to build evidence for behavioral change

### **Priority 5: Develop Clear Treatment Conceptualization and Strategy**
- Articulate explicitly: What maintains Ian's depression? What are the treatment targets? How do interventions address these targets?
- Example: "Your depression appears maintained by low activity (which reinforces low mood), automatic thoughts ('I can't do this'), and social avoidance. We'll address each through behavioral activation, thought records, and gradual reconnection."

### **Priority 6: Assign Structured, Specific Homework with Accountability**
- Formally assign homework with clear rationale, specificity, and commitment check
- Review homework at start of every session
- Example: "Here's your activity log. Track daily activities and mood. Schedule one 15-minute walk this week. We'll review together next session."

### **Priority 7: Pursue Formal CBT Training**
- Enroll in CBT certification program or intensive workshop
- Study evidence-based CBT manuals (e.g., "Cognitive Therapy of Depression" by Beck et al.)
- Seek supervision from experienced CBT practitioner
- Practice techniques with feedback until competency achieved
