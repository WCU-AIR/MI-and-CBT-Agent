**Session Quality:** Poor (1.5/6 overall)

- No safety planning or relapse prevention
- Homework design and review fundamentally flawed

**Strengths to Build Upon:**

- Patient remained engaged

- Adaptation of techniques for cognitive limitations

**Recommendation:** Suspend independent practice pending supervision and competency demonstration on above areas.
