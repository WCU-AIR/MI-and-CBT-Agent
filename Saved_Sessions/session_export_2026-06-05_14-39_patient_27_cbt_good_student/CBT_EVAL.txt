## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Session Strengths:**
- Collaborative, patient-centered agenda-setting with explicit confirmation
- Strong empathic understanding of cognitive-emotional patterns
- Skillful Socratic questioning facilitating independent thought examination
- Warm, genuine interpersonal style with appropriate boundaries
- Reasonable pacing avoiding tangential discussions
- Practical behavioral planning with confidence and barrier assessment

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- **Safety:** Incomplete assessment of harm-to-self and harm-to-others ideation; no formal safety plan despite recent threats
- **Conceptualization:** No explicit case formulation integrating bipolar disorder, cannabis use disorder, anxiety, and psychotic symptoms
- **Scope:** Narrow focus on urge management without relapse prevention, withdrawal management, psychiatric coordination, or core belief work
- **Technique Application:** Limited to cognitive restructuring; no behavioral experiments, formal tools, or technique variety
- **Homework:** Lacks specificity, success criteria, written documentation, and comprehensive rationale

**Risk Assessment:**
Patient at significant risk of relapse within weeks to months without comprehensive treatment expansion. Current session provides false reassurance that problem is adequately addressed when critical clinical needs remain unmet.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
1. Safety assessment and planning protocols
2. Case conceptualization for complex presentations
3. Comprehensive relapse prevention planning
4. Integration of psychiatric and psychological treatment
5. Deeper cognitive work on core beliefs
6. Application of multiple CBT techniques with precision
