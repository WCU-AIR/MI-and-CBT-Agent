## **OVERALL CLINICAL SUMMARY**

**Total MI Competency Score: 1.1/5 (Minimal to No Competence)**

### **Critical Findings:**

This session represents a **fundamental departure from Motivational Interviewing principles and spirit**. The psychiatrist demonstrates a consistently authoritarian, confrontational approach that is **antithetical to MI across virtually all domains**.

### **Core Deficiencies:**

1. **Complete absence of acceptance and unconditional positive regard** – Repeatedly labels client statements as "excuses," communicates judgment and contempt
2. **No empathic understanding** – Fails to reflect client perspective, explicitly rejects relevance of feelings and readiness
3. **Rejection of autonomy and partnership** – Uses controlling language, explicitly states "no working together," imposes solutions
4. **Failure to evoke change talk** – Despite substantial client change talk, fails to draw it out, amplify, or reinforce
5. **Confrontational approach to sustain talk** – Uses pressure and dismissal rather than skillful exploration

### **Impact on Client Engagement:**

This session is **likely to DECREASE client motivation for change and INCREASE resistance**. Client enters with genuine ambivalence and substantial change talk. Psychiatrist's confrontational, controlling approach triggers defensiveness and pushes client toward sustain talk and resistance. Client's increasingly firm refusals to set quit date and repeated assertions that "I need a different approach" evidence emerging psychological reactance.

### **Immediate Recommendations (Priority Order):**

1. **Adopt collaborative, partnership-based stance** – Shift from expert/authority role to collaborative partner; use inclusive language; seek client input
2. **Develop genuine empathic understanding** – Practice reflective listening; demonstrate understanding of client's perspective, emotions, and barriers
3. **Evoke and amplify change talk** – Ask open-ended questions; reflect back and deepen client's own reasons for change
4. **Respect autonomy** – Eliminate controlling language ("you have to," "you must"); emphasize client's right to choose
5. **Seek formal MI training and supervision** – This session reveals fundamental misunderstandings requiring formal reorientation, not just technique refinement

### **Competency Assessment:**

**Current Status:** Minimal to no MI competence. This is not "MI with room for improvement"—this is anti-MI practice more consistent with confrontational, expert-driven counseling known to be ineffective and potentially harmful for behavior change.

**Prognosis for Change:** Requires fundamental philosophical reorientation from expert-driven prescription to client-centered collaboration. Formal MI training and ongoing supervision essential.
