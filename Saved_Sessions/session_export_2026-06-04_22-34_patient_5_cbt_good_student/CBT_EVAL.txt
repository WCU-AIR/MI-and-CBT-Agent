## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Action Items:

1. **Expand Treatment Strategy:** Before next session, create written case formulation addressing all maintaining factors (physiological, cognitive, emotional, behavioral, environmental). Ensure multimodal approach.

2. **Implement Physiological Interventions:** Teach patient relaxation/mindfulness techniques (progressive muscle relaxation, diaphragmatic breathing, grounding exercises) to manage withdrawal symptoms without alcohol.

3. **Assign Structured Written Homework:** Provide thought record worksheet for daily practice. Develop detailed behavioral coping plans for high-risk situations. Establish clear review plan for next session.

4. **Address Emotion-Regulation:** Explore guilt and anxiety as maintaining factors. Teach distress tolerance and emotion-regulation skills. Explain emotion-regulation function of drinking.

5. **Develop Relapse Prevention Plan:** Given history of failed attempts, create explicit plan identifying high-risk situations, early warning signs, coping strategies, and what to do if lapse occurs.

### Strengths to Reinforce:

- Continue using Socratic questioning and guided discovery—this is a significant strength
- Maintain the warm, collaborative therapeutic relationship—excellent rapport established
- Continue accurate empathic reflections—demonstrates sophisticated understanding
- Continue collaborative agenda-setting and language—patient feels heard and respected

### Areas for Development:

- Expand technical repertoire beyond cognitive restructuring
- Develop comprehensive case formulation skills
- Learn to integrate physiological, emotional, and behavioral interventions with cognitive work
- Improve homework specificity and structure
- Systematically elicit process feedback about therapeutic approach
- Develop explicit session closure and summary practices

### Recommended Supervision Focus:

- Review comprehensive CBT case formulation for substance use disorder
- Practice teaching relaxation and mindfulness techniques
- Role-play developing detailed behavioral coping plans
- Review relapse prevention planning strategies
- Practice eliciting and responding to patient feedback about therapeutic process
