## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment: INADEQUATE**

This therapist demonstrates insufficient competency for independent CBT practice with this patient population. The session lacked fundamental CBT structure (agenda, case conceptualization, strategy, measurement) and failed to apply core CBT techniques. Most critically, the therapist did not assess or address suicidal ideation in a patient with moderate-severity MDD and explicit passive suicidal ideation—a serious clinical oversight with potential safety implications.

**Strengths Identified:**
- Warm, non-hostile interpersonal tone
- Non-judgmental stance toward patient's alcohol use
- Appropriate professional boundaries
- Willingness to listen

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Remediation:**
1. Failure to conduct suicide risk assessment
2. Absence of CBT structure (no agenda, no case conceptualization, no strategy, no measurement)
3. Failure to apply CBT techniques (no cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, thought records, etc.)
4. Inadequate understanding of clinical presentation (missed severity of suicidal ideation, neurovegetative symptoms, emerging substance misuse)
5. Excessive self-disclosure that minimized patient's presentation
6. Lack of genuine collaboration despite collaborative language
7. Inadequate homework assignment and follow-up
8. No feedback elicitation or session adjustment

**Recommended Supervision Plan:**
- Weekly individual supervision with focus on CBT fundamentals
- Review of suicide risk assessment protocols and safety planning
- Case conceptualization development and practice
- Guided discovery and Socratic questioning practice
- Behavioral activation and cognitive restructuring skill-building
- Role-plays with feedback on technique application
- Review of recorded sessions to identify self-disclosure patterns
- Consideration of whether therapist should continue with this patient or transfer care

**Timeline:** Reassess competency after 8 weeks of intensive supervision. If significant improvement not demonstrated, recommend additional training or role reassignment.
