Current approach may produce modest anxiety reduction but is unlikely to achieve significant improvement in health anxiety or functional impairment without these behavioral components. |

## CLINICAL NOTES FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Strengths Demonstrated:**
- Excellent empathic attunement and understanding of patient's internal experience
- Warm, genuine, and professional therapeutic presence
- Systematic cognitive conceptualization with accurate thought record
- Skillful cognitive restructuring with evidence examination
- Practical, feasible homework with clear rationale and obstacle-planning
- Consistent feedback-seeking and collaborative decision-making
- Good session pacing and flow without tangents

**Areas Requiring Development:**
- **Behavioral Conceptualization:** Expand assessment of reassurance-seeking, symptom documentation, and avoidance behaviors as maintaining factors in SSD
- **Behavioral Interventions:** Integrate exposure, behavioral activation, and acceptance-based strategies into treatment plan
- **Socratic Questioning:** Allow patient more space to draw own conclusions from evidence rather than proposing alternative thoughts
- **Measurement:** Establish explicit tracking of anxiety, health anxiety, and behavioral targets (reassurance-seeking frequency, symptom monitoring time)
- **Depression Integration:** Address depressed mood (7/10) with behavioral activation and mood monitoring
- **Uncertainty Tolerance:** Develop strategies to help patient tolerate uncertainty rather than relying solely on cognitive reassurance

**Immediate Action Items:**
1. In next session, conduct detailed functional analysis of reassurance-seeking and symptom monitoring behaviors
2. Develop graded plan to reduce reassurance-seeking and online research
3. Introduce acceptance-based strategies for uncertainty and somatic sensations
4. Create behavioral activation plan for depression
5. Implement weekly measurement of anxiety, health anxiety, and behavioral targets
