## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Patient Presentation:** 19-year-old female with anorexia nervosa, restricting type. BMI 14.7 (severely underweight), amenorrhea, malnutrition-related cognitive impairment, fatigue, headaches, poor concentration. Consuming approximately 600 calories daily; exercising 3-4 hours daily (walking). Expressing ambivalence about recovery ("I want to get better, but I'm worried that if I eat more...I might feel uncomfortable or different").

**Session Quality:** This session represents a critical failure in eating disorder treatment. The therapist demonstrates inadequate understanding of eating disorder pathology, lacks CBT competency, and provides generic wellness advice inappropriate for medical severity. The patient leaves without a clear treatment plan, specific behavioral targets, or understanding of her eating disorder.

**Immediate Risk:** Patient remains at serious medical risk. Without intensive, evidence-based intervention, complications (cardiac arrhythmias, electrolyte imbalances, organ damage) are likely. Current therapeutic approach is insufficient and potentially harmful through normalization of severity.

**Recommendation:** This therapist requires immediate remediation before continuing to treat eating disorder patients. Specialized training, supervision, and case consultation are essential.
