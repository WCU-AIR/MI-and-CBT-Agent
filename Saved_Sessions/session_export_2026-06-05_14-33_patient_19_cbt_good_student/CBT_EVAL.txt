## CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Immediate Actions Required:**
1. **Safety Planning (URGENT):** Implement comprehensive suicide risk assessment and collaborative safety plan before next session
2. **Intervention Reorientation:** Prioritize behavioral activation and risk management over cognitive restructuring for acute phase
3. **Structured Materials:** Provide written thought records, behavioral activation schedules, and safety plans
4. **Bipolar Integration:** Explicitly address mood disorder conceptualization, medication adherence, and mood monitoring
5. **Technique Expansion:** Develop competence in multiple CBT modalities beyond cognitive restructuring

**Supervisor Consultation Recommended:** This case requires close supervision given patient's high-risk presentation and current treatment gaps. Consider joint session or detailed case review to establish appropriate safety protocols and intervention sequencing.
