## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

### Session Classification
**NOT Evidence-Based CBT. Generic Supportive Counseling with Significant Clinical Gaps.**

### Immediate Actions Required
1. **Psychiatric Referral:** Patient requires urgent evaluation by psychiatrist for medication consideration (antipsychotics are first-line for schizophreniform disorder)
2. **Safety Assessment:** Conduct formal suicide/self-harm risk assessment given guilt-content hallucinations
3. **Clinician Supervision:** Weekly supervision required with focus on: (1) psychiatric assessment skills, (2) CBT for psychosis training, (3) boundary management and elimination of self-disclosure, (4) treatment planning and agenda-setting
4. **Training Needs:** Clinician must complete formal training in CBT for psychosis (CBTp) before continuing treatment of this patient

### Strengths to Build Upon
- Genuine warmth and non-judgmental demeanor
- Consistent availability and engagement
- Patient feels heard and supported at basic level
- No hostility or harm through criticism

### Critical Areas for Development
- Psychiatric assessment and diagnosis
- Understanding of psychotic illness and first-episode psychosis
- Evidence-based CBT techniques
- Professional boundaries and appropriate self-disclosure
- Treatment planning and agenda-setting
- Safety assessment and risk management

### Prognosis for Clinician Development
**Moderate.** With structured supervision, formal training in CBT for psychosis, and psychiatric consultation, this clinician can develop competency. The foundation of interpersonal warmth is present. However, significant knowledge gaps and skill deficits must be addressed urgently to prevent harm through delayed appropriate treatment.
