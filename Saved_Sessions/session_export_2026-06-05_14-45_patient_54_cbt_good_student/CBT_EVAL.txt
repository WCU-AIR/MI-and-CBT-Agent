- Patient-generated solutions and balanced thoughts

**Critical Deficiencies:**

- **ASSESSMENT:** Incomplete evaluation of medication, alcohol use, sleep patterns, behavioral activation needs
- **TECHNIQUE:** Limited to basic cognitive restructuring; missing behavioral activation, exposure, acceptance-based approaches
- **PLANNING:** No comprehensive treatment plan; no coordination with prescribing psychiatrist

**Risk Level:** MODERATE-TO-HIGH due to unaddressed suicidal ideation and psychotic features

**Supervision Priority:** Safety assessment and psychotic symptom recognition
