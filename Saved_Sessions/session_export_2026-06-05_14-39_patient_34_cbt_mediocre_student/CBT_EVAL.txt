## SUMMARY OF CLINICAL CONCERNS

### Critical Deficiencies:

1. **Fundamental Misunderstanding of Diagnosis**: Therapist treated depersonalization/derealization disorder as primarily a cognitive/motivational problem rather than a dissociative condition requiring specialized intervention.

2. **No Evidence-Based Treatment Strategy**: Interventions were ad hoc and not guided by case conceptualization or knowledge of evidence-based treatments for depersonalization/derealization disorder.

3. **Inappropriate Intervention Focus**: Cognitive restructuring targeted secondary cognitions about academics and social judgment rather than core dissociative symptoms and maintaining factors.

4. **Absent Homework Review**: Second session completely failed to review homework from first session, breaking fundamental CBT principle and preventing assessment of intervention effectiveness.

5. **Minimal Collaboration**: Therapist directed interventions and sought compliance rather than engaging Ken as equal partner in treatment planning.

6. **No Feedback Elicitation**: Therapist did not check understanding, solicit reactions, or explore Ken's skepticism about interventions.

7. **No Psychoeducation**: Failed to explain depersonalization/derealization, normalize symptoms, or reduce Ken's fear and misinterpretation.

8. **Superficial Guided Discovery**: Relied on direct suggestion rather than Socratic questioning; undermined patient's independent insight development.

### Likelihood of Therapeutic Benefit: **LOW**

Current approach is unlikely to produce meaningful improvement in Ken's depersonalization/derealization symptoms. Risk of treatment dropout, worsening symptoms, and increased hopelessness without immediate intervention.

### Immediate Actions Required:

1. Develop evidence-based case conceptualization for depersonalization/derealization disorder
2. Provide psychoeducation to normalize symptoms and reduce fear
3. Shift from cognitive restructuring to exposure and habituation
4. Implement systematic homework review at beginning of each session
5. Identify and address avoidance behaviors and safety behaviors
6. Establish clear collaborative treatment plan with specific goals and timeline
7. Increase Socratic questioning and guided discovery
8. Enhance collaboration and shared decision-making throughout treatment

**Supervisor Recommendation**: Immediate clinical supervision and specialized training in evidence-based treatment of depersonalization/derealization disorder required before continuing with this patient.
