## SUPERVISOR RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Actions Required:

1. **Suspend independent trauma treatment** until competency is demonstrated through training and supervised practice.

2. **Implement intensive supervision** focused on:
   - Trauma-focused CBT protocols (Prolonged Exposure Therapy, Cognitive Processing Therapy)
   - Exposure therapy hierarchy development and implementation
   - Trauma narrative and cognitive processing techniques
   - Systematic trauma assessment
   - Case conceptualization for trauma disorders

3. **Provide structured training** in:
   - Evidence-based treatment for Acute Stress Disorder and PTSD
   - Socratic questioning and guided discovery
   - Collaborative agenda-setting and treatment planning
   - Homework design, assignment, and review
   - Adolescent trauma treatment

4. **Arrange co-therapy or close observation** with an experienced trauma therapist until competency is demonstrated.

5. **Reassess Dylan's case** with an experienced trauma clinician to ensure appropriate treatment is provided. Current treatment trajectory is unlikely to produce meaningful improvement.

### Competency Benchmarks for Return to Independent Practice:

- Demonstrates ability to conduct systematic trauma assessment
- Develops and implements graduated exposure hierarchies
- Conducts cognitive processing of trauma using Socratic method
- Designs specific, measurable homework with clear rationale
- Reviews homework systematically and troubleshoots barriers
- Establishes clear, collaborative treatment plans with explicit goals
- Applies trauma-focused CBT techniques with precision and appropriate timing

**Timeline:** Minimum 8-12 weeks of intensive supervision with weekly case review and recorded session analysis.
