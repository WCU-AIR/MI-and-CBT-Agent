## Overall Clinical Summary

**Overall MI Fidelity Score: 1.5/5**

### Session Strengths
- Eventual recognition of ineffective approach with shift toward MI-consistent practice in final exchanges
- Maintained focus on target behavior (dietary change and weight loss) throughout session
- Avoided severe confrontation or hostility that would completely rupture therapeutic alliance
- Later use of genuinely open-ended questions inviting client perspective

### Critical Deficiencies
- **Expert-driven stance instead of partnership:** Opening statement explicitly positions clinician as authority imposing solutions; violates core MI principle that expertise resides within client
- **Suppression rather than cultivation of change talk:** Multiple instances of client motivation language met with criticism, pressure, and contradiction rather than amplification
- **Confrontation of sustain talk:** Legitimate concerns dismissed as excuses; MI-consistent softening techniques absent
- **Pressure and coercion:** Moral pressure, fear-based language, and demands for commitment undermine autonomy support
- **Prescription instead of evocation:** Directives imposed rather than collaboratively developed; clinician tells rather than asks
- **Inconsistency and late course correction:** Dramatic shift in approach in final exchanges suggests recognition of failure but does not repair damage to alliance

### Impact on Client Engagement
This session is likely to **decrease** Marco's intrinsic motivation for change. Early expert-driven, pressuring approach triggers defensive responses and resistance. Client's initial willingness ("I do want to feel better," "I'm willing to try") likely diminished by session end. Later MI-consistent practice partially mitigates damage but insufficient to repair alliance compromised during critical early phase.

### Clinician Competency Assessment
**Current MI Competency: Minimal**

Clinician demonstrates basic supportive counseling skills but does not embody MI spirit or employ MI-specific techniques skillfully. Fundamental shift from expert-driven to collaborative partnership stance required. Structured training and supervision in MI principles and techniques essential.

### Most Critical Recommendations
1. Adopt consistent collaborative partnership stance from session opening
2. Systematically evoke and amplify client change talk rather than suppress it
3. Replace directives with open questions that invite client thinking
4. Use MI-consistent techniques (double-sided reflections, reframing) to soften sustain talk
5. Eliminate pressure and coercion; explicitly support client autonomy
6. Develop genuine curiosity about client's perspective and constraints
7. Seek regular supervision focused on MI fidelity with recorded session review
