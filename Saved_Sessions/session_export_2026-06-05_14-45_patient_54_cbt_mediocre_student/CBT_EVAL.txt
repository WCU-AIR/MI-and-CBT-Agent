## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Critical Safety Issue
**Suicidal ideation was not adequately assessed or addressed.** Patient stated "I feel like killing myself" and expressed preference for suicide over imprisonment. Therapist did not conduct explicit risk assessment, develop safety plan, or provide crisis resources. This represents a serious clinical gap requiring immediate remediation.

### Most Urgent Clinical Priorities for Next Session
1. **Conduct explicit suicide risk assessment and develop written safety plan** (non-negotiable)
2. **Provide psychoeducation: Explain that guilt beliefs are symptoms of depression, not reality**
3. **Develop coherent treatment strategy** with explicit prioritization and rationale
4. **Replace directive advice with Socratic questioning** to facilitate guided discovery
5. **Implement systematic cognitive restructuring** using thought records for guilt beliefs

### Recommended Supervision Plan
- **Frequency:** Weekly individual supervision with direct observation or recording review
- **Focus areas:** (1) Suicide risk assessment protocols, (2) Recognition of psychotic symptoms, (3) CBT case conceptualization, (4) Guided discovery and Socratic questioning, (5) Systematic technique application
- **Competency demonstration required** before independent patient care
- **Role-play practice** for safety planning, risk assessment, and Socratic questioning
- **Case consultation** on this patient before next session
