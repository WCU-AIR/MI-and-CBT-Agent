## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Critical Diagnostic Error:**
The therapist has fundamentally misidentified the clinical presentation. Dorothy does not have primary anxiety or depression; she has **Huntington's disease with psychotic features and mild neurocognitive disorder**. This is a medical-neurological condition, not a primary psychiatric disorder. The therapist's approach is therefore categorically inappropriate.

**Evidence-Based Practice Violation:**
This session does **not** constitute evidence-based CBT. It is generic supportive counseling with misapplied anxiety-focused techniques. Evidence-based CBT for psychosis requires: reality testing, coping strategies specific to hallucinations/delusions, acceptance-based approaches, and coordination with medication management. None are present.

**Patient Safety Concern:**
By misdirecting care away from medical management toward ineffective psychotherapy, the therapist may delay Dorothy's access to appropriate treatment (antipsychotic medication optimization, neurology consultation, Huntington's disease specialty care). This represents a potential harm.

**Recommendation:**
This therapist requires immediate supervision, additional training, and case reassignment pending competency development. Dorothy should be referred to a provider with expertise in psychosis and neurocognitive disorders, with coordination between psychiatry, neurology, and psychology.
