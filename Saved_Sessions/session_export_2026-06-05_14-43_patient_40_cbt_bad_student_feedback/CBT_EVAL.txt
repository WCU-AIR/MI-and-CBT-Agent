## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

### Strengths Demonstrated
- Basic warmth and non-judgmental stance
- Patient felt safe enough to disclose sensitive information
- Supportive tone maintained throughout
- No hostile or dismissive responses

### Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Intervention
1. **No CBT structure or strategy** – Session lacked agenda, conceptualization, and coherent treatment plan
2. **No CBT techniques applied** – Only generic stress-management advice offered
3. **Boundary violations** – Excessive self-disclosure centering therapist's experiences
4. **Clinical issues unaddressed** – Alcohol use normalized rather than explored; avoidance not examined; trauma not assessed
5. **No collaboration or guided discovery** – Therapist-driven advice-giving rather than Socratic questioning
6. **No homework** – Session ended without between-session tasks or action steps

### Recommended Supervision Focus
- Training in CBT for sexual dysfunction (sensate focus, exposure, cognitive restructuring)
- Supervision on Socratic questioning and guided discovery techniques
- Review of professional boundaries and appropriate self-disclosure
- Case formulation development and treatment planning
- Homework assignment and between-session work protocols

### Next Steps
Therapist should not continue with this patient without significant training and supervision. Consider referral to CBT specialist for sexual dysfunction or intensive supervision with CBT expert before next session.
