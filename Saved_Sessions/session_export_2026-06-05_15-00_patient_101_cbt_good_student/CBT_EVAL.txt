- **Medication Management Gap:** Despite psychiatrist role, did not review medication effects or appropriateness of current sertraline regimen

5. Integrated treatment planning for complex cases
6. Systematic feedback elicitation throughout sessions

**Clinical Competency Level:** Demonstrates solid competence in basic CBT skills but lacks diagnostic sophistication and comprehensive treatment planning necessary for complex cases. Requires enhanced training in bipolar disorder assessment and integrated psychiatric-behavioral treatment approaches.
