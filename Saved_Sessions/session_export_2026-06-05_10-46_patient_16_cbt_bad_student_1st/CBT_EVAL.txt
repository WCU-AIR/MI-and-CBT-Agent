**CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW:**

This therapist demonstrates basic interpersonal warmth but lacks fundamental CBT competence and critical clinical judgment. The session with a patient presenting with severe postpartum psychosis represents a serious clinical failure. The therapist did not recognize a psychiatric emergency, did not apply any CBT techniques, and offered interventions contraindicated for psychotic symptoms. Immediate remediation through specialized training, supervision, and competency assessment is required before this therapist should continue treating patients with serious mental illness.
