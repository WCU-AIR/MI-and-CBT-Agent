## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Classification:** Supportive Counseling (Not CBT)

**Primary Concerns:**
1. **Medical Safety:** No assessment of withdrawal severity or safety planning despite acute withdrawal presentation
2. **Clinical Misunderstanding:** Therapist appears to misunderstand nature and severity of alcohol withdrawal
3. **Lack of Structure:** Complete absence of CBT framework, techniques, and strategic planning
4. **Ineffective Interventions:** Generic reassurance repeated without adaptation or troubleshooting
5. **Therapeutic Alliance Risk:** Excessive self-disclosure undermines clinical authority despite good rapport

**Immediate Recommendations:**
- Mandatory CBT training and supervision
- Comprehensive assessment and safety planning in next session
- Development of case conceptualization before next patient contact
- Review of CBT manuals for substance use disorders
- Recording and review of sessions with supervisor

**Strengths to Build Upon:**
- Genuine warmth and concern for patient
- Appropriate professional boundaries
- Patient engagement and cooperation maintained
- Non-judgmental stance

**Timeline for Improvement:** Significant change required before this therapist should continue treating patients with alcohol use disorder without direct supervision.
