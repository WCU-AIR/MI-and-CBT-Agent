## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CLINICAL SUPERVISION

### Priority 1: Develop Comprehensive, Autism-Informed Treatment Strategy
- Explicitly conceptualize Brandon's difficulties within autism framework
- Acknowledge how social communication deficits, restricted interests, and sensory sensitivities contribute to peer difficulties, anxiety, and behavioral dysregulation
- Develop multi-component treatment plan addressing cognitive, behavioral, sensory, and social domains

### Priority 2: Implement Behavioral Interventions for Dysregulation
- Conduct functional analysis of screaming outbursts
- Introduce sensory regulation strategies (noise-canceling headphones, quiet spaces, proprioceptive input)
- Teach emotion regulation skills beyond deep breathing (TIPP skills, opposite action, self-soothing)

### Priority 3: Conduct Explicit Safety Planning
- Assess suicidal ideation, past attempts, protective factors, and warning signs
- Develop written safety plan with coping strategies, people to contact, and crisis resources
- Involve mother in safety planning process

### Priority 4: Enhance Homework Design and Support
- Provide explicit rationale for all assignments
- Include specific implementation instructions (when, how, how often)
- Create monitoring logs for tracking completion and impact
- Develop detailed troubleshooting strategies
- Involve mother in supporting homework completion

### Priority 5: Increase Family Involvement
- Schedule session with Brandon and mother to discuss treatment goals
- Provide psychoeducation about ASD, anxiety, and CBT techniques
- Assign mother specific, concrete roles in supporting Brandon's progress
