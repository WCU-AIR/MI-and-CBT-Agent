## SUPERVISOR SUMMARY

**Therapist Competency Level:** Pre-CBT / Supportive Counseling Only

**Session Classification:** Supportive counseling, NOT evidence-based CBT

**Immediate Actions Required:**
1. Cease delivery of CBT to anxiety disorder patients until training completed
2. Enroll in structured CBT training program with weekly supervision
3. Focus on: agenda-setting, CBT technique application, homework assignment, elimination of excessive self-disclosure
4. Attend Beck Institute or ABCT-certified training in anxiety disorders

**Prognosis Without Intervention:** Patient symptoms unlikely to improve; avoidance patterns will persist; anxiety will likely increase with new social demands.
