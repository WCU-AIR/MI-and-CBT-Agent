## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Context:** Two sessions with 16-year-old female patient with recent high-lethality suicide attempt (overdose), presenting with flat mood, anxiety about recurrence, catastrophic thoughts, somatic symptoms (racing heart), and social withdrawal/shame.

**Strengths Observed:**
- Consistent warmth and non-judgmental demeanor
- Basic empathic listening and reflection of explicit content
- Patient remained engaged and expressed willingness to try interventions
- Identified multiple relevant problem areas
- Gentle pacing created safe environment for disclosure

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:**

1. **Safety Planning (URGENT):** No explicit assessment of suicidal ideation, warning signs, coping strategies, or means restriction. For recent high-lethality attempt, this is a critical clinical gap.

2. **Structured Agenda-Setting (URGENT):** Both sessions lacked explicit collaborative agenda-setting. Essential for containment and safety with high-risk adolescent.

3. **Homework Review (URGENT):** Session 2 failed to review Session 1 homework. Breaks treatment continuity and suggests homework not viewed as central.

4. **Technique Application:** CBT techniques applied superficially without systematic rigor. Cognitive reframing lacked evidence examination. Behavioral interventions vague and untracked. Thought records, exposure, and other core techniques absent.

5. **Guided Discovery:** Therapist relied on advice-giving and reassurance rather than Socratic questioning. Patient not guided to examine evidence or reach own insights.

6. **Treatment Conceptualization:** No clear, coherent strategy tailored to patient's specific presentation. Generic anxiety management rather than targeted treatment for adjustment disorder with suicidal behavior.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- Safety planning protocol and implementation
- Structured agenda-setting practice
- Homework review and tracking systems
- Guided discovery/Socratic questioning skills
- Cognitive restructuring with evidence examination
- Thought record introduction and use
- Treatment conceptualization development

**Recommended Training:**
- Formal CBT training course or workshop
- Supervision with CBT-trained supervisor (weekly recommended)
- Review of recorded sessions with feedback
- Role-play practice of core skills
