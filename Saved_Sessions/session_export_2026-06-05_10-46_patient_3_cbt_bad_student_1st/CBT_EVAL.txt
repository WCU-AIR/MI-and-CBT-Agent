## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment:** Poor (1.2/6 average rating)

**Immediate Safety Concerns:**
- No explicit safety plan established for patient with recent high-lethality suicide attempt
- No identification of warning signs or crisis resources
- No discussion of means restriction or emergency protocols
- Patient at continued risk without adequate safety planning

**Critical Training Needs:**
1. **CBT Structure & Agenda-Setting** — Essential foundation
2. **Safety Planning** — Non-negotiable for high-risk patients
3. **Professional Boundaries** — Eliminate inappropriate personal disclosure
4. **Case Conceptualization** — Develop coherent treatment strategy
5. **Behavioral Activation** — Move beyond vague suggestions
6. **Cognitive Restructuring** — Teach systematic thought challenging
7. **Socratic Questioning** — Replace advice-giving with guided discovery
8. **Homework Design** — Create structured, monitored assignments

**Recommended Supervision Plan:**
- Weekly 1-hour supervision sessions
- Review session recordings with focus on technique application
- Role-play CBT interventions (behavioral activation, cognitive restructuring, Socratic questioning)
- Case conceptualization exercises
- Safety planning protocol training
- Boundary management and professional role clarity

**Patient Care Recommendation:**
Consider co-therapy or increased supervision frequency given patient's high-risk status and current therapist's skill level. Ensure safety planning is completed before next patient contact.
