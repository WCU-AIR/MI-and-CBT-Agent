## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Competency Assessment:** Below entry-level for CBT practice with psychotic disorders.

**Immediate Recommendations:**
- Suspend independent treatment of psychotic patients pending specialized training
- Mandatory referral to psychiatry for medication evaluation
- Enroll in CBT for Psychosis training program (e.g., Kingdon & Turkington, Morrison protocols)
- Weekly supervision with expert in psychosis treatment
- Review of session recordings with focus on Socratic questioning, guided discovery, and engagement with delusional content

**Strengths to Build Upon:**
- Patient remained engaged; rapport not irreparably damaged
- Therapist demonstrated basic empathic tone and acknowledgment of distress
- Foundation for improvement exists with appropriate training and supervision

**Critical Learning Needs:**
- Delusional disorder phenomenology and first-rank psychotic symptoms
- Medication assessment and psychiatric collaboration
- CBT techniques specific to psychosis (reality-testing, thought records, behavioral experiments)
- Elimination of excessive self-disclosure
- Structured session format (agenda, homework, feedback)
- Collaborative empiricism and Socratic questioning
