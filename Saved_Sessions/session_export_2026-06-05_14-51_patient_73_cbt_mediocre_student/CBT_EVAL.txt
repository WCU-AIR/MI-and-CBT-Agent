## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Observed:**
- Warm, genuine, non-judgmental interpersonal presence
- Basic empathy and validation of patient experience
- Correct identification of panic symptoms and thought-feeling cycle
- Patient engagement and willingness to try interventions
- Appropriate professional boundaries

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Agenda Setting:** Vague, non-directive session openings; no prioritization or time allocation; no homework review in Session 2
2. **Collaboration:** Advice-giving without shared decision-making; patient positioned as recipient rather than partner
3. **Feedback Elicitation:** Leading questions; patient concerns not explored; no understanding checks
4. **Technique Application:** Superficial execution; no structured tools; patient resistance not addressed
5. **Homework:** Vague assignments; no rationale; no review; no structured tools
6. **Strategy:** No coherent conceptualization; multiple interventions without clear connection to symptom reduction
7. **Guided Discovery:** Directive suggestions rather than Socratic questioning

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- Review session recordings to identify and correct technique deficiencies
- Practice Socratic questioning through role-plays
- Develop structured treatment plans with explicit conceptualization
- Create exposure hierarchies and behavioral experiment protocols
- Study evidence-based panic disorder manuals (Barlow & Craske)
- Implement systematic homework review procedures

**Prognosis with Current Approach:** Modest, temporary improvement likely; full remission unlikely without significant clinical skill development.
