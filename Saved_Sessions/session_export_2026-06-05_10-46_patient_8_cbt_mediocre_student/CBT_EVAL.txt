## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### Priority 1: Recognize and Communicate Medical Severity
- Explicitly state diagnosis and urgency in next session
- Communicate: "Your BMI is 14.7 (severe malnutrition). Your body cannot function on 600 calories/day. This is a medical emergency requiring immediate intervention."

### Priority 2: Establish Explicit Treatment Goals and Strategy
- Weight restoration to healthy BMI (18.5-24.9)
- Normalized eating (3 meals + 2-3 snacks daily)
- Resolution of medical complications
- Create written treatment plan with specific targets and timelines

### Priority 3: Conduct Comprehensive Eating Disorder Assessment
- Beliefs about eating, weight, body image
- Specific barriers to eating (postprandial discomfort, lack of appetite, cultural/religious factors, financial limitations)
- Compensatory behaviors and medical history
- Family dynamics and cultural context
- Motivation for change

### Priority 4: Implement Structured Meal Planning
- Replace vague "small snacks" with specific written meal plan
- Include target calories (gradually increased), specific foods, meal times, portion sizes
- Address postprandial discomfort systematically (education, graded exposure, symptom management)
- Involve registered dietitian if possible

### Priority 5: Coordinate with Medical Team and Family
- Schedule medical evaluation and establish monitoring protocol
- Involve family in treatment planning and support
- Establish hospitalization criteria and emergency protocols
- Communicate medical necessity clearly to patient and family

### Priority 6: Systematically Review and Adjust Homework
- Begin every session with homework review
- Ask about completion, barriers, outcomes, and adjustments needed
- Use information to refine treatment plan
- Assign specific, written homework with clear rationale
