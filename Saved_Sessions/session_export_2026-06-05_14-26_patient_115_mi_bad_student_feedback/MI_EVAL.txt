## Overall Summary

**Overall Session Score: 1.4/5**

### Strengths:
- Maintains clear focus on target behavior (alcohol use and risk reduction)
- Demonstrates genuine concern for Ryan's wellbeing and future
- Brief collaborative moments near session end with open-ended questions
- Acknowledges Ryan's proposed strategies and identified supportive friends
- Maintains professional demeanor throughout

### Critical Deficiencies:
- **Fundamental MI Spirit Violation:** Operates from authoritarian, expert-driven stance rather than collaborative partnership—the most critical deficiency undermining all MI principles
- **Prescription Over Evocation:** Repeatedly tells Ryan what to do rather than evoking his own motivations, values, and resources, reducing intrinsic motivation
- **Confrontation of Sustain Talk:** Arguments with resistance strengthen defensiveness and entrench client's position
- **Minimal Autonomy Support:** Uses directive language, pressure, and implicit threats to coerce compliance
- **Fear-Based Messaging:** Relies heavily on consequences and threats rather than exploring client's own reasons for change
- **Missed Evocation Opportunities:** Does not explore Ryan's values (education, friendship), concerns (peer pressure, isolation), or ideas about change

### Impact on Client Engagement:
This session is likely to **reduce** Ryan's intrinsic motivation and **increase** defensiveness and resistance. While Ryan enters with genuine ambivalence and change talk, the psychiatrist's authoritarian approach polarizes him into defensive compliance ("I guess I can try," "I'm not making any promises I can't keep") rather than genuine commitment. Psychological reactance likely to undermine follow-through on agreed-upon strategies.

### Primary Recommendations:
1. **Adopt collaborative, client-centered stance** (HIGHEST PRIORITY)—fundamental reorientation from expert-driven to partnership model
2. **Evoke rather than impose** motivation through open-ended questions about client's own reasons for change
3. **Soften sustain talk** through reflection and reframing, not confrontation
4. **Support autonomy** by consistently respecting client's right to choose
5. **Demonstrate genuine empathy** through complex reflections capturing ambivalence and underlying values
6. **Affirm strengths** and build self-efficacy rather than emphasizing willpower
7. **Reinforce change talk** through strategic questioning and explicit affirmation
8. **Reduce fear-based messaging** and focus on intrinsic motivation
9. **Maintain collaborative stance** throughout using inclusive language and genuine partnership
