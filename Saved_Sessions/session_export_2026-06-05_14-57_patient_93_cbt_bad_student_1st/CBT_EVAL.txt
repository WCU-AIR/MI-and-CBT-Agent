## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment:** This session represents a fundamental failure to deliver evidence-based CBT. While the therapist demonstrated basic warmth and non-hostility, these foundational skills are insufficient without evidence-based technique. The therapist is functioning at a pre-CBT level, practicing supportive counseling with generic wellness advice rather than CBT.

**Patient Safety Concerns:** Michelle presents with three diagnosed disorders (SSD, MDD, Cannabis Use Disorder) requiring specific, evidence-based intervention. Generic wellness advice and unsupported reassurance are inadequate and potentially harmful. Without proper intervention, her conditions are likely to worsen, increasing suicide risk and substance use escalation.

**Therapist Competency:** The therapist requires immediate training and supervision. Before continuing to treat Michelle or other patients with complex presentations, competence in evidence-based CBT techniques, treatment conceptualization, and case formulation must be developed.

**Recommended Next Steps:** (1) Immediate supervision meeting to review this session; (2) Enrollment in CBT training program; (3) Weekly supervision with CBT-trained supervisor; (4) Study of treatment manuals for SSD, depression, and substance use; (5) Practice of CBT techniques in role-plays before clinical application; (6) Recording and review of subsequent sessions with supervisor.
