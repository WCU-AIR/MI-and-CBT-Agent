## SUPERVISOR RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Actions Required:

1. **URGENT MEDICAL REFERRAL:** Recognize documented Obstructive Sleep Apnea Hypopnea as primary diagnosis. Refer patient immediately to sleep medicine specialist or primary care physician for CPAP therapy or other medical treatment.

2. **ADDRESS SAFETY RISK:** Patient is at risk of falling asleep while driving. Discuss seriousness of risk, medical treatment as primary intervention, and temporary driving restrictions if appropriate.

3. **ESTABLISH GENUINE COLLABORATION:** Shift from expert-directive mode to collaborative partnership. Involve patient in case formulation and treatment planning.

4. **IMPROVE DIAGNOSTIC ASSESSMENT:** Develop ability to recognize symptoms of medical conditions (sleep apnea, thyroid disease, anemia, etc.) and refer for appropriate medical evaluation before assuming problem is primarily psychological.

5. **APPLY CBT TECHNIQUES WITH PROPER STRUCTURE:** When using cognitive restructuring, follow full process: identify thought, examine evidence for/against, identify distortions, develop balanced thought. Use written thought records. Do not skip steps.

6. **IMPLEMENT SYSTEMATIC FEEDBACK-SEEKING:** Regularly elicit patient feedback about understanding, reactions, and session helpfulness. Ask comprehension checks after explaining concepts.

7. **ESTABLISH EXPLICIT COLLABORATIVE AGENDAS:** At beginning of each session, set mutually satisfactory agenda with specific target problems, priorities, and time allocation.

8. **REVIEW HOMEWORK AND MAINTAIN CONTINUITY:** At start of each session, review previous session outcomes, check homework completion, and assess progress toward goals.

### Recommended Training:

- Comprehensive diagnostic assessment and differential diagnosis
- Recognition of medical presentations vs. psychological presentations
- Collaborative CBT practice and shared decision-making
- Proper application of cognitive restructuring with evidence examination
- Socratic questioning and guided discovery techniques
- Integration of medical and psychological treatment planning
- Safety assessment and risk management protocols
