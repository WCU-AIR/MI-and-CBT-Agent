## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Strengths Demonstrated
- Consistent warmth, empathy, and professional demeanor across both sessions
- Appropriate therapeutic boundaries maintained
- Attempt to introduce cognitive restructuring as intervention
- Patient felt heard and validated in session

### Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention
1. **Absent Case Conceptualization**: No evidence of understanding Histrionic Personality Disorder or how it manifests in this patient's presentation
2. **No Structured Agenda**: Both sessions lacked clear, collaborative goal-setting and time allocation
3. **Superficial Cognitive Work**: Cognitive restructuring applied without systematic process, evidence examination, or behavioral testing
4. **No Functional Analysis**: Failed to explore why patient uses dramatic behavior or what maintains it
5. **Missed Safety Assessment**: Suicidal ideation mentioned but never directly assessed or explored
6. **Generic Interventions**: Coping strategies offered do not target core personality patterns
7. **Poor Homework Implementation**: Vague, non-collaborative, lacking clear rationale and follow-up plan
8. **Minimal Feedback Elicitation**: Did not verify patient understanding or genuine engagement with interventions

### Risk Assessment
- **Therapeutic Effectiveness**: Low likelihood of producing meaningful change in core patterns
- **Potential for Harm**: Therapeutic relationship may inadvertently reinforce attention-seeking behavior through excessive validation without addressing function
- **Safety**: Suicidal ideation not properly assessed; risk determination not documented

### Recommended Supervisor Actions
1. Provide direct feedback on case conceptualization deficits
2. Require completion of written case formulation before next patient session
3. Observe next session and provide real-time coaching on agenda-setting and guided discovery
4. Assign readings on HPD and functional analysis
5. Role-play structured interventions (thought records, behavioral experiments)
6. Establish competency benchmarks for next evaluation
