## CRITICAL CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS (URGENT)

### 1. **Safety Assessment and Planning (IMMEDIATE)**
- Conduct detailed suicide risk assessment: frequency/intensity of ideation, presence of plan/intent, access to means, protective factors
- Develop written safety plan with crisis protocol, emergency resources, and explicit procedures
- Establish clear crisis boundaries and response procedures

### 2. **Case Conceptualization and Treatment Plan**
- Develop explicit BPD-focused case formulation addressing: emotion dysregulation, identity disturbance, abandonment fears, self-harm, suicidality
- Select evidence-based BPD treatment (DBT, mentalization-based therapy, or schema therapy)
- Create written treatment plan with specific interventions, timeline, and expected outcomes
- Share collaboratively with patient

### 3. **Structured Session Format**
- Implement consistent agenda-setting with homework review (5-10 min), focused intervention (20-30 min), homework assignment (5 min)
- Use whiteboard to make agenda visible
- Summarize key points and confirm homework at session end

### 4. **Shift to Guided Discovery**
- Replace direct advice with Socratic questioning
- Use hypothesis-testing questions to help patient examine beliefs
- Practice collaborative empiricism: "Let's test that belief together"
- Record sessions and review with supervisor to increase ratio of Socratic questions to directives

### 5. **Address Core BPD Features**
- Explore identity disturbance, abandonment fears, emotion dysregulation
- Teach emotion regulation skills (distress tolerance, mindfulness, opposite action)
- Address self-harm and suicidality directly with safety planning
- Work on interpersonal effectiveness and communication

### 6. **Implement Structured Homework**
- Provide clear rationale, specific written instructions, and worksheets
- Plan for barriers collaboratively
- Check confidence level (0-10 scale)
- Review explicitly in next session
- Use thought records, activity logs, behavioral experiments with structured formats

### 7. **Increase Collaboration**
- Actively invite patient input: "What do you think would help?", "What feels doable?", "What barriers do you anticipate?"
- Adjust approach based on feedback
- Promote shared decision-making throughout treatment

### 8. **Seek Supervision and Training**
- Obtain regular supervision from therapist experienced in BPD treatment
- Complete formal training in DBT, mentalization-based therapy, or schema therapy
- Review session recordings with supervisor for specific feedback
- Commit to weekly supervision and ongoing professional development

Intensive supervision and formal training are required before this therapist should continue treating patients with BPD or chronic suicidality.
