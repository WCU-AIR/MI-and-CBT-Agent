## SUMMARY OF CRITICAL DEFICIENCIES

### Fundamental Issues:
- **Inappropriate diagnosis and treatment selection** for patient with progressive neurodegenerative disorder
- **Inadequate case conceptualization** missing neurological basis of symptoms
- **Missing caregiver involvement** despite wife's active role in symptom management
- **Lack of psychoeducation** about NCDLB and realistic expectations
- **Insufficient exploration** of neurological symptoms (apathy, parkinsonian features, cognitive fluctuation)

### Strengths Demonstrated:
- Collaborative agenda-setting and feedback elicitation
- Empathic listening and warm professional demeanor
- Skillful Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Specific, detailed, barrier-informed homework assignment
- Logical session structure and appropriate pacing

### Recommendation:
**This case requires immediate reassessment and reframing.** If therapist continues to see patient, treatment approach must shift fundamentally from cognitive restructuring to acceptance, adaptation, environmental modification, and caregiver support. Consultation with neurology or neuropsychology strongly recommended.
