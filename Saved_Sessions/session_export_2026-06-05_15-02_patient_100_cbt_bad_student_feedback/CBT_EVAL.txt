## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL FEEDBACK

**Session Rating: 9/66 (Critically Deficient)**

This session demonstrates adequate interpersonal warmth but fundamental clinical incompetence in CBT delivery. The therapist's approach—generic positive thinking, distraction, and wellness advice—is not evidence-based for trichotillomania and excoriation disorder and was explicitly contradicted by the patient's own feedback.

**Immediate priorities for supervision:**

1. **Learn Habit Reversal Training** – This is the gold-standard intervention for these disorders and must become the core of treatment.
2. **Conduct functional analysis** – Systematically explore the post-work ritual, tension-relief cycle, and shame-avoidance cycle.
3. **Establish structured agendas** – Every session must have explicit, prioritized goals.
4. **Shift to Socratic questioning** – Replace advice-giving with guided discovery.
5. **Assign specific homework** – Every session must end with measurable between-session tasks.
6. **Recognize and respond to patient feedback** – When patient indicates an approach isn't working, adjust rather than repeat.

- Habit Reversal Training protocol and demonstration videos

**Follow-up supervision:** Schedule intensive supervision focused on CBT case conceptualization and Habit Reversal Training implementation before next patient session.
