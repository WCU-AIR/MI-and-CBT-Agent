## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL DISCUSSION

**Strengths to Reinforce:**
- Exemplary use of Socratic questioning and guided discovery throughout session
- Consistent collaborative stance positioning patient as active agent
- Skillful cognitive restructuring with measurable anxiety reduction (8→6)
- Warm, validating therapeutic tone establishing good rapport
- Clear teaching of cognitive model with accessible language

**Areas Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Incomplete Agenda Adherence:** Sleep difficulties were identified as agenda item but never addressed. Establish protocol: explicitly allocate time to each item, check in mid-session, reschedule if needed.

2. **Insufficient Case Conceptualization:** Session treated Carlos as straightforward test anxiety case without adequately integrating documented ADHD, learning disability, depression, panic, and cultural stressors. Develop comprehensive case formulation before next session distinguishing anxiety-driven vs. ADHD-driven vs. depression-driven factors.

3. **Missing Critical Interventions:** No discussion of ADHD medication evaluation or academic accommodations despite documented learning disability and inattentive ADHD. These are likely more important than cognitive restructuring for Carlos's core difficulties.

4. **Narrow Intervention Focus:** Session relied almost exclusively on cognitive techniques. Expand to include ADHD strategies (task breakdown, environmental modification, external structure), behavioral activation for depression, and cultural exploration.

5. **Homework Structure Deficiency:** Provide written forms, specific timing, tracking mechanisms, and contingency plans. For ADHD patients, vague homework ("next couple of days") significantly reduces follow-through.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- Complex case conceptualization and multi-diagnostic treatment planning
- ADHD-informed cognitive-behavioral therapy
- Culturally-informed therapy and identity exploration
- Structured agenda management and time allocation
- Detailed homework assignment with tracking and accountability
