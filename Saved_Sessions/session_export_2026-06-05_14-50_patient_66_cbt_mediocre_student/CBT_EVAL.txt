## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Critical Issues Requiring Immediate Attention:

**1. Fundamental CBT Structure Absent (Items 1, 2, 6)**
- No agenda-setting across any session
- Explicit rejection of structure ("go with the flow")
- Repetitive sessions with no evidence of continuity or progress
- **Action Required:** Implement mandatory agenda-setting in every session with explicit time allocation and priorities.

**2. Misdiagnosis of Clinical Presentation (Items 8, 9, 10)**
- Therapist treating OCPD as anxiety disorder
- Applying OCD-appropriate techniques to personality disorder
- Missing core OCPD features: ego-syntonic perfectionism, rigidity, control as core need
- **Action Required:** Comprehensive training in OCPD phenomenology and personality disorders; case reformulation required.

**3. Inadequate Collaboration (Item 5)**
- Therapist-driven interventions without genuine co-creation
- Patient compliance without authentic buy-in
- No shared problem formulation
- **Action Required:** Reframe all interventions as collaborative options; explicitly solicit patient input and preferences.

**4. Insufficient Technique Application (Items 7, 10, 11)**
- Cognitive restructuring therapist-provided rather than patient-generated
- Behavioral experiments lack explicit hypotheses and success criteria
- Homework vague and not systematically reviewed
- **Action Required:** Supervised practice in Socratic questioning, behavioral experiment design, and homework development.

### Recommended Supervision Plan:

- **Immediate:** Weekly 1-hour supervision with focus on CBT fundamentals and OCPD case formulation
- **Week 1-2:** Review and practice agenda-setting and session structure
- **Week 3-4:** Develop comprehensive OCPD case conceptualization; practice Socratic questioning
- **Week 5-6:** Design and practice behavioral experiments and homework assignments
- **Ongoing:** Review session recordings with supervisor; practice with role-play before seeing patients

### Prognosis for Current Patient:

**Without intervention changes: Poor.** Risk of treatment failure, symptom substitution, and therapeutic rupture is high.

**With immediate corrective supervision: Moderate.** If therapist implements recommended changes, patient may benefit from personality-focused CBT approach tailored to OCPD.

**Recommendation:** Continue treatment with this patient only under close supervision with mandatory implementation of corrective measures outlined above.
