## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Strengths:**
- Warm, empathic interpersonal style with genuine respect for patient perspective
- Skilled use of Socratic questioning and guided discovery for peripheral issue
- Accurate cognitive-behavioral formulation of party incident
- Smooth session flow without tangents
- Consistent validation and encouragement

**Critical Areas Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Recognize lack of insight as primary clinical barrier** — This is not a minor issue; it is central to Emily's presentation and must be addressed before standard CBT can be effective
2. **Conduct comprehensive substance use assessment** — Nightly alcohol use and polysubstance use require urgent clinical attention and cannot be addressed through cognitive restructuring alone
3. **Incorporate neurocognitive disorder into all case formulation and treatment planning** — TBI-related cognitive deficits are not peripheral; they are central to understanding presentation and planning effective treatment
4. **Shift from cognitive restructuring to behavioral interventions** — Given cognitive deficits and substance use disorder, behavioral activation, functional analysis, and psychoeducation are more appropriate than cognitive work
5. **Establish true collaboration addressing resistance** — Use motivational interviewing to explore ambivalence; do not proceed with standard CBT until some insight is established

**Recommended Next Steps:**
- Seek supervision from substance use disorder and neurocognitive disorder specialist
- Complete training in motivational interviewing
- Develop comprehensive case formulation integrating TBI, substance use, and lack of insight
- Conduct safety assessment and substance use evaluation in next session
- Provide psychoeducation about TBI and its effects
- Consider referrals to neuropsychology, cognitive rehabilitation, and substance use treatment
- Involve family in treatment planning with consent
