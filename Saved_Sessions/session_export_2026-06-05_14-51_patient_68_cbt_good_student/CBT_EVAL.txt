**Evaluator Notes:**

This session represents competent application of general CBT techniques to a narrow slice of a complex clinical problem. The therapist's guided discovery, collaboration, and cognitive restructuring skills are exemplary. However, the session lacks the comprehensive, integrated approach required for severe opioid use disorder in acute withdrawal. The patient leaves with useful cognitive tools and a behavioral experiment but without the medical management, relapse prevention planning, and contextual support necessary for sustained recovery. Recommend specialized training in addiction treatment while maintaining and building upon existing CBT strengths.
