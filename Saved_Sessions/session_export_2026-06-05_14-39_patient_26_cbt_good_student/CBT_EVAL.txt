## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths to Reinforce:**
- Explicit, collaborative agenda-setting with patient confirmation
- Accurate empathic reflection of cognitive-affective-behavioral chain
- Warm, non-judgmental interpersonal presence with consistent validation
- Skillful Socratic questioning, particularly "friend" technique
- Specific, measurable behavioral experiment with clear rationale
- Strong therapeutic alliance and patient engagement

**Critical Areas for Development:**
- **Substance use intervention:** Conduct functional analysis; explore anxiety-substance use relationship; develop alternative coping strategies; assign substance use monitoring homework
- **Trauma-focused assessment:** Assess exposure history, intrusive symptoms, nightmares; integrate trauma-informed interventions
- **Sleep management:** Develop sleep-focused interventions; teach nightmare management techniques
- **Technique expansion:** Introduce thought records, behavioral activation, relaxation techniques, exposure therapy
- **Time management:** Establish explicit time allocation at session start; use signposting; ensure all agenda items receive attention
- **Mid-session feedback:** Routinely check understanding of CBT concepts and reactions to interventions throughout session

**Recommended Next Steps:**
1. Supervision focus on substance use assessment and intervention techniques
2. Training on trauma-informed CBT and trauma-focused interventions
3. Practice with comprehensive case conceptualization that integrates multiple presenting problems
4. Development of structured homework templates and written materials
5. Role-play practice on time management and agenda prioritization with complex cases
