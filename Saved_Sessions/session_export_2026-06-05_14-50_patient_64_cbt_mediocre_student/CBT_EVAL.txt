## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Identified:**
- Adequate warmth, professionalism, and non-judgmental demeanor
- Some initial Socratic questioning attempted
- Patient remained engaged and willing to try suggestions
- Basic empathy and validation demonstrated

**Critical Areas for Immediate Development:**
1. **Evidence-Based Treatment for OCD:** Urgent need for training in Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP). This is the gold-standard, empirically-supported treatment for OCD. Current approach using general anxiety management is not appropriate for this diagnosis.

2. **Case Conceptualization:** Develop ability to articulate clear understanding of how patient's problems developed and what maintains them. This is foundational to all CBT work.

3. **Structured Agenda-Setting:** Implement collaborative agenda-setting at beginning of each session with explicit problem prioritization and time allocation.

4. **Homework Structure and Review:** Assign specific, written homework with clear rationale. **Most critically, review homework at beginning of each session.** This is non-negotiable in CBT.

5. **Collaborative Approach:** Shift from therapist-directed advice-giving to genuine partnership. Explore patient's perspective, prior attempts, preferences, and barriers before suggesting interventions.

6. **Focused Intervention:** Identify and target key maintaining cognitions and behaviors rather than addressing surface symptoms. For this patient: compulsions, avoidance, inflated responsibility, and moral perfectionism.

**Recommended Next Steps:**
- Enroll in advanced CBT training program with focus on OCD and ERP
- Obtain supervision from OCD specialist (minimum 1 hour/week)
- Observe experienced OCD therapist conducting ERP sessions
- Review CBT manuals on case conceptualization and ERP
- Do not continue treating this patient without ERP training and supervision
- Consider referral to OCD specialist if training/supervision not immediately available

**Timeline for Competency Development:**
- Weeks 1-2: Complete ERP training module; develop case conceptualization with supervision
- Weeks 3-4: Implement structured agendas and homework review; receive feedback on recordings
- Weeks 5-8: Begin implementing ERP under close supervision; practice with at least 2 cases
- Ongoing: Weekly supervision; monthly competency review

This therapist demonstrates foundational interpersonal skills but requires substantial development in CBT-specific competencies, particularly evidence-based treatment for OCD.
