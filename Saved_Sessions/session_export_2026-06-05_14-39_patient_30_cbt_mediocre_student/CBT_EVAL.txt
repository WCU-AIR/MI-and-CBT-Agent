## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

**Priority 1 (Implement Before Next Session):**
- Develop written treatment plan with Rachel including specific, measurable goals and prioritized problems
- Create structured mood tracking form (3x daily: time, mood 1-10, activity, thoughts)
- Prepare specific, detailed homework assignments with written forms and clear rationale

**Priority 2 (Implement in Next Session):**
- Review previous week's homework in detail (completion, observations, barriers, adaptations)
- Teach behavioral activation with specificity (scheduled activities, mood monitoring before/after)
- Address social withdrawal and fear of burdening others through guided discovery and behavioral experiment

**Priority 3 (Ongoing):**
- Coordinate with prescribing psychiatrist regarding medication review and mood stabilizer consideration
- Apply cognitive restructuring systematically using thought records
- Elicit feedback routinely and adjust approach based on patient input
- Establish relapse prevention plan once patterns are identified

**Supervision Requirement:** Weekly case supervision with experienced CBT supervisor focused on treatment planning, homework management, and systematic technique application until competency improves.
