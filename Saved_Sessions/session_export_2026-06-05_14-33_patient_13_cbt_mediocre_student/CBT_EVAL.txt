## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Priority Actions:

1. **Develop Explicit Case Conceptualization** – Create written formulation linking patient's history (critical father, perfectionist family) to current symptoms and identifying core maintaining factors (perfectionism, fear of judgment, avoidance).

2. **Implement Systematic Guided Discovery** – Replace direct suggestions with Socratic questioning. Use sequence: identify thought → examine evidence → consider alternatives → develop balanced thought → plan behavioral testing.

3. **Develop Structured Behavioral Exposure Hierarchy** – Collaboratively create detailed hierarchy of avoided social situations rated by anxiety level (0-100). Plan gradual exposure with clear goals and monitoring.

4. **Establish Clear, Specific Homework** – Provide written instructions with specific format, explicit rationale, in-session practice, commitment check, and plan for review at next session.

5. **Implement Collaborative Agenda-Setting** – At beginning of each session, explicitly set agenda with patient. Identify 2-3 target problems, prioritize together, allocate time, establish specific goals.

### Recommended Supervision Focus:

- **Guided discovery practice** – Role-play Socratic questioning with supervisor; record sessions for review
- **Case conceptualization development** – Write out formulation before each session; review with supervisor
- **Behavioral planning** – Practice developing exposure hierarchies and behavioral experiments
- **Homework design** – Create homework templates with clear instructions, rationale, and follow-up plans
- **Session structure** – Practice agenda-setting, mid-session check-ins, and end-of-session summaries

### Training Resources:

- Review CBT protocol for social anxiety disorder (e.g., Clark & Wells model)
- Study Socratic questioning techniques through recorded examples
- Practice behavioral exposure planning with case examples
- Review thought record and behavioral experiment procedures
