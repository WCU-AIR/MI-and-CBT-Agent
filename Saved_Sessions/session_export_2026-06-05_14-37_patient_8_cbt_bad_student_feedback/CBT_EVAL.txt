## SUMMARY OF CLINICAL FINDINGS

### Strengths Identified:
- Basic interpersonal warmth and non-judgmental stance
- Patient remained engaged throughout session
- Therapist was present and available
- No shaming or criticism of patient's symptoms

### Critical Deficiencies:

**Structural/Process Failures:**
- No agenda-setting or session structure (0/6)
- Minimal feedback elicitation (1/6)
- Poor time management and pacing (1/6)
- No homework or between-session planning (0/6)

**Clinical/Technical Failures:**
- Zero CBT techniques applied (0/6)
- No coherent treatment strategy (0/6)
- No Socratic questioning or guided discovery (0/6)
- Failure to focus on central eating disorder cognitions/behaviors (1/6)

**Conceptual Failures:**
- No eating disorder-specific formulation
- Misattribution of symptoms to stress rather than malnutrition
- No recognition of restriction-nausea maintaining cycle
- Inappropriate minimization of life-threatening medical condition

**Interpersonal/Professional Failures:**
- Excessive self-disclosure shifting focus from patient to therapist
- Lack of demonstrated clinical expertise
- Insufficient understanding of eating disorder pathophysiology
- Inappropriate normalization of anorexia nervosa

---

## CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Actions Required:

1. **Do not continue treating this patient without specialized eating disorder training.** Current approach is inadequate for life-threatening medical condition.

2. **Pursue specialized training in eating disorder-specific CBT.** Recommend: CBT-E (Enhanced Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Eating Disorders) certification or equivalent.

3. **Establish basic CBT structure immediately:** Every session must include agenda-setting, feedback-checking, and homework assignment.

4. **Address the restriction directly.** Focus on eating anxiety, feared consequences of eating, and the maintaining cycle—not mood or fatigue.

5. **Coordinate medical care.** Ensure patient is medically monitored and weight restoration is an explicit treatment goal.

6. **Eliminate self-disclosure and generic platitudes.** Replace with Socratic questioning and evidence-based interventions.

7. **Seek intensive supervision** from an eating disorder specialist before continuing treatment.

**Current Competency Level:** NOT COMPETENT to treat eating disorders
