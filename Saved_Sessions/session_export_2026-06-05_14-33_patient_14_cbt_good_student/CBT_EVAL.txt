## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR TRAINEE

**Strengths to Build Upon:**
- Your therapeutic relationship and collaboration skills are exemplary—this is the foundation of effective therapy
- Your guided discovery and Socratic questioning were skillfully executed
- Your empathic attunement and understanding of the patient's experience were exceptional
- Your homework assignment was specific, collaborative, and well-planned

**Critical Areas for Development:**
1. **Comprehensive Case Conceptualization:** Develop ability to identify all maintaining factors (not just the primary fear) and understand how they interact
2. **Treatment Planning:** Create explicit exposure hierarchies and treatment roadmaps that provide clear direction for therapy
3. **Psychoeducation:** Provide explicit education about the disorder and why the CBT approach works—this enhances patient motivation and engagement
4. **Parent Involvement:** In child cases, actively involve parents as treatment partners through coaching and guidance
5. **Technique Breadth:** Expand your application of CBT techniques beyond cognitive restructuring to include written thought records, interoceptive exposure, activity scheduling, and relaxation training

**Next Steps:**
- Review the detailed evaluation text for specific recommendations on each deficiency
- Practice developing exposure hierarchies for anxiety disorders
- Study parent coaching protocols for child anxiety disorders
- Expand your knowledge of ARFID-specific CBT interventions

**Overall Assessment:** You are a competent, warm, and skilled therapist with strong foundational CBT abilities. Your next step is to deepen your expertise in comprehensive case formulation and treatment planning. With these enhancements, you will move from good to excellent CBT practice.
