## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment:** This session does not meet standards for evidence-based CBT and represents a significant clinical concern.

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- Complete absence of collaborative agenda-setting
- Failure to respond to explicit patient feedback rejecting the therapeutic approach
- Poor understanding of patient's actual presentation (personality disorder traits vs. generic stress)
- Zero application of CBT techniques; only generic wellness advice
- No homework assignment or between-session work
- Excessive therapist self-disclosure undermining focus on patient

**Immediate Actions Required:**
1. Therapist must receive intensive supervision on CBT competence
2. Therapist should not continue treating this patient without significant changes in approach
3. Focus supervision on: guided discovery, case conceptualization, identification of key cognitions/behaviors, CBT techniques for personality disorders, collaboration and feedback responsiveness
4. Consider referral to CBT-trained clinician if therapist cannot rapidly improve competence

**Likelihood of Therapeutic Benefit:** Low. Current approach unlikely to produce meaningful change in patient's core difficulties and may damage therapeutic relationship.
