## CRITICAL CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Actions Required:

1. **Develop Coherent Conceptualization**: Recognize NPD as primary diagnosis; secondary depression stems from narcissistic injury (gap between grandiose self-perception and reality).

2. **Shift to Collaborative Model**: Replace advice-giving with Socratic questioning. Position therapist and patient as collaborative team.

3. **Address Treatment Skepticism Directly**: Acknowledge patient's valid concerns. Propose different approach. Explicitly contract around therapy's realistic goals.

4. **Apply Specific CBT Techniques**: Implement behavioral experiments, thought records, cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, and exposure—not generic advice.

5. **Establish Session Structure and Homework**: Create accountability and extend therapy beyond session.

6. **Seek Specialized Supervision**: This patient requires expertise in personality disorders and treatment resistance. Current approach poses risk to patient safety given passive suicidal ideation.

### Clinical Risk Assessment:

**Patient has expressed passive suicidal ideation** ("if he did not improve after his mother's death, he would see no reason to live"). Therapist's failure to deliver effective treatment increases suicide risk. This is not a matter of suboptimal technique—it is a matter of patient safety.
