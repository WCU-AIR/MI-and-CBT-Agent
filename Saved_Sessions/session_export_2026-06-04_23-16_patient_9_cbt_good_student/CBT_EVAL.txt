## COMPETENCY ASSESSMENT

**Current Competency Level:** Intermediate (General Therapeutic Skills) / Novice (Diagnosis-Specific Treatment Planning)

**Strengths:**
- Collaborative, empathic therapeutic stance
- Appropriate pacing and session structure
- Basic CBT technique application
- Professional demeanor and rapport-building

**Critical Gaps:**
- Lack of diagnosis-specific conceptualization for APD
- Misalignment between treatment strategy and patient diagnosis
- Insufficient focus on behavioral change and accountability
- Limited exploration of resistance and external motivation
- Minimal psychoeducation and rationale-building

**Priority Development Areas:**
1. Specialized training in Antisocial Personality Disorder assessment and treatment
2. Skill development in behavioral contingency management and accountability-focused interventions
3. Enhanced ability to recognize and address personality pathology
4. Improved mid-session feedback-seeking and collaborative case conceptualization
5. Supervision and consultation on complex cases
