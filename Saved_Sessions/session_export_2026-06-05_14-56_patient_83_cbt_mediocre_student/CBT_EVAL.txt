## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Immediate Actions Required:**

1. **Safety Assessment Training (URGENT):** Therapist must receive immediate training and supervision on suicide risk assessment, safety planning, and crisis protocols before continuing to see this patient.

2. **Structured Agenda-Setting:** Therapist requires training on collaborative agenda-setting with prioritization based on clinical urgency.

3. **CBT for Psychosis Specialization:** Therapist should pursue specialized training in delusion-focused CBT and voice-focused CBT to appropriately treat schizoaffective disorder.

4. **Collaborative Empiricism:** Therapist needs coaching on Socratic questioning and collaborative hypothesis-testing—core to CBT but largely absent.

5. **Structured Homework Protocol:** Establish clear homework assignment and review procedures with written templates and verification of understanding.

**Strengths to Build Upon:**

- Warm, validating therapeutic presence
- Non-judgmental stance appreciated by patient
- Appropriate pacing and space for reflection
- Recognition that multiple approaches may be needed

**Recommended Supervision Frequency:** Weekly individual supervision with CBT expert until competency demonstrated in: safety assessment, structured agenda-setting, collaborative empiricism, and CBT for psychosis.
