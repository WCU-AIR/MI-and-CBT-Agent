## CLINICAL SUPERVISION NOTES

**Competency Assessment:** Below Average (2.5/6)

**Strengths Demonstrated:**
- Appropriate opening with agenda-setting and mood assessment
- Consistent empathy, warmth, and non-judgmental stance
- Accurate reflection of explicit content and automatic thoughts
- Appropriate use of Socratic questioning for cognitive restructuring
- Collaborative language and tone throughout
- Specific, relevant homework assignment with behavioral component

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Failure to recognize acute mania** – Therapist did not identify or appropriately respond to acute manic episode with grandiosity, decreased need for sleep, poor insight, and functional impairment
2. **Absent safety assessment** – No assessment for suicidal/homicidal ideation, substance use escalation, financial risk, or relationship risk
3. **Inappropriate agenda** – Accepted patient's defensive framing rather than addressing acute psychiatric crisis
4. **Misaligned treatment strategy** – Applied cognitive restructuring (appropriate for chronic depression/anxiety) rather than evidence-based acute mania treatment (psychiatric stabilization, behavioral intervention, psychoeducation)
5. **Failure to address poor insight** – Did not recognize or explore patient's defensive denial and lack of illness awareness
6. **Inadequate problem formulation** – Did not collaboratively reality-test patient's framing of problem
7. **Focus on secondary issues** – Addressed secondary cognition about others' understanding rather than key manic cognitions (grandiosity, urgency, poor insight) and behaviors (sleep deprivation, excessive activity, substance use)
8. **Limited technique diversity** – Relied exclusively on cognitive restructuring without behavioral techniques appropriate for acute mania
9. **Inadequate psychiatric coordination** – No discussion of medication, psychiatrist contact, or coordination of care
10. **Insufficient psychoeducation** – Did not provide education about bipolar disorder, manic episodes, or relapse prevention

**Recommended Supervision Plan:**
- **Immediate:** Review case with bipolar disorder specialist; conduct safety assessment; coordinate with patient's psychiatrist
- **Short-term:** Provide education on bipolar disorder presentation and evidence-based acute mania treatment; review safety assessment and crisis planning protocols; practice motivational interviewing for poor insight
- **Ongoing:** Close supervision of bipolar cases; consultation with psychiatrist before each session; review of session recordings; practice with less acute presentations until competency demonstrated
- **Training:** Recommend specialized training in bipolar disorder treatment or reassignment to cases with less acute psychiatric presentations

**Likelihood of Therapeutic Change:** Low to Moderate. Session may have established rapport and introduced cognitive model, but unlikely to produce meaningful change in acute manic symptoms. May have inadvertently reinforced denial and delayed appropriate psychiatric intervention.
