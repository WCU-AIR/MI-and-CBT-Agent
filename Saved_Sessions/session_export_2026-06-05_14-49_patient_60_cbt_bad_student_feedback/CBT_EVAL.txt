## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Session Quality Assessment:** This session fails to meet standards for evidence-based cognitive-behavioral therapy. While the therapist demonstrates warmth and creates a safe environment for disclosure, the clinical work is fundamentally non-CBT in nature.

**Key Strengths:**
- Warm, non-judgmental tone
- Patient feels safe disclosing sensitive material
- One attempt at collaborative agenda-setting
- Genuine expressions of concern

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- Misdiagnosis of the clinical problem (treats iatrogenic sexual dysfunction as motivational issue)
- No CBT techniques applied
- No case formulation or treatment strategy
- Excessive therapist self-disclosure
- Failure to address relational/communication barriers
- No homework or between-session structure

**Immediate Actions Required:**
1. Supervisor review of recorded session
2. Training in CBT case formulation and assessment
3. Training in Socratic questioning and guided discovery
4. Training in cognitive restructuring and behavioral activation
5. Weekly supervision with recorded session review
6. Consider pairing with experienced CBT mentor
