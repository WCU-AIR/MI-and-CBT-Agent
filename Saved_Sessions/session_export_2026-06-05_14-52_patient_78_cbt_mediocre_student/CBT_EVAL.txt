**SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW:**

This therapist demonstrates adequate foundational counseling skills but requires immediate, intensive training in trauma-focused CBT. The session, while not harmful, is unlikely to produce meaningful PTSD symptom reduction. Recommend: (1) Mandatory training in Prolonged Exposure Therapy (PE) and Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT) for PTSD; (2) Supervision focused on trauma-specific case conceptualization; (3) Practice with Socratic questioning and guided discovery; (4) Development of structured PTSD assessment protocols; (5) Consultation with trauma-specialized supervisor for this case. Patient remains cooperative and willing to engage—with significant improvements in therapist technique, this case could respond well to evidence-based PTSD treatment.
