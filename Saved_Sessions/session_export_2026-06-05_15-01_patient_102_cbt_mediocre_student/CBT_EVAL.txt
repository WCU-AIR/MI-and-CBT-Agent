## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE ACTION

### **1. DISCONTINUE CURRENT CBT APPROACH IMMEDIATELY**
- Delirium is a medical emergency requiring medical intervention, not psychotherapy
- Cognitive restructuring is contraindicated for acute delirium
- Behavioral activation may be medically inappropriate post-surgery

### **2. COORDINATE WITH MEDICAL TEAM**
- Assess and treat delirium causes (surgery, medications, dehydration, infection, etc.)
- Determine medical appropriateness of any psychological intervention
- Establish expected trajectory and prognosis

### **3. SHIFT TO SUPPORTIVE CARE**
- Provide orientation support, reassurance, and emotional support
- Focus on pain management, nutrition, hydration, and sleep
- Avoid cognitive restructuring and behavioral activation until acute delirium resolves

### **4. URGENT SUPERVISION AND RETRAINING**
- Therapist requires immediate supervision by experienced clinician
- Training needed in delirium recognition and management
- Training needed in case conceptualization integrating medical factors
- Training needed in clinical judgment about contraindications to CBT

### **5. ASSESSMENT OF PATIENT SAFETY**
- Monitor for adverse effects of inappropriate psychotherapy
- Ensure medical team is aware of psychological intervention
- Establish clear communication between therapist and medical team

**This session does NOT qualify as evidence-based CBT and is likely to produce harm rather than therapeutic change.**
