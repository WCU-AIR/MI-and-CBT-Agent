## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Summary:**

This session demonstrates adequate interpersonal skills but inadequate CBT competency for treating alcohol use disorder. The therapist's warm, supportive demeanor created a safe environment, but the clinical approach was fundamentally misaligned with evidence-based treatment for this patient's dual diagnoses (moderate alcohol use disorder + alcohol-induced depressive disorder).

**Critical Clinical Concerns:**

1. **Misdiagnosis of Primary Problem:** Therapist treated this as a depression case with alcohol involvement, rather than as an alcohol use disorder case with secondary depression. This represents a fundamental conceptualization error.

2. **High Relapse Risk:** Patient is 3 days abstinent after 20 years of heavy daily use (6-12 beers/day). Without systematic relapse prevention and coping strategies, relapse is highly likely within days to weeks.

3. **Unaddressed Withdrawal Risk:** No assessment of withdrawal symptoms despite patient's heavy use and early abstinence. Potential medical danger not addressed.

4. **Insufficient Collaboration:** Patient expressed ambivalence multiple times ("I guess that makes sense, but..."; "It feels a bit optimistic"). This was not explored or addressed, reducing treatment engagement and commitment.

5. **Vague Homework:** Homework lacks specificity, rationale, and follow-up plan. Patient unlikely to complete it consistently.

**Strengths to Build Upon:**

- Warm, empathic interpersonal style
- Ability to establish rapport
- Awareness that CBT techniques exist
- Patient engagement and willingness to participate

**Areas Requiring Immediate Development:**

- Treatment conceptualization for substance use disorders
- Functional analysis and understanding of maintaining factors
- Guided discovery and Socratic questioning
- Collaborative, shared decision-making approach
- Relapse prevention planning
- Specific, measurable intervention design
- Assessment of medical and psychiatric risk

**Recommended Supervision Plan:**

1. **Weekly 1-hour supervision** focusing on case conceptualization and treatment planning
2. **Session recording review** with feedback on guided discovery, collaboration, and technique application
3. **Training in evidence-based protocols** (SAMHSA CBT for substance use manual)
4. **Role-plays** with feedback before seeing additional substance use disorder patients
5. **Consultation with addiction medicine specialist** regarding medical management and withdrawal assessment

**Patient Prognosis Without Intervention Changes:**

**Poor.** Without significant changes to the therapist's approach, this patient is at high risk for:
- Relapse within 1-2 weeks
- Worsening depression if relapse occurs
- Family relationship deterioration
- Loss of motivation and disengagement from treatment
- Potential medical complications from withdrawal or relapse

**Patient Prognosis With Recommended Changes:**

**Improved.** If the therapist implements the recommended changes, patient outcomes would likely improve through:
- Systematic relapse prevention reducing relapse risk
- Collaborative approach increasing engagement and commitment
- Functional analysis addressing core maintaining factors
- Specific coping strategies providing concrete alternatives to drinking
- Clear measurement allowing progress monitoring and adjustment
