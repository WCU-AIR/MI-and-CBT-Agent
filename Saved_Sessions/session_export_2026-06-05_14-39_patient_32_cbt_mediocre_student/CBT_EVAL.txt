## SUMMARY COMMENTARY

**Competency Level:** Novice/Below Competency Standard

**Strengths:**
- Adequate interpersonal warmth, gentleness, and professionalism
- Correct identification of presenting problem (delusions about listening devices)
- Non-confrontational approach that did not damage therapeutic relationship
- Recognition of value of behavioral activation and activity engagement

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- **Absence of structured agenda-setting and session planning** (Item 1: Score 2)
- **Minimal feedback elicitation and no homework review** (Item 2: Score 1)
- **Failure to engage in guided discovery and Socratic questioning** (Item 7: Score 1)
- **Absence of collaborative empiricism and reality-testing** (Items 5, 9, 10)
- **Superficial cognitive work without systematic restructuring** (Item 10: Score 2)
- **Lack of coherent, theory-driven treatment strategy** (Item 9: Score 1)
- **Inadequate homework assignment and review** (Item 11: Score 2)
- **Fundamental misunderstanding of delusional disorder** as distinct from anxiety disorders

**Clinical Impact:**
The current approach is unlikely to produce meaningful change in delusional beliefs or functioning. Interventions reflect generic anxiety management rather than evidence-based CBT for psychotic disorders. Patient remains at risk for continued social withdrawal, academic decline, and reinforcement of avoidance behaviors.

**Immediate Recommendations:**
1. Implement structured agenda-setting in all sessions
2. Systematically review homework and track progress
3. Introduce guided discovery and Socratic questioning
4. Develop collaborative empiricism and reality-testing protocols
5. Create coherent, theory-driven treatment strategy specific to delusional disorder
6. Assess for medication need and coordinate with psychiatry
7. Provide intensive supervision focused on CBT fundamentals and psychotic disorder specialization

**Recommendation:** Therapist should not continue treating patients with psychotic disorders without additional specialized training and close supervision.
