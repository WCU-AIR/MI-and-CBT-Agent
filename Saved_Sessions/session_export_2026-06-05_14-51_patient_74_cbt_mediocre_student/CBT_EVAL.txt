## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **HIGHEST PRIORITY (Must implement before next session):**

1. **Establish Clear, Collaborative Agenda** – Do not offer vague options. Identify 2-3 specific priorities and ask for patient input.

2. **Develop Explicit Case Conceptualization** – Articulate core beliefs (paranoid + perfectionist), behavioral patterns (hypervigilance, over-functioning), and how interventions will address them.

3. **Implement Structured Homework with Mandatory Follow-Up** – Assign with written instructions, clear rationale, and practice during session. Review at start of every subsequent session without exception.

4. **Use Guided Discovery/Socratic Questioning** – Replace directive suggestions with skillful questions that help patient generate insights and examine evidence.

5. **Design Behavioral Experiments** – Help patient test paranoid and perfectionist beliefs through real-world observation and behavioral trials.

### **SECONDARY PRIORITY (Implement within 2-3 sessions):**

6. Build genuine collaboration—solicit patient input on goals and interventions; adjust based on feedback.
7. Regularly elicit feedback throughout session—check understanding, solicit reactions, adjust approach.
8. Address paranoid ideation directly and systematically—explore evidence, consider alternatives.
9. Address perfectionism and control needs—help patient understand costs and build motivation for flexibility.
10. Introduce structured CBT tools (thought records, behavioral activation schedules, exposure hierarchies).
11. Develop multi-session treatment plan with clear progression from assessment to intervention to consolidation.
12. Seek supervision from therapist specializing in personality disorder treatment.
