## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Identified:**
- Basic empathy and warmth maintained throughout
- Non-hostile, professional demeanor
- Patient remained engaged despite inadequate clinical work
- Appropriate boundaries maintained

**Critical Areas Requiring Immediate Intervention:**
1. **Agenda-Setting:** Implement structured, collaborative agendas in every session with explicit priorities and time allocation
2. **Homework Management:** Conduct detailed review of homework in every session; problem-solve barriers; adjust assignments based on patient feedback
3. **Functional Analysis:** Conduct comprehensive analysis of anger episodes using ABC model or detailed functional analysis worksheet
4. **Guided Discovery:** Replace advice-giving with Socratic questioning; teach therapist to ask questions rather than provide solutions
5. **Treatment Strategy:** Develop coherent, evidence-based strategy for IED including emotion regulation, targeted cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, and consideration of pharmacological intervention
6. **Session Continuity:** Establish clear connection between sessions; reference previous work; demonstrate preparation and engagement

**Supervision Recommendation:** Weekly individual supervision with focus on CBT competencies. Role-play of Socratic questioning and functional analysis. Review of session recordings. Completion of advanced CBT training for anger disorders/IED. Reassessment of competency after 8 weeks of intensive supervision.
