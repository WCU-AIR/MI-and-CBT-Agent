## SUPERVISOR RECOMMENDATIONS

**Priority Level: URGENT**

This case requires immediate intervention to redirect treatment toward evidence-based approaches. The therapist demonstrates adequate general therapeutic skills but critical deficiencies in case conceptualization and treatment planning for early childhood attachment disorders. Recommend:

1. **Immediate supervision meeting** to discuss case conceptualization and treatment approach
2. **Consultation with attachment disorder specialist** to develop evidence-based treatment plan
3. **Professional development** in attachment disorders, early childhood trauma, and Parent-Child Interaction Therapy
4. **Caregiver involvement** in all future sessions and treatment planning
5. **Safety assessment and planning** to address risk of child leaving with strangers
6. **Shift to behavioral intervention** with clear, measurable goals and systematic progress monitoring

**Without these changes, risk of continued ineffective treatment or potential harm is significant.**
