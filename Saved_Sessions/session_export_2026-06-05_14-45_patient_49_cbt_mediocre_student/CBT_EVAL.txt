## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Strengths Demonstrated:
- Adequate warmth, professionalism, and non-judgmental stance
- Patient feels comfortable sharing and is willing to engage
- Awareness of some CBT concepts (cognitive reframing, behavioral tracking, sleep hygiene)
- Appropriate professional boundaries maintained
- Patient demonstrates engagement and willingness to try interventions

### Critical Areas for Improvement:

**1. Case Conceptualization (Priority 1)**
- Develop explicit formulation identifying: presenting problem, maintaining factors, treatment approach, expected timeline, measurable goals
- Communicate this formulation to patient collaboratively
- Reference: CBT-I manual (Perlis, Aloia, & Kuhn)

**2. Evidence-Based Interventions (Priority 1)**
- Implement stimulus control: bed only for sleep/sex, get up if awake >20 minutes, consistent schedule
- Implement sleep restriction: limit time in bed to match actual sleep time, gradually increase
- These are most effective behavioral interventions for insomnia; currently absent

**3. Collaborative Empiricism (Priority 2)**
- Shift from prescriptive ("you could try") to collaborative ("what do you think?" "let's test this together")
- Frame interventions as experiments, not directives
- Invite patient to generate solutions before offering therapist suggestions
- Practice: 70% open questions, 30% closed questions

**4. Systematic Assessment (Priority 2)**
- Explore anticipatory anxiety: specific content, timing, duration, feared consequences
- Assess secondary anxiety about sleep itself
- Inquire about behavioral patterns: getting up to work, lying awake with partner
- Assess substance use: alcohol timing/amount, caffeine timing/amount
- Assess medication timing: β2-agonists for asthma
- Assess sleep environment and schedule consistency

**5. Homework Review & Treatment Adjustment (Priority 1)**
- Session 2 must begin with: "How did the homework go? What worked? What didn't? What barriers did you encounter?"
- Use this information to adjust treatment
- Do not repeat same suggestions without modification
- Introduce new techniques as appropriate

**6. Explicit Homework Assignment (Priority 2)**
- Assign explicitly, not tentatively
- Provide clear rationale for each task
- Give specific instructions (e.g., sleep diary format)
- Discuss anticipated barriers
- Check commitment ("On a scale 0-10, how likely are you to complete this?")
- Plan for review in next session

**7. Feedback Elicitation (Priority 2)**
- Use open questions: "What are your thoughts?" "What concerns do you have?" "How is this feeling?"
- Avoid yes/no questions
- Elicit feedback at least 3-4 times per session
- Adjust approach based on feedback

**8. Thought Records & Behavioral Experiments (Priority 2)**
- Use structured thought records: trigger → automatic thought → evidence for/against → alternative thought → outcome
- Design behavioral experiments to test beliefs
- Example: "Let's test whether you really can't function when tired. Notice times this week when you're tired but still accomplish things."

**9. Worry Management (Priority 2)**
- Implement worry time scheduling: schedule specific time (e.g., 3:00 PM) for 15 minutes to worry
- Outside this time, postpone worry to scheduled time
- Addresses anticipatory anxiety maintaining the insomnia

**10. Session Structure (Priority 3)**
- Establish clear phases: opening → agenda → assessment → intervention → homework → feedback/summary
- Explicit time boundaries: "We have 45 minutes. First 10 minutes reviewing homework, 20 minutes on strategy, 15 minutes planning next steps."
- Clear transitions between topics

### Supervision Plan:
1. **Immediate:** Review CBT-I manual and evidence-based protocols
2. **Before next session:** Write out explicit case formulation; plan stimulus control and sleep restriction introduction
3. **Next session:** Implement recommendations above; record session for review
4. **Post-session:** Review recording with supervisor; identify specific moments to practice collaborative language, Socratic questioning, homework review
5. **Ongoing:** Weekly supervision with focus on these priority areas until competency demonstrated
