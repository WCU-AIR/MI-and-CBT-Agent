### OVERALL EVALUATION

| Item | Scale (0-66) | Comments |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Score (Sum of Items 1–11)** | **51/66** | **Overall Assessment:** This session demonstrates solid foundational CBT skills with competent agenda-setting, collaboration, and interpersonal effectiveness. The therapist accurately conceptualized the anxiety cycle and skillfully used Socratic questioning to facilitate patient insight. Cognitive restructuring was appropriate, and behavioral exposure planning was well-graduated. However, the session falls short of evidence-based CBT for specific phobia due to significant gaps in technique application and incomplete assessment. Most critically, shame-related and social anxiety cognitions were not adequately explored; specific relaxation techniques were not taught or practiced in-session; no thought records or cognitive homework were assigned; the mechanism of change (habituation) was not explicitly explained; and alcohol use was not integrated into the treatment plan. These gaps limit the likelihood of optimal treatment outcomes. **Therapist Competency:** The therapist demonstrates solid competency in general therapeutic skills and basic CBT conceptualization but shows inconsistent competency in specialized phobia treatment. Foundational CBT training is evident, but specialized training in exposure-based treatment for specific phobias appears limited. **Immediate Priorities for Improvement:** (1) Explore shame and social anxiety cognitions; (2) Teach and practice specific relaxation techniques in-session; (3) Assign thought records as cognitive homework; (4) Explicitly explain habituation and disconfirmation of predictions; (5) Integrate alcohol use into treatment plan. **Likelihood of Therapeutic Change:** Reasonable foundation provided, but without above modifications, patient may struggle during exposure and may not develop full range of skills for long-term recovery. Implementation of recommendations in subsequent sessions will significantly improve treatment effectiveness. |

**SUPERVISOR NOTES:**

This therapist demonstrates competent foundational skills but requires targeted training in specialized phobia treatment. Recommend: (1) Review and practice exposure-based CBT protocols for specific phobias; (2) Develop skill in exploring shame and social anxiety cognitions using Socratic questioning; (3) Practice teaching and demonstrating relaxation techniques; (4) Integrate thought records and behavioral experiments into standard practice; (5) Develop explicit multi-session treatment planning; (6) Address substance use in context of exposure-based treatment. Recommend supervision focused on these areas before independent phobia treatment cases are assigned.
