**Competency Level:** Novice (with this population)

**Immediate Actions Required:**

1. **URGENT:** Conduct comprehensive functional behavior analysis of self-injury and aggression before continuing treatment.

2. **URGENT:** Develop specialized conceptualization accounting for autism, intellectual disability, sensory sensitivities, and communication limitations.

3. **URGENT:** Shift from cognitive restructuring to behavioral and sensory-based interventions appropriate for severe intellectual disability.

4. **URGENT:** Involve parents/caregivers as active co-therapists in all treatment planning and implementation.

5. **URGENT:** Pursue specialized training in evidence-based interventions for autism and intellectual disability.

**Key Learning Needs:**

- Functional behavior analysis and behavioral intervention
- Sensory assessment and regulation strategies
- Communication support for individuals with intellectual disability
- Adaptation of therapeutic techniques for severe cognitive limitations
- Family-centered intervention approaches
- Evidence-based practices for autism and intellectual disability

**Supervision Plan:**

- Weekly supervision with specialist in autism and intellectual disability
- Review of functional assessment and behavioral intervention planning
- Observation of sessions with feedback on technique application
- Training in concrete, scaffolded teaching methods
- Development of individualized treatment conceptualization
- Systematic progress monitoring and data-based decision making

**Prognosis with Current Approach:** POOR

**Prognosis with Recommended Changes:** FAIR TO GOOD (with appropriate intervention, behavioral and emotional regulation improvements are achievable)
