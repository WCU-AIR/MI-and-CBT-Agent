## CRITICAL CLINICAL NOTES FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Immediate Actions Required:**

1. **URGENT SAFETY INTERVENTION:** Conduct comprehensive suicide risk assessment with patient immediately (phone or in-person). Develop written safety plan including crisis resources (988 Lifeline, Crisis Text Line, emergency services), reasons for living, warning signs, coping strategies, and means restriction discussion.

2. **PSYCHIATRIC COORDINATION:** Contact patient's psychiatrist immediately regarding suicidal ideation, method-seeking behavior, and current medication regimen. Discuss hospitalization decision if imminent risk present.

3. **THERAPIST SUPERVISION:** Implement immediate, intensive supervision. Therapist requires training in: (a) suicide risk assessment and safety planning; (b) structured agenda-setting and prioritization; (c) Socratic questioning and guided discovery; (d) systematic CBT technique application; (e) bipolar-specific treatment; (f) high-risk patient management.

4. **CASE REASSIGNMENT CONSIDERATION:** Given severity of clinical failure and risk to patient, consider whether this therapist should continue treating this patient or whether case should be reassigned to more experienced clinician pending training completion.
