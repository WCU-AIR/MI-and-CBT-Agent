## CLINICAL SUMMARY

### Strengths Identified:
- Genuine warmth and non-judgmental stance
- Basic empathic listening
- Safe, accessible therapeutic environment
- Consistent supportive tone

### Critical Deficiencies:
1. **Complete absence of CBT structure and techniques** – No agenda, no case conceptualization, no evidence-based interventions
2. **Failure to address core clinical issues** – Alcohol use disorder not systematically addressed; no relapse prevention; alcohol-depression link never explained
3. **Lack of collaboration** – Patient positioned as passive recipient of advice rather than active partner
4. **Inefficient time use** – Repetitive generic advice, excessive personal narratives, no prioritization
5. **No accountability structure** – No homework, no between-session tasks, no mechanism for behavioral change
6. **Inappropriate therapeutic stance** – Excessive self-disclosure, false reassurance, blurred boundaries

### Impact on Patient Care:
**Likelihood of therapeutic change: LOW.** Without coherent treatment strategy, specific CBT techniques, relapse prevention planning, and between-session structure, patient is unlikely to achieve sustained abstinence or significant mood improvement. High risk for relapse within days to weeks.

### Recommendations:
1. Develop CBT case conceptualization immediately
2. Implement relapse prevention planning (URGENT)
3. Apply specific CBT techniques (behavioral activation, cognitive restructuring, functional analysis)
4. Establish structured agenda-setting and homework
5. Shift from advice-giving to guided discovery
6. Pursue intensive CBT training and weekly expert supervision
