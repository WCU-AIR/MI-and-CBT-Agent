## CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SUPERVISION

**Immediate Priority Actions:**

1. **Establish Structured Agenda-Setting:** Begin every session with explicit, collaborative identification and prioritization of 1-2 target problems. Allocate time accordingly.

2. **Conduct Comprehensive Safety Assessment:** Assess suicidal ideation, mood cycling, and antidepressant effects at every session. Establish safety planning if needed.

3. **Develop Written Case Formulation:** Create explicit formulation explaining how patient's problems are maintained and what treatment strategy will address them. Share collaboratively with patient.

4. **Replace Directive Advice with Socratic Questioning:** Practice using questions to facilitate discovery rather than telling patient what to think. Aim for 80% questions, 20% statements.

5. **Implement Systematic Behavioral Activation:** Make behavioral activation primary intervention with specific activity scheduling, mood monitoring, and clear rationale.

6. **Assign Structured, Specific Homework:** Replace vague suggestions with detailed assignments including rationale, tracking mechanisms, and commitment checks.

7. **Review Homework Systematically:** Begin every session with homework review. Assess completion, troubleshoot barriers, and reinforce progress.

8. **Address Patient Resistance Collaboratively:** When patient expresses doubt or difficulty, explore alternatives collaboratively rather than repeating same approach.

**Supervision Focus:** Therapist demonstrates adequate interpersonal skills but requires intensive training in CBT structure, case formulation, Socratic questioning, and systematic technique application. Recommend weekly supervision with recorded session review until competency demonstrated.
