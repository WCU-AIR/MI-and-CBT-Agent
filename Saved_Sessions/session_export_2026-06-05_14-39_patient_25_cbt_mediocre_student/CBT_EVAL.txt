## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Observed:**
- Adequate interpersonal warmth and non-judgmental stance
- Safe environment created for disclosure of sensitive eating disorder behaviors
- Identification of some relevant automatic thoughts
- Patient remained engaged and cooperative

**Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Lack of eating disorder-specific expertise** – No CBT-E framework, no functional analysis, no exposure/response prevention
2. **Inadequate comorbidity integration** – Depression symptoms not systematically assessed; no behavioral activation
3. **Directive rather than collaborative approach** – Shift away from Socratic method; patient positioned as advice recipient
4. **Superficial cognitive work** – Automatic thoughts identified but not systematically explored; core beliefs missed
5. **Inadequate behavioral intervention** – Central maintaining factors (dietary restriction, purging) not addressed
6. **Poor homework design and review** – Vague assignments, no rationale, no baseline monitoring, superficial follow-up

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- CBT-E training and case conceptualization for eating disorders
- Functional analysis skills
- Guided discovery and Socratic questioning practice
- Behavioral activation for depression
- Homework design and review procedures
- Recording and review of sessions with supervisor
