## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Patient Presentation:** 27-year-old female with Borderline Personality Disorder, treatment-resistant depression, chronic suicidal ideation, three prior psychiatric hospitalizations for overdose, social isolation, identity disturbance, and multiple failed treatment attempts.

**Session Quality:** Poor (9/66)

**Critical Safety Concern:** Patient explicitly stated "Sometimes I just want to disappear" with documented history of three overdose attempts. Therapist acknowledged this statement once but never conducted suicide risk assessment, explored suicidal cognitions, developed safety plan, or discussed crisis resources. This represents a serious breach of standard of care.

**Key Deficiencies:**
- No agenda or session structure
- No suicide risk assessment or safety planning
- No CBT techniques applied
- No BPD-specific conceptualization
- Excessive self-disclosure and boundary violations
- Minimal genuine collaboration
- Generic advice without coherent treatment strategy
- No meaningful homework

**Immediate Supervisor Actions Recommended:**
1. Review suicide risk assessment and safety planning protocols with therapist
2. Require specialized training in DBT or BPD-specific treatment
3. Mandate supervisor consultation before next patient session
4. Consider referral of this patient to more experienced clinician
5. Provide intensive feedback and skill-building in CBT technique application
