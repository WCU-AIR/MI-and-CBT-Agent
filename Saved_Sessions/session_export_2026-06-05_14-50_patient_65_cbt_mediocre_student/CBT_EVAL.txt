## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Quality Summary:**
This session represents a missed opportunity for evidence-based OCD treatment. While rapport was established, the clinical approach was fundamentally inappropriate for this patient's presentation. The therapist's interventions—distraction, cognitive restructuring, and reassurance—are either counterproductive or ineffective for OCD, particularly for a patient with delusional-intensity beliefs and absent insight.

**Most Critical Issues Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Delusional-intensity beliefs not recognized or appropriately addressed**
2. **No evidence-based OCD treatment strategy (ERP) implemented**
3. **Counterproductive interventions (reassurance, distraction) reinforcing OCD cycle**
4. **Inadequate assessment of obsessions, compulsions, and maintaining factors**
5. **No consideration of pharmacological augmentation or family involvement**

**Recommended Actions:**
- Immediate consultation with OCD specialist
- Formal training in Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP)
- Assessment of patient's insight level and discussion of pharmacological options with psychiatry
- Restructuring of treatment plan around evidence-based OCD interventions
- Ongoing clinical supervision focused on OCD treatment competency

**Prognosis Without Intervention:**
Without significant changes to treatment approach, patient is at high risk for treatment dropout, symptom worsening, and continued functional impairment (unemployment, social isolation, family conflict).
