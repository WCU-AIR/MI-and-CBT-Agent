## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Classification:** NOT CBT. Generic supportive counseling with inappropriate reassurance for psychotic illness.

**Competency Assessment:** Psychiatrist demonstrates inadequate competence in psychiatric assessment, psychotic disorder treatment, and CBT application. Fundamental misunderstanding of schizophreniform disorder evident throughout.

**Likelihood of Therapeutic Change:** Very low. Active psychotic symptoms require medication + specialized psychotherapy, not lifestyle suggestions. Patient's growing hopelessness suggests current approach is failing.

**Most Critical Deficiencies:**
- No psychiatric assessment or safety evaluation
- No medication discussion
- Normalization of severe psychotic symptoms
- Invalidating minimization
- Absence of evidence-based interventions
- Inappropriate self-disclosure

**Immediate Intervention Required:** Yes. Patient requires referral to specialized first-episode psychosis program with antipsychotic medication evaluation and evidence-based psychotherapy.
