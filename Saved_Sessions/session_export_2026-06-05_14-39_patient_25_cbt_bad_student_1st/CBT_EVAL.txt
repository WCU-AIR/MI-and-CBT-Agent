## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Patient Safety Concern:** Patient disclosed "thoughts of death" and recurrent binge-purge episodes with compensatory behaviors. Therapist did not assess suicide risk, explore ideation, or develop safety plan. This represents a critical clinical oversight requiring immediate remediation.

**Therapeutic Alliance Risk:** Patient's statements ("I want to believe that, really," "It means a lot to hear that") suggest polite compliance rather than genuine engagement. Therapist's repeated personal anecdotes and generic advice may further erode alliance and increase patient hopelessness.

**Recommendation:** This therapist requires immediate, intensive supervision and comprehensive CBT training before continuing to see patients with serious psychiatric conditions. Consider temporary case reassignment pending competency demonstration.
