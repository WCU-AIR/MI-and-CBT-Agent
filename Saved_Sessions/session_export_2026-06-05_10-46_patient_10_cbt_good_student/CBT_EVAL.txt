## SUMMARY OF CRITICAL DEFICIENCIES

✗ **Incomplete case conceptualization** – Failed to integrate ADHD, learning disorder, panic, and depression into treatment planning; treated anxiety as primary problem without addressing how other diagnoses contribute; no medication evaluation discussion despite documented ADHD history  

✗ **Narrow treatment strategy** – Focused exclusively on anxiety and cognitive restructuring; did not address ADHD attention difficulties, learning disorder academic struggles, or depression-related hopelessness; no prioritization or longer-term planning  

✗ **Limited CBT technique application** – Cognitive restructuring competent but lacked depth (no thought records, limited practice); deep breathing mentioned but not taught or practiced; missing techniques: behavioral experiments, exposure, comprehensive activity scheduling, psychoeducation, interoceptive awareness, values clarification  

✗ **Insufficient homework specification** – Assignment lacked important details (which subject, how many sessions, specific timing, break structure); no monitoring tool or explicit review plan; deep breathing rationale and technique not explained; missing complementary homework for sleep, social engagement, accommodations  

✗ **Neglect of cultural and identity factors** – Did not acknowledge experience as only Dominican student, acculturation stress, or identity questions; missed opportunity to build alliance through cultural understanding  

✗ **Inadequate assessment of complex presentation** – Did not explore ADHD manifestations, learning disorder's academic impact, panic symptoms, sleep, social support, or available accommodations  

✗ **Pacing and prioritization issues** – Spent substantial time on cognitive restructuring without addressing breadth of assessment needed; no explicit time allocation or discussion of realistic scope; depth on one issue at expense of breadth needed for complex case

## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPROVEMENT

**Priority 1 (Critical):** Develop comprehensive case conceptualization integrating all diagnoses and identifying how they interact. Create shared formulation with patient.

**Priority 2 (Critical):** Assess and address ADHD directly, including medication evaluation discussion and ADHD-specific study strategies.

**Priority 3 (Critical):** Integrate learning disorder into academic planning; connect patient to disability services and accommodations.

**Priority 4 (High):** Specify and detail homework with monitoring tools and explicit review plans; teach deep breathing explicitly with practice in session.

**Priority 5 (High):** Address depression and hopelessness explicitly; implement behavioral activation and sleep intervention.

**Priority 6 (High):** Explore and validate cultural and identity factors; acknowledge acculturation stress and identity questions.

**Priority 7 (Moderate):** Improve Socratic questioning through more open-ended inquiry; reduce therapist-structured questions.

**Priority 8 (Moderate):** Develop longer-term treatment plan with clear goals, milestones, and referral needs.

**Priority 9 (Moderate):** Connect patient to systemic supports (disability services, psychiatry, cultural/LGBTQ+ resources).

**Priority 10 (Moderate):** Implement systematic homework review and adjustment process.

## CLINICAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT

**Likelihood of Therapeutic Change:** Moderate – Patient may experience temporary anxiety reduction and modest improvement in study avoidance if 15-minute sessions and cognitive reframing are successfully implemented. However, without addressing ADHD, learning disorder, depression, and systemic factors, sustained academic improvement is unlikely. Patient will likely return to hopelessness and academic struggle when anxiety intervention alone proves insufficient.

**Evidence-Based CBT Qualification:** This session does not fully qualify as evidence-based CBT for this patient's complex presentation. While cognitive restructuring and behavioral activation components are evidence-based for anxiety, the session lacks comprehensive assessment, multi-modal treatment planning, and integration of evidence-based interventions for ADHD, learning disorder, depression, and panic needed for this case.

**Supervision Recommendation:** Intensive supervision recommended focusing on: (1) complex case conceptualization, (2) comorbidity management, (3) integration of multiple CBT techniques, (4) cultural competence. Therapist shows promise and should continue CBT training with emphasis on advanced skills for complex presentations.
