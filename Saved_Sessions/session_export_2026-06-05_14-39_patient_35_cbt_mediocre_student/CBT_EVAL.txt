## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL FEEDBACK

**Session Strengths to Reinforce:**
- Genuine therapeutic warmth and professionalism
- Accurate empathic reflection of patient concerns
- Patient feels heard and is engaged
- Appropriate professional boundaries
- Clear, accessible language

**Critical Areas Requiring Immediate Development:**

1. **Guided Discovery (Priority 1):** Move from telling to asking. Practice Socratic questioning in supervision. Record sessions and analyze ratio of questions to statements.

2. **Collaboration (Priority 2):** Position patient as active partner in problem-solving. Implement shared decision-making about interventions. Elicit patient's own ideas before offering suggestions.

3. **Assessment & Case Formulation (Priority 3):** Conduct comprehensive symptom assessment. Develop explicit written formulation integrating medical and psychological factors. Present formulation collaboratively to patient.

4. **Systematic Technique Application (Priority 4):** Teach one technique thoroughly per session rather than multiple techniques superficially. Provide clear rationale, step-by-step instruction, and in-session practice.

5. **Medical Integration (Priority 5):** Develop competence in treating depression secondary to medical illness. Coordinate with neurology. Address neurobiological basis of fatigue. Integrate medical optimization with psychological treatment.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- Role-play Socratic questioning techniques
- Review recorded sessions for guided discovery opportunities
- Practice collaborative agenda-setting
- Develop case formulation template for medically complex patients
- Establish communication protocols with medical providers

**Training Resources:**
- Padesky & Mooney (2012) on Socratic questioning
- Westbrook, Kennerley, & Kirk (2011) on CBT techniques
- Specialized training on depression in medical illness
- Supervision with experienced CBT trainer
