## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Overall Clinical Impression:**

This session represents a **fundamental failure to deliver evidence-based cognitive-behavioral therapy**. While the therapist demonstrates basic interpersonal warmth and maintains a non-hostile tone, the session lacks any coherent CBT framework, diagnostic formulation, or therapeutic strategy. The therapist's approach is reactive, unfocused, and potentially counterproductive for a patient with Antisocial Personality Disorder.

**Key Concerns:**

1. **No CBT Delivery:** Zero application of CBT techniques; session consists of supportive counseling and generic advice.
2. **Diagnostic Blindness:** Complete failure to recognize or address APD features (externalization, lack of insight, lack of remorse).
3. **Poor Boundary Management:** Excessive self-disclosure and personal questions compromise professional therapeutic relationship.
4. **Resistance Not Addressed:** Patient explicitly rejects interventions; therapist continues same approach without modification.
5. **No Homework or Between-Session Work:** Critical CBT component completely absent.
6. **Lack of Treatability Assessment:** No exploration of whether patient is amenable to standard CBT or what realistic goals might be.

**Recommendation:**

**Immediate supervision and retraining required.** This therapist requires intensive consultation on: (1) CBT technique application, (2) personality disorder treatment, (3) professional boundary management, and (4) case conceptualization and treatment planning. Consider pairing with experienced CBT supervisor for next 5-10 sessions with recorded session review.
