## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Patient Presentation:**
Oscar presents with Illness Anxiety Disorder (care-seeking type) and Panic Disorder, with 20-year psychiatric history and recent ER admission. Primary symptoms: persistent preoccupation with Lyme disease relapse despite negative tests, fatigue, physical sensations interpreted as illness, sleep disruption, avoidance of outdoor activity, reassurance-seeking, and functional impairment.

**Session Outcome:**
Ineffective. No evidence-based CBT delivered. Therapist provided supportive counseling with generic lifestyle advice and excessive self-disclosure. Maintaining factors of illness anxiety (avoidance, reassurance-seeking, body monitoring, catastrophic thinking) were not addressed.

**Recommended Immediate Interventions:**
1. Establish structured CBT framework with agenda-setting and case conceptualization
2. Eliminate reassurance and excessive self-disclosure; maintain professional boundaries
3. Replace reassurance with guided discovery (Socratic questioning)
4. Identify and address safety behaviors and reassurance-seeking patterns
5. Plan behavioral experiments to test catastrophic predictions
6. Implement structured behavioral activation to counter avoidance
7. Provide psychoeducation about cognitive-behavioral model of health anxiety
8. Apply evidence-based techniques: thought records, cognitive restructuring, exposure
9. Assign structured homework with clear rationale and follow-up
10. Seek intensive supervision and training in CBT for health anxiety disorders

**Competency Assessment:**
Current competency level: **BELOW STANDARD.** Therapist demonstrates basic interpersonal warmth but lacks fundamental competency in evidence-based CBT. Does not understand cognitive-behavioral model of illness anxiety disorder, does not apply evidence-based techniques, and does not structure sessions according to CBT principles. **NOT QUALIFIED** to independently treat patients with illness anxiety disorder or panic disorder without intensive supervision and retraining.
