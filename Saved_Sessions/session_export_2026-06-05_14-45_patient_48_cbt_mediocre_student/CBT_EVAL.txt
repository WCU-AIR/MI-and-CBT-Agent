## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Observed:**
- Adequate warmth, empathy, and non-judgmental stance
- Professional demeanor and calm tone
- Some collaborative language used
- Identified some relevant automatic thoughts
- Patient appeared comfortable in session

**Critical Areas Requiring Immediate Attention:**

1. **Agenda-Setting and Structure:** Implement collaborative agenda at start of every session with specific target problems, prioritization, and time allocation. Review prior homework systematically.

2. **Guided Discovery:** Replace direct advice with Socratic questioning. Develop 5-10 questions per session to help patient reach insights independently.

3. **Case Conceptualization:** Create explicit, written formulation explaining how reassurance-seeking and avoidance maintain Oscar's illness anxiety disorder. Share with patient.

4. **Targeting Maintaining Mechanisms:** Make primary focus the reduction of reassurance-seeking (repeated testing) and avoidance (staying home). Use behavioral experiments to test patient's predictions.

5. **Homework Protocol:** Assign specific, detailed homework with written instructions and explicit rationale. Practice in-session before assigning. Review thoroughly in next session and adjust based on outcomes.

6. **Panic Disorder Intervention:** Develop specific exposure-based interventions for comorbid panic disorder, including interoceptive and situational exposure.

**Recommendation:** Therapist should engage in intensive supervision with CBT specialist focusing on evidence-based treatment of illness anxiety disorder and panic disorder. Consider additional training in Socratic questioning and behavioral experiments.
