| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Score (Sum of Items 1–11)** | **11/66** | **CRITICAL DEFICIENCIES:** This session represents a fundamental departure from evidence-based CBT. While the therapist demonstrated genuine warmth and created a supportive environment, the session lacked core CBT components: no agenda-setting, no case formulation, no systematic technique application, and no coherent treatment strategy. The therapist provided generic supportive counseling rather than evidence-based treatment for hoarding disorder and depression. Excessive self-disclosure and boundary violations undermined the therapeutic frame. Core maintaining cognitions and behaviors were not addressed. For a patient with severe functional impairment (15 years of social isolation, severely cluttered living space), this approach is insufficient. **IMMEDIATE ACTIONS REQUIRED:** (1) Establish collaborative agendas with explicit goals; (2) Develop written case formulation; (3) Eliminate self-disclosure and maintain boundaries; (4) Replace advice with Socratic questioning; (5) Systematically explore core cognitions; (6) Implement structured behavioral interventions; (7) Assign specific monitored homework; (8) Seek CBT-focused supervision. **This session does not qualify as evidence-based CBT.** |

## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Identified:**
- Genuine warmth and non-judgmental stance created safe environment
- Patient felt heard and less alone
- No hostility or harm; supportive interaction maintained
- Patient remained engaged throughout

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Remediation:**
1. **Absence of CBT structure** – No agenda, formulation, or systematic technique application
2. **Failure to address core mechanisms** – Fear of loss, catastrophizing, acquisition behavior, social avoidance never explored
3. **Boundary violations** – Excessive self-disclosure shifted focus from patient to therapist
4. **Prescriptive rather than collaborative** – Direct advice without genuine collaborative empiricism
5. **Poor time management** – Aimless, tangential session with repetitive suggestions
6. **Inadequate homework** – Vague suggestions without rationale, specificity, or monitoring

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- CBT case formulation and treatment planning
- Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Boundary maintenance and professional frame
- Structured behavioral interventions (activation, exposure, response prevention)
- Homework design and monitoring
- Session agenda-setting and time management

**Next Steps:**
Schedule weekly supervision with CBT-focused feedback. Bring session recordings. Practice specific skills before next patient session.
