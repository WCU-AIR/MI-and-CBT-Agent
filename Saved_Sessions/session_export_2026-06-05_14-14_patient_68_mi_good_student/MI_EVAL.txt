### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Adequate but inconsistent softening of sustain talk. Notable gaps: (1) Insufficient strategic reframing—could reframe lack of awareness as emerging awareness; (2) Limited use of double-sided reflections throughout session; (3) Missed opportunity to explore function of sustain talk—when Kenji says "helps me relax," could ask "What if we found other ways to get that relaxation?"; (4) Insufficient pivoting toward change talk; (5) Limited explicit framing of ambivalence as sign of readiness.

## Overall Clinical Summary

**Current MI Competency Level:** Solid, Competent Practice (4.1/5)

**Key Strengths:**
- Exceptional empathic accuracy with sophisticated, insight-deepening reflections
- Exemplary partnership, collaboration, and autonomy support throughout
- Warm, genuine affirmation grounded in client's actual statements
- Skillful handling of ambivalence with double-sided reflections
- Strategic session progression from awareness to behavioral commitment

**Primary Areas for Development:**
- Systematic evocation of core values and deeper reasons for change to build intrinsic motivation
- Strategic amplification of change talk through targeted follow-up questions
- Functional analysis of drinking behavior to deepen understanding of triggers and alternatives
- More robust building of discrepancy between current behavior and client values/health
- Consistent strategic softening of sustain talk through reframing and pivoting

**Client Motivation Outcome:** Moderate success. Kenji moves from minimal awareness to willingness to experiment, but confidence remains at 5/10, indicating ambivalence is still strong. Safe, collaborative space created, but deep intrinsic motivation not yet fully developed.

**Recommendation:** With focused practice on values evocation, change talk amplification, and functional analysis, this clinician could advance from competent to excellent MI practice and significantly increase likelihood of evoking sustained client motivation for change.
