## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Identified:**
- Appropriate warmth, gentleness, and non-judgmental tone
- Correct identification of some relevant automatic thoughts related to social anxiety
- Attempt to introduce multiple intervention modalities
- Appropriate reassurance and normalization of difficulty
- Patient remained engaged across sessions

**Critical Areas for Immediate Improvement:**
1. **Exposure Therapy Implementation** – HIGHEST PRIORITY. This is gold standard for social anxiety disorder and is essential for meaningful improvement. Develop fear hierarchy and systematically expose patient to feared situations.

2. **Systematic Homework Review** – HIGHEST PRIORITY. Begin each session with 10-15 minute detailed review of homework. Explore barriers, adjust assignments, and problem-solve collaboratively when homework fails.

3. **Treatment Conceptualization** – Explicitly explain to patient how her anxiety disorder works and why treatment approach will help. Increase engagement and collaboration.

4. **Collaborative Approach** – Shift from directive advice-giving to genuine collaboration. Actively solicit patient input, involve in decision-making, use guided discovery.

5. **Guided Discovery** – Replace direct suggestion with skillful Socratic questioning. Help patient reach insights independently rather than imposing therapist's perspective.

**Supervision Recommendation:** Weekly supervision with CBT specialist recommended until competency standards met. Consider formal CBT training or certification program if not already completed.
