## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Therapist Performance Summary:**

This therapist demonstrates adequate interpersonal warmth and concern but lacks the clinical competence and technical skill required to deliver evidence-based CBT. The overall score of 11/66 reflects critical deficiencies across all major CBT competency domains.

**Strengths:**
- Maintains appropriate professional boundaries
- Expresses genuine concern and warmth
- Allows patient adequate space to speak
- Not hostile or demeaning

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- No agenda-setting or session structure
- Misunderstands psychotic symptoms (normalizes hallucinations and delusions as universal experience)
- No suicide risk assessment despite patient history of suicidal ideation
- No CBT techniques implemented; relies on positive thinking platitudes
- No case conceptualization or treatment strategy
- Advice-giving dominates; collaboration absent
- Guided discovery not used; Socratic questioning absent
- Repetitive, circular sessions with no progression
- No measurement of progress or accountability
- Inadequate homework with no follow-up

**Most Critical Concern:**
Patient has schizoaffective disorder with active auditory hallucinations, persecutory delusions, anhedonia, social withdrawal, and history of suicidal ideation. Current therapeutic approach is unlikely to produce meaningful symptom improvement and may be harmful. Therapist does not assess suicide risk, does not address psychotic symptoms appropriately, and does not implement evidence-based interventions.

**Immediate Actions Required:**
1. Conduct comprehensive suicide risk assessment at next session
2. Develop written safety plan collaboratively with patient
3. Establish clear agenda and case conceptualization
4. Implement CBT for psychosis training
5. Arrange clinical supervision with CBT-trained clinician

**Recommendation:**
This therapist requires immediate training and ongoing supervision in CBT, particularly CBT for psychosis. Until competence is demonstrated, consider referring this patient to a clinician with appropriate expertise.
