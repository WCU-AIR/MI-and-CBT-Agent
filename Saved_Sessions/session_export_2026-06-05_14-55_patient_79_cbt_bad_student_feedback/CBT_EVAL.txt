## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Summary:**
This session does not meet the standard of care for evidence-based CBT. The therapist's approach is more consistent with supportive counseling than structured psychotherapy. While interpersonal warmth is present, clinical competence in CBT is absent.

**Immediate Action Items:**
1. **Safety Planning** (URGENT): Conduct functional analysis of self-injury and develop safety plan with alternatives before next session
2. **Diagnostic Assessment** (URGENT): Implement prospective daily symptom tracking to confirm PMDD diagnosis
3. **Professional Boundaries** (HIGH): Significantly reduce personal self-disclosure; establish clinical authority
4. **CBT Structure** (HIGH): Implement agenda-setting, feedback elicitation, and homework assignment in all future sessions
5. **Guided Discovery** (HIGH): Replace advice-giving with Socratic questioning; practice in supervision

**Supervision Plan:**
- Weekly 1-hour supervision sessions
- Review recorded sessions focusing on agenda-setting, feedback elicitation, and guided discovery
- Role-play Socratic questioning techniques
- Develop case formulation template for Emma's case
- Training in PMDD assessment and treatment
- Training in safety assessment and planning for self-injury

Without significant changes in therapist approach, prognosis for meaningful symptom improvement is poor. Patient may experience temporary support but is unlikely to develop specific skills or achieve sustained change. Risk of self-injury escalation is elevated due to lack of systematic safety planning.
