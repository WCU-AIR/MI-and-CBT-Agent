## CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Immediate Priorities (Next Session):

1. **Conduct comprehensive assessment** of alliance rupture with medical team, maternal dependence, and illness-focused identity—these are likely more central to noncompliance than specific mask anxiety thought.

2. **Develop structured exposure hierarchy** with clear success criteria and graduated mask-wearing durations (not 5-10 second pauses).

3. **Teach grounding and breathing techniques** (5-4-3-2-1 grounding, box breathing, tactical breathing) for acute panic management.

4. **Coordinate with medical team** to rebuild alliance and ensure psychological and medical interventions are aligned.

### Ongoing Development Areas:

- Comprehensive assessment of maintaining factors in medical-psychological presentations
- Designing and implementing exposure-based interventions with appropriate intensity
- Addressing relational and alliance factors in medical settings
- Integrating medical context into psychological formulation
- Written homework materials with specific data collection and contingency planning
- Behavioral activation and activity scheduling techniques
- Relaxation and grounding technique instruction
