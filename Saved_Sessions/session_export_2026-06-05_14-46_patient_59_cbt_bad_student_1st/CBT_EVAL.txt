## DETAILED RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

### Critical Priority Actions (Implement Immediately):

1. **Establish CBT Session Structure in Every Session**
   - Agenda setting (5 min) → Bridge from previous session (2 min) → Homework review (5 min) → Main work (30 min) → Homework assignment (5 min) → Feedback (3 min)
   - Use this structure as a template for every session until it becomes automatic

2. **Eliminate Excessive Self-Disclosure**
   - Stop sharing personal anecdotes
   - When tempted to say "I remember when...", ask a question instead: "Tell me more about that" or "What happened next?"
   - Maintain professional boundaries while remaining warm and empathic

3. **Master Socratic Questioning**
   - Replace advice-giving with guided discovery questions
   - When patient expresses a worry, ask: "What evidence do you have for that thought?" "What evidence contradicts it?" "What would you tell a friend who had that worry?"
   - Practice until guided discovery becomes your default approach

4. **Identify and Target Automatic Thoughts Systematically**
   - In every session, explicitly identify 1-3 core automatic thoughts driving distress
   - Write them down and explore using guided discovery
   - Develop behavioral experiments to test them
   - Track changes over time

5. **Assign Specific Homework with Rationale**
   - At end of every session, assign 1-2 specific tasks (not vague suggestions)
   - Provide clear rationale: "The reason we're doing this is..."
   - Check feasibility: "Does this feel doable? What might get in the way?"
   - Establish follow-up: "Next session, we'll review what you discovered"
   - Provide written materials (worksheets, forms)

### Secondary Priority Actions (Implement Within 1 Month):

6. **Develop Case Conceptualization Skills**
   - After each session, write a one-page case conceptualization including: presenting problem, maintaining factors, treatment targets, proposed interventions
   - Use this to guide the next session

7. **Understand Medical Context**
   - For patients with medical complexity, research the condition and medications
   - Understand how medical factors affect psychiatric symptoms and treatment
   - Coordinate with medical team when appropriate

8. **Establish Clear Goals and Measure Progress**
   - In first session, establish baseline symptoms and measurable goals
   - Use rating scales (0-10 anxiety, worry, etc.)
   - Track progress across sessions
   - Adjust treatment if progress stalls

9. **Seek Formal Supervision and Training**
   - Engage in weekly clinical supervision with a CBT expert
   - Review session recordings for feedback on technique
   - Complete a formal CBT training program (Beck Institute or equivalent)
   - Read CBT manuals: "Cognitive Therapy: Basics and Beyond" by Judith Beck

10. **Distinguish Between Supportive Counseling and CBT**
    - Understand that warmth and reassurance are necessary but not sufficient for CBT
    - CBT requires systematic identification and modification of thoughts/behaviors through guided discovery and behavioral experiments
    - Be clear about which approach you're using and why
