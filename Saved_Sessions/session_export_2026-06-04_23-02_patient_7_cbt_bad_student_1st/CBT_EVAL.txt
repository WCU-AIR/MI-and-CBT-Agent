## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment:** This session does not qualify as evidence-based CBT. It is supportive counseling with generic life advice.

**Patient Safety Concern:** Patient expressed fear that hallucinated judge "threatens to kill me." No suicide risk assessment, safety plan, or psychiatric monitoring discussed. This is a critical clinical omission.

**Coordination of Care:** Patient has Huntington's disease requiring specialized neurological and psychiatric care. No coordination with medical team evident. Therapist should establish relationships with specialists and coordinate care.

**Recommended Supervision Plan:** 
- Weekly individual supervision focusing on CBT structure and technique
- Formal training in CBT for psychosis (minimum 40 hours)
- Recording review of all sessions with feedback on agenda-setting, guided discovery, and technique application
- Competency assessment before independent practice with psychotic patients
- Minimum 10 supervised cases with psychotic symptoms before independent practice

**Timeline for Competency Development:** 6-12 months of intensive supervision and training required before this therapist can be considered competent to deliver CBT to patients with psychotic symptoms.
