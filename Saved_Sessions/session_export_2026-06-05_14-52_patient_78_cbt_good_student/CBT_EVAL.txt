## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

- ✓ Warm, validating interpersonal style that reduces shame

### Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention
- ✗ **Incomplete treatment strategy:** Addresses only cognitive restructuring; does not target hyperarousal, avoidance, trauma processing, or shame
- ✗ **Limited technique application:** Single example without formal structure, behavioral integration, or core belief exploration
- ✗ **Insufficient focus on avoidance:** Primary maintaining factor in PTSD not systematically addressed
- ✗ **Inadequate exploration of shame/guilt:** Central PTSD emotions not targeted for intervention
- ✗ **No somatic interventions:** Hyperarousal symptoms (racing heart, chest tightness) not addressed
- ✗ **Lack of explicit treatment rationale:** Patient not informed why cognitive restructuring is starting point or how it fits into comprehensive plan
- ✗ **No written materials:** No thought record or formal structure to support independent practice

### Recommended Actions for Next Session
1. **Develop comprehensive treatment plan** addressing all PTSD symptom clusters; explicitly discuss with patient
2. **Teach somatic intervention** (breathing technique, grounding) linked to cognitive work
3. **Identify avoidance behaviors** as maintaining factor; develop behavioral activation plan
4. **Explore shame cognition** ("I'm failing my family") using guided discovery
5. **Provide written thought record** with clear instructions for independent practice
6. **Discuss patient's reluctance** to address trauma collaboratively; assess readiness for trauma-focused work
7. **Practice cognitive restructuring** with 2-3 additional examples; explore underlying core beliefs
8. **Establish clear review plan** for homework and session-by-session outcome monitoring

### Professional Development Recommendations
- Pursue training in evidence-based trauma treatments (Prolonged Exposure or Cognitive Processing Therapy)
- Study comprehensive PTSD treatment protocols to move beyond single-technique approach
- Practice integrating somatic, cognitive, and behavioral interventions in trauma treatment
- Develop competence in shame-focused interventions for PTSD
- Consult with trauma-focused CBT supervisor to develop skills in trauma processing
