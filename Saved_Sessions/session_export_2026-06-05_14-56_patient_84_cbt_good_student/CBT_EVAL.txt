## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CLINICAL SUPERVISION

### Immediate Priorities (Before Next Session)

1. **Develop Comprehensive Case Formulation** – Create written formulation integrating: presenting problem, precipitating factors (sister's death), maintaining factors (avoidance, grief avoidance, decision-making difficulty), personality factors (schizoid traits), possible comorbid features (hoarding), and age-related factors.

2. **Assess and Address Grief and Loss** – In next session, explicitly explore connection between sister's death and apartment disorganization. Ask about emotional experience of loss and whether organizing feels like accepting her death.

3. **Explore Deeper Core Beliefs** – Use guided discovery to identify beliefs underlying surface thought. Ask: "What would it mean about you if you couldn't organize?" "What do you fear would happen?" "What beliefs do you have about your ability to make decisions?"

4. **Tailor to Schizoid Personality Structure** – Recognize that standard motivation-enhancement strategies may be counterproductive. Frame change in terms of patient's values and intellectual interests. Emphasize autonomy rather than external pressure.

5. **Assess for Hoarding Disorder** – Conduct detailed assessment of attachment to research materials. Determine whether this reflects normal collecting, hoarding disorder, or obsessive-compulsive features.

### Important Enhancements

6. **Develop Explicit Treatment Plan** – Create written plan specifying: overall goals, specific targets, timeline, interventions for each target, and how personality factors will be addressed.

7. **Enhance Behavioral Activation** – Develop broader behavioral activation plan beyond single task, including meaningful activities aligned with patient's interests and values.

8. **Develop Detailed Contingency Plans** – For each obstacle (fatigue, decision-making difficulty), specify concrete strategies and problem-solving steps.

9. **Assign Written Materials** – Provide written thought record form and homework instructions. Create patient-friendly, structured documentation aligned with patient's cognitive style.

10. **Establish Systematic Feedback Process** – At end of each session, routinely elicit feedback: "What was most helpful? What was less helpful? What should we do differently?" Use feedback to adjust approach.

11. **Plan Homework Review and Adjustment** – At next session, explicitly review homework completion. Treat non-completion as data about what is/isn't working. Adjust assignments collaboratively based on patient experience.

**Supervisor Notes:** This therapist demonstrates good basic CBT skills and would benefit from training in: (1) comprehensive case formulation for complex cases, (2) grief-focused CBT, (3) personality-disorder-specific CBT, and (4) integration of multiple treatment modalities. Recommend continued supervision with focus on developing deeper case conceptualization and tailoring interventions to patient complexity.
