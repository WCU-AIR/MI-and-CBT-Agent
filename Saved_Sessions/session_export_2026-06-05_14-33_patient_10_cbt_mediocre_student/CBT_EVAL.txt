## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Actions Required:

1. **Develop Case Conceptualization** – Integrate ADHD, SLD, depression, panic, and psychosocial stressors into coherent treatment plan before next session.

2. **Conduct Thorough Assessment** – Systematically assess ADHD symptoms, learning disability impact, depression severity, panic symptoms, avoidance patterns, and cultural factors before intervening.

3. **Address ADHD with Behavioral Strategies** – Recognize executive dysfunction as neurological problem requiring task breakdown, external structure, and environmental modifications—not just cognitive reframing.

4. **Target Avoidance Through Behavioral Experiments** – Design specific experiments to test avoidance predictions and gradually expose patient to feared situations (studying).

5. **Establish Structured Agenda** – Collaboratively set clear goals, prioritize problems, allocate time, and follow agenda in each session.

6. **Redesign Homework** – Provide one or two specific, structured, written tasks with clear rationale, feasibility check, and explicit commitment.

7. **Increase Socratic Questioning** – Replace direct suggestions with guided discovery questions that help patient develop own insights.

8. **Seek Specialized Training** – Pursue formal CBT training and ADHD-specific CBT consultation. Consider weekly supervision with CBT expert.

### Strengths to Build Upon:
- Genuine warmth and empathy
- Professional demeanor and appropriate boundaries
- Ability to create safe therapeutic environment
- Capacity to identify some relevant cognitions

### Priority Areas for Development:
- CBT case conceptualization and treatment planning
- ADHD-specific interventions and executive function strategies
- Behavioral techniques (experiments, exposure, activity scheduling)
- Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Structured agenda-setting and time management
- Homework design and implementation
- Assessment of complex, multifaceted presentations
