## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Competency Assessment:** Therapist demonstrates basic counseling skills (empathy, warmth, non-judgmental stance) but lacks competency in evidence-based CBT for ARFID. Does not appear to have clear understanding of ARFID maintenance factors or specialized treatment protocols.

**Most Critical Changes Needed Immediately:**
1. Develop evidence-based ARFID formulation identifying avoidance and anxiety conditioning
2. Replace cognitive interventions with exposure-based interventions as primary treatment
3. Implement structured agenda-setting at beginning of every session
4. Involve parents as active treatment partners
5. Conduct thorough behavioral assessment before introducing interventions
6. Use Socratic questioning (guided discovery) rather than direct suggestion
7. Develop specific, measurable behavioral homework with clear rationale

**Clinical Risk:** Without immediate intervention, patient's ARFID will likely worsen, weight will continue to decline, and social withdrawal may increase. Current approach may inadvertently reinforce avoidance by suggesting coping strategies at home rather than addressing core problem of eating avoidance at school.
