## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Strengths Identified:
- Adequate interpersonal warmth and professional demeanor
- Appropriate use of validating language
- Patient remained engaged and expressed willingness to try interventions
- Identified some relevant depressive cognitions

### Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:
1. **Absence of structured agenda and case formulation** (Items 1, 9)
2. **Minimal collaborative empiricism** (Items 2, 5, 7)
3. **Inadequate systematic assessment** (Item 8)
4. **Vague, unstructured homework** (Item 11)
5. **No homework review or session progression** (Item 11)
6. **Absence of relapse prevention planning** (Item 9)
7. **Premature and poorly applied cognitive techniques** (Item 10)

### Recommended Supervision Focus:
- Developing explicit case formulations integrating depression and substance use disorder
- Conducting systematic functional analysis of substance use
- Implementing structured relapse prevention planning
- Mastering Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Applying CBT techniques systematically using structured tools (thought records, activity schedules)
- Establishing baseline measurements and progress monitoring
- Reviewing and adjusting homework in every session

### Patient Safety Concerns:
- Suicide risk not assessed (depressive symptoms + substance use = elevated risk)
- Withdrawal risk not assessed
- Relapse risk not addressed with explicit prevention planning

### Recommendation:
**Intensive supervision required.** Therapist should not continue treating patients with substance use disorders without significant skill development in relapse prevention, functional analysis, and systematic CBT application. Consider pairing with experienced CBT supervisor for weekly case review and recorded session feedback.
