- No CBT techniques applied
- No trauma-focused intervention
- No behavioral activation or cognitive restructuring

### Diagnostic Gaps

- Dissociative amnesia not addressed
- PTSD symptoms (nightmares, hyperarousal) not explored
- Anhedonia and hopelessness not targeted
- Avoidance patterns not addressed

### Recommendation
**This therapist is not currently competent to provide evidence-based CBT for patients with complex trauma, suicidal ideation, and dissociative disorders. Immediate specialized training and supervision are required.**
