## CRITICAL CLINICAL NOTES FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### **URGENT SAFETY CONCERN:**
Despite patient's presenting complaint of depression and documented history of suicide attempt at age 21, therapist conducted **no assessment of current depressive symptoms, suicidal ideation, or safety planning**. This represents a significant clinical gap with potential safety implications. **Recommend immediate comprehensive depression and suicide risk assessment at next session.**

### **Key Deficiencies Requiring Intervention:**
1. **Gender-specific anxiety not adequately explored** — Patient explicitly mentioned concerns about being judged related to transgender identity; therapist treated as generic anxiety
2. **No depression assessment** — Despite presenting complaint; no PHQ-9 or equivalent screening
3. **Narrow case formulation** — Single situation focus without exploring broader patterns, core beliefs, or maintaining factors
4. **Limited behavioral assessment** — Avoidance behaviors identified but not comprehensively explored
5. **No written CBT tools** — Thought records, monitoring forms, or documentation provided to patient

### **Recommended Supervisor Actions:**
- Review therapist's suicide risk assessment protocols and provide training/practice
- Discuss gender-affirming CBT competencies and how to integrate gender-specific assessment
- Provide feedback on comprehensive case formulation development
- Review written CBT tool templates and documentation standards
- Observe next session or review recording to ensure safety assessment is conducted
