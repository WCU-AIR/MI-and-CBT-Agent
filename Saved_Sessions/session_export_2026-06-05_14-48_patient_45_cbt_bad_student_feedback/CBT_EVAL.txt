### OVERALL EVALUATION

| Item | Scale (0-66) | Comments |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Score (Sum of Items 1–11)** | **9/66** | **CRITICAL DEFICIENCIES:** This session represents a fundamental failure to deliver evidence-based CBT. While the therapist demonstrated adequate interpersonal warmth and maintained a supportive stance, the core elements of CBT were entirely absent. No agenda was established, no case conceptualization was articulated, no CBT techniques were applied, and no structured homework was assigned. The therapist functioned as a supportive counselor offering generic wellness advice rather than as a CBT practitioner. **PATIENT IMPACT:** Peggy presents with severe GAD and emerging agoraphobia—conditions that respond well to evidence-based CBT but will likely worsen without it. She is already avoiding leaving her apartment and seeking excessive reassurance. Generic reassurance and positive thinking, while temporarily soothing, reinforce anxiety long-term by validating catastrophic thoughts without addressing them systematically. After two sessions, she has received no psychoeducation, no structured assessment, no cognitive interventions, and no graded exposure plan. **IMMEDIATE ACTIONS REQUIRED:** (1) Establish CBT-based case conceptualization explaining anxiety maintenance cycle; (2) Transition from advice-giving to guided discovery using Socratic questions; (3) Assign structured homework (thought records, behavioral experiments) with clear rationale; (4) Develop graded exposure hierarchy; (5) Seek CBT-specific supervision or training to develop competency in evidence-based techniques for anxiety disorders. Without these changes, treatment is unlikely to produce meaningful improvement and may result in patient disengagement and worsening agoraphobic avoidance. |

## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Identified:**
- Therapist maintained warm, non-hostile, professional demeanor
- Patient felt heard and continued to engage across sessions
- Appropriate boundaries maintained
- Supportive and encouraging tone

**Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Remediation:**
- Complete absence of CBT structure, conceptualization, and technique
- No agenda-setting or collaborative treatment planning
- No guided discovery or Socratic questioning
- No identification or targeting of central cognitions and behaviors
- No structured homework or between-session skill practice
- Reliance on generic reassurance and platitudes
- Excessive therapist self-disclosure

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- CBT case conceptualization for anxiety disorders
- Socratic questioning and guided discovery techniques
- Thought records and cognitive restructuring
- Graded exposure planning for agoraphobia
- Structured homework assignment and review
- Anxiety cycle psychoeducation

**Training Resources Recommended:**
- "Cognitive Therapy of Anxiety Disorders" (David Clark)
- "Mastery of Your Anxiety and Panic" (MAPS) manual
- CBT-specific supervision with anxiety disorder specialist
- Session recording review with trained CBT supervisor
