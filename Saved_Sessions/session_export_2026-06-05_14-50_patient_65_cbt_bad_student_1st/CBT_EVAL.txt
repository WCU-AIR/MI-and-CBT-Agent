## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Critical Findings

**Strengths (Limited):**
- Therapist maintains warm, non-hostile tone
- Therapist is present and engaged in conversation
- Therapist does not appear judgmental or demeaning in demeanor

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- No agenda or session structure
- No application of any CBT techniques
- Fundamental misunderstanding of OCD pathology
- No guided discovery or Socratic questioning
- No collaboration or shared decision-making
- No focus on central OCD cognitions/behaviors
- No coherent treatment strategy
- No homework assignment
- Excessive self-disclosure
- Minimal feedback elicitation

### Immediate Supervisor Actions

1. **Restrict OCD caseload** until therapist completes specialized training
2. **Mandate enrollment** in IOCDF-approved CBT for OCD training program (within 3 months)
3. **Arrange weekly supervision** with OCD specialist
4. **Provide psychoeducation** on obsession-compulsion cycle and evidence-based OCD treatment
5. **Review and redesign** therapist's treatment approach using CBT protocol
6. **Monitor subsequent sessions** for implementation of recommendations

### Prognosis for Improvement

With focused training, supervision, and commitment to evidence-based practice, this therapist can develop competence in OCD treatment. However, without immediate intervention, patient outcomes will remain poor and potential for harm exists.
