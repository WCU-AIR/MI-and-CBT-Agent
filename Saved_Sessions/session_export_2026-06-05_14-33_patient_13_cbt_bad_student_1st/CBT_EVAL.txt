## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Identified:**
- Genuine warmth and accessibility; created safe therapeutic environment
- Non-judgmental stance; patient felt comfortable sharing
- Absence of criticism or shaming; appropriate for patient with hypersensitivity to negative evaluation
- Patient remained engaged and willing to attempt behavioral steps

**Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Remediation:**

1. **CBT Structure:** No agenda-setting, no guided discovery, no systematic homework review
2. **Core Techniques:** Zero Socratic questions; no thought records; no behavioral experiments; no exposure planning
3. **Conceptualization:** Failed to target mechanisms maintaining anxiety (avoidance, catastrophic predictions, perfectionism, safety behaviors)
4. **Collaboration:** Expert-advice stance undermines patient agency and collaboration
5. **Strategy:** Disconnected suggestions lack theoretical rationale or logical sequence

**Recommended Training Priorities:**
- Formal CBT training program (e.g., Beck Institute, ABCT-approved)
- Weekly supervision with experienced CBT supervisor
- Study of CBT manuals for social anxiety and avoidant personality disorder
- Practice with role-plays and recorded session review
- Re-evaluation after 8-12 weeks of focused training

**Clinical Concern:**
Continuing to provide supportive counseling under the label of "CBT" is misleading to patients and delays access to evidence-based treatment. This patient requires structured cognitive-behavioral intervention to achieve meaningful, sustained symptom reduction.
