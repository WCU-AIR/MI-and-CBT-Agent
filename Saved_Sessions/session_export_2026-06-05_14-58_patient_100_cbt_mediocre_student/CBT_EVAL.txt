## DETAILED COMPETENCY SUMMARY

### Strengths Demonstrated:
- Warm, welcoming, non-judgmental therapeutic presence
- Basic empathic validation of patient's emotional experience
- Professional boundaries maintained throughout
- Partial implementation of awareness/monitoring (appropriate for trichotillomania)
- Appropriate reassurance that change takes time and perfection is not expected

### Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:
1. **No evidence-based treatment approach:** Habit reversal training, competing response training, and exposure and response prevention—first-line treatments for trichotillomania—are not implemented
2. **Lack of functional analysis:** Maintaining factors (tension-relief cycle, habit, avoidance, shame cycle) not systematically identified
3. **No treatment conceptualization:** Therapist does not communicate clear understanding of why patient engages in behavior or how treatment will address it
4. **Minimal guided discovery:** Reliance on direct suggestion rather than Socratic questioning undermines patient agency and insight development
5. **Inadequate homework:** Lacks specificity, rationale, and critically, no review in subsequent session
6. **No structured collaboration:** Interventions therapist-directed rather than jointly developed
7. **Scattered intervention focus:** Multiple interventions suggested without clear prioritization or connection to maintaining factors

### Recommendations for Supervision:
- Intensive training in habit reversal training (HRT) protocol for trichotillomania
- Skill development in functional analysis and treatment conceptualization
- Practice with Socratic questioning and guided discovery techniques
- Training in structured agenda-setting and collaborative treatment planning
- Development of specific, detailed homework protocols with systematic review procedures
- Supervision of at least 2-3 additional sessions with this patient with real-time feedback

### Likelihood of Therapeutic Effectiveness:
**Low.** Without evidence-based technique application and a clear treatment plan, meaningful symptom improvement is unlikely. Patient's statement in session two ("stuck in this cycle") suggests session one interventions were ineffective, and therapist's failure to adjust approach indicates treatment is not responsive to patient needs.

### Competency Level:
**Trainee/Developing.** Therapist demonstrates foundational therapeutic skills but lacks competency in CBT-specific techniques and treatment planning. Not ready for independent CBT practice without significant supervision and skill development.
