## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Observed:**
- Appropriate warmth, professionalism, and non-judgmental stance
- Basic empathic reflection of explicit content
- Identification of some relevant cognitions (pain catastrophizing, opioid-seeking beliefs)
- Recognition of multiple problems and attempt to address them
- Maintenance of basic session structure

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Intervention:**
1. **No coherent opioid use disorder treatment strategy** – Generic depression interventions applied without addressing core mechanisms of addiction
2. **Absence of functional analysis** – No systematic exploration of antecedents, behaviors, and consequences of opioid-seeking
3. **Abandonment of guided discovery** – Directive advice-giving replaces Socratic questioning
4. **Minimal feedback elicitation** – Patient's doubts and concerns not explored
5. **Inadequate homework structure and complete absence of review** – Fundamental CBT failure
6. **No relapse prevention or coping skills** – Critical gap for opioid use disorder treatment
7. **Failure to address shame, identity conflict, and ambivalence** – Powerful maintaining factors unexplored

**Recommended Actions:**
- Intensive training in evidence-based treatment for opioid use disorder (CBT for substance use, relapse prevention therapy)
- Supervised practice in functional analysis and behavioral conceptualization
- Skill-building in Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Development of structured homework protocols with systematic review
- Consultation with addiction specialists
- Regular supervision with focus on collaborative agenda-setting and feedback elicitation

**Risk Assessment:**
Current approach unlikely to produce sustained therapeutic change. Patient at significant risk for continued opioid use, overdose, and deterioration in family/professional functioning without intervention strategy modifications.
