## SUMMARY ASSESSMENT

### Overall Competency Level: **Pre-CBT / Supportive Counseling Only**

**Strengths:**
- Demonstrated warmth, acceptance, and non-judgmental stance
- Validated patient's distress and normalized some experiences
- Offered genuine encouragement and support
- Maintained patient engagement throughout session

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- **No CBT methodology:** Zero application of core CBT techniques, processes, or structure
- **No case conceptualization:** Failed to develop understanding of how anxiety, shame, and enuresis are functionally related
- **No guided discovery:** Relied entirely on advice-giving rather than Socratic questioning
- **No treatment strategy:** Offered disconnected suggestions without clinical rationale or logical progression
- **No collaboration:** Patient positioned as passive recipient rather than active partner
- **No homework:** Session ended without structured between-session tasks
- **Excessive self-disclosure:** Repeated personal anecdotes shifted focus from patient to therapist
- **Superficial understanding:** Missed clinical significance of symptoms and anxiety-enuresis connection

### Clinical Impact:

**This session does not constitute evidence-based CBT.** The therapist is providing supportive counseling with generic advice-giving. While supportive counseling has value, it is insufficient for a child with primary nocturnal enuresis and context-specific anxiety. Without targeted CBT intervention, Zack's symptoms are unlikely to improve and may worsen.

### Immediate Recommendations:

1. **Develop a coherent case conceptualization** explaining the anxiety-enuresis-shame connection
2. **Establish clear agenda and treatment strategy** with specific, measurable goals
3. **Replace advice-giving with guided discovery** using Socratic questioning
4. **Implement specific CBT techniques:** psychoeducation, anxiety management, cognitive restructuring, behavioral experiments, exposure
5. **Assign structured homework** with clear rationale and follow-up
6. **Eliminate excessive self-disclosure** and maintain professional boundaries
7. **Seek intensive supervision and training** in child CBT methodology before continuing treatment

**This therapist requires immediate, structured supervision and training in CBT competency.**
