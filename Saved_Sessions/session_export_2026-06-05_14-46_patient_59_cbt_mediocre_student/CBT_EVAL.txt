## DETAILED RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Immediate Priority Actions (Implement Before Next Session)

1. **Establish Mandatory CBT Structure**
   - Create written session template: (1) Agenda setting (5 min), (2) Homework review (10 min), (3) Main work (25 min), (4) Homework assignment (5 min)
   - Before each session, write 2-3 agenda items with time allocation
   - Review agenda with patient at session start
   - Summarize and assign homework at session end

2. **Implement Structured Homework System**
   - Create homework templates: Thought Record form, Behavioral Activation Log, Relapse Prevention Worksheet
   - Provide written instructions for all homework
   - Establish baseline measurements (anxiety 0-10, relapse urge 0-10, activity level)
   - Check understanding by asking patient to summarize homework
   - Mandatory homework review at start of every subsequent session

3. **Develop Coherent Case Formulation**
   - Write one-paragraph formulation: "Robert's anxiety appears multifactorial: (1) steroid-induced medication effects (prednisolone causing initial euphoria followed by negative mood), (2) transplant-related uncertainty and health preoccupation, (3) early alcohol remission with relapse vulnerability (10-year drinking history), (4) loss of identity/control (unable to work as delivery driver, dependent on medical system), (5) mild cognitive impairment (MoCA 26/30) and attention difficulties. Primary focus: relapse prevention and medication psychoeducation. Secondary focus: anxiety management through cognitive and behavioral strategies."
   - Share formulation with patient and refine based on feedback
   - Use formulation to guide all interventions

4. **Prioritize Relapse Prevention**
   - Dedicate at least one full session to relapse prevention
   - Develop written plan identifying: personal triggers, warning signs, coping strategies for each trigger, high-risk situations, support resources
   - Connect relapse prevention to anxiety management (e.g., "When anxiety spikes, use coping strategy instead of drinking")
   - Review relapse prevention plan regularly

5. **Address Steroid-Induced Anxiety**
   - Provide psychoeducation handout on steroid effects on mood and anxiety
   - Discuss with patient: "The anxiety and irritability you're experiencing are partly caused by the steroids you're taking. This is a medication effect, not something you're doing wrong."
   - Help patient prepare questions for prescribing physician about dose adjustment or medication change
   - Adjust CBT expectations accordingly (cognitive interventions alone may be insufficient while on steroids)

### Secondary Priority Actions (Implement Within 2-3 Sessions)

6. **Implement Systematic Cognitive Restructuring**
   - Move beyond thought replacement to structured thought records
   - For each catastrophic thought, complete full record: Situation → Automatic thought → Emotion/sensation → Evidence for → Evidence against → Alternative thought → Belief in alternative (0-100%)
   - Use Socratic questioning to help patient discover alternatives, not therapist suggestion
   - Track changes in belief over time

7. **Implement Systematic Behavioral Activation**
   - Establish baseline activity level
   - Create graded hierarchy of activities (easy to harder)
   - Schedule specific activities with concrete details (day, time, duration)
   - Track mood before/after each activity (0-10 scale)
   - Review results weekly and adjust based on what works

8. **Use Socratic Questioning Instead of Suggestion**
   - For every suggestion tempted to make, ask Socratic question instead
   - Practice questions: "What do you think...?" "What evidence do you have...?" "What would happen if...?" "How might someone else view this...?" "What's another way to think about this...?"
   - Record sessions and count suggestion vs. question ratio
   - Goal: 80% questions, 20% suggestions

9. **Establish Measurement and Progress Tracking**
   - Use simple weekly measurements: Anxiety (0-10), Irritability (0-10), Sleep hours, Relapse urge (0-10), Activity level (days active/week)
   - Graph progress weekly
   - Review with patient
   - Adjust strategy if no progress after 3-4 weeks

10. **Accommodate Cognitive Impairment**
    - Simplify interventions given MoCA 26/30 and attention difficulties
    - Provide written materials for all interventions
    - Check understanding frequently by asking patient to summarize
    - Use concrete, specific examples rather than abstract concepts
    - Repeat key points
    - Slower pacing with more frequent understanding checks

### Supervision and Training Recommendations

- **Immediate:** Supervisor should observe next 2-3 sessions and provide real-time feedback on agenda-setting, homework review, and Socratic questioning
- **Weekly:** Therapist should record sessions and review with supervisor, focusing on: (1) Agenda adherence, (2) Homework review quality, (3) Ratio of questions to suggestions, (4) Technique application quality
- **Training:** Therapist should complete CBT skills training focusing on: (1) Structured thought records, (2) Behavioral activation, (3) Socratic questioning, (4) Case formulation, (5) Homework design and review
- **Competency Assessment:** Reassess therapist competency after 4-6 sessions of supervised practice using CTRS. Target score: 40+/66 (Good range) before independent practice
