## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Quality Assessment:** Poor (0.8/6 average)

**Immediate Safety Concerns:**
- Suicidal ideation and self-injury mentioned in case formulation but never assessed during session
- No risk assessment conducted
- No safety plan developed
- No crisis protocol established
- Patient with active suicidal ideation left session without safety resources

**Critical Competency Gaps:**
- Complete absence of CBT framework (agenda, strategy, techniques, homework)
- Excessive self-disclosure and boundary violations
- Lack of guided discovery and Socratic questioning
- Failure to identify and target central cognitions and behaviors
- No trauma-informed approach despite PTSD diagnosis

**Recommended Actions:**
1. **Immediate:** Require therapist to conduct comprehensive safety assessment in next session and develop written safety plan
2. **Supervision:** Increase to weekly individual supervision with CBT-trained supervisor
3. **Training:** Enroll in evidence-based CBT training program (minimum 40 hours)
4. **Specialization:** Complete trauma-focused CBT training (TF-CBT or PE/CPT)
5. **Monitoring:** Record and review all sessions with this patient for next 8 weeks
6. **Competency Assessment:** Re-evaluate after 12 weeks of intensive supervision and training

**Prognosis Without Intervention:** Poor. Patient unlikely to benefit from current approach and at risk of harm.
