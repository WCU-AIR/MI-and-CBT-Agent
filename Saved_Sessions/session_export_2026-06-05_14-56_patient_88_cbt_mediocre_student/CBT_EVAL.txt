## SUPERVISOR RECOMMENDATIONS

**Priority Actions (Required Before Next Session with This Patient):**

1. ☑ **Implement structured agenda-setting** — Begin Session 3 by explicitly collaborating with patient to set clear priorities, allocate time, and reference previous session content.

2. ☑ **Review all previous homework systematically** — Ask specifically what was completed, what was learned, what was difficult, and what barriers arose.

3. ☑ **Replace direct suggestion with Socratic questioning** — Use guided discovery to help patient explore and reality-test paranoid beliefs independently.

4. ☑ **Address magical thinking explicitly** — Explore patient's beliefs about coins predicting the future and thoughts influencing events through guided discovery and gentle reality-testing.

5. ☑ **Develop written case formulation** — Create coherent treatment strategy specific to schizotypal and paranoid personality disorder features. Share with patient and refine collaboratively.

6. ☑ **Provide structured thought records** — Teach patient to use standardized form with clear instructions and purpose before assigning as homework.

7. ☑ **Frame behavioral activation as behavioral experiment** — Specify hypothesis, observation plan, and what patient will learn from the experiment.

8. ☑ **Increase session structure and predictability** — Establish consistent session format and explain rationale for interventions transparently to reduce paranoid ideation.

**Supervision Requirements:**

- Weekly individual supervision with CBT specialist
- Review of recorded sessions (with patient consent)
- Role-play practice of agenda-setting, guided discovery, and homework review
- Competency assessment before continuing independent treatment
- Consider co-therapy or observation of expert CBT practitioner with similar patient population
