## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **URGENT (Before Next Session with This Patient)**

1. **Establish CBT Structure:** Every session must include agenda-setting, guided discovery, specific interventions, feedback-seeking, and homework.
2. **Conduct Comprehensive Assessment:** Systematically evaluate TBI history, cognitive changes, substance use patterns, behavioral disturbance, family dynamics, and safety.
3. **Eliminate Ineffective Advice-Giving:** Replace generic suggestions with evidence-based CBT techniques. When patient rejects approach, adjust rather than repeat.
4. **Maintain Professional Boundaries:** Reduce self-disclosure. Establish clinical authority through expertise and evidence-based treatment.

### **HIGH PRIORITY (Within 2 Weeks)**

5. **Master Guided Discovery:** Replace advice-giving with Socratic questioning. Practice asking 10 questions without offering advice.
6. **Develop Competence in Neurocognitive Disorders:** Obtain training in TBI assessment and treatment. Understand how frontal lobe injury affects insight, impulse control, and behavioral regulation.
7. **Learn Substance Use Assessment:** Study AUDIT, DAST, timeline followback. Develop systematic substance use evaluation protocol.
8. **Apply Specific CBT Techniques:** Learn and practice psychoeducation, motivational interviewing, cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, safety planning.

### **ONGOING**

9. **Obtain Intensive Supervision:** Present all complex cases in supervision. Record sessions for review. Seek consultation with specialists (neuropsychology, addiction medicine).
10. **Respond to Patient Feedback:** When patient rejects intervention, recognize as critical feedback and adjust approach.
11. **Assign Tailored Homework:** End every session with specific, explained, committed-to homework assignments that extend learning and build skills.
