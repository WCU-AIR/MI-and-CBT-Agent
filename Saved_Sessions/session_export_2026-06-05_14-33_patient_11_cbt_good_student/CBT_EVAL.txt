## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Strengths Demonstrated:**
- Strong collaborative agenda-setting with explicit options and confirmation
- Excellent empathic understanding and accurate reflection of patient's experience
- Skillful Socratic questioning enabling patient self-discovery
- Appropriate cognitive restructuring with evidence examination
- Well-designed behavioral experiments directly testing catastrophic predictions
- Warm, validating interpersonal style creating psychological safety
- Clear connection between cognitive and behavioral work
- Respect for patient autonomy in homework planning
- Appropriate session pacing without rushing

**Critical Areas for Development:**
1. **Comprehensive Assessment:** Systematically assess and monitor all presenting symptoms (school avoidance, somatic symptoms, suicidal ideation) using structured questions and standardized measures.
2. **Autism-Informed CBT:** Explicitly address patient's autism diagnosis, normalize neurodevelopmental differences, and develop autism-specific treatment strategies.
3. **Anxiety Management:** Teach and practice evidence-based anxiety management techniques (diaphragmatic breathing, progressive muscle relaxation, grounding, cognitive coping statements).
4. **Homework Specification:** Provide written assignments with detailed instructions, tracking forms, obstacle problem-solving, and attention to neurodevelopmental barriers.
5. **Behavioral Rehearsal:** Conduct in-session role-play and practice to build confidence and identify specific challenges before assigning homework.
6. **Broader Case Conceptualization:** Expand treatment strategy beyond peer interaction to address school avoidance, somatic symptoms, and environmental factors.

**Recommended Next Steps:**
- Schedule supervision to review comprehensive assessment protocols
- Discuss autism-informed CBT approaches and resources
- Practice anxiety management techniques for teaching to patients
- Develop homework assignment templates with tracking forms
- Role-play behavioral rehearsal scenarios
