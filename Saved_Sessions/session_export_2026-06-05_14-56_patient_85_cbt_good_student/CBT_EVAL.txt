## CLINICAL NOTES FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Session Strengths
- Collaborative, empathic therapeutic relationship established
- Clear introduction of cognitive model appropriate to patient's cognitive level
- Skillful evidence examination and alternative thought generation
- Specific, measurable homework with confidence assessment
- Appropriate pacing and logical session flow

### Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Attention
1. **Psychotic Symptom Assessment (HIGHEST PRIORITY):** Next session must include direct assessment of auditory hallucinations, paranoid thoughts, and command hallucinations. Bus incident may have been driven by psychotic symptoms, not just cognitive distortions.

2. **Medication and Medical Factors:** Assess clozapine adherence, side effects (weight gain, sedation), and impact on motivation and functioning. Coordinate with psychiatrist.

3. **Concrete Thinking Intervention:** Implement explicit teaching of abstract problem-solving and social rules. Use structured worksheets and role-plays.

4. **Crisis Planning:** Develop written relapse prevention plan including warning signs, coping strategies, and when to seek emergency help.

5. **Second Agenda Item:** Address managing nervousness around people/outside with exposure-based or behavioral activation interventions.

### Recommended Training Focus
- Specialized schizophrenia treatment (psychotic symptoms, negative symptoms, cognitive remediation)
- Advanced CBT techniques (behavioral experiments, structured worksheets, exposure therapy)
- Comprehensive psychiatric assessment
- Crisis planning and safety planning
- Multidisciplinary care coordination
