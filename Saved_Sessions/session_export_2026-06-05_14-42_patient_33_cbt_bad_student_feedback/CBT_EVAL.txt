## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **HIGHEST PRIORITY (Address Before Next Session)**

1. **Develop Coherent CBT Conceptualization:** Create written formulation of how dependent personality disorder, catastrophic thinking, avoidant coping, and benzodiazepine dependence interact. Share with patient. Use to guide all interventions.

2. **Establish Structured Agenda:** Collaboratively set agenda at start of each session. Explicitly prioritize among multiple issues. Allocate time. Explain rationale. Refer back to agenda during session.

3. **Address Benzodiazepine Dependence:** Conduct thorough assessment. Coordinate with prescribing physician for medical management (slow taper, monitoring). Develop alternative coping strategies for anxiety. Assign medication monitoring homework.

### **HIGH PRIORITY (Implement in Next 2-3 Sessions)**

4. **Replace Reassurance with Guided Discovery:** Use Socratic questions instead of statements. Help patient reach own conclusions rather than providing answers.

5. **Implement Cognitive Restructuring:** Teach thought record format. Identify and challenge catastrophic thoughts systematically.

6. **Design Behavioral Experiments:** Collaboratively design experiments to test beliefs. Predict outcomes, conduct experiment, review results.

7. **Reduce Reassurance-Seeking:** Recognize when patient seeks reassurance and redirect to own judgment. Gradually increase responsibility for decisions.

### **MEDIUM PRIORITY (Implement Over Next Month)**

8. **Implement Exposure for Avoidant Behaviors:** Create graded exposure hierarchy. Start with low-anxiety situations. Gradually progress.

9. **Eliminate Excessive Self-Disclosure:** Reduce personal anecdotes by 80%. Maintain professional boundaries.

10. **Assign Specific Homework:** Provide written instructions with clear rationale and review plan.

11. **Elicit Feedback Throughout Session:** Ask "Does this make sense?" "How are you feeling?" "What are your thoughts?" at least 3 times per session.

12. **Provide Psychoeducation:** Help patient understand dependent personality patterns, how they develop, and how they change.
