However, the session falls short of evidence-based ASD treatment due to critical gaps: (1) Incomplete clinical assessment—did not thoroughly assess all ASD symptoms (sleep, nightmares, intrusive images, broader avoidance pattern, anger, shame, hyperarousal); (2) Narrow symptom focus—addressed one catastrophic thought and one avoidance behavior rather than broader threat appraisal and avoidance pattern; (3) Unclear treatment strategy—did not articulate overall treatment conceptualization or rationale; (4) Missing evidence-based ASD interventions—did not introduce exposure therapy, trauma-focused techniques, or arousal management; (5) Limited technique application—applied cognitive restructuring competently but did not use formal thought records, exposure hierarchies, trauma-focused techniques, or sleep interventions; (6) Incomplete homework structure—lacked written format, rationale, data collection, and behavioral components. **Likelihood of Therapeutic Change:** This session does not fully qualify as evidence-based ASD treatment.
