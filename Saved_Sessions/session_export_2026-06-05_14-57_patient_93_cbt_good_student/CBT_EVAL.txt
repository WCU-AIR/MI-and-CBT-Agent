## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:

1. **Cannabis Use Disorder Integration (HIGHEST PRIORITY)**
   - Explicitly acknowledge cannabis as maintaining factor in avoidance cycle
   - Conduct functional analysis of use patterns
   - Develop gradual reduction plan with behavioral alternatives
   - Link cannabis reduction to treatment goals

2. **Homework Review Failure (CRITICAL)**
   - Session 2 opened with new agenda without reviewing Session 1 homework
   - Implement homework review as first item in every session
   - Ask: "Did you try it? What happened? What did you notice? What got in the way?"
   - Use data to collaboratively adjust treatment plan

3. **Structured Monitoring Tools (CRITICAL)**
   - Provide thought records or activity logs for all homework assignments
   - Transform vague instructions ("notice how it feels") into data-collection exercises
   - Include: date/time, duration, pain before/after, thoughts, observations
   - Review data in session to generate disconfirming evidence for feared outcomes

4. **Systematic Obstacle Planning (HIGH PRIORITY)**
   - For each identified barrier, develop specific, concrete coping strategies
   - Example: "If pain spikes, you will: [specific steps]. You will tell yourself: [coping thought]."
   - Write down strategies for patient reference
   - Review in next session: "Did obstacles come up? How did you handle them?"

### Secondary Recommendations:

5. **Cognitive Restructuring** — Systematically challenge catastrophic thoughts and core beliefs about disability
6. **Comprehensive Behavioral Activation** — Extend beyond pain-related movement to address social withdrawal, self-care, sleep
7. **Exposure Hierarchy** — Develop systematic progression of avoided activities ranked by difficulty
8. **Explicit Expectation-Setting** — Before each homework, discuss what patient should expect and how to interpret results
9. **Safety Planning** — Develop explicit plans for pain exacerbation; provide clear contact guidelines
10. **Hopelessness Intervention** — Directly address "feels pointless to try" cognition through behavioral activation and evidence-gathering

### Training Focus:
Recommend focused training on: (1) tri-morbidity case formulation, (2) substance use integration in CBT, (3) structured homework monitoring, (4) systematic obstacle planning, (5) consistent homework review protocols. Therapist has solid foundational skills and warm interpersonal style; targeted training on these specific areas will substantially improve treatment effectiveness.
