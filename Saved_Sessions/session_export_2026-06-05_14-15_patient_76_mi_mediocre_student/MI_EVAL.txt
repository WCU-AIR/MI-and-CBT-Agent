## Summary of Clinical Findings

### Critical Deficiencies

This session is **fundamentally inconsistent with Motivational Interviewing principles**. The psychiatrist adopts an expert-driven, directive stance that violates core MI values of collaboration, partnership, and autonomy support. Rather than evoking Linda's intrinsic motivation, the psychiatrist prescribes solutions. Rather than validating concerns, the psychiatrist moves quickly to problem-solving. Rather than supporting autonomy, the psychiatrist uses directive language that frames change as something the psychiatrist is directing.

### Impact on Therapeutic Outcome

**This session is unlikely to result in sustained behavior change.** Linda demonstrates compliance ("I'll try," "I guess," "I'm willing to try") rather than commitment. Her low confidence (4-5/10), tentative language, and unaddressed core concerns (medication safety fears) suggest she will likely abandon prescribed tasks when psychiatrist is no longer present. Research demonstrates compliance-based change is less sustainable than commitment-based change rooted in intrinsic motivation.

### Key Recommendations

1. **Shift to collaborative stance** (HIGHEST PRIORITY): Ask rather than tell; invite Linda's ideas before offering solutions
2. **Develop genuine empathy**: Move beyond factual reflections to emotional reflections capturing feeling and meaning
3. **Evoke rather than impose**: Use open-ended questions to draw out Linda's own motivations and values
4. **Amplify change talk**: Recognize and reinforce Linda's spontaneous statements about desire and ability to change
5. **Use double-sided reflections**: Validate both sides of Linda's ambivalence to soften sustain talk
6. **Support autonomy**: Replace directive language with autonomy-supportive language emphasizing Linda's choice
7. **Clarify and focus**: Identify primary change goal early and maintain strategic focus throughout
8. **Explore before prescribing**: Assess confidence and barriers before recommending interventions
9. **Increase open-ended questions**: Replace closed-ended and factual questions with exploratory questions
10. **Develop collaborative action plan**: Co-construct plan reflecting Linda's ideas, preferences, and what she thinks will work
