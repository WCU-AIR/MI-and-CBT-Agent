## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE ACTION

1. **Establish CBT Case Conceptualization:** Develop written formulation identifying core beliefs, behavioral patterns, emotional consequences, and relationship impact specific to OCPD.

2. **Implement Collaborative Agenda-Setting:** Begin each session with explicit goal-setting: "What feels most important to focus on today?"

3. **Replace Advice with Socratic Questioning:** Use guided discovery to help patient reach insights independently about catastrophic beliefs and perfectionism.

4. **Apply Cognitive Restructuring:** Use thought records to examine evidence for catastrophic thoughts ("If I don't control it, things will be lost").

5. **Design Behavioral Experiments:** Test patient's predictions in controlled way (e.g., "Let Peter pack one box; observe what actually happens").

6. **Implement Exposure/Response Prevention:** Gradually reduce compulsive organizing and increase tolerance for imperfection using hierarchy.

7. **Eliminate Personal Anecdotes:** Maintain professional boundaries; redirect patient questions back to patient's experience.

8. **Systematically Review Homework:** At start of each session, explicitly review what was assigned, whether attempted, what barriers arose, and adjust accordingly.

9. **Integrate Peter into Treatment:** Acknowledge couples context; involve Peter in goal-setting and consider joint sessions for communication skills.

10. **Obtain CBT Supervision:** Seek consultation from CBT-trained clinician on personality disorder treatment before continuing with this patient.
