## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Summary:**

This session represents **competent but incomplete CBT practice**. The therapist excels in alliance-building, guided discovery, and collaboration—creating an excellent therapeutic relationship and facilitating patient agency. However, the session demonstrates significant gaps in comprehensive assessment, multi-component treatment strategy development, and systematic homework protocols.

**Immediate Strengths to Reinforce:**
- Exemplary interpersonal effectiveness and rapport-building
- Masterful use of Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Strong collaborative stance positioning patient as active partner
- Appropriate pacing and focus maintenance

**Critical Areas for Development:**
- Comprehensive functional analysis addressing multiple maintaining factors (compulsive behaviors, social anxiety, perfectionism, family dynamics)
- Multi-component treatment strategy with explicit rationale and clear progression
- Formal homework protocols with documentation, tracking, and review plans
- Explicit psychoeducation on CBT model and treatment rationale
- Behavioral experiment framing as hypothesis-testing
- Concrete barrier management and safety planning

**Likelihood of Therapeutic Success:**
**Moderate.** Initial progress likely (weeks 1-4) with behavioral experiment and anxiety reduction, but plateau or regression probable without comprehensive intervention addressing all maintaining factors.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
1. Case conceptualization and functional analysis for complex disorders
2. Multi-component treatment planning
3. Structured homework and tracking protocols
4. Treatment planning and goal-setting
5. Observation of experienced ARFID therapists
