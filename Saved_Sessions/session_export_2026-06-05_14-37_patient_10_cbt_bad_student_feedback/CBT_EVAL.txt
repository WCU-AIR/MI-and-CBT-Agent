## CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Intervention:

1. **Absence of CBT-informed case formulation** – Therapist must develop explicit understanding of how ADHD, SLD, depression, and panic interact to maintain Carlos's difficulties before next session.

2. **Lack of cognitive targeting** – Therapist must learn to identify and systematically address automatic thoughts driving anxiety and avoidance through cognitive restructuring.

3. **No homework assignment** – Therapist must understand that homework is the mechanism for between-session change and must assign specific, measurable, rationale-based tasks.

4. **Reliance on advice-giving** – Therapist must shift from directive advice to Socratic questioning to build patient's problem-solving skills and increase ownership of solutions.

5. **Session structure collapse** – Therapist must establish explicit agenda with time allocation and maintain focus throughout session.

### Recommended Supervision Plan:

- **Immediate:** Review CBT fundamentals (case formulation, cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, guided discovery).
- **Week 1-2:** Therapist observes experienced CBT clinician conducting session with similar patient presentation.
- **Week 2-3:** Role-play cognitive restructuring and Socratic questioning with supervisor; record and review.
- **Week 3-4:** Therapist conducts next session with Carlos; supervisor reviews recording and provides detailed feedback.
- **Ongoing:** Weekly supervision with focus on CBT technique development and case formulation refinement.

### Prognosis for Carlos Without Intervention Change:

**Poor.** If subsequent sessions follow this pattern, Carlos is unlikely to experience significant symptom improvement. He may continue to struggle with concentration, sleep, and anxiety. Academic performance may not improve. He may become discouraged and consider dropping out of college, as he mentioned to his family. Immediate intervention is necessary to redirect treatment toward evidence-based CBT practice.
