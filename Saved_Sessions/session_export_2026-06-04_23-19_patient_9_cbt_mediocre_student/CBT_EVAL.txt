## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Rating: 1.3/6 (Poor)**

**Key Findings:**

- **Diagnostic Misalignment:** Therapist treated Antisocial Personality Disorder as anxiety/adjustment disorder. No recognition of core features: deceit, aggression, lack of remorse, irresponsibility, external attribution.

- **No Treatment Strategy:** Interventions were reactive responses to patient complaints rather than strategic movement toward defined goals. No case conceptualization provided.

- **Resistance Not Addressed:** Patient explicitly rejected cognitive reframing and coping strategies. Therapist did not explore this clinically significant resistance or adjust approach.

- **Behavioral Focus Absent:** Patient's actual problems (falsified credentials, aggression, irresponsibility, blame-shifting) were not addressed. Session focused on emotions and thoughts rather than behavior.

- **No Accountability Mechanism:** No homework, no between-session tasks, no mechanism for behavioral change or accountability.

**Immediate Actions Required:**

1. Supervisor review and case consultation
2. Therapist training on CBT for personality disorders
3. Development of revised treatment plan with behavioral accountability focus
4. Establishment of clear therapeutic frame and expectations
5. Assignment of specific, relevant homework with clear rationale

**Recommendation:** This therapist requires immediate supervision and additional training before continuing with this case.
