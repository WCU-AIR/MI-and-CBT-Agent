## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths to Reinforce:**
- Exemplary guided discovery and Socratic questioning
- Excellent empathic understanding and therapeutic relationship
- Strong collaborative approach with patient as active partner
- Clear, systematic cognitive-behavioral analysis
- Appropriate use of cognitive restructuring techniques

**Critical Areas for Development:**
1. **Behavioral Interventions:** Implement systematic behavioral activation for depression; address social withdrawal and anhedonia as primary treatment targets
2. **Sleep Management:** Develop sleep-focused interventions including stimulus control, sleep restriction, worry postponement, and relaxation techniques
3. **Neurocognitive Adaptation:** Provide written materials, explicit consolidation, repetition, and discussion of how cognitive impairment relates to presenting problems
4. **Treatment Planning:** Articulate clear multi-session strategy with specific goals, timeline, and progress measurement
5. **Comprehensive Assessment:** Address medical complexity, explore core beliefs about identity and purpose, assess social support and isolation

**Recommended Next Steps:**
- Review behavioral activation literature and practice with supervisor
- Develop sleep intervention protocols for older adults
- Create templates for written homework instructions
- Practice developing multi-session treatment plans
- Explore core beliefs and identity issues in future sessions
