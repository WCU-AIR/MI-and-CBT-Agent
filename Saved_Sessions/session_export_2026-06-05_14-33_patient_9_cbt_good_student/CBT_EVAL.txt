## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Therapist Strengths:**
- Demonstrated genuine warmth, empathy, and professional demeanor
- Followed logical session structure (incident → thoughts → feelings → behaviors → restructuring → planning)
- Maintained appropriate focus without tangential discussion
- Used open-ended questions and Socratic method to some degree
- Tracked mood changes and noted meaningful shift in anger intensity (8→5)
- Employed collaborative language and invited patient input

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- **Diagnostic formulation:** Failed to recognize or address pervasive pattern of externalization, poor insight, lack of accountability, and lack of remorse characteristic of APD
- **Clinical strategy:** Inadequate for APD; assumes standard CBT is appropriate without assessment of patient's suitability or motivation
- **Cognitive work:** Superficial application of cognitive restructuring; patient's responses suggest compliance without genuine belief change
- **Focus:** Addressed surface-level automatic thought rather than core beliefs and behavioral patterns driving occupational and relational failures
- **Homework:** Lacks specificity, clear success criteria, obstacle planning, and monitoring strategy
- **Motivation assessment:** Did not explore whether patient is genuinely motivated to change or seeking validation and accommodations

**Immediate Actions Required:**
1. Conduct comprehensive assessment of patient motivation and treatment suitability before continuing
2. Establish explicit boundaries around accountability and externalization of blame
3. Develop treatment strategy addressing core clinical issues of APD
4. Assess appropriateness of standard CBT versus alternative approaches
5. Seek clinical supervision and training in personality disorder treatment

**Overall Assessment:** Session demonstrates basic therapeutic competence but significant deficiencies in CBT-specific skills and clinical judgment for this complex patient. Unlikely to produce meaningful, sustained therapeutic change without substantial modifications to approach.
