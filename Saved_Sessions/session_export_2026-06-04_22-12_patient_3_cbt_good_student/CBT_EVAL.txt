## CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Strengths to Reinforce:**
- Excellent guided discovery and Socratic questioning
- Strong empathic accuracy and understanding
- Skillful cognitive restructuring execution
- Consistent collaboration and feedback-seeking
- Appropriate pacing and session flow
- Specific, detailed homework with clear rationale

**Critical Areas Requiring Immediate Supervision:**
1. **Suicide Risk Assessment and Safety Planning** – Therapist must conduct comprehensive risk assessment at every high-risk patient session and develop written safety plans with explicit warning signs, coping strategies, crisis contacts, and means restriction.

2. **Case Conceptualization for High-Risk Patients** – Therapist requires training in understanding how to integrate standard CBT with suicide-specific interventions. Must learn to identify the behavioral chain leading to suicide attempts and develop multi-level interventions.

3. **Distress Tolerance and Emotion Regulation Skills** – Therapist should observe experienced clinicians teaching these skills and practice teaching them in supervision before applying with high-risk patients.

4. **Connecting Cognitive Work to Suicide Prevention** – Therapist must learn to explicitly frame all cognitive and behavioral interventions as suicide prevention tools and help patients understand the connection.

5. **Family Involvement and Means Restriction** – Therapist should schedule family sessions for high-risk patients and establish clear protocols for means restriction and monitoring.

**Supervision Plan:**
- Review this case in detail with focus on what was missed (safety assessment, risk monitoring, skills-building)
- Have therapist observe experienced clinician conducting safety assessment and planning with high-risk patient
- Role-play safety assessment and planning with supervisor
- Review therapist's next session with this patient to ensure safety components are addressed
- Assign reading on Suicidal Behavior Disorder, safety planning, and distress tolerance skills
- Increase supervision frequency to weekly for cases involving recent suicide attempts
