## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Summary:**

This therapist demonstrates genuine warmth and empathy but operates at a supportive counseling level rather than an evidence-based CBT level. The session reveals significant gaps in addiction-specific CBT knowledge and technique implementation.

**Strengths to Build Upon:**
- Warm, non-judgmental therapeutic presence
- Patient felt safe disclosing struggles
- Genuine interest in patient wellbeing
- No boundary violations or unethical behavior

**Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Attention:**
- No agenda-setting or collaborative goal-setting
- No specific CBT techniques applied
- No medical coordination for acute withdrawal (safety concern)
- Excessive therapist self-disclosure
- Minimal collaboration and patient agency
- Inadequate homework structure and review

**Recommended Actions:**
1. **Immediate:** Refer patient for medical evaluation and pharmacological management of acute withdrawal.
2. **Short-term:** Enroll therapist in structured CBT for addiction training program (NIDA, SAMHSA-approved).
3. **Ongoing:** Provide weekly supervision focused on: agenda-setting, CBT technique application, cognitive restructuring, behavioral interventions, and addiction-specific treatment planning.
4. **Monitoring:** Record and review sessions regularly. Provide specific feedback on technique implementation and clinical decision-making.

**Prognosis for Patient:** Without significant changes in treatment approach, likelihood of sustained therapeutic change is low. Patient requires intensive, structured, evidence-based addiction treatment with medical coordination.
