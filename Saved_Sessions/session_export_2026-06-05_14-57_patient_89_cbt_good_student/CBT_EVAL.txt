## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT

**Immediate Priorities:**

1. **Case Conceptualization:** Develop explicit written case formulation articulating precipitating factors, maintaining factors, treatment targets, and multi-session plan before next session.

2. **Psychoeducation:** Integrate 10-15 minute psychoeducational component in next session covering separation anxiety, panic cycle, reassurance-seeking maintenance, and CBT model.

3. **Scope Expansion:** Conduct comprehensive behavioral assessment to identify all avoidance behaviors, somatic symptoms, and parental accommodation patterns. Develop interventions targeting each.

4. **Homework Structure:** Create written homework sheet with thought record format, frequency specifications, obstacle problem-solving, and explicit tracking instructions.

5. **Exposure Planning:** Develop systematic exposure hierarchy for separation situations ranked by anxiety level to guide treatment progression.

**Strengths to Maintain:**

- Excellent empathic understanding and accurate reflection of patient experience
- Strong use of Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Collaborative, non-judgmental therapeutic stance
- Logical session structure and appropriate pacing
- Specific, testable behavioral experiment design

**Areas for Continued Development:**

- Comprehensive case conceptualization and multi-session strategic planning
- Integration of psychoeducation with technique application
- Systematic assessment and intervention for all maintaining factors
- Structured homework with written tracking and monitoring
- Explicit rationale communication for all interventions
