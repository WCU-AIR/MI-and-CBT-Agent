## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Strengths Identified:**
- Maintained friendly, welcoming tone throughout both sessions
- Demonstrated basic listening skills and acknowledged patient statements
- Patient remained cooperative and engaged; no hostile environment created
- Maintained appropriate professional boundaries
- Sessions began and ended appropriately with basic structure

**Critical Areas Requiring Immediate Intervention:**
1. **CBT Framework Development** – Therapist must learn to establish collaborative agendas, develop case conceptualizations, and create coherent treatment strategies
2. **Technique Training** – Formal instruction needed in thought records, behavioral activation, worry time, behavioral experiments, and other core CBT techniques
3. **Socratic Questioning** – Replace advice-giving with guided discovery through open-ended questioning
4. **Homework Structure** – Learn to assign specific, rationale-driven, trackable between-session assignments with systematic follow-up
5. **Self-Disclosure Management** – Significantly reduce personal anecdotes; redirect focus to patient's experience
6. **Cultural Competence Integration** – Acknowledge and address patient's experience as first-generation student and experiences with racial discrimination

**Recommended Action Plan:**
- Enroll therapist in structured CBT certification program
- Arrange weekly individual supervision with CBT-trained supervisor
- Implement session recording and review protocol
- Assign CBT manual reading (e.g., Cognitive Therapy of Depression, Anxiety and Phobia Workbook)
- Consider peer consultation group for practice and feedback
- Reassess competency after 8-12 weeks of intensive training

**Patient Care Recommendation:**
Pending therapist training completion, consider referral to CBT-trained clinician to ensure patient receives evidence-based treatment for adjustment disorder.
