## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL CONFERENCE

**Session Quality Assessment:** This session represents a fundamental failure to deliver evidence-based CBT and contains elements likely to worsen the patient's condition.

**Most Critical Issues for Immediate Intervention:**

1. **Reinforcement of reassurance-seeking (HARMFUL):** Patient explicitly stated, "I find myself asking the nurses the same questions over and over, just wanting to hear reassurance." Therapist responded by providing more reassurance. This directly maintains the anxiety cycle and must be stopped immediately.

2. **Absence of CBT structure:** No agenda, no guided discovery, no thought records, no behavioral activation, no homework. This is not CBT.

3. **Inappropriate self-disclosure:** Therapist's personal stories consumed session time and violated professional boundaries.

4. **False reassurance:** Disconnected from patient's medical reality and damages therapeutic credibility.

**Required Actions Before Next Patient Session:**

- [ ] Supervisor observation and feedback on next session
- [ ] Training on reassurance-seeking cycles and exposure-based intervention
- [ ] Training on CBT agenda-setting and session structure
- [ ] Training on guided discovery and Socratic questioning
- [ ] Training on behavioral activation for depression/anhedonia
- [ ] Training on cognitive restructuring for catastrophic thoughts
- [ ] Review of professional boundaries and appropriate self-disclosure
- [ ] Development of treatment conceptualization for this patient
- [ ] Role-play practice on key interventions

**Patient Safety Consideration:** While not acutely dangerous, this treatment approach is unlikely to help and may worsen anxiety through reinforcement of reassurance-seeking. Consider whether patient would benefit from reassignment to more experienced CBT therapist or intensive supervision model.
