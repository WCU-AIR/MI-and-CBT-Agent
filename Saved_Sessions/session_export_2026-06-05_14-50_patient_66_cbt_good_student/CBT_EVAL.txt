## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Strengths Demonstrated:**
- Collaborative agenda-setting with clear patient buy-in
- Accurate case formulation using cognitive-behavioral model
- Appropriate use of Socratic questioning in evidence examination
- Warm, validating interpersonal presence
- Relevant cognitive target selection
- Introduction of behavioral experiment
- End-of-session feedback solicitation

**Areas Requiring Development:**
- Deeper conceptualization of OCPD-specific features and core beliefs
- Shift from cognitive-heavy to behavioral-focused approach
- Integration of couple-level interventions and relationship dynamics
- Development of comprehensive, progressive behavioral homework plans
- Explicit rationale for all interventions
- Systematic homework review and treatment plan adjustment

**Recommended Next Steps:**
1. Provide specialized training in OCPD treatment protocols
2. Supervise next session with focus on behavioral interventions and core belief work
3. Review evidence-based literature on OCPD treatment
4. Practice designing exposure hierarchies and progressive behavioral experiments
5. Develop competence in couples therapy interventions
