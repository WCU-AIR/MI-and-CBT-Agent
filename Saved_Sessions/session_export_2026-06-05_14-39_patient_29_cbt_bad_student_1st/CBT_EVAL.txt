## SUPERVISOR SUMMARY & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Critical Findings

**Competency Level:** Below minimum standard for independent CBT practice with complex cases (conduct disorder, ADHD, adolescent).

**Primary Deficiencies:**
- Complete absence of agenda-setting and session structure
- No use of Socratic questioning or guided discovery
- Zero application of CBT techniques
- No coherent treatment strategy or case conceptualization
- No homework assignment or between-session accountability
- Excessive therapist self-disclosure undermining patient focus
- Poor responsiveness to patient feedback and rejection of interventions

**Therapeutic Alliance Impact:**
Kyle entered the session skeptical and left increasingly disengaged. Therapist's failure to listen, collaborate, or apply evidence-based techniques likely reinforced Kyle's belief that "nobody really knows what it's like to be me" and that treatment is ineffective.

### Immediate Supervisor Actions

1. **Do not allow independent continuation** of treatment with this patient until competency is demonstrated.
2. **Assign intensive CBT training** (e.g., CBT certification program, structured workshops, supervised practice).
3. **Implement weekly supervision** with a CBT expert experienced in adolescent conduct disorder.
4. **Require case formulation** before next session with Kyle.
5. **Mandate session recordings** for all future sessions with this patient for supervision review.
6. **Establish competency benchmarks** (e.g., demonstrated Socratic questioning, agenda-setting, homework assignment) before resuming independent practice.
