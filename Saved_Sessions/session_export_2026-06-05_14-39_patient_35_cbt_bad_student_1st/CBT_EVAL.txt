## CLINICAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT

**Likelihood of Producing Therapeutic Change:** LOW

This session is unlikely to produce meaningful therapeutic change. The patient leaves without:
- Clear treatment plan or priorities
- Concrete homework or between-session structure
- Understanding of how suggestions address specific problems
- Systematic intervention targeting core mechanisms of depression

Generic advice (think positive, breathe, get outside) may provide temporary comfort but will not address: behavioral avoidance, loss-focused rumination, hopelessness about future, or sleep disturbance.

**Potential Harm:** The therapist's approach may inadvertently harm the patient by:
- Minimizing legitimacy of concerns about progressive illness
- Suggesting positive thinking alone can address medical condition
- Failing to address REM sleep behavior disorder (safety risk)
- Leaving patient without coherent plan, potentially increasing hopelessness

## COMPETENCY DETERMINATION

**Current CBT Competency Level:** NOT COMPETENT

The therapist demonstrates adequate interpersonal warmth but lacks fundamental CBT competence. This is supportive counseling with CBT-like elements, not evidence-based CBT.

**Immediate Actions Required:**
1. ☐ Suspend independent clinical work pending training
2. ☐ Enroll in structured CBT training program
3. ☐ Establish weekly supervision with experienced CBT supervisor
4. ☐ Record and review all sessions with supervisor feedback
5. ☐ Complete CBT manual study and protocol training
