## Summary Assessment

### Strengths
- ✓ Consistent empathic accuracy with strong reflective listening
- ✓ Solid collaborative stance with genuine invitation of client input
- ✓ Effective handling of ambivalence without confrontation
- ✓ Appropriate directional focus with progressive movement toward action
- ✓ Respectful autonomy support throughout
- ✓ Skillful softening of sustain talk through reframing and validation
- ✓ Professional, warm, nonjudgmental tone

### Primary Areas for Improvement
- **Insufficient strategic cultivation of change talk:** Inconsistent amplification and reinforcement of Laura's motivational statements; missed follow-up questions to deepen change talk
- **Limited emotional depth:** Surface-level exploration of feeling states and what change means to Laura on a personal level
- **Missed evocation opportunities:** Could ask more open-ended questions inviting client problem-solving before offering reflections
- **Insufficient explicit affirmation:** While validating insights, could more actively affirm character strengths as resources for change
- **Limited exploration of intrinsic motivation:** Could more explicitly explore why Laura's values matter and what makes change meaningful beyond practical strategies

### Recommendation
This session demonstrates **adequate MI competence with solid foundational skills**. To advance to excellent MI practice, focus on: (1) consistent strategic amplification of change talk through follow-up questions, (2) deeper exploration of emotional experience and intrinsic motivation, (3) more frequent invitation of client problem-solving, and (4) explicit connection between action steps and core values. These refinements would significantly strengthen Laura's intrinsic motivation and likelihood of sustained behavior change.
