## SUMMARY OF CRITICAL FINDINGS

### Strengths (Limited)
- Therapist displayed some warmth and genuine concern
- Therapist did not display hostility or overt harm
- Therapist asked some open-ended questions to initiate conversation
- Therapist offered some validation of patient's statements

### Critical Deficiencies
- **Complete absence of safety assessment** for high-risk patient with chronic suicidal ideation and prior overdose hospitalizations
- **No agenda-setting or treatment planning** – session lacked structure and direction
- **No CBT techniques applied** – session consisted of generic advice and reassurance only
- **Excessive therapist self-disclosure** – violated professional boundaries
- **Failure to identify core problems** – ignored suicidal ideation, identity disturbance, affective instability, impulsivity
- **No collaboration** – patient positioned as passive recipient of advice
- **No homework** – no structured between-session tasks
- **Poor pacing** – session drifted; critical clinical content not addressed
- **Minimal feedback-seeking** – did not check understanding or adjust approach

### Clinical Risk
This session is unlikely to produce meaningful therapeutic change and may harm the patient by reinforcing beliefs that "no one understands me" and "treatment doesn't work." For a patient with BPD and chronic suicidal ideation who has failed multiple prior treatments, generic supportive counseling is clinically insufficient.
