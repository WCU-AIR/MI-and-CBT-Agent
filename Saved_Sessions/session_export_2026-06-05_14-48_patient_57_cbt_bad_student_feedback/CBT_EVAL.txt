## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality:** Poor (1.2/6)

**Primary Concerns:**
- Fundamental misdiagnosis: Depression-focused treatment for likely neurocognitive disorder
- Absence of CBT structure and framework
- Inadequate diagnostic and functional assessment
- Excessive therapist self-disclosure
- Advice-giving without collaboration or rationale
- No coherent treatment strategy
- Inadequate homework with no prior review

**Strengths:**
- Consistent warmth and non-judgment
- Basic empathic reflection
- Willingness to listen
- Genuine interest in patient

**Recommendations for Supervision:**
1. Review case formulation and diagnostic assessment with therapist
2. Clarify likely diagnosis (major neurocognitive disorder vs. depression)
3. Develop appropriate, evidence-based treatment plan
4. Provide direct feedback on CBT competencies
5. Assign additional training in geriatric psychiatry and neurocognitive disorders
6. Require intensive supervision before next patient contact
7. Consider consultation with neuropsychologist or geriatric psychiatrist

**Does this qualify as evidence-based CBT?** No.
