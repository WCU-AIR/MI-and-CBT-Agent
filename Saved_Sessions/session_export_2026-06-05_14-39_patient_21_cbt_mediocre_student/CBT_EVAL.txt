## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL CONFERENCE

**Therapist Strengths:**
- Appropriate warmth, professionalism, and non-judgmental stance
- Adequate rapport-building and patient engagement
- Recognition that patient's thoughts about medication were relevant
- Attempt to use multiple CBT interventions
- Patient remained engaged and willing to try suggestions

**Critical Areas Requiring Immediate Intervention:**

1. **Case Conceptualization** (Priority: URGENT)
   - Develop written formulation before next session
   - Identify: primary problem, maintaining factors, treatment targets, treatment strategy
   - Share with supervisor for feedback

2. **Address Grandiose/Delusional Beliefs** (Priority: URGENT)
   - "God has cured me" must become primary clinical focus
   - Use Socratic questioning to explore this belief
   - Do not treat as simple cognitive distortion

3. **Safety Assessment** (Priority: URGENT)
   - Conduct comprehensive suicide risk assessment
   - Assess substance use, sexual risk, medical risk
   - Develop safety plan

4. **Structured Agenda-Setting** (Priority: HIGH)
   - Establish clear, prioritized agenda each session
   - Allocate time explicitly
   - Return to agenda mid-session

5. **Socratic Questioning** (Priority: HIGH)
   - Replace suggestions with open-ended questions
   - Help patient discover insights rather than imposing them
   - Practice with supervisor in role-plays

6. **Collaborative Empiricism** (Priority: HIGH)
   - Design behavioral experiments to test patient's beliefs
   - Move from "think differently" to "test your beliefs"
   - Create tracking forms for data collection

7. **Psychoeducation** (Priority: HIGH)
   - Educate about mania, sleep deprivation, HIV medication adherence
   - Provide written materials
   - Assess understanding

8. **Medication Adherence Planning** (Priority: HIGH)
   - Collaboratively problem-solve barriers
   - Explore ambivalence using motivational interviewing
   - Develop concrete adherence plan

9. **Structured Homework** (Priority: MEDIUM)
   - Provide written instructions and forms
   - Review homework at start of each session
   - Troubleshoot barriers to completion

10. **Interdisciplinary Coordination** (Priority: MEDIUM)
    - Contact psychiatrist regarding mood stabilizer/antipsychotic consideration
    - Coordinate with HIV care team
    - Ensure integrated treatment approach

**Recommended Supervision Plan:**
- Weekly 1-hour supervision sessions
- Review session recordings (audio or video)
- Role-play Socratic questioning and motivational interviewing
- Develop case conceptualization collaboratively
- Practice CBT techniques with supervisor feedback
- Recommend additional training: CBT for bipolar disorder, motivational interviewing, complex case management

**Timeline for Competency Development:**
- Weeks 1-2: Focus on case conceptualization and safety assessment
- Weeks 3-4: Focus on Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Weeks 5-6: Focus on collaborative empiricism and behavioral experiments
- Weeks 7-8: Focus on homework structure and follow-up
- Ongoing: Integration of all skills with supervisor feedback
