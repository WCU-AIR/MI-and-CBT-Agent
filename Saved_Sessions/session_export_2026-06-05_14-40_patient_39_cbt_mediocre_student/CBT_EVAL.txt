## CRITICAL CLINICAL SUMMARY

**Diagnostic Recognition Failure:**
The therapist has failed to recognize factitious disorder despite explicit case documentation. Treatment is based on misdiagnosis (adjustment disorder/anxiety disorder).

**Contraindicated Treatment:**
Standard CBT techniques (cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation) provide secondary gain and reinforce illness behavior. This approach will strengthen rather than challenge the patient's maladaptive pattern.

**Core Deficiencies:**
- No guided discovery or Socratic questioning
- No focus on central cognitions/behaviors of factitious disorder
- No coherent treatment strategy
- Vague, unstructured agenda repeated across sessions
- Minimal feedback elicitation
- Inappropriate homework
- No review of prior homework

**Impact on Patient Care:**
- Reinforcement of factitious behavior through therapist attention contingent on illness presentation
- Missed opportunity to leverage patient's insight ("sometimes I wonder if I'm just making things worse for myself")
- Collusion with deceptive system rather than corrective intervention
- Risk of continued medical utilization, escalation of symptoms, and potential serious medical harm

**Competency Assessment:**
Therapist is functioning at a level that is harmful to this patient. Immediate intervention and specialized supervision required.
