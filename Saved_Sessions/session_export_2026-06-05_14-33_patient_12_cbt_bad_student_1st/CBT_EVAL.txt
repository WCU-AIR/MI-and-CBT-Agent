## SUPERVISOR RECOMMENDATIONS

**Immediate Priority Actions:**

1. **Establish CBT Conceptualization & Functional Analysis** – Develop clear understanding of self-injury triggers and function before next session
2. **Adapt Language & Communication** – Simplify all language to concrete, 5-10 word sentences; check understanding frequently
3. **Recognize Autism-Related Needs** – Stop treating sensory sensitivities as anxiety; accommodate rather than challenge
4. **Apply Evidence-Based Techniques** – Implement functional behavior assessment, behavioral interventions, environmental modification
5. **Involve Parents/Caregivers** – Engage family as active treatment partners
6. **Assign Structured Homework** – Provide specific, monitored between-session tasks
7. **Pursue Specialized Training** – Seek consultation and training in CBT for intellectual disability and autism

**Clinical Supervision Needed:** Weekly supervision with expert in developmental disabilities and autism recommended until competency demonstrated.
