## Overall Session Summary

**Overall MI Competency Score: 1.9/5**

**Session Strengths:**
- Clear, consistent focus on presenting problem without topic drift
- Warm, genuine opening establishing basic rapport
- Concrete planning with specific timeline and action steps
- Genuine encouragement and expressed confidence in Oliver's ability
- Basic reflective listening demonstrating ability to reflect content

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- **Fundamental misalignment with MI principles:** Session structured around advice-giving and problem-solving rather than evocation and collaboration
- **Expert-driven stance:** Clinician adopts authority role, generates solutions, invites agreement rather than co-constructing
- **Minimal validation:** Oliver's ambivalence, guilt, and fear treated as obstacles rather than valid human experiences
- **Insufficient empathic depth:** Reflections factual rather than emotionally resonant; core conflicts unexplored
- **Reduced intrinsic motivation:** Directive approach shifts locus of motivation from internal (Oliver's values) to external (clinician's suggestions)

**Impact on Client Engagement:**
Oliver likely experiences **reduced intrinsic motivation** despite appearing to move toward action. Compliance-based agreement substitutes for values-based commitment. Unresolved guilt and worry create risk of backing down during actual conversation if flatmate expresses distress. While concrete planning supports follow-through, lack of internal ownership compromises sustainability.

**Priority Recommendations:**
1. Shift from advice-giving to evocation through strategic questioning
2. Develop empathic depth using complex reflections capturing emotional nuance
3. Establish true collaboration by seeking client input first
4. Validate and explore ambivalence rather than rushing to solutions
5. Amplify spontaneous change talk through deepening questions
6. Use double-sided reflections to soften sustain talk
7. Explicitly affirm client autonomy and right to choose
8. Connect boundary-setting to Oliver's core values
9. Replace prescriptive language with collaborative language
10. Develop comfort with silence and ambivalence

**Path Forward:**
Clinician demonstrates genuine warmth and desire to help. With focused MI training emphasizing evocation and collaboration, this clinician could become effective motivational interviewer. Key learning: trust client wisdom; ask rather than tell; evoke rather than install; collaborate rather than direct.
