## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

**Priority 1: Conduct Proper Psychiatric Assessment**
- Comprehensive evaluation of current manic symptoms (sleep, grandiosity, distractibility, risky behavior)
- Safety assessment (suicidality, homicidality, financial risk)
- Medication history and compliance review
- Substance use assessment
- Functional impairment evaluation

**Priority 2: Establish Collaborative Agenda with Clear Goals**
- Identify 2-3 specific, prioritized problems
- Focus on: understanding mood episode, medication management, safety planning, family responsibilities
- Write agenda visibly; allocate time explicitly

**Priority 3: Recognize Poor Insight as Symptom**
- Use psychoeducation and gentle reality-testing, not confrontation
- Apply Socratic questioning to help Nancy discover patterns
- Reference previous episodes to establish baseline

**Priority 4: Provide Psychoeducation About Bipolar Disorder**
- Explain manic symptoms and their connection to decreased sleep and risky behavior
- Use visual aids (mood charts, symptom checklists)
- Help Nancy understand neurobiological basis

**Priority 5: Conduct Safety Assessment and Planning**
- Explicitly assess financial risk, substance use, and suicidality
- Develop written safety plan with Nancy and husband
- Document thoroughly

**Priority 6: Implement Specific CBT Techniques**
- Assign sleep log to track relationship between sleep and mood
- Develop thought records to examine beliefs about blog
- Design behavioral experiments to test assumptions
- Use guided discovery (Socratic questioning) throughout

**Priority 7: Assign Structured, Specific Homework**
- Provide written instructions with clear rationale
- Check understanding and commitment
- Plan for obstacles

**Priority 8: Eliminate Personal Self-Disclosure**
- Maintain professional boundaries
- Keep focus on patient's experience and needs
- Redirect urge to share personal stories

**Priority 9: Involve Husband as Collateral Support**
- Obtain permission; include in sessions when appropriate
- Use as source of information about baseline and previous episodes
- Involve in safety planning and medication compliance monitoring

**Priority 10: Elicit Feedback Regularly**
- Check understanding after explaining concepts
- Ask for reactions to interventions
- Adjust approach based on patient feedback
- Explore resistance rather than insisting
