## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths:**
- Excellent collaborative stance with consistent choice-offering and patient positioning as active agent
- Strong empathic understanding and accurate reflections of patient's conscious experience
- Clear, specific agenda-setting with priority-checking
- Coherent basic CBT strategy with logical progression
- Skillful cognitive restructuring exercise producing measurable emotional shift
- Appropriate interpersonal warmth and professionalism establishing good rapport
- Specific, feasible homework assignment directly linked to session work
- Good pacing and focus without tangents

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **No case conceptualization or multi-session plan:** Therapist did not explicitly link patient's presentation to PPD/OCPD diagnoses or identify core beliefs and maintaining factors
2. **Insufficient Socratic questioning:** Provided alternatives prematurely rather than continuing guided discovery until patient generated insights independently
3. **Narrow treatment focus:** Addressed only one situation and one thought pattern; did not develop comprehensive strategy for personality disorder traits
4. **Limited technique range:** Relied on cognitive restructuring and behavioral experiments; did not introduce emotion regulation, behavioral activation, or interpersonal skills despite clear clinical need
5. **Inadequate homework structure:** No monitoring format provided; anticipatory problem-solving lacked role-play and specific language practice

**Recommendations for Next Session:**
- Develop explicit case conceptualization before session
- Establish collaborative multi-session treatment plan with specific goals
- Explore core beliefs about trustworthiness and control using deeper questioning
- Introduce emotion regulation skills for anger/irritability management
- Create structured homework monitoring form
- Conduct more thorough anticipatory problem-solving with role-play
