## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment: INADEQUATE**

This session fails to meet minimum standards for evidence-based CBT treatment of panic disorder. The therapist's approach—characterized by generic reassurance, excessive self-disclosure, and absence of structured CBT techniques—is unlikely to produce therapeutic benefit and may reinforce maintaining factors of the patient's panic disorder.

**Key Concerns:**
- Complete absence of panic disorder psychoeducation
- No systematic assessment of panic symptoms or maintaining factors
- Repeated reassurance despite patient's explicit feedback that it is ineffective
- Zero application of evidence-based CBT techniques
- No homework or between-session structure
- Potential reinforcement of reassurance-seeking behavior

**Recommended Actions:**
1. Immediate enrollment in structured CBT training program
2. Regular clinical supervision with experienced CBT therapist

4. Recording and review of subsequent sessions with supervisor
