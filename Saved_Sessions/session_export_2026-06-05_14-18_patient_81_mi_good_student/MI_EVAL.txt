## Overall Summary

### Strengths:
- **Exemplary autonomy support:** Consistently affirms Luka's right to choose pace and direction; validates partial progress as legitimate
- **Strong collaborative stance:** Positions Luka as expert; invites co-creation of agenda; uses inclusive language
- **Solid empathy and reflective listening:** Accurate, complex reflections capturing ambivalence; validates both progress and struggles
- **Effective evocation:** Draws out powerful intrinsic motivations related to relationships and self-respect; amplifies success stories
- **Clear direction maintenance:** Consistently returns to stated goal of reducing alcohol use
- **Strong acceptance:** Warm, nonjudgmental stance; validates current stage of change

### Areas for Improvement:
- **Sustain talk management:** Could more strategically soften resistance through discrepancy exploration and reframing
- **Premature strategy introduction:** Shifts to psychoeducation before fully exploring Luka's own ideas about managing obstacles
- **Deepening emotional exploration:** Opportunities to more fully explore emotional nuance of internal conflict
- **Strategic discrepancy exploration:** Could more explicitly highlight gap between values and current behavior
- **Confidence scaling follow-up:** Could explore what would move confidence higher rather than pivoting to past successes
