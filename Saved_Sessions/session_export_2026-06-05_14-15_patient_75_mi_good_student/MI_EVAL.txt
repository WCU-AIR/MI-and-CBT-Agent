When Linda expresses doubt ("It seems so hard"), psychiatrist does not ask about past accomplishments to evoke ability talk. ## Summary of Key Areas for Improvement

✗ **Insufficient evocation of values and intrinsic motivation** — missed opportunities to deeply explore what kind of mother Linda wants to be and what change would make possible  
✗ **Inconsistent cultivation of change talk** — while some change talk is reflected, psychiatrist does not strategically amplify desire, ability, reasons, need, and commitment language  
✗ **Limited strategic softening of sustain talk** — sustain talk is validated but not strategically reframed; double-sided reflections underutilized  
✗ **Information-sharing positioned as somewhat expert-driven** — could have invited Linda's ideas first before offering suggestions  
✗ **Missed exploration of readiness markers** — when Linda rates importance at 4/10, psychiatrist does not strategically explore what would increase that number  
✗ **Insufficient exploration of referral source** — daughter's school program mentioned but not explored as potential motivator  
✗ **Ambiguous change goals** — session does not clearly establish whether focus is reduction, cessation, or finding alternatives  
✗ **Minimal normalization of non-adherence** — does not explicitly address what happens if Linda does not follow through

---

## Recommendations for Clinical Development

**HIGHEST PRIORITY:**

1. **Deepen evocation of values and intrinsic motivation** through targeted open questions: "What kind of mother do you want to be?" "What does being a good role model mean to you?" "What would protecting your kids from smoking make possible?"

2. **Strategically cultivate and amplify change talk** through follow-up questions that deepen desire, ability, reasons, need, and commitment language. When Linda rates importance at 4/10, ask: "What's the 4 about?" and "What would move it to a 5 or 6?"

3. **Use double-sided reflections to soften sustain talk** and pivot toward change talk: "On one hand, you enjoy smoking and it helps you relax. On the other hand, you're genuinely worried about your kids. Tell me more about that worry."

4. **Invite client ideas before offering expert suggestions** — ask "What have you thought about?" or "What ideas do you have?" before providing information.

5. **Explore scaling responses strategically** — always follow up with questions to understand what the number represents and what would increase it.
