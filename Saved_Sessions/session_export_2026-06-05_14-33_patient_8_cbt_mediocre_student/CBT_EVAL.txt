## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Clinical Urgency:** This case requires immediate intervention. Valerie has a BMI of 14.7 with amenorrhea—a medical emergency. The current outpatient cognitive therapy approach is inadequate and potentially dangerous if it delays necessary medical intervention and nutritional rehabilitation.

**Recommended Immediate Actions:**
1. **Medical Assessment:** Obtain vital signs, ECG, electrolytes, and other labs. Determine hospitalization criteria.
2. **Medical Coordination:** Establish relationship with eating disorders medical specialist. Develop coordinated treatment plan.
3. **Behavioral Intervention:** Shift from cognitive work to structured meal planning with specific caloric targets and systematic barrier assessment.
4. **Family Involvement:** Include family in psychoeducation and meal support.
5. **Therapist Training:** Therapist requires specialized training in eating disorder treatment. Recommend: CBT-E manual, family-based treatment training, eating disorder workshop, supervision from eating disorder specialist.

**Prognosis Without Changes:** Without significant modifications to treatment approach, patient will likely continue to restrict, weight will decline further, and medical complications will worsen. Risk of serious medical consequences or death is real.
