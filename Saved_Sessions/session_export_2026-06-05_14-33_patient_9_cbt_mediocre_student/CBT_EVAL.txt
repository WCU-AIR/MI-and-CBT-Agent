## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **1. URGENT: Reassess Appropriateness of Standard CBT**
Consult with supervisor before next session. Determine whether patient is appropriate for outpatient CBT or whether different intervention is needed (structured behavioral program, forensic evaluation, specialist referral).

### **2. Develop Clear Treatment Conceptualization**
Explicitly state understanding of patient's problems and goal of treatment. Challenge patient's narrative without hostility: "I notice you see yourself as treated unfairly by others across work, marriages, and parenting. One possibility is others are genuinely unfair. Another is your behavior contributes to conflicts. My job is to help you figure out which is true."

### **3. Assess Motivation and Readiness for Change**
Directly ask: "Are you here because you want to change, or because your employer required it? Do you think your behavior at work is part of the problem?"

### **4. Use Confrontation and Behavioral Contingencies, Not Cognitive Reframing**
Recognize cognitive reframing is ineffective for this patient. Use confrontation respectfully, behavioral contingencies, and appeals to self-interest.

### **5. Address Lack of Remorse Directly**
When patient demonstrates indifference to harm, do not normalize it. Ask directly: "Do you think you have responsibility for safety at work? If someone got hurt because of your mistake, how would you feel?"

### **6. Assign Behavioral Experiments, Not Thought Monitoring**
Assign specific, testable behavioral experiments: "Try accepting feedback without arguing. Notice what happens. Does the supervisor respond differently?"

### **7. Do Not Validate Narrative Without Question**
Introduce alternative perspectives: "I also notice from the referral there were concerns about work quality and safety. How do you think those fit together?"

### **8. Develop Competence in Personality Disorder Treatment**
Read: Livesley (2003), Hare (2003). Seek supervision from clinician experienced in personality disorders. Attend specialized training.
