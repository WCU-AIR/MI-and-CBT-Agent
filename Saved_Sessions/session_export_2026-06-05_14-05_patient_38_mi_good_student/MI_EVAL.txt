### Evocation

Elena generates her own change talk ("If the vaccines can really stop something worse from happening, maybe that's important"). Past successes are evoked effectively, building self-efficacy. However, evocation could be more proactive—earlier exploration of core parental values and more direct elicitation of commitment language would strengthen this domain.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Genuine partnership is established with shared power and collaborative stance. Elena's ideas shape session direction; she is positioned as expert on her own values. Language is consistently inclusive ("It sounds like," "You're weighing"). However, partnership could be strengthened by more actively engaging Elena as a partner in evaluating information sources and by sharing clinical perspective at key moments while maintaining collaborative equality.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist demonstrates solid skill in identifying and amplifying Elena's change talk around desire, ability, reasons, and commitment. Key instances are reinforced effectively ("It sounds like you have the ability to make informed choices that feel right for you and your child"). However, opportunities were missed to evoke stronger commitment language through follow-up questions like "What would it take to move from a 7 to an 8?" or "Where are you leaning right now?" More strategic cultivation of commitment talk would strengthen this domain.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** This is a notable weakness. Elena expresses multiple concerns—vaccine side effects, skepticism about government/pharmaceutical sources, desire to hear "both sides" from parent forums—and the psychiatrist primarily reflects these without strategically addressing them. When Elena says "I'm still worried about the vaccines causing problems too," the concern is validated but not corrected with evidence. Most critically, when Elena expresses skepticism about mainstream sources and plans to seek "both sides," the psychiatrist simply reflects this without gently introducing reality about vaccine safety evidence. Double-sided reflections and gentle education are underutilized. This creates risk that Elena may encounter vaccine-hesitant misinformation that reverses her progress.

## Summary of Clinical Findings

### Strengths
- **Exceptional empathic accuracy** with sophisticated, progressive reflections that capture emotional nuance and underlying ambivalence
- **Strong collaborative stance** that positions Elena as expert on her own values and decision-making
- **Effective evocation of change talk** through strategic questioning that invites Elena to generate her own reasons for change
- **Consistent acceptance and warmth** that normalizes concerns and affirms Elena's care for her child
- **Clear direction** maintained throughout without drift; concrete next steps identified
- **Skillful use of scaling questions** to evoke commitment language and assess readiness

### Critical Areas for Improvement
- **Insufficient softening of sustain talk:** Concerns about vaccine side effects and skepticism about information sources are primarily reflected without strategic addressing through education or double-sided reflections
- **Missed opportunities to provide evidence-based perspective:** Medical evidence about vaccine safety is not gently introduced; misinformation about "both sides" is not addressed
- **Limited active co-creation of solutions:** Elena's stated needs are reflected but not actively problem-solved
- **Incomplete addressing of information source skepticism:** Validation occurs without helping Elena develop criteria for evaluating source reliability or redirecting away from potentially misleading parent forums

### Impact on Client Engagement
Elena successfully moves from initial hesitancy to tentative openness and identifies concrete next steps. Her confidence (8/10) and importance (7/10) ratings indicate genuine movement toward change. However, insufficient addressing of sustain talk—particularly skepticism about information sources—creates risk that Elena may encounter vaccine-hesitant misinformation that reverses this progress.

### Recommendations for Excellence
1. Strategically address sustain talk through double-sided reflections and gentle education
2. Help Elena develop criteria for evaluating information source reliability
3. More actively co-create solutions rather than simply reflecting needs
4. Evoke stronger commitment language through more direct questions
5. Provide evidence-based information about vaccine safety earlier in the session
6. Use affirmations more strategically to reinforce movement toward change
7. Address the "both sides" framing more directly and honestly
8. Explore Elena's underlying fears and values more deeply

---

**Overall Assessment:** This session demonstrates solid motivational interviewing practice with particular excellence in empathic accuracy and collaborative stance. The psychiatrist successfully engages Elena and shifts her ambivalence toward change. To reach exceptional practice (5/5), the psychiatrist should strengthen the softening of sustain talk through more direct provision of evidence-based information and strategic use of double-sided reflections, while maintaining the collaborative, empathic stance that characterizes this session's strengths.
