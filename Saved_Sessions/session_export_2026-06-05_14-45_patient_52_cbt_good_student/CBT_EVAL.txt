## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

### **Immediate Actions (Before Next Session):**
1. **Conduct comprehensive suicide risk assessment** via phone or in-person before next session
   - Assess frequency, duration, and content of daydreams about death
   - Determine intent and plan
   - Identify protective factors
   - Create safety plan with patient

2. **Expand treatment strategy** to include behavioral activation for depression
   - Assess current activity level and identify valued activities
   - Create behavioral activation schedule
   - Explain rationale to patient

### **Next Session Priorities:**
3. **Integrate grief-specific CBT interventions**
   - Meaning reconstruction exercises
   - Continuing bonds work
   - Identity exploration post-loss
   - Imaginal exposure to memories

4. **Develop explicit comprehensive treatment plan**
   - Overall goals (work performance, social reconnection, grief processing, sleep improvement)
   - Specific interventions for each domain
   - Session frequency and anticipated duration
   - Measurement tools (PHQ-9, PG-13, work metrics)

5. **Assess and address occupational/professional identity concerns**
   - Explore relationship to surgical career
   - Address perfectionism and "being strong" in professional context
   - Plan return-to-work strategy

6. **Create detailed, multi-domain homework**
   - Cognitive: Continue balanced thought with measurement
   - Behavioral: Schedule valued activities (3+ times/week)
   - Social: Reach out to one friend/colleague
   - Occupational: Attend work on time, notice one thing done well daily
   - Grief: Write about positive memory with grandmother

### **Ongoing Development:**
7. **Seek supervision or consultation** on:
   - Complex case formulation and treatment planning
   - Behavioral interventions for depression
   - Grief-specific CBT techniques
   - Risk assessment and safety planning

8. **Implement structured measurement tools**
   - Use PHQ-9 and PG-13 at baseline and every 2-4 sessions
   - Track work performance and social engagement
   - Monitor sleep and concentration
