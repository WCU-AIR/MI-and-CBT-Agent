## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Classification:** Supportive Counseling (Not CBT)

**Key Findings:**
- Therapist demonstrates interpersonal warmth but lacks CBT competence
- Complete absence of CBT structure (agenda, guided discovery, techniques, homework)
- No case conceptualization or individualized treatment strategy
- Generic interventions mismatched to patient's clinical presentation and cognitive level
- Critical clinical material (psychotic symptoms, confusion about behavior, automatic thoughts) not addressed
- Excessive self-disclosure and casual professional tone

**Recommendation:** Therapist requires immediate supervision and specialized training in CBT for psychosis before continuing to treat this patient. Current approach is unlikely to produce therapeutic benefit and may reinforce maladaptive patterns.
