## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Observed:**
- Basic empathic listening and non-judgmental tone
- Maintained professional boundaries without hostility
- Identified some relevant automatic thoughts
- Attempted to normalize patient experience
- Demonstrated willingness to engage patient in treatment

**Priority Areas for Improvement (In Order of Urgency):**

1. **Develop Clear Case Conceptualization** — Integrate trauma history, current symptoms, and maintaining factors. All interventions must flow logically from this formulation.

2. **Implement Behavioral Activation** — For chronic depression with withdrawal, behavioral activation is often more effective than cognitive work alone. This must be primary initial intervention.

3. **Shift to Guided Discovery** — Replace directive suggestions with Socratic questions. Help patient reach own insights rather than telling her what to think.

4. **Establish Structured Homework with Systematic Review** — Provide clear instructions, rationale, and always review in subsequent session. This is non-negotiable in CBT.

5. **Conduct Comprehensive Assessment** — Systematically assess trauma history, core beliefs, behavioral patterns, baseline symptom severity using validated measures (PHQ-9).

**Immediate Action Required:** Recommend intensive CBT supervision or training before continuing with this patient. Current delivery does not meet evidence-based standards.
