## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Identified:**
- Basic warmth and non-hostility
- Acknowledgment of patient concerns
- Patient did not feel actively harmed

**Immediate Action Items (Priority Order):**

1. **STOP therapist self-disclosure** – Eliminate personal anecdotes; redirect focus to patient experience through Socratic questioning
2. **Establish structured agenda** – Every session must have clear, prioritized targets with explicit time allocation
3. **Conduct systematic assessment** – Assess fear of vomiting onset, avoidance hierarchy, catastrophic thoughts, safety behaviors
4. **Introduce psychoeducation** – Teach anxiety cycle and how avoidance maintains fear
5. **Develop graded exposure hierarchy** – Collaboratively identify feared eating situations from least to most anxiety-provoking
6. **Teach cognitive restructuring** – Identify and challenge catastrophic thoughts using Socratic method
7. **Assign specific homework** – Every session must end with concrete, tailored between-session task with clear rationale
8. **Eliminate generic reassurance** – Replace with evidence-based interventions and specific coping skills
9. **Implement behavioral activation** – Address social withdrawal through scheduled valued activities
10. **Establish systematic feedback review** – Review prior homework and elicit session feedback at end of each session

**Competency Assessment:** Therapist demonstrates basic interpersonal warmth but lacks fundamental CBT competence. Current approach will not produce therapeutic change for ARFID or anxiety disorders. Intensive supervision and training required before continuing to treat patients with eating disorders.
