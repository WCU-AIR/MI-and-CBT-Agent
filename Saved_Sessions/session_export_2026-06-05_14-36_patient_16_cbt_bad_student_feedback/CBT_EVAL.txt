## SUPERVISOR REVIEW NOTES

**Session Context:** Patient presented with acute postpartum psychosis including fixed delusional beliefs about harming infant, hyperreligious ideation, severe sleep deprivation, racing thoughts, reality confusion, and affective lability. Prior psychiatric hospitalization. Ambivalence about seeking help.

**Critical Findings:**

- **Psychiatric Emergency Not Recognized:** Therapist normalized severe symptoms and offered self-help strategies instead of recognizing need for immediate hospitalization.
- **Boundary Violations:** Excessive self-disclosure with multiple personal anecdotes shifted focus from patient and inappropriately normalized symptoms.
- **No CBT Delivered:** Zero application of evidence-based CBT techniques, guided discovery, cognitive restructuring, behavioral experiments, or structured interventions.
- **Inadequate Safety Assessment:** No thorough evaluation of risk to self or infant despite clear indicators requiring detailed assessment.
- **Collaboration Absent:** Unilateral advice-giving without patient input, treatment rationale discussion, or shared decision-making.
- **No Treatment Plan:** Disconnected suggestions without coherent strategy, conceptualization, or monitoring plan.

**Recommendation:** Immediate remediation required. Therapist should not see patients independently until competencies are demonstrated through formal training and supervised practice.
