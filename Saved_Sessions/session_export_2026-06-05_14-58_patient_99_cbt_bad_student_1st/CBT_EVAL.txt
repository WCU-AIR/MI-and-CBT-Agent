2. **Inappropriate Self-Disclosure** – 6+ personal anecdotes shifted focus from patient to therapist
3. **Failure to Address Presenting Problem** – Tobacco Use Disorder never systematically explored
4. **No Treatment Conceptualization** – Patient has no coherent plan for change
5. **Lack of Collaboration** – Therapist positioned as advice-giver, not collaborative partner
6. **No Measurement or Progress Tracking** – No objective goals or systematic monitoring
