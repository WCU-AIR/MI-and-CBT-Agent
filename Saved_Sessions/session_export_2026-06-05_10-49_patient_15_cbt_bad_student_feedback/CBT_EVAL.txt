## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **Priority 1: Develop Case Conceptualization and Treatment Strategy**
- Identify primary problem (primary nocturnal enuresis vs. anxiety vs. shame/teasing)
- Map maintaining factors and relationships between problems
- Develop coherent, evidence-based treatment plan before next session

### **Priority 2: Conduct Thorough Structured Assessment**
- Systematically assess enuresis pattern (frequency, timing, triggers, previous interventions)
- Assess anxiety symptoms (onset, severity, triggers, impact on functioning)
- Explore automatic thoughts, core beliefs, and behavioral patterns
- Document all findings in writing

### **Priority 3: Implement Evidence-Based Interventions**
- For enuresis: Consider moisture alarm training, fluid management, scheduled voiding (not generic advice)
- For anxiety: Teach specific anxiety management skills (progressive muscle relaxation, grounding) with practice during session
- Implement graded exposure to feared situations (sleepovers) rather than avoidance-compatible coping

### **Priority 4: Establish Collaborative Structure**
- Set mutually satisfactory agenda with explicit prioritization
- Elicit feedback throughout session
- Assign specific, structured homework with clear rationale
- Eliminate excessive self-disclosure; focus on patient's unique experience

### **Priority 5: Seek Supervision**
- Consultation with experienced CBT therapist strongly recommended
- Review CBT case formulation frameworks and evidence-based treatment protocols
- Practice specific CBT techniques before next session with patient
