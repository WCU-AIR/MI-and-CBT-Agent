## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE ACTION

1. **URGENT—Ensure Medical Safety:** Confirm patient received appropriate medical evaluation and is on pharmacological treatment for acute withdrawal. Coordinate with medical providers. Understand signs of severe withdrawal requiring emergency referral.

2. **Conduct Comprehensive Assessment:** Gather detailed information about drinking history, previous treatment attempts, family history, triggers, social support, and motivation to change using standardized tools (AUDIT, CIWA-Ar).

3. **Develop Comprehensive Treatment Plan:** Integrate medical management, psychological intervention, relapse prevention, motivation enhancement, and family involvement. Establish clear goals and milestones.

4. **Provide Formal Skills Training:** Teach and practice coping skills in session before assigning as homework. Provide written instructions and materials.

5. **Extend Session Length and Increase Frequency:** Recognize that 20-25 minutes is insufficient for severe AUD with acute withdrawal. Extend initial sessions to 50-60 minutes and increase frequency (twice weekly) during acute phase.

6. **Integrate Specialized Addiction Treatment:** Pursue specialized training in evidence-based substance use disorder treatment, motivational interviewing, and addiction medicine to develop competence in this complex clinical area.
