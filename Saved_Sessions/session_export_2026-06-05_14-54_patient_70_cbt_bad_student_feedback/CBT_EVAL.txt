## SUPERVISOR NOTES

### Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Attention:

1. **Agenda-Setting:** Establish agenda within first 5-10 minutes of every session with specific target problems, time allocation, and clinical rationale.

2. **CBT Techniques:** Implement specific, evidence-based techniques: thought records, behavioral activation, behavioral experiments, activity scheduling, mood monitoring. Do not rely on generic supportive suggestions.

3. **Homework Structure:** Assign specific between-session tasks with clear rationale, detailed instructions, monitoring/tracking, and explicit patient agreement.

4. **Guided Discovery:** Replace direct advice-giving with Socratic questioning that enables patient self-discovery and insight development.

5. **Case Conceptualization:** Develop coherent formulation identifying maintaining factors and how specific interventions address these factors.

6. **Professional Boundaries:** Reduce therapist self-disclosure; maintain professional authority and expert positioning.

7. **Collaboration:** Engage patient as active partner in problem-solving; systematically elicit and integrate feedback throughout session.

8. **Clinical Understanding:** Deepen assessment of anhedonia, apathy, cognitive-motivational paralysis, and role of recent loss in maintaining depression.

### Recommended Actions:

- Enroll in intensive CBT skills training or workshop
- Engage in weekly clinical supervision with CBT-trained supervisor
- Review session recordings with supervisor to identify specific skill gaps
- Practice specific techniques (thought records, behavioral activation) in role-plays before implementing with patients
- Do not continue seeing patients for CBT without supervision until competency demonstrated
