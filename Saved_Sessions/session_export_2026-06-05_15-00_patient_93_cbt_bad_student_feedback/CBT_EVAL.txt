## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **HIGHEST PRIORITY ACTIONS:**

1. **Establish Collaborative Agenda** – Begin each session with explicit goal-setting and prioritization
2. **Develop Case Conceptualization** – Create written formulation of how pain, depression, and substance use interconnect
3. **Replace Advice-Giving with Guided Discovery** – Use Socratic questioning instead of direct suggestions
4. **Explore Catastrophic Thoughts About Pain** – Systematically identify and examine pain-related beliefs
5. **Address Substance Use Explicitly** – Assess cannabis use as maintaining factor in depression and avoidance
6. **Implement Structured Behavioral Activation** – Create graded hierarchy of activities with clear rationale
7. **Systematically Elicit Feedback** – Check understanding and adjust approach based on patient responses
8. **Provide Clear Rationale for All Interventions** – Explain the "why" behind every suggestion
9. **Assign Specific, Monitored Homework** – Replace vague suggestions with structured assignments
10. **Avoid Personal Anecdotes** – Focus on patient's experience; normalize through psychoeducation
11. **Develop Explicit Treatment Goals** – Establish measurable goals and regularly review progress
12. **Seek Supervision on CBT Competence** – Receive regular supervision from experienced CBT supervisor; review session recordings
