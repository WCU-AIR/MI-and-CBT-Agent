## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CORRECTIVE ACTION

**Immediate Competency Concerns:**
- Therapist demonstrates minimal CBT competence and should not provide independent treatment for complex cases without intensive supervision
- Approach is more consistent with general supportive counseling than evidence-based CBT
- Critical gaps in understanding of ARFID as serious medical condition requiring specialized intervention

**Priority Training Needs:**
1. CBT session structure and agenda-setting
2. Cognitive restructuring and thought records
3. Behavioral experiments and exposure therapy
4. Psychoeducation about fear-avoidance cycles
5. Specialized training in eating disorder treatment
6. Professional boundary maintenance and appropriate self-disclosure

**Recommended Supervision Plan:**
- Weekly 1-2 hour supervision sessions with CBT/eating disorder specialist
- Review of session recordings (minimum 1 per week)
- Training in evidence-based ARFID treatment protocols
- Role-play practice of CBT techniques with supervisor feedback
- Competency assessment before resuming independent practice
