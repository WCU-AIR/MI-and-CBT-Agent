## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Strengths Identified:**
- Warm, friendly, non-threatening demeanor
- Patient remained engaged and cooperative throughout
- Non-judgmental stance appropriate for eating disorder treatment

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Intervention:**
1. Complete absence of CBT structure and framework
2. Failure to assess and conceptualize the disorder
3. Misunderstanding of anorexia nervosa as motivational vs. psychiatric/medical condition
4. Inadequate clinical response to medical severity (BMI 14.7, amenorrhea)
5. Repeated generic advice without evidence of effectiveness or adjustment
6. No collaboration or shared decision-making
7. Excessive self-disclosure shifting focus from patient
8. No homework or between-session structure

**Recommended Immediate Actions:**
- Arrange weekly supervision with eating disorder specialist
- Complete training in CBT for eating disorders (recommend Fairburn's Enhanced CBT manual)
- Develop written case formulation before next session
- Coordinate with medical team regarding nutritional rehabilitation and monitoring
- Implement structured agenda-setting in all future sessions
- Establish formal homework assignments with clear rationale and tracking
