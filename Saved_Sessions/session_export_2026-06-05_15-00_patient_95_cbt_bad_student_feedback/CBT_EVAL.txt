## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment:** This session represents a significant departure from evidence-based CBT practice and falls substantially short of the standard of care for a 13-year-old with Specific Learning Disorder and Generalized Anxiety Disorder.

**Key Strengths:**
- Warm, non-hostile demeanor
- Genuine expression of care
- Appropriate validation of normalcy
- Safe environment for disclosure

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- Complete absence of CBT structure and technique
- Excessive self-disclosure violating professional boundaries
- Reassurance-based approach without cognitive work
- Failure to identify core maintaining factors
- No collaborative partnership or guided discovery
- No between-session structure or homework
- Poor continuity across sessions

**Immediate Action Required:** This therapist requires direct supervision, feedback on recorded sessions, and formal training in evidence-based CBT before continuing to treat patients with anxiety disorders. Recommend enrollment in accredited CBT training program and weekly supervision with CBT-trained clinician.
