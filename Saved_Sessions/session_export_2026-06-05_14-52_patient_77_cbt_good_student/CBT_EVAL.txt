## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Strengths Demonstrated
- **Exemplary interpersonal skills:** Warm, genuine, validating, age-appropriate communication creating safety and engagement
- **Strong empathic understanding:** Accurately grasped patient's emotional experience and internal reality
- **Consistent feedback-seeking:** Regularly checked understanding, elicited reactions, adjusted responsively
- **Appropriate Socratic questioning:** Facilitated self-discovery through perspective-taking and evidence examination
- **Competent cognitive techniques:** Applied thought examination and thought replacement with moderate skill
- **Clear homework assignment:** Provided explicit, relevant homework with rationale and feasibility check
- **Good session structure:** Logical flow from problem identification through intervention to summary

### Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Intervention
- **Complete failure to address primary diagnosis:** Pica behavior never mentioned, explored, or targeted despite being reason for emergency room visit and lead exposure
- **Inadequate case conceptualization:** Focused on anxiety as primary problem rather than recognizing pica as primary diagnosis
- **No functional analysis:** Did not explore triggers, maintaining factors, or functions of pica behavior
- **No behavioral interventions:** Applied only cognitive techniques; no behavioral activation, habit reversal, competing response training, or environmental modification
- **Missing psychoeducation:** Did not explain cognitive model, pica etiology, or connection between anxiety and pica
- **Incomplete agenda:** Did not address environmental safety (lead exposure), recent move/transition, or behavioral targets
- **Limited consideration of developmental/intellectual factors:** Cognitive restructuring may exceed capabilities of child with mild-to-moderate intellectual developmental disorder
- **No parent/caregiver involvement:** Session did not include or explicitly plan for parental involvement despite critical importance
- **No safety planning:** No discussion of lead exposure risks, environmental modifications, or emergency protocols

### Recommendation
Therapist should receive immediate supervision focused on: (1) comprehensive case formulation and differential diagnosis; (2) functional analysis and behavioral assessment; (3) evidence-based interventions for pica and behavioral disorders; (4) adaptation of CBT for children with intellectual disability; (5) systems-level intervention (parent training, medical coordination). Therapist has strong foundational skills and should be encouraged to continue developing expertise in complex cases. **Do not allow independent treatment of pica cases until competency in pica-specific interventions is demonstrated.**
