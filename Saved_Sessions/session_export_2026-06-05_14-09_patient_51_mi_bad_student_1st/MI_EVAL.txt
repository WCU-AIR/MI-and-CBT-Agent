## Overall Clinical Summary

**Overall MI Competency Score: 1.0/5 – Fundamental Rejection of MI Principles**

### Critical Findings

This session represents a **systematic violation of Motivational Interviewing principles** across all nine competency domains. The psychiatrist demonstrates:

- **Authoritarian, coercive stance** with ultimatums and explicit statements that change is "non-negotiable"
- **Systematic dismissal** of patient's emotional experience and anxiety as "excuses" and "overthinking"
- **Complete absence of reflective listening** throughout entire session
- **Ignoring patient's stated priorities** (test anxiety) while imposing different agenda
- **Active suppression of change talk** when patient expresses willingness
- **Rejection of patient autonomy** through pressure and removal of choice
- **Escalating resistance** through confrontation rather than strategic MI techniques

### Impact on Client Engagement

**This session actively harms the therapeutic relationship and decreases likelihood of change.** By session end, patient has:
- Become more defensive and resistant
- Expressed increased fear and anxiety
- Felt judged and invalidated
- Withdrawn from the process ("I'm feeling more stuck, not less")

Research demonstrates this approach—characterized by confrontation, pressure, and dismissal—is associated with **worse outcomes** and increased resistance to change.
