## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Identified:**
- Genuine warmth and supportive presence
- Consistent encouragement and positive regard
- Appropriate tone and rapport-building
- Recognition of patient's strengths (family relationships)
- Non-hostile, non-demeaning demeanor

**Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Attention:**
- Complete absence of CBT structure (agenda, feedback, homework)
- No application of evidence-based CBT techniques
- Failure to recognize medical complexity of RLS in dialysis patients
- Passive patient role; no collaborative partnership
- Repetitive suggestions without progression or depth
- No assessment of catastrophic thinking or hopelessness
- Inappropriate recommendations (water intake) despite patient's medical constraints

**Recommended Training Focus:**
1. CBT session structure and agenda-setting
2. Guided discovery and Socratic questioning
3. Cognitive and behavioral technique application
4. Homework design and tracking
5. Collaborative practice and shared decision-making
6. Assessment of RLS and related conditions
7. Medical-psychiatric coordination

**Risk Level:** Moderate. Patient unlikely to experience meaningful improvement with current approach. Risk of patient disengagement and demoralization. Medical causes of RLS may go unaddressed.
