## Summary of Areas for Improvement

- **Psychoeducation delivery** shifts toward expert mode; could co-explore rather than deliver information didactically
- **Deepening evocation of problem-solving** when exploring peer pressure; could more explicitly draw out client's own strategies
- **Exploring appeal of drinking** beyond peer pressure; could ask directly what makes it attractive to better understand and reframe sustain talk
- **Tightening directional focus** during tangential exploration; could explicitly reconnect back to alcohol-specific decision-making
- **Amplifying importance ratings** after 7/10 rating; could ask what would move number higher to deepen motivation
- **Celebrating client strengths** without immediately instrumentalizing them; could affirm competence and worth before extracting lessons

## Recommendations for Clinical Development

1. **Enhance Collaborative Psychoeducation:** Ask clients what they already know or suspect before providing information. Co-explore mechanisms together rather than delivering in expert mode.

2. **Deepen Evocation of Problem-Solving:** When clients mention barriers, explicitly ask them to generate solutions: "You've already shown you can handle this. What do you think you could do?"

3. **Explore Reinforcing Functions:** Ask directly what makes the problematic behavior appealing beyond surface reasons. Identify underlying needs and strategically reframe how change could meet those needs better.

4. **Tighten Directional Focus:** After tangential explorations, explicitly reconnect to change goal: "How does this connect back to your decision about drinking?"

5. **Amplify Numerical Ratings:** When clients give importance or confidence ratings, follow up with: "What would move that to [higher number]?" This powerful evocation technique deepens motivation.

6. **Celebrate Before Analyzing:** After client successes, spend time offering genuine affirmation before extracting lessons. This strengthens self-efficacy and partnership.

7. **Develop Sophisticated Sustain Talk Reframing:** Explore underlying needs beneath sustain talk, then reframe how change could actually meet those needs better than the status quo.

8. **Reinforce Ownership in Action Planning:** When clients generate plans, explicitly validate that wisdom comes from them: "That's your plan, and it sounds like something you're genuinely committed to."
