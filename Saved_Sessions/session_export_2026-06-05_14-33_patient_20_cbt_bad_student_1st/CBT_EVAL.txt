## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Competency Assessment:** The therapist demonstrates basic interpersonal warmth but lacks fundamental CBT competence. This session would not qualify as evidence-based CBT.

**Most Critical Changes Needed (Immediate):**
1. Conduct comprehensive suicide risk assessment and develop safety plan
2. Eliminate false reassurance and excessive self-disclosure
3. Learn and implement systematic behavioral activation
4. Develop collaborative case conceptualization
5. Replace advice-giving with Socratic questioning

**Recommended Next Steps:** Intensive supervision with recorded role-plays, study of CBT protocols for depression and bipolar II, and reassessment before independent patient contact.
