## Overall Clinical Summary

**Overall Session Score: 1.2/5**

**Assessment:** This session represents a **critical failure in Motivational Interviewing practice**. The psychiatrist demonstrates authoritarian, coercive clinical practice that is antithetical to MI principles across all nine competency domains.

**Key Deficiencies:**
- Complete absence of acceptance, empathy, and partnership
- Systematic undermining of patient autonomy and self-determination
- Zero collaboration or joint problem-solving
- Failure to evoke intrinsic motivation; reliance on external pressure instead
- Confrontational handling of ambivalence that strengthens resistance
- Violation of MI spirit (partnership, acceptance, compassion, evocation)

**Current MI Competency Level:** Pre-MI level. This is not MI with poor execution; this is traditional directive counseling that actively violates MI principles.

**Likely Clinical Outcome:** This session is likely to **decrease patient's intrinsic motivation for change** and damage the therapeutic alliance. Patient leaves more defensive and entrenched in ambivalence. While coercion might produce short-term compliance, it will not produce sustained behavior change or genuine commitment to contraception use.

**Critical Recommendations:**
1. Fundamental mindset shift from expert-driven to client-centered practice
2. Development of genuine empathy and reflective listening as foundation
3. Elimination of confrontational language; adoption of curiosity-based exploration
4. Explicit support for patient autonomy and self-determination
5. **Immediate enrollment in accredited MI training program with ongoing supervision**

**Clinical Recommendation:** This clinician should not continue conducting MI sessions without formal training and supervision. Current approach risks harming therapeutic relationship and decreasing patient motivation for health behavior change.
