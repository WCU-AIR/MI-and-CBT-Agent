## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Patient Presentation:** 28-year-old male with comorbid OCD (contamination obsessions, excessive hand washing 30+ times daily, checking compulsions, scrupulosity) and Major Depressive Disorder (anhedonia, social isolation, insomnia, hopelessness). History of suicide attempt during alcohol abuse. Treatment-resistant depression (failed fluoxetine, citalopram). Prior psychotherapy with limited benefit.

**Session Overview:** Therapist conducted 45-minute session using supportive counseling approach rather than evidence-based CBT. Session lacked agenda, strategy, and structured intervention. Therapist dominated conversation with personal anecdotes. No CBT techniques applied. No safety assessment despite prior suicide attempt.

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- No agenda or treatment planning
- No exposure and response prevention (gold-standard for OCD)
- No cognitive restructuring for catastrophic thoughts
- No behavioral activation for depression
- Repeated advice to "think positive" (contraindicated for OCD)
- Excessive therapist self-disclosure
- No safety assessment or planning
- No homework assignment
- No guided discovery or Socratic questioning
- Inefficient use of session time

**Strengths:**
- Therapist displayed warmth and respect
- Patient felt heard and appreciated support
- Therapist acknowledged patient's small victory
- Non-judgmental, respectful tone maintained

**Recommendation:** Therapist requires immediate, intensive CBT training and clinical supervision before continuing to treat this patient. Current approach is clinically ineffective and potentially harmful. Patient's prognosis is poor if this approach continues.
