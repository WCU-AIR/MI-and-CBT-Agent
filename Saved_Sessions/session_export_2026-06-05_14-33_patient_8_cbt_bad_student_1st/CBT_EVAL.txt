## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Context:** Patient presents with anorexia nervosa, restricting type (BMI 14.7, amenorrhea, 600 calories/day intake, 3-4 hours daily walking). Medical risk is significant.

**Key Findings:**

- **Agenda & Structure (0/6):** Absent entirely. Session drifted without direction.
- **Feedback (0/6):** Never solicited. No check-ins on understanding or satisfaction.
- **Understanding (2/6):** Surface-level only. Missed critical eating disorder pathology.
- **Interpersonal Effectiveness (3/6):** Warm but boundary violations present (excessive self-disclosure).
- **Collaboration (1/6):** Minimal. Patient positioned as passive recipient.
- **Pacing (2/6):** Inefficient. Tangents and repetition without progression.
- **Guided Discovery (1/6):** Virtually absent. Direct advice instead of Socratic questioning.
- **Key Cognitions/Behaviors (1/6):** Central eating disorder targets not identified or explored.
- **Strategy for Change (0/6):** No coherent eating disorder treatment strategy.
- **CBT Techniques (0/6):** None applied. Supportive counseling only.
- **Homework (0/6):** None assigned.

**Recommendation:** Immediate supervision and retraining in evidence-based eating disorder CBT required before continuing treatment with this patient.
