## CRITICAL CLINICAL SUMMARY

### Strengths Identified:
- Basic empathy and warmth in interpersonal style
- Non-judgmental, supportive tone
- Attempt to maintain session structure
- Patient felt heard and engaged

### Critical Deficiencies:
1. **Failure to recognize psychiatric emergency:** Acute postpartum psychosis misidentified as anxiety
2. **Absence of safety assessment:** No evaluation of risk to self or infant
3. **Inappropriate treatment selection:** Anxiety-management CBT applied to psychosis
4. **No hospitalization recommendation:** Patient sent home with homework instead of emergency referral
5. **Medication not addressed:** No discussion of antipsychotics or mood stabilizers
6. **No care coordination:** Obstetrics and pediatrics not involved
7. **False reassurance:** Minimized severity and delayed appropriate treatment
8. **Contraindicated techniques:** Cognitive restructuring and thought journaling inappropriate for fixed delusions

### Immediate Actions Required:
- **URGENT:** Recognize postpartum psychosis as psychiatric emergency
- **URGENT:** Conduct immediate safety assessment
- **URGENT:** Refer for psychiatric hospitalization evaluation
- Coordinate with psychiatry, obstetrics, and pediatrics
- Establish safety planning for mother and infant
- Pursue specialized training in postpartum psychiatric disorders

### Competency Assessment:
**INADEQUATE.** Therapist demonstrates basic interpersonal skills but lacks competency to recognize and appropriately respond to psychiatric emergencies. Critical failure in clinical judgment and diagnostic assessment. **Not appropriate to continue treating this patient without immediate psychiatric consultation and supervision.**
