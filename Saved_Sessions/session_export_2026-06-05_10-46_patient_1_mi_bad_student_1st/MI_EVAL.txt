## Overall Session Score: **1.1/5**

---

### Summary Assessment

**This session represents a fundamental failure to implement Motivational Interviewing principles.** The psychiatrist demonstrates a confrontational, coercive approach that is **highly likely to reduce Adrian's motivation for change and damage the therapeutic relationship.**

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- Explicit rejection of MI spirit (partnership, acceptance, compassion, evocation)
- Authoritarian stance with controlling language throughout
- Active suppression of Adrian's change talk and amplification of sustain talk
- Direct confrontation triggering reactance and entrenchment
- Invalidation of Adrian's emotions and autonomy
- Zero competence demonstrated across all nine MI evaluation criteria

**Predicted Outcomes:**
- Adrian is likely to leave session feeling disrespected and misunderstood
- Psychological reactance triggered by demands and denial of autonomy
- Adrian's commitment to current behavior strengthened, not weakened
- Therapeutic relationship damaged
- Any agreement to change would be externally motivated (less likely to sustain)

**Immediate Action Required:**
Comprehensive retraining in MI principles and techniques is essential before this clinician should conduct further MI sessions. Priority areas: (1) shift from expert/directive to collaborative stance, (2) replace confrontation with double-sided reflections, (3) actively evoke and amplify change talk, (4) ask open-ended questions, (5) respect autonomy and stage of change, (6) receive formal MI training.
