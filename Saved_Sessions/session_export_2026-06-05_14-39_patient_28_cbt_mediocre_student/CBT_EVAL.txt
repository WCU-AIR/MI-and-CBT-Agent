## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Strengths Demonstrated:**
- Warm, non-judgmental therapeutic tone
- Adequate basic empathy and reflection skills
- Safe environment for discussing sensitive topics
- Patient engagement and rapport maintained
- Appropriate professional boundaries

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Agenda-Setting (Score: 2/6):** No collaborative agenda established in either session. Passive information-gathering rather than structured goal-setting.
2. **Feedback Elicitation (Score: 1/6):** Minimal systematic checking for understanding. Patient concerns not explored or addressed.
3. **Collaboration (Score: 2/6):** Superficial use of collaborative language; actual partnership minimal. Suggestions presented rather than joint problem-solving.
4. **Guided Discovery (Score: 2/6):** Reliance on suggestion over Socratic questioning. Questions leading or closed-ended rather than exploratory.
5. **Strategy for Change (Score: 2/6):** Multiple generic interventions without coherent plan or functional analysis.
6. **Technique Application (Score: 2/6):** CBT techniques applied with significant gaps; rationale not explained; patient concerns not addressed.
7. **Homework (Score: 2/6):** Vague instructions, unclear rationale, no review in subsequent session.

**Recommended Immediate Actions:**
- Implement explicit agenda-setting at start of every session
- Conduct thorough functional analysis before developing treatment plan
- Replace suggestions with Socratic questioning
- Develop coherent, targeted strategy based on functional analysis
- Systematically apply CBT techniques with clear rationale
- Design specific, concrete homework with implementation plans
- Review homework at start of every session

**Competency Level:** Novice. Therapist requires intensive supervision and training before continuing to treat patients with substance use disorder.
