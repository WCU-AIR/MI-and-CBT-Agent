- ✗ **Minimal feedback elicitation** – No homework review in Session 2 (critical failure)
- ✗ **Over-reliance on advice-giving** – Violates core CBT principle of guided discovery

6. **Trauma-Focused Interventions** – Imaginal exposure, trauma processing, nightmare management
