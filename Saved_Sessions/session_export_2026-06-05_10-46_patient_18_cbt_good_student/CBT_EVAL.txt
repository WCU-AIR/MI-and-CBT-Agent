## SUPERVISOR REVIEW SUMMARY

**Session Rating: 2.1/6 (Poor to Barely Adequate)**

**Key Findings:**

- **Fundamental Clinical Error:** Therapist failed to recognize acute mania with poor insight as the primary clinical problem and instead accommodated patient's manic ideation by agreeing to focus on work productivity.

- **Misaligned Intervention:** CBT techniques were applied to secondary issue (work efficiency) rather than core clinical problem (acute mania, grandiosity, poor insight, decreased need for sleep, risky financial behavior).

- **Safety Concerns:** No safety assessment conducted. No evaluation of financial overreach, impulsive behavior, judgment impairment, or substance use risks.

- **Inadequate Psychiatric Coordination:** No discussion of medication management, mood stabilization, or consideration of hospitalization despite clear indicators of acute mania.

- **Reinforcement of Symptoms:** Homework assignment reinforces manic ideation rather than addressing acute mania symptoms.

**Recommendation:** This patient requires immediate psychiatric intervention. Therapist should not continue with current approach without significant supervision and reorientation to evidence-based treatment for bipolar disorder.
