## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment:** This session does not qualify as evidence-based CBT. It is more accurately described as supportive counseling with generic wellness suggestions. For a patient with factitious disorder—a complex condition requiring sophisticated psychological intervention—this approach is inadequate.

**Immediate Training Needs:**
1. CBT conceptualization and treatment planning
2. Socratic questioning and guided discovery techniques
3. Specific treatment protocol for factitious disorder
4. Professional boundary establishment and elimination of personal disclosure
5. Formal agenda-setting and homework assignment procedures
6. Addressing deceptive behavior and identity issues in factitious disorder

**Recommendation:** Therapist should not continue treating this patient without intensive supervision and additional training in evidence-based CBT and factitious disorder treatment.
