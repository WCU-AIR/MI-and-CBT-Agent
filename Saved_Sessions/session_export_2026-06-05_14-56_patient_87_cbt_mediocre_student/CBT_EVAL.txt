## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Context:** Two consecutive sessions with a patient presenting with auditory hallucinations (command content with guilt themes), headaches, social withdrawal, ideas of reference, and functional impairment consistent with schizophreniform disorder.

**Key Strengths Observed:**
- Consistent warmth, gentleness, and non-judgmental stance
- Patient-centered approach with invitation for patient input
- Validation of patient's experience and normalization of struggle
- Accurate identification of presenting problems
- Maintenance of appropriate professional boundaries

**Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Attention:**

1. **Safety Assessment (URGENT):** No explicit inquiry about suicidal or self-harm ideation despite command hallucinations with guilt content. This represents a significant clinical risk and ethical concern.

2. **Psychiatric Referral (URGENT):** No discussion of medication evaluation or psychiatric referral. For schizophreniform disorder, antipsychotic medication is first-line treatment. Therapist's failure to address this represents a fundamental misalignment with evidence-based practice.

3. **Diagnosis-Specific Knowledge Gap:** Therapist does not demonstrate understanding of schizophreniform disorder's core features (positive symptoms, negative symptoms, cognitive impairment) or evidence-based treatment approaches. Generic supportive counseling is insufficient.

4. **CBT Technique Deficiency:** Attempted techniques (cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation) were poorly executed. Critical techniques for psychosis (psychoeducation, reality testing, coping strategies) were absent.

5. **Treatment Continuity Failure:** Session 2 began identically to Session 1 with no homework review, suggesting no integration of between-session data into treatment planning.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- CBT for psychotic disorders (specialized training)
- Safety assessment and risk management
- Case conceptualization for complex presentations
- Guided discovery and Socratic questioning
- Structured homework assignment and monitoring
- Collaboration with psychiatric providers

**Recommendation:** This therapist requires intensive supervision and specialized training before continuing to treat patients with psychotic disorders. Immediate consultation with a CBT supervisor experienced in schizophreniform disorder is essential.
