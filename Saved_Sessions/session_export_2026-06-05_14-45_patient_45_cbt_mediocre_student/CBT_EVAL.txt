## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

### Immediate Action Items:

1. **Establish Collaborative Agenda** (Next Session)
   - Begin with explicit agenda-setting: "What's most important to work on today?"
   - Prioritize problems collaboratively
   - Establish specific session goals
   - Review at end of session

2. **Implement Homework Review** (Next Session - CRITICAL)
   - Allocate 10-15 minutes to review previous homework
   - Ask specific questions: frequency, duration, what happened, thoughts/feelings, barriers, successes
   - Troubleshoot barriers
   - Adjust homework based on outcomes

3. **Develop Explicit Case Formulation**
   - Explain anxiety maintenance cycle to patient
   - Identify core maintaining factors: avoidance, reassurance-seeking, catastrophic thinking
   - Outline coherent treatment strategy with phases

4. **Replace Advice-Giving with Socratic Questioning**
   - Ask questions to facilitate discovery rather than telling patient what to think
   - Use guided discovery to help patient reach insights independently
   - Build self-efficacy through patient-generated solutions

5. **Develop Systematic Exposure Hierarchy**
   - Create detailed, collaborative exposure hierarchy
   - Provide specific, written instructions for each exposure
   - Include rationale for why exposure helps
   - Plan for behavioral experiments to test catastrophic predictions

### Competency Assessment:

**Current Level**: Below-Average CBT Competency (2/6)

**Required Training**: Intensive supervision in evidence-based CBT fundamentals, particularly collaborative empiricism, Socratic questioning, and systematic exposure therapy for anxiety disorders.

**Supervision Recommendation**: Weekly individual supervision with CBT expert until competency improves to at least 4/6 across all domains.
