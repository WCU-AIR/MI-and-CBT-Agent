### **SUMMARY ASSESSMENT**

**Current MI Competency Level:** Minimal to absent. This session represents traditional medical directive counseling (telling client what to do) rather than Motivational Interviewing (evoking client's own motivation for change). This is not MI practice.

**Session Impact:** Actively counterproductive. Maria enters with some underlying engagement (researched inhalers, attended appointment, stated desire to care for self) and leaves more entrenched in resistance, more defensive, and explicitly refusing to commit to change. Therapeutic relationship damaged. Likelihood of future engagement or behavior change decreased.

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- Directive rather than evocative stance throughout
- Repeated confrontation of client concerns (15+ instances)
- Absence of reflective listening (foundation of MI)
- Pressure and coercion instead of autonomy support
- Dismissal of legitimate concerns rather than exploration
- Complete absence of evocation and partnership
- No recognition or reinforcement of change talk

**Most Urgent Improvements Needed:**
1. Shift from directive to evocative stance (ask, don't tell)
2. Replace confrontation with reflection
3. Develop genuine empathic understanding through reflective listening
4. Support autonomy rather than pressuring compliance
5. Explore and validate concerns rather than dismissing them

**Recommendation:** Comprehensive MI training and ongoing supervision required before continuing MI-based interventions. Current approach is counterproductive and may harm client outcomes. Immediate action needed.
