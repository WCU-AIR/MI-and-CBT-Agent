## **SUMMARY OF CLINICAL FINDINGS**

### **Strengths:**
- Maintains clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction as target behavior
- Demonstrates persistence in attempting to address the issue
- Shows genuine concern for Anya's wellbeing

### **Critical Deficiencies:**

This session represents a **comprehensive failure to implement Motivational Interviewing principles**. The psychiatrist operates from an authoritarian, expert-driven model fundamentally incompatible with MI.

**Core Problems:**

1. **Coercive, Autonomy-Undermining Approach:** Repeated use of demand language ("you have to," "you must," "you need to," "there's no choice"). When Anya explicitly requests autonomy support, clinician pressures her further, triggering psychological reactance. Anya progresses from openness to explicit resistance.

2. **Confrontational Stance:** Positions self as expert who knows what Anya needs. Dismisses Anya's self-knowledge and assessment of her own experience. Strengthens defensiveness rather than evoking motivation.

3. **Failure to Evoke:** Provides reasons for change rather than drawing out Anya's own motivations and values. No genuine exploration of her perspective occurs.

4. **Absence of Empathy and Acceptance:** Does not reflect Anya's stated concerns or validate her experience. Pathologizes normal adolescent questions rather than normalizing them. No affirmation of strengths.

5. **Judgment and Moral Prescription:** Language communicates disapproval and moral judgment rather than unconditional positive regard. Anya positioned as irresponsible rather than as capable young person.

### **Impact on Client Engagement:**

**Actively counterproductive to motivation for change.** Anya enters with openness to reflection; exits with explicit resistance and statement that she is "not ready." Coercive approach triggers reactance—Anya becomes more entrenched in position that this is not serious. Unlikely to make changes as result of session. May be less likely to engage in future counseling.

### **MI Competency Assessment:**

**Minimal to no MI competency (1.2/5).** This is not a matter of needing refinement; it is fundamental misalignment with MI principles and spirit. Current approach is more consistent with confrontational advice-giving than with MI.

### **Critical Recommendations:**

1. **Shift from authoritarian to collaborative stance** (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
2. **Respect and support autonomy—eliminate coercive language**
3. **Develop genuine empathy and reflective listening skills**
4. **Evoke rather than impose—draw out Anya's own motivations**
5. **Normalize adolescent development and affirm strengths**
6. **Soften sustain talk through strategic responses, not confrontation**
7. **Establish true collaboration—invite Anya's input**
8. **Cultivate change talk by amplifying Anya's own language**
9. **Eliminate judgment and moral prescription**
10. **Receive formal MI training and supervision before conducting further MI sessions**

---

**RECOMMENDATION:** Psychiatrist should not conduct further MI sessions until receiving formal training and demonstrating competency in MI principles and techniques. Current approach is counterproductive and potentially harmful to client engagement and motivation for change.
