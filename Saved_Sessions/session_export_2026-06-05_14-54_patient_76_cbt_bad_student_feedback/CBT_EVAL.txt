## CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SKILL DEVELOPMENT

**Priority Areas for Immediate Attention:**

1. **Establish and maintain structured agendas** with explicit time allocation and ongoing reference throughout sessions
2. **Implement systematic feedback elicitation** at multiple points during each session and at conclusion
3. **Develop Socratic questioning skills** to replace directive advice-giving
4. **Create coherent case formulations** that guide all treatment interventions
5. **Apply specific CBT techniques** with precision: cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, thought records
6. **Assign specific, rationale-based homework** in every session with clear explanation and follow-up plan
7. **Seek regular CBT-focused supervision** with session recordings for feedback and skill development

**Supervisor Review Note:** This therapist demonstrates foundational interpersonal skills but lacks essential CBT competencies. Continued practice without intensive supervision and training is not recommended for complex cases such as this patient's presentation.
