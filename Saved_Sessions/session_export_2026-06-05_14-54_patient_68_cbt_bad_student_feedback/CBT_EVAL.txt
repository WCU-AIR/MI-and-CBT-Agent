## CRITICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Session Quality Assessment:** This session represents a **fundamental failure to deliver evidence-based CBT**. While the therapist demonstrated basic interpersonal warmth and did not engage in hostile behavior, the clinical approach is insufficient for Raymond's severe presentation.

**Key Deficiencies:**
- No structured agenda or prioritization of acute clinical issues
- Excessive self-disclosure violating professional boundaries
- Superficial understanding of opioid withdrawal, cognitive distortions, and relapse risk
- Zero CBT techniques applied; generic supportive counseling substituted
- No collaboration, guided discovery, or Socratic questioning
- Tangential focus on reminiscence rather than treatment goals
- No homework or between-session skill-building

**Clinical Impact:** Raymond is in acute opioid withdrawal with severe homelessness, estrangement from his daughter, and 10 prior treatment failures. This session does not address his neurobiological needs (medication-assisted treatment), cognitive distortions (hopelessness, catastrophizing), behavioral patterns (isolation, substance use as coping), or relapse prevention. **High risk of continued relapse and treatment dropout.**

**Immediate Actions Required:**
1. Supervisor-led review of session recording
2. Training in CBT fundamentals (agenda-setting, Socratic questioning, cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation)
3. Coordination with prescribing physician on medication-assisted treatment
4. Weekly clinical supervision with CTRS-based feedback
5. Role-play practice of CBT techniques with supervisor

**Recommendation:** This therapist requires intensive training and supervision before continuing to treat patients with substance use disorders. Consider reassignment to lower-acuity cases pending competency improvement.
