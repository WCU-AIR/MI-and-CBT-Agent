## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Identified:**
- Consistent warmth, professionalism, and non-judgmental stance
- Patient engagement and willingness to participate
- Basic empathic listening skills
- Clear, accessible language

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Absence of diagnosis-specific conceptualization** – Patient has brief psychotic disorder; therapist treats as generalized anxiety
2. **No homework review protocol** – Second session fails to review previous homework (fundamental CBT failure)
3. **Minimal Socratic questioning** – Advice-giving predominates; guided discovery nearly absent
4. **Vague, unstructured interventions** – Lack specificity, rationale, and systematic application
5. **Missing core interventions** – No exposure, behavioral experiments, thought records, or relapse prevention
6. **No coherent treatment strategy** – Interventions are ad hoc rather than strategically sequenced

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- CBT technique application (especially Socratic questioning and guided discovery)
- Homework structure, assignment, and review protocols
- Brief psychotic disorder conceptualization and psychosis-informed CBT
- Treatment planning and strategy development
- Behavioral interventions (exposure, behavioral experiments)

**Professional Development Recommendations:**
- Attend workshop on psychosis-informed CBT
- Read literature on brief psychotic disorder and stress-related psychosis
- Seek specialized supervision from therapist with psychosis expertise
- Practice Socratic questioning in role-plays with feedback
