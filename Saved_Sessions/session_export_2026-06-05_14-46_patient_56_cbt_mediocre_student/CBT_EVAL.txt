- Attempt to introduce cognitive and behavioral interventions

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:**

2. **No homework review in Session 2** – critical failure in CBT practice
3. **Directive approach rather than guided discovery** – misses core Socratic method
4. **Vague, unstructured behavioral interventions** – lacks specificity and structure necessary for effectiveness
5. **Missed key clinical issues** – religious/moral guilt, marital conflict, sleep deprivation, social isolation not adequately addressed
6. **No coherent treatment strategy** – interventions appear ad hoc rather than strategically planned

**Recommended Actions:**
- Immediate clinical supervision before next patient session
- Training on CBT agenda-setting and treatment planning
- Training on Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Training on structured behavioral activation and activity scheduling
- Training on systematic homework assignment and review
- Review of postpartum depression presentation and treatment
- Consider case consultation with CBT specialist

**Patient Safety Consideration:**
Patient presents with moderate-severity postpartum depression with significant guilt, social isolation, and marital conflict. Without more comprehensive, strategically planned intervention, there is risk of symptom persistence or worsening, potentially affecting parenting capacity and marital relationship. Recommend increased session frequency (weekly) and psychiatry consultation for medication evaluation.
