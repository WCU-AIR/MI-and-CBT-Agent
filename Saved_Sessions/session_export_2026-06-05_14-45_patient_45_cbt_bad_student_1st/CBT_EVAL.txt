## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality:** Poor (0.5/6 average across all items)

**Strengths Identified:**
- Basic interpersonal warmth and non-hostility
- Attentiveness to patient statements
- Attempt to be encouraging and supportive
- Non-threatening environment established

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Remediation:**
1. **Complete absence of CBT structure** – No agenda, no treatment conceptualization, no coherent strategy
2. **Zero CBT techniques applied** – No cognitive restructuring, exposure, behavioral activation, or evidence-based interventions
3. **Failure to assess key clinical features** – Avoidance, catastrophic thoughts, worry triggers, reassurance-seeking patterns never systematically explored
4. **Inappropriate self-disclosure** – Repeated personal anecdotes violated professional boundaries and shifted focus from patient
5. **Minimization of severity** – Generic reassurance failed to validate intensity of GAD and emerging agoraphobia
6. **No homework or between-session structure** – Session ended without tasks, rationale, or accountability
7. **Reinforcement of maladaptive patterns** – Excessive reassurance likely reinforced reassurance-seeking; distraction suggestions may reinforce avoidance
8. **No feedback elicitation** – Patient's signals that advice wasn't working were ignored; no adjustment of approach
9. **Lack of collaboration** – Expert-dispensing-advice model rather than collaborative partnership
10. **No psychoeducation** – Patient left without understanding anxiety disorder or how treatment works

**Recommendation:** This therapist requires immediate, intensive CBT training and weekly supervision from a CBT-trained supervisor. The therapist should not continue treating patients with CBT until core competencies are demonstrated. Consider referral to structured CBT training program (e.g., Beck Institute, ABCT-approved training). Current practice does not meet standards for evidence-based CBT delivery.
