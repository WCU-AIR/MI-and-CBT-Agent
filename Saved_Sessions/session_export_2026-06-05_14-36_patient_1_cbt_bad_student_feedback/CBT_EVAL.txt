## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **HIGHEST PRIORITY (Implement Before Next Session):**

1. **Develop Structured Exposure Hierarchy** – Conduct detailed assessment of Dylan's avoided situations; create graded exposure plan (imaginal and in vivo); explain rationale collaboratively
2. **Plan Imaginal Exposure to Trauma Memory** – Systematically address intrusive crash imagery through repeated, detailed recounting of accident
3. **Identify and Target Catastrophic Cognitions** – Elicit automatic thoughts about safety, driving, and another accident; use Socratic questioning and cognitive restructuring

### **ESSENTIAL (Implement in Next 1-2 Sessions):**

4. Establish clear collaborative session agendas with prioritization of problems
5. Eliminate excessive self-disclosure and platitudes; replace with validation and psychoeducation
6. Replace directive advice with Socratic questioning
7. Provide psychoeducation about ASD, trauma processing, and role of avoidance
8. Systematically review and adjust homework with specific troubleshooting

### **ONGOING:**

9. Establish collaborative empiricism and active patient engagement
10. Develop comprehensive written treatment plan with specific goals and timeline
11. Address avoidance systematically through graded exposure
12. Monitor and adjust strategy based on patient response
