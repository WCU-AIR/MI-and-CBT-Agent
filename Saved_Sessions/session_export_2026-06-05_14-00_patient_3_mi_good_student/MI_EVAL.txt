## Overall Clinical Summary

**Overall MI Competency Level:** Good, solid MI practice with consistent application of core principles but notable gaps in advanced skills.

**Session Strengths:**
- Exemplary autonomy support with consistent affirmation of Aisha's right to choose and own change process
- Strong opening and agenda-setting establishing genuine partnership
- Skillful handling of initial resistance to discussing alcohol with validation and flexible pivoting
- Effective double-sided reflections capturing her ambivalence
- Solid collaborative stance positioning her as co-expert
- Logical progression toward concrete, achievable first step (tracking)
- Warm, supportive tone creating safe space for exploration

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Insufficient exploration and strategic reframing of sustain talk about stress, cravings, and maintaining change
- Limited evocation of alternative coping strategies when client expresses uncertainty
- Missed opportunity to gently explore alcohol connection to smoking/stress patterns
- Incomplete exploration of confidence shifts (5/10 to 6/10)
- Subtle shifts toward expert stance in final exchanges
- Limited emotional depth in exploring her experience of past relapse and current stressors

**Impact on Client Engagement:** Moderate to good. Aisha's confidence increased from 5/10 to 6/10, and she has a concrete first step. Strong autonomy support and collaborative stance create safe environment. However, without deeper preparation for stress management and obstacle-handling, sustained change likelihood is uncertain.

**Critical Recommendations:**
1. Deepen exploration of sustain talk about stress and cravings as barriers
2. Evoke alternative coping strategies more thoroughly rather than accepting "I'm not sure"
3. Gently explore potential connections between alcohol and smoking/stress
4. Explicitly highlight and explore confidence shifts
5. Develop more comprehensive stress management plan collaboratively
6. Prepare Aisha for obstacles and setbacks before session end
