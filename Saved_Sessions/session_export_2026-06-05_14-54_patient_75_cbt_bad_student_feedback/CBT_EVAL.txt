## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Competency Level:** Not competent to deliver CBT, particularly to patients with psychotic disorders.

**Strengths Identified:**
- Non-hostile, warm demeanor
- Patient felt safe and not judged
- Maintained engagement throughout session
- Professional boundaries maintained (aside from self-disclosure)

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- Complete absence of CBT structure and technique
- Fundamental misunderstanding of psychotic symptoms
- Failure to assess safety (no suicidality screening despite hopelessness)
- Excessive self-disclosure (6+ personal anecdotes)
- Unilateral advice-giving without collaboration
- No focus on key cognitions or behaviors
- No coherent treatment strategy
- No homework assignment or between-session planning

**Recommended Interventions:**
1. Mandatory training in CBT for psychosis (Kingdon & Turkington manual recommended)
2. Weekly individual supervision with experienced CBT therapist
3. Recording and review of all sessions using CTRS
4. Focused training on safety assessment and suicidality screening
5. Practice in Socratic questioning and guided discovery
6. Competency reassessment before resuming clinical work with psychotic patients

**Timeline:** Reassessment recommended after 12 weeks of intensive supervision and training.
