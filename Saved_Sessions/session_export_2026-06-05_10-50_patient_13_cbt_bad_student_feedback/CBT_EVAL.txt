## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Strengths:**
- Warm, genuine interpersonal presence
- Patient felt heard and supported
- Non-destructive therapeutic environment
- Patient attempted homework despite vague instructions

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Remediation:**
1. **Agenda Setting** (Rating: 1/6) – No collaborative agenda established; sessions lack structure and direction
2. **Feedback Elicitation** (Rating: 0/6) – Zero instances of checking understanding or soliciting reactions
3. **Guided Discovery** (Rating: 1/6) – Reassurance substituted for Socratic questioning
4. **Key Cognitions/Behaviors** (Rating: 1/6) – Core maintaining factors not identified or targeted
5. **Strategy for Change** (Rating: 0/6) – No coherent treatment plan or case conceptualization
6. **CBT Techniques** (Rating: 1/6) – Techniques absent or poorly applied; no cognitive restructuring or exposure planning
7. **Homework** (Rating: 1/6) – Vague assignments, inadequate review, no systematic monitoring

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- Training in collaborative agenda-setting and case conceptualization
- Training in Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Training in cognitive restructuring and thought records
- Training in behavioral experiments and exposure planning
- Reducing reassurance and excessive self-disclosure
- Systematic homework assignment and review

**Risk Assessment:**
Without significant changes in approach, this treatment is unlikely to produce meaningful symptom improvement. Patient may experience temporary relief from supportive relationship but core mechanisms maintaining disorder will not be addressed. Recommend intensive supervision and possible additional CBT training before continuing with this patient.
