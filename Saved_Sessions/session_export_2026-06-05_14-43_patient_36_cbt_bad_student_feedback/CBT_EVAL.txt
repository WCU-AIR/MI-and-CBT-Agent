## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE ACTION

1. **Establish Structured Session Agenda and Treatment Planning** – Conduct comprehensive functional assessment with caregivers; identify 2-3 specific, measurable target behaviors; establish clear session goals
2. **Develop Accurate Diagnostic Conceptualization of DSED** – Study neurobiology and etiology; recognize as trauma-related attachment disorder, NOT typical shyness; understand symptoms as adaptive responses to early deprivation now maladaptive and dangerous
3. **Eliminate Excessive Self-Disclosure** – Stop sharing personal anecdotes; model clear professional boundaries; child with poor boundaries needs therapist demonstrating appropriate professional structure
4. **Implement Caregiver-Involved Treatment** – Involve adoptive parents as active treatment partners; educate about DSED; develop behavioral safety plan; teach parent coaching; establish reinforcement system
5. **Use Guided Discovery and Socratic Questioning** – Replace generic reassurance with questions exploring patient's thoughts, feelings, observations about behavior and consequences
6. **Conduct Systematic Behavioral Assessment and Safety Planning** – Assess specific dangerous behaviors in detail; develop safety plan with clear rules, procedures, graduated exposure, consequences
7. **Apply Specific CBT Techniques: Behavioral Rehearsal and Role-Play** – Practice scenarios with patient; teach appropriate social behavior; conduct rehearsal in every session with caregivers present
8. **Teach Anxiety Management and Emotional Regulation Skills** – Recognize anxiety/dysregulation driving indiscriminate approach; teach age-appropriate skills: breathing, progressive muscle relaxation, grounding, co-regulation
9. **Assign Specific, Measurable Homework with Clear Rationale** – Assign 1-2 specific tasks per session with caregiver involvement; review at beginning of next session; troubleshoot barriers; celebrate successes
10. **Eliminate Generic Wellness Advice** – Focus all interventions on core disorder: attachment security, boundary-respecting behavior, appropriate stranger interaction, peer social skills
11. **Develop Measurement and Progress Monitoring System** – Establish clear, measurable outcome metrics; collect data weekly; graph progress; adjust treatment based on data
12. **Seek Supervision and Consultation on Attachment Disorders** – Consult with attachment-focused therapist or supervisor experienced in institutional deprivation and attachment disorders; discuss case in detail; request specific feedback on technique and conceptualization
