## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT

**Strengths to Build Upon:**
- Strong interpersonal warmth and therapeutic alliance
- Accurate identification of key surface-level cognitions
- Willingness to suggest concrete behavioral interventions
- Clear, accessible communication style
- Patient engagement and reported hopefulness

**Critical Areas Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Structured Protocol Adherence:** Implement explicit agenda-setting and mandatory homework review in every session
2. **Case Formulation:** Develop written, individualized case formulations explaining the maintaining cycle
3. **Systematic Technique Application:** Move from superficial suggestions to structured cognitive restructuring (thought records) and behavioral experiments
4. **Comprehensive Assessment:** Gather information on exercise, medical status, cultural factors, and maintaining behaviors
5. **Medical Coordination:** Establish communication with medical team and address urgency of weight restoration

**Recommended Training:**
- Attend evidence-based CBT for eating disorders workshop
- Engage in weekly supervision with eating disorder specialist
- Review Fairburn's Enhanced CBT manual
- Record and review sessions with supervisor
- Develop competency in thought records, behavioral experiments, and activity scheduling
