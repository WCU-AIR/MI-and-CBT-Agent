## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality:** Poor to Barely Adequate (1.5/6)

**Immediate Actions Required:**
1. Implement systematic CBT technique application in next session (conduct actual thought record, not just explanation)
2. Assign formal homework with clear rationale, demonstration, and follow-up plan
3. Eliminate excessive self-disclosure; redirect focus to patient
4. Establish formal agenda-setting with time allocation in every session
5. Develop cultural competence; explore patient's background and adapt interventions accordingly

**Supervision Frequency:** Weekly recommended until competency improves to 4/6 or higher

**Training Needs:** CBT technique application, homework assignment, Socratic questioning, cultural competence with Hmong patients

**Patient Safety Consideration:** Current treatment approach is unlikely to produce meaningful symptom improvement. Risk of patient disengagement due to feeling misunderstood or invalidated. Recommend close monitoring and adjustment of therapeutic approach.
