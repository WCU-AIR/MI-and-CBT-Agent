## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Quality Assessment:** Poor (Score: 9/66)

**Key Observations:**
- Therapist demonstrated genuine warmth and created safe environment but failed to implement evidence-based CBT
- Session functioned as supportive counseling rather than structured cognitive-behavioral intervention
- Multiple critical clinical oversights: no suicide risk assessment despite depressed mood and hopelessness; no assessment of alcohol use severity; no exploration of ambivalence about change
- Excessive therapist self-disclosure (6+ instances) violated professional boundaries and diverted focus from patient's needs
- Patient presented with clear readiness for change but received generic reassurance rather than concrete tools

**Recommended Interventions:**
1. **Immediate:** Provide intensive training in CBT fundamentals, functional analysis, and alcohol use disorder treatment
2. **Supervision:** Require weekly individual supervision with review of session recordings
3. **Training Focus:** Guided discovery, cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, relapse prevention planning, homework assignment
4. **Competency Check:** Require demonstration of proper assessment and case conceptualization before independent practice with addiction cases
5. **Patient Safety:** Ensure proper suicide risk assessment is conducted at next session; consider higher level of care if risk identified

**Prognosis for This Patient:** Guarded without significant intervention changes. Patient at risk for continued drinking and relapse without evidence-based CBT techniques.
