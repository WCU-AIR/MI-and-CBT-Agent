## CRITICAL CLINICAL NOTES FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Strengths Demonstrated:**
- Exemplary interpersonal presence and rapport-building
- Outstanding Socratic questioning and guided discovery technique
- Genuine collaborative stance throughout session
- Competent cognitive restructuring and behavioral planning execution
- Efficient session pacing and logical flow

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Intervention:**
- **Catastrophic case conceptualization error:** Treating fatigue as primary problem rather than symptom of anorexia nervosa with severe malnutrition
- **Complete absence of eating disorder intervention:** No assessment of eating behaviors, eating disorder cognitions, or function of restriction
- **Misaligned treatment strategy:** Cognitive work on work performance does not address core maintaining factors of eating disorder
- **Missing eating disorder-specific techniques:** No behavioral experiments, exposure work, activity monitoring, psychoeducation, or motivational interviewing
- **Inadequate homework:** Addresses fatigue management, not eating disorder; no prior homework review
- **Incomplete agenda:** Eating disorder diagnosis not addressed or negotiated as session focus

**Recommended Immediate Actions:**
1. Conduct comprehensive eating disorder assessment in next session
2. Explicitly address eating disorder diagnosis and establish appropriate treatment priorities
3. Provide psychoeducation about relationship between malnutrition and symptoms
4. Shift cognitive work to core eating disorder cognitions (beliefs about weight, body, food, control)
5. Implement eating disorder-specific behavioral interventions (food monitoring, meal planning, gradual caloric increase)
6. Coordinate with medical provider and registered dietitian
7. Explore ambivalence about weight gain and recovery using motivational interviewing

**Supervision Recommendation:** This therapist requires focused supervision on eating disorder conceptualization and evidence-based eating disorder treatment protocols. Foundational CBT skills are solid and should be reinforced, but application must be redirected toward appropriate clinical targets.
