## SUPERVISOR SUMMARY & RECOMMENDATIONS

**Session Competency Level:** Below standard for psychiatrist or CBT therapist. Functioning at level of supportive counselor without specialized training.

**Immediate Priorities:**

1. **URGENT: Understand NCDLB** — Educate on neurobiology, clinical presentation, and treatment. Recognize neurological vs. psychological etiology.

2. **URGENT: Assess REM Sleep Behavior Disorder** — Conduct detailed assessment and refer to sleep medicine specialist. This is serious neurological condition requiring medical intervention.

3. **Establish CBT Structure** — Implement agenda-setting, guided discovery, systematic technique application, and homework in every session.

4. **Reduce Self-Disclosure** — Eliminate personal anecdotes; shift to collaborative inquiry positioning patient as expert on own experience.

5. **Develop Coherent Treatment Strategy** — Create written plan with primary targets, rationale, intervention sequence, measurement, and medical management.

6. **Seek Supervision** — Arrange regular CBT supervision with experienced trainer. Consult with neurologist/geriatrician on NCDLB management.

**Recommendation:** This therapist requires intensive training before continuing to treat patients with neurocognitive disorders. Current approach risks ineffective or potentially harmful care.
