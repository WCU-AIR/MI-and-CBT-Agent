## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Strengths:**
- Appropriate suicide risk assessment (asked about ideation, intent, plan, means, protective factors)
- Non-threatening demeanor; warm, non-judgmental tone
- Acknowledgment of patient suffering and expression of concern
- Identification of support system (husband) and encouragement to reach out

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Intervention:**
1. **Absence of CBT framework** – No agenda, no conceptualization, no coherent strategy
2. **Failure to address psychotic features** – Delusions not reality-tested; normalization may reinforce them
3. **Misunderstanding of depression** – Anhedonia and hopelessness treated as motivational problems
4. **Inappropriate self-disclosure** – Personal anecdotes violate boundaries and minimize patient's experience
5. **Absence of guided discovery** – Advice-giving replaces Socratic questioning
6. **Lack of collaboration** – Directive stance; patient positioned as passive recipient
7. **No formal homework** – Vague suggestions unlikely to be completed
8. **Failure to coordinate psychiatric care** – No medication review or hospitalization assessment

**Recommended Supervision Plan:**
- Weekly individual supervision with CBT expert
- Focus on: agenda-setting, guided discovery, reality-testing delusions, behavioral activation, safety planning
- Role-play practice for each session
- Recording review with supervisor feedback
- Completion of specialized training in CBT for psychotic depression (required before independent practice with this population)

**Patient Safety Concern:**
Barbara presents with active suicidal ideation, mood-congruent delusions, and moderate-to-severe depression with psychotic features. This session did not adequately address safety or establish coherent treatment plan. Recommend: (1) Psychiatry consultation for medication optimization and hospitalization assessment; (2) Detailed written safety plan; (3) Follow-up within 24-48 hours; (4) Clear crisis resources and emergency contacts provided to patient.
