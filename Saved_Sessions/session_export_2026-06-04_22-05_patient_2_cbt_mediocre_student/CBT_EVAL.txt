## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Strengths:**
- Warm, empathic therapeutic alliance
- Patient felt heard and expressed increased hopefulness
- Accessible, non-jargonistic language
- Introduction of relevant CBT concepts (thought reframing, behavioral coping, values/goals)
- Appropriate encouragement and normalization of difficulty

**Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Agenda-Setting:** Establish explicit, collaborative agenda at session start with prioritization and time allocation
2. **Feedback:** Implement systematic feedback-checking throughout session (minimum 3 times per session)
3. **Guided Discovery:** Replace advice-giving with Socratic questioning; practice ratio of 60% questions to statements
4. **Homework Management:** Assign 1-2 focused tasks with clear rationale and structured format; implement mandatory homework review at session start
5. **Treatment Strategy:** Develop and communicate coherent CBT conceptualization with explicit rationale for intervention sequencing
6. **Behavioral Activation:** Conduct activity monitoring and structured activity scheduling for depression/withdrawal
7. **Contextual Assessment:** Explore cultural identity, systemic stressors, and contextual factors affecting patient

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- Weekly recorded session review with feedback on agenda-setting and guided discovery
- Role-play practice of Socratic questioning and homework assignment
- Study of CBT manuals for adjustment disorder and depression
- Development of session-by-session treatment plans with clear intervention sequencing
