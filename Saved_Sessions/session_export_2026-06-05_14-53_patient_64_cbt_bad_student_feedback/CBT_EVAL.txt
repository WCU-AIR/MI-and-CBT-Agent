## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CLINICAL IMPROVEMENT

### 1. **Establish Clear CBT Agenda and Conceptualization (HIGHEST PRIORITY)**
- Explicitly collaborate with patient to establish session agenda and develop shared understanding of conditions
- Write one-page case conceptualization before each session: What are OCD symptoms? Depression symptoms? How do they interact? What is evidence-based treatment?

### 2. **Learn and Apply Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) for OCD (CRITICAL)**
- ERP is gold-standard treatment for OCD: systematic exposure to feared situations combined with prevention of compulsive responses
- Review treatment manuals (Foa & Kozak, Hyman & Pedrick); watch training videos; practice developing exposure hierarchies

### 3. **Conduct Thorough Suicide Risk Assessment and Develop Safety Plan (URGENT)**
- Assess current suicidal ideation, intent, plan, and access to means
- Develop written safety plan with warning signs, coping strategies, people to contact, crisis resources

### 4. **Use Guided Discovery (Socratic Questioning) Instead of Directive Advice**
- Replace statements with skillful questions that help patient discover insights independently
- Record sessions and count questions versus statements; aim for 70% questions

### 5. **Eliminate Contraindicated Interventions**
- Stop suggesting positive thinking, distraction, or accommodation of avoidance
- These are ineffective or harmful for OCD

### 6. **Implement Behavioral Activation for Depression**
- Develop structured behavioral activation plan with specific, scheduled activities linked to patient's values
- Use activity logs to track activities and mood

### 7. **Elicit and Respond to Patient Feedback Throughout Session**
- Regularly check for understanding, ask for reactions to interventions, adjust approach based on feedback
- Make feedback elicitation routine part of every session

### 8. **Reduce Self-Disclosure and Maintain Professional Boundaries**
- Minimize personal anecdotes; focus on patient's experience, not therapist's
- Review recordings and count personal stories; aim to eliminate or drastically reduce

### 9. **Assign Specific, Structured Homework with Clear Rationale**
- At end of each session, assign specific between-session tasks with clear rationale and tracking mechanism
- Always provide rationale and check for understanding and commitment

### 10. **Develop Comorbidity-Informed Treatment Plan**
- Recognize that OCD and depression interact and maintain each other
- Develop integrated treatment plan addressing both conditions simultaneously

### 11. **Manage Session Time Efficiently with Clear Structure**
- Establish clear session structure: opening (agenda), middle (intervention), closing (summary and homework)
- Allocate time to different topics; use timer if needed; practice redirecting tangential discussions

### 12. **Pursue Ongoing Supervision and Training in CBT for OCD**
- Seek supervision from CBT expert, particularly in OCD treatment
- Consider formal training in ERP; enroll in CBT training program or workshop
- Join consultation group focused on OCD treatment

Immediate, intensive training and supervision are essential. Without corrective intervention, this patient will not benefit from treatment and remains at significant risk.
