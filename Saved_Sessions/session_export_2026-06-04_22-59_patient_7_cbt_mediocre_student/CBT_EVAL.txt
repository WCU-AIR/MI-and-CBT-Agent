## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Summary:**
This therapist demonstrates fundamental misunderstanding of the clinical presentation and lacks the specialized knowledge required to treat patients with organic psychotic disorders. The application of standard CBT for psychosis to a patient with Huntington's disease represents a category error that undermines the entire treatment approach.

**Key Concerns:**
- No acknowledgment of organic etiology or neurobiological basis of symptoms
- No medication management or coordination with medical providers
- Repeated ineffective interventions without adaptation
- Failure to accommodate documented cognitive deficits
- Minimal genuine collaboration or feedback elicitation
- Repeated identical sessions suggesting lack of learning or responsiveness

**Strengths to Build Upon:**
- Basic warmth and professionalism
- Willingness to engage patient
- Familiarity with CBT techniques
- Patient engagement and openness

**Required Training:**
- Neurocognitive disorders and their psychological management
- Huntington's disease specifically (etiology, progression, prognosis)
- Psychosis in organic disorders vs. functional psychosis
- Acceptance-based approaches for persistent symptoms
- Collaborative, feedback-responsive treatment
- Family-inclusive treatment planning
- Interdisciplinary coordination of care

**Supervision Plan:**
- Weekly individual supervision focused on diagnosis-informed treatment planning
- Case consultation with neurology and psychiatry specialists
- Training in acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT)
- Observation and feedback on collaborative agenda-setting and feedback elicitation
- Development of simplified, externally supported interventions for cognitively impaired patients
- Reassessment after 4 weeks of intensive supervision
