## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **HIGHEST PRIORITY:**

1. **Conduct explicit safety planning in next session** using structured format (e.g., Stanley & Brown Safety Planning Intervention). Include: warning signs, internal coping strategies, people/settings for distraction, people to ask for help, professional resources, ways to make environment safer.

2. **Review homework at start of every session** before establishing new agenda. Ask: "How did homework go?" "What did you notice?" "What was easy/difficult?" "What did you learn?" Problem-solve barriers.

3. **Introduce behavioral activation** for Hannah's depressed mood (4/10). Have her identify meaningful/pleasurable activities, schedule explicitly, track mood before/after to demonstrate mood-behavior connection.

4. **Teach distress tolerance skills** (e.g., TIPP: Temperature, Intense exercise, Paced breathing, Paired muscle relaxation) for managing acute emotional crises without self-harm.

5. **Develop explicit relapse prevention plan** identifying vulnerability factors, early warning signs, and specific steps if warning signs appear.

### **IMPORTANT:**

6. Provide written materials and formal thought records for all interventions to increase compliance and portability.

7. Introduce problem-solving skills to address underlying relationship stressor (boyfriend with another girl).

8. Provide psychoeducation about cognitive-behavioral model, anxiety physiology, depression, and suicide risk factors.

9. Expand range of CBT techniques to include exposure, behavioral experiments beyond thought-challenging, and skills training.

10. Establish explicit agenda-setting with time allocation and safety monitoring as first agenda item at every session.

11. Increase frequency of mid-session feedback checks and teach-back verification of concepts.
