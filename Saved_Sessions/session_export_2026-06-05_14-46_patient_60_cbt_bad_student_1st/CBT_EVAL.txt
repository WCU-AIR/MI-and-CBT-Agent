## SUMMARY OF CLINICAL EVALUATION

### Overall Rating: 0.8/6 (Poor)

**Critical Findings:**

This session represents a **fundamental failure to deliver evidence-based CBT**. The therapist scored 0/6 on six of eleven core competencies: agenda-setting, guided discovery, strategy for change, application of CBT techniques, and homework assignment. These are foundational CBT skills, and their complete absence indicates the therapist is providing supportive counseling with generic wellness advice rather than CBT.

**Strengths (Limited):**
- Demonstrated warmth and non-judgmental stance
- Patient remained engaged across two sessions
- Did not dismiss or minimize patient's concerns
- Created safe environment for disclosure of sensitive material

**Critical Deficiencies:**

1. **Structural Failures:**
   - No collaborative agenda; session lacked direction
   - No coherent treatment strategy or case conceptualization
   - No CBT techniques applied; only generic advice
   - No homework assigned; no between-session work

2. **Assessment Failures:**
   - No systematic exploration of medication-induced sexual dysfunction
   - No identification of maintaining factors (anxiety, shame, avoidance, rumination)
   - No discussion of medication alternatives despite documented bupropion tolerance
   - No exploration of relationship impact or communication patterns

3. **Technique Failures:**
   - No cognitive restructuring of worry loop or catastrophic thoughts
   - No behavioral activation or activity scheduling
   - No exposure or behavioral experiments
   - No communication skills training
   - No worry management techniques
   - No thought records or behavioral monitoring

4. **Collaboration Failures:**
   - Unilateral advice-giving rather than collaborative problem-solving
   - Minimal feedback elicitation; patient remained passive
   - No checking of understanding or feasibility
   - No adjustment based on patient resistance

5. **Professionalism Failures:**
   - Excessive personal self-disclosure (5+ anecdotes)
   - Performative empathy without genuine understanding
   - Overly casual tone for serious clinical presentation
   - Lack of clinical confidence or expertise demonstration
