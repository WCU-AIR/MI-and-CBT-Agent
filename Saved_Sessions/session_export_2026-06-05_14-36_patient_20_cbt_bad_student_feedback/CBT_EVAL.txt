## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment:** Poor (Score: 9/66 = 13.6%)

**Strengths Identified:**
- Non-hostile, warm demeanor; patient did not disengage
- Acknowledgment of patient statements through reflective language ("I hear you," "I get that")
- Attempt at normalization to reduce stigma (though applied poorly)

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Intervention:**

1. **Suicide Risk Assessment (URGENT):** Patient has documented suicidal ideation. No assessment conducted. Implement systematic risk assessment using validated tool (e.g., Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale) in next session. Develop safety plan with crisis resources.

2. **CBT Structure:** No agenda, no conceptualization, no strategy. Therapist must establish these foundational elements before proceeding.

3. **Excessive Self-Disclosure:** Therapist inserted 5+ personal anecdotes. This blurred boundaries and shifted focus from patient. Limit to zero to one brief, relevant anecdote per session.

4. **Collaboration:** Patient positioned as passive recipient. Implement shared decision-making, collaborative empiricism, and Socratic questioning.

5. **Technique Application:** Only one CBT technique attempted (positive self-talk), applied poorly. Therapist must learn and systematically apply behavioral activation, cognitive restructuring, guided discovery, and other core techniques.

**Recommended Supervision Plan:**
- Weekly 1-hour supervision sessions focusing on CBT fundamentals
- Review recordings of sessions with supervisor
- Practice CBT techniques through role-play
- Complete formal CBT training (40+ hours)
- Develop case formulations and treatment plans for review
- Study evidence-based protocols for depression and bipolar disorder

**Clearance Status:** NOT CLEARED to continue treating patients with serious mental illness without intensive supervision. Recommend reassignment to lower-acuity cases or suspension of independent practice pending training completion.
