## CRITICAL CLINICAL SUMMARY

**Diagnostic Failure:** Postpartum psychosis was not recognized. Patient presented with fixed delusions about infanticide, complete sleep deprivation, pressured speech, flight of ideas, mood lability, and impaired insight—all hallmark features of postpartum psychosis. Therapist reframed as anxiety amenable to cognitive restructuring.

**Safety Failure:** No safety assessment conducted. No evaluation of suicidal ideation, infanticidal ideation, command hallucinations, or risk to infant. No assessment of insight or capacity to consent to treatment. No safety planning.

**Clinical Judgment Failure:** No recommendation for hospitalization despite psychiatric emergency. Patient left session believing condition manageable outpatient, potentially delaying admission and increasing risk.

**Technical Failure:** CBT techniques applied inappropriately to psychotic symptoms. Cognitive restructuring does not treat delusions. Sleep hygiene insufficient for mania. Mindfulness potentially harmful in psychosis.

**Outcome:** Patient at risk. Infant at risk. Appropriate treatment delayed. This session required immediate referral to emergency psychiatry, not outpatient CBT.
