## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Strengths Identified
- Basic interpersonal warmth and non-hostility
- Patient felt heard on surface level regarding validation of experience
- Cooperative engagement and supportive tone
- No overt harm from suggestions offered

### Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Intervention

**Structural/Process Failures:**
- Complete absence of agenda-setting and session structure
- No feedback elicitation or course-correction mechanisms
- Severe repetition without progression (same advice 5-6 times)
- No between-session homework or treatment continuity

**Clinical/Technical Failures:**
- Zero application of evidence-based CBT techniques
- No cognitive or behavioral assessment
- No psychoeducation about functional neurological disorder
- No exploration of patient's beliefs about diagnosis
- Failure to address core treatment barrier (fear of invalidation)

**Professional Boundary Violations:**
- Excessive personal self-disclosure (multiple anecdotes)
- Overly colloquial tone undermining clinical authority
- Premature reassurance without establishing treatment foundation

### Impact on Patient Care
**High Risk of Negative Outcome:**
- Patient's symptoms likely to persist or worsen
- Core barrier to treatment remains unresolved
- Patient may terminate treatment again (history of leaving hospital AMA)
- No mechanism for symptom change introduced
- Patient left without diagnosis clarity or treatment plan

### Required Actions
1. **Immediate:** Supervisor review of session recording
2. **Short-term:** Establish weekly supervision with CBT-trained clinician
3. **Ongoing:** Complete formal training in CBT for functional neurological disorders
4. **Practice:** Conduct supervised practice sessions with colleagues before resuming patient care
5. **Next Session:** Implement psychoeducation protocol and establish coherent treatment strategy

**Evaluator Note:** This session does not meet standards for evidence-based CBT practice. Supportive counseling with generic coping advice is insufficient for treating complex neuropsychiatric presentations. Immediate intervention required to prevent further therapeutic harm.
