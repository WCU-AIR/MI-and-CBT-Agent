## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment:** These sessions represent a significant departure from evidence-based CBT practice. While the therapist demonstrated warmth and professionalism, the fundamental clinical approach was misaligned with the patient's primary diagnosis and evidence-based treatment protocols.

**Key Clinical Concerns:**
- Primary diagnosis (nocturnal enuresis) was never directly addressed
- Treatment focused on anxiety management rather than enuresis-specific intervention
- No psychoeducation provided about the physiological nature of bed-wetting
- Critical cognitive distortion (belief in volitional control) was not identified or addressed
- Fundamental CBT structure was absent (no agenda, no homework review, no case conceptualization)

**Recommended Supervisor Actions:**
1. Meet with therapist to review case formulation and clarify primary vs. secondary diagnoses
2. Provide training on evidence-based treatment for childhood nocturnal enuresis
3. Review CBT session structure (agenda-setting, homework review, guided discovery)
4. Observe next session and provide real-time feedback on Socratic questioning and collaboration
5. Consider co-therapy or consultation with specialist in pediatric enuresis treatment

**Prognosis for Current Treatment:** Low likelihood of meaningful symptom improvement without significant changes to treatment approach.
