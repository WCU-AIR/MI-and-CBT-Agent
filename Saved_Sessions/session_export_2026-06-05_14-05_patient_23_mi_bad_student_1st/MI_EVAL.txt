- Authoritarian expert stance with explicit rejection of collaboration

- Coercive language ("must," "have to," "no excuses") that removes autonomy
- Abundant change talk ignored; sustain talk amplified through confrontation
- Repetitive scripted approach (same demand repeated six times) despite clear client feedback that approach is failing

**Impact:** This session is likely to **decrease** patient's motivation for change and damage therapeutic alliance. Patient began with clear change talk ("I guess I just wanna stop") and ended more defensive and entrenched. Research on MI is clear: confrontational, authoritarian approaches increase resistance and reduce engagement.

**Urgent Recommendation:** Comprehensive retraining in MI principles, immediate clinical supervision with MI-trained supervisor, and fundamental reorientation to collaborative, client-centered approach. Without these changes, this clinician will continue to harm clients by increasing resistance and damaging therapeutic alliance.
