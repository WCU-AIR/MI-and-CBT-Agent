## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Summary:**

This session does not meet standards for evidence-based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy. While the therapist demonstrated appropriate warmth and maintained engagement, fundamental CBT components were absent or severely deficient. The therapist failed to establish agenda, elicit feedback, implement guided discovery, focus on central cognitions, develop coherent strategy, apply CBT techniques systematically, or assign homework.

**Most Concerning Clinical Issue:**

The therapist's failure to recognize and address Antisocial Personality Disorder features represents a critical clinical gap. The patient's pervasive blame externalization, aggressive control-seeking, and lack of remorse were normalized rather than addressed. Generic stress management advice was offered repeatedly despite explicit patient rejection. This mismatch between patient pathology and intervention is unlikely to produce therapeutic change and may reinforce the patient's existing patterns.

**Immediate Actions Required:**

1. **Suspend independent practice** with personality disorder cases pending competency reassessment
2. **Initiate weekly supervision** with CBT expert specializing in personality disorders
3. **Complete training** in CBT fundamentals and personality disorder treatment
4. **Record and review** all sessions with supervisor
5. **Reassess competency** before resuming independent practice

**Strengths to Build Upon:**

- Genuine warmth and respect toward patient
- Non-hostile, professional demeanor
- Maintained engagement despite patient resistance
- Clear, coherent communication

**Priority Development Areas:**

1. Agenda-setting and session structure
2. Recognition of personality disorder presentations
3. Guided discovery and Socratic questioning
4. Responsiveness to patient feedback
5. Coherent treatment conceptualization and strategy
6. Systematic application of CBT techniques
7. Homework assignment and follow-up
