**Session Context:** Patient (Helena) presented with Major Depressive Disorder with mixed features, recent suicide attempt, passive suicidal ideation, insomnia with nocturnal hyperactivity, racing thoughts, rumination about ex-boyfriend, hopelessness, weight gain, and concentration difficulties. This was a follow-up session in an intensive outpatient program.

**Critical Safety Concern:** Therapist did not assess current suicidality, develop a safety plan, identify warning signs, or provide crisis resources despite patient's recent attempt and ongoing passive ideation. This represents a serious clinical failure with potential life-threatening consequences.

**Recommendation:** This therapist should not continue treating this patient or other patients with severe mental illness without immediate intensive supervision and formal CBT training. Consider reassignment of this case to a CBT-trained clinician.
