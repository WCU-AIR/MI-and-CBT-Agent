## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

### Critical Safety Concern
**Suicidal ideation documented in case formulation ("mild to moderate suicidal ideation without plan") was never assessed or addressed in either session.** This represents a serious clinical and ethical failure. Immediate intervention required.

### Primary Disorder Misconceptualization
The therapist's focus on work-related perfectionism and anxiety-based interventions is misaligned with the patient's primary presentation of **Bipolar II depression with secondary anxiety features.** Treatment strategy must be adjusted to address depressive symptoms (behavioral withdrawal, hopelessness, anhedonia, guilt) rather than anxiety-focused cognitive restructuring.

### Evidence-Based Treatment Gaps
**Missing interventions critical for depression treatment:**
- Behavioral activation (systematic scheduling of valued activities)
- Mood monitoring and sleep/wake cycle tracking
- Behavioral experiments to test depressive predictions
- Structured thought records for depressive cognitions
- Safety planning for suicidal ideation
- Psychoeducation about bipolar disorder and mood cycling

### Collaboration and Engagement
Patient's passive responses ("I'll try, I guess"; "I'm not sure if I'll remember") indicate low commitment and active engagement. Therapist must shift from directive advice-giving to collaborative problem-solving using Socratic questioning.

### Recommended Supervision Focus
1. **Immediate:** Suicide risk assessment protocols and safety planning
2. **Week 1-2:** Case reconceptualization and treatment planning for depression
3. **Week 3-4:** Behavioral activation and mood monitoring implementation
4. **Week 5-6:** Guided discovery and Socratic questioning practice
5. **Ongoing:** Homework review, feedback-seeking, and collaborative empiricism
