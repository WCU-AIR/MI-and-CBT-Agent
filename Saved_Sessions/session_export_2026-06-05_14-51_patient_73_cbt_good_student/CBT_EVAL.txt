## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Demonstrated:**
- Excellent collaborative agenda-setting with genuine patient input
- Skillful use of Socratic questioning, particularly "friend perspective" technique
- Accurate conceptualization of panic cycle with appropriate emphasis on safety behaviors
- Warm, professional interpersonal style with good rapport
- Efficient session pacing through distinct phases
- Well-designed, specific behavioral homework with obstacle-planning
- Concrete measurement of progress (anxiety scale: 7→5)

**Critical Areas for Development:**
- Comprehensive psychoeducation about panic disorder (fight-or-flight, cycle rationale, exposure principles)
- Systematic exposure hierarchies and graded exposure planning across all avoidance behaviors
- Interoceptive exposure (deliberately inducing panic sensations for habituation)
- Structured cognitive tools (thought records) for independent patient work
- Explicit addressing of health anxiety and reassurance-seeking maintenance factors
- Core beliefs identification and targeting (fragility, vulnerability)
- Written session summaries and homework assignments
- Explicit treatment plan with timeline and phase structure
- Relapse prevention and crisis planning

**Recommended Training:**
- Panic Control Treatment protocol training (Barlow & Craske)
- Unified Protocol for Transdiagnostic Treatment of Emotional Disorders
- Advanced exposure therapy techniques and interoceptive exposure
- Health anxiety and reassurance-seeking management strategies

**Patient Prognosis:**
With current approach: Likely moderate improvement with slower progress. With recommended enhancements: Significantly increased likelihood of full remission and sustained improvement aligned with evidence-based standards.
