## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Observed:**
- Adequate interpersonal warmth and non-judgmental tone
- Some accurate empathic reflection of patient's explicit statements
- Patient remained engaged throughout session
- Attempted multiple intervention approaches
- Maintained appropriate professional boundaries

**Critical Gaps Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Agenda-Setting:** Vague opening language repeated three times; no explicit prioritization or session goals
2. **Assessment:** Failed to systematically assess cannabis use, medication adherence, mood, or safety
3. **Conceptualization:** No coherent treatment plan addressing primary diagnoses
4. **Cannabis-Psychosis Link:** Not explicitly addressed despite being central to patient's presentation
5. **Feedback Elicitation:** No meaningful attempt to check understanding or gather patient reactions
6. **Technique Application:** CBT techniques applied superficially without clear rationale or precision
7. **Homework:** Vague, unspecific, and not integrated with treatment strategy

**Priority Training Areas:**
- Structured agenda-setting and collaborative prioritization
- Comprehensive assessment protocols
- Treatment conceptualization and case formulation
- Guided discovery and Socratic questioning
- Structured CBT techniques (thought records, behavioral experiments)
- Relapse prevention planning
- Feedback elicitation throughout sessions

**Recommended Next Steps:**
- Enroll in structured CBT training program (IAPT, Beck Institute, or equivalent)
- Obtain weekly supervision from CBT-certified expert
- Record and review sessions with supervisor using CTRS
- Practice specific skills (agenda-setting, assessment, guided discovery) in role-plays
- Develop case formulations for all patients before sessions
- Use structured homework forms and assessment tools
