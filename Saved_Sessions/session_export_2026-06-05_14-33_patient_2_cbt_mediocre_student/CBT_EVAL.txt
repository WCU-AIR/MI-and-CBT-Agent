## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Observed:**
- Maintains respectful, non-hostile therapeutic environment
- Successfully engages patient in conversation
- Patient remains cooperative and willing to try suggestions
- Identifies some relevant automatic thoughts
- Attempts to provide interventions rather than passive listening

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:**
1. **Homework Review (CRITICAL):** Failure to review homework in session two is a fundamental violation of CBT protocol. This must be implemented in every subsequent session.
2. **Agenda-Setting:** No structured, collaborative agenda in either session. Sessions lack clear goals and prioritization.
3. **Guided Discovery:** Over-reliance on direct suggestion and leading questions; minimal Socratic questioning.
4. **Treatment Strategy:** Lacks coherence and may not be appropriate for patient's presentation (symptoms remit with rest, suggesting exhaustion/overload rather than primary cognitive distortion).
5. **Comprehensive Assessment:** Missing critical content—grief from breakup, racial discrimination, work/school balance, patient strengths.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- Weekly supervision with emphasis on CBT fundamentals
- Practice agenda-setting and homework review in role-plays
- Training in Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Development of coherent case conceptualization and treatment strategy
- Assessment of patient's full context and systemic stressors

**Recommendation:** Therapist should not treat patients independently until core CBT competencies are demonstrated. Consider pairing with experienced CBT supervisor for all cases.
