## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Strengths Demonstrated:**
- Clear, collaborative agenda-setting with patient input
- Accurate empathic reflection of depersonalization phenomenology
- Warm, non-judgmental interpersonal presence with appropriate boundaries
- Effective use of Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Practical, patient-tailored grounding intervention
- Explicit end-of-session feedback solicitation
- Consistent collaborative tone throughout

**Critical Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention:**
- **Case Formulation:** Develop written formulation addressing triggers, maintaining factors, and relationship between depersonalization and depression
- **Clinical Scope:** Expand assessment and treatment to include depressive symptoms, academic/social functioning, and breakup impact
- **Behavioral Intervention:** Implement behavioral activation as core intervention; address avoidance and safety behaviors
- **Psychoeducation:** Provide clear explanation of depersonalization, why avoidance maintains it, and how treatment works
- **Homework Precision:** Provide written instructions, clear rationale, specific predictions, and detailed barrier problem-solving

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
1. CBT case formulation and treatment planning
2. Behavioral activation and exposure techniques
3. Comprehensive assessment of complex presentations
4. Homework assignment and compliance monitoring
5. Integration of cognitive and behavioral interventions

**Session Outcome:** Patient likely to feel supported but unlikely to experience meaningful symptom improvement without addressing above deficiencies.
