## Overall Session Summary

**Overall MI Competency Score: 4.3/5**

**Strengths:**
- Exemplary acceptance and unconditional positive regard; clinician never judges or dismisses concerns
- Sophisticated empathic practice with complex, layered reflections capturing emotional nuance
- Genuine partnership and power-sharing with client positioned as expert on her own care
- Consistent autonomy support with permission-seeking language and emphasis on client choice
- Strong affirmation of client strengths and past successes
- Clear, organized provision of medical information about medications and safety
- Skillful reframing of concerns as strengths; sustain talk not reinforced

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Limited strategic evocation of intrinsic motivation for starting citalopram
- Insufficient cultivation of explicit change talk and commitment language
- Missed opportunity to explore ambivalence about starting medication
- Lack of explicit connection between provided information and stated confidence goal
- Insufficient use of confidence scale as strategic tool to evoke change talk
- No explicit follow-up planning regarding side effect monitoring or concrete next steps
- Limited exploration of potential barriers to medication adherence

**Recommendation:** To reach excellent MI practice (5/5), clinician should focus on more strategic evocation of intrinsic motivation, deeper cultivation of commitment language, explicit exploration of ambivalence, and concrete action planning with clear follow-up.
