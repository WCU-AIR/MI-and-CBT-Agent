## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Session Context:** Psychiatric consultation with Sofia, a 22-year-old woman with cystic fibrosis hospitalized for acute respiratory exacerbation. Presenting problem: anxiety-driven treatment refusal (removing oxygen mask despite medical necessity). Patient has history of anxious attachment to mother and perception that hospital staff doesn't understand her concerns.

**Critical Findings:**

**Strengths:**
- Warm, non-hostile therapeutic tone
- Genuine attempts at reassurance and encouragement
- Appropriate professional boundaries
- Patient felt minimally heard ("It helps more than you know")

**Deficiencies:**
- **No CBT structure:** No agenda, no conceptualization, no coherent strategy, no homework
- **Generic advice instead of targeted intervention:** Wellness tips rather than evidence-based CBT techniques
- **No cognitive work:** Failed to identify or challenge catastrophic thoughts driving anxiety and avoidance
- **No behavioral work:** Failed to address anxiety-avoidance cycle or design exposure interventions
- **Excessive self-disclosure:** Therapist's personal anecdotes consumed time and shifted focus away from Sofia
- **Minimal guided discovery:** Offered advice rather than asking questions that would help Sofia reach insights
- **No focus on key issues:** Failed to address maternal dependency, catastrophic thinking, or anxiety-avoidance cycle
- **Repetitive and circular:** Same suggestions offered multiple times without progression
- **Minimal collaboration:** Sofia positioned as passive recipient rather than active partner
- **Minimal feedback-seeking:** Failed to check understanding or adjust based on patient responses
- **No homework:** No between-session tasks to extend treatment or build independent coping skills

**Clinical Impact:** Sofia was intubated the night after this consultation, indicating the psychiatric intervention failed to address her treatment refusal. Without targeted cognitive-behavioral intervention addressing her anxiety-avoidance cycle, catastrophic thinking, and maternal dependency, her medical status will continue to deteriorate.

**Supervisor Recommendations:**
1. Provide immediate training in CBT case formulation and conceptualization
2. Teach guided discovery (Socratic questioning) as alternative to directive advice
3. Provide training in cognitive restructuring for catastrophic thoughts
4. Teach behavioral exposure design for anxiety disorders
5. Provide training in anxiety management skills (diaphragmatic breathing, grounding, progressive muscle relaxation)
6. Supervise next 10-15 sessions with focus on developing CBT competency
7. Consider pairing with experienced CBT clinician as co-therapist or mentor

**Likelihood of Therapeutic Change with Current Approach:** Low. Generic reassurance without targeted intervention is insufficient for medically acute psychiatric crisis.
