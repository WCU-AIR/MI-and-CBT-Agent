## CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

### **Priority 1 (Implement Immediately):**
1. **Structured Agenda & Homework Review** – Begin every session with written agenda including homework review as first item
2. **Genuine Guided Discovery** – Replace therapist-directed advice with Socratic questioning (80% questions, 20% statements)
3. **Address Core Hopelessness** – Design behavioral experiments that generate hope and evidence of change
4. **Cultural Assessment & Adaptation** – Explore Hmong background, spiritual practices, trauma history; adapt interventions accordingly
5. **Systematic Thought Records** – Move beyond vague "practice thoughts" to structured thought record work

### **Priority 2 (Implement in Next 2-3 Sessions):**
6. **Structured Behavioral Activation Plan** – Create graded activity schedule with pain management strategies
7. **Improve Homework Specificity** – Assign specific, rationale-explained, feasible homework with barrier problem-solving
8. **Assess Literacy & Accommodate** – Explicitly assess reading/writing comfort; use pictures, oral instructions, family involvement
9. **Explore Trauma History** – Acknowledge refugee experience and losses; explore connection to current symptoms
10. **Enhance Collaboration** – Explicitly involve patient in all major decisions; ask for input on every intervention
11. **Systematic Feedback Elicitation** – Check for understanding, reactions, and satisfaction throughout session
12. **Develop & Share Case Conceptualization** – Create clear diagram of symptom maintenance; share with patient; use to guide treatment

### **Supervision Structure:**
- **Weekly 1-hour supervision** with experienced CBT supervisor
- **Session recording review** – Bring audio/video of sessions for feedback
- **Skill practice** – Role-play Socratic questioning, agenda-setting, homework review
- **Cultural consultation** – Involve Hmong cultural broker or community member
- **Trauma training** – Complete training on refugee trauma and trauma-informed CBT adaptations
