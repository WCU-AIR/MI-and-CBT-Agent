## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Strengths Demonstrated:**
- Collaborative agenda-setting with explicit patient choice and preference honored
- Solid empathic listening and accurate reflection of immediate experience
- Effective use of Socratic questioning and perspective-taking for insight development
- Warm, validating interpersonal style creating safe therapeutic environment
- Logical session structure with appropriate pacing and focus maintenance
- Basic CBT technique application (thought records, evidence examination, cognitive restructuring, behavioral experiments)

**Critical Areas Requiring Immediate Attention:**
- **Narrow Clinical Focus**: Session did not address opioid use disorder (primary diagnosis) or develop comprehensive treatment strategy
- **Missing Functional Analysis**: No exploration of triggers, maintaining factors, or functions of substance use
- **Absent Core Techniques**: No behavioral activation (depression), exposure/acceptance work (avoidance), relapse prevention (substance use), or coping skills training
- **Inadequate Homework Specificity**: Lacked measurement criteria, written materials, and thorough commitment-building
- **Unaddressed Core Beliefs**: Perfectionism, self-sufficiency, and shame not explored as drivers of multiple problems
- **Pain Management Gap**: Chronic knee pain (primary opioid use trigger) not addressed

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
1. Comprehensive case conceptualization integrating multiple diagnoses
2. Functional analysis of substance use disorders
3. Evidence-based treatment for opioid use disorder (MAT, relapse prevention, pain management)
4. Behavioral activation protocols for depression
5. Shame-focused and values-based CBT interventions
6. Structured homework design with measurement criteria

**Training Needs:**
- Specialized training in substance use disorder treatment (SAMHSA, ASAM guidelines)
- Advanced CBT techniques (behavioral activation, exposure, acceptance-based interventions)
- Pain management in context of opioid use disorder
- Comprehensive assessment and case conceptualization
- Motivational interviewing and relapse prevention
