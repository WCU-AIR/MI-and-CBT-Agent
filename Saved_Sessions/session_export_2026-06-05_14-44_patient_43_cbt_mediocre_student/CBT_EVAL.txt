## CRITICAL CLINICAL CONCERNS

**Severity of Current Deficiencies:**

1. **No Homework Review in Session 2** — Violates core CBT protocol and represents failure to ensure continuity of care.
2. **Identical Session Content** — Session 2 repeats Session 1 without modification, suggesting no assessment of progress or learning.
3. **No Crisis Intervention** — Patient faces marital dissolution threat and $30,000 debt; therapist's casual tone does not reflect appropriate urgency.
4. **Missing Evidence-Based Interventions** — Stimulus control, response prevention, behavioral experiments, thought records, relapse prevention, and family involvement are absent.
5. **Superficial Collaboration** — Patient positioned as passive recipient rather than active partner in treatment planning.

**Immediate Actions Required:**

- Develop coherent case conceptualization identifying maintaining factors
- Implement structured agenda-setting with homework review
- Conduct functional analysis to understand gambling's role
- Implement behavioral interventions (stimulus control, response prevention)
- Involve spouse in treatment for marital crisis and accountability
- Use Socratic questioning instead of direct instruction
- Address shame and self-worth through systematic cognitive restructuring
- Develop specific, detailed homework with clear rationale and commitment check
- Create relapse prevention plan for high-risk situations
- Refer for financial counseling and crisis intervention

**Supervision Recommendation:** Intensive supervision required. Therapist should not continue treating this patient without significant skill development in evidence-based gambling disorder treatment.
