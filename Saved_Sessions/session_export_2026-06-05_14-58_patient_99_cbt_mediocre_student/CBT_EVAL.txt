## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

### Strengths Identified:
- Consistent warmth, empathy, and non-judgmental stance
- Correct identification of some relevant automatic thoughts
- Attempted behavioral interventions with concrete suggestions
- Assigned homework in both sessions
- Patient remained engaged and willing to try suggestions

### Critical Areas Requiring Immediate Intervention:

**1. AGENDA-SETTING (Score: 2/6)**
- Establish explicit, collaborative agenda at start of every session
- Include: homework review, specific target problems, time allocation, prioritization
- Refer back to agenda throughout session to maintain structure

**2. FUNCTIONAL ANALYSIS (Score: 2/6 - Strategy for Change)**
- Conduct thorough assessment before suggesting interventions
- Identify specific triggers, consequences, and function of smoking
- Explore broader life context (work stress, perfectionism, social isolation, sexual identity)
- Assess motivation and readiness to change

**3. GUIDED DISCOVERY (Score: 2/6)**
- Replace direct advice-giving with Socratic questioning
- Ask open-ended questions that invite patient exploration
- Help patient discover insights and solutions independently
- Resist urge to provide answers

**4. HOMEWORK DESIGN AND REVIEW (Score: 2/6)**
- Design specific, trackable homework with clear rationale
- Use structured tools (thought records, behavior logs, craving logs)
- Systematically review prior homework at start of each session
- Adjust interventions based on what patient learned

**5. COLLABORATION (Score: 2/6)**
- Solicit patient input before offering suggestions
- Present interventions as hypotheses to test, not directives to follow
- Explore patient's concerns and ambivalence rather than minimizing
- Engage patient as active partner in decision-making

### Recommended Training/Supervision:
- Intensive supervision focusing on CBT fundamentals (agenda-setting, guided discovery, case conceptualization)
- Role-play practice with Socratic questioning
- Review of evidence-based CBT protocols for tobacco use disorder
- Observation of expert CBT sessions
- Structured feedback on recorded sessions

### Timeline for Reassessment:
- Reassess after 4-6 supervised sessions with focus on agenda-setting and guided discovery
- Conduct full CTRS evaluation after 10-12 sessions with supervision
