## Overall Clinical Summary

**Overall MI Competency Score: 1.6/5 – Poor**

**Session Assessment:** This session demonstrates **poor motivational interviewing competence** with critical deficiencies across nearly all core MI domains. While the psychiatrist demonstrates basic warmth and maintains focus on a clear target behavior (exercise), the session fundamentally violates MI principles by operating as expert-driven advice-giving rather than collaborative evocation of intrinsic motivation.

**Strengths Identified:**
- Clear, consistent focus on target behavior throughout session
- Logically coherent, organized problem-solving approach
- Warm, welcoming opening tone
- Practical, realistic suggestions (10-15 minute workouts, home-based options, weekly check-ins)
- Recognition of real barriers (time, fatigue)

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- **Collaboration and partnership absent:** Psychiatrist generates all solutions; patient input does not substantially shape session direction
- **Autonomy undermined:** Prescriptive language pressures patient toward predetermined solutions rather than supporting choice
- **Evocation minimal:** Patient's own motivations, values, and resources not drawn out; psychiatrist installs rather than evokes motivation
- **Sustain talk unaddressed:** Patient's repeated doubts, worries, and fears met with prescriptive advice rather than skillful softening techniques
- **Acceptance compromised:** Patient labeled as "lazy"; minimal affirmation; failure to normalize difficulty of behavior change
- **Empathy superficial:** Basic reflections present but lack emotional depth; ambivalence not captured; emotional content not explored

**Impact on Patient Engagement and Change Likelihood:**

This session is likely to **decrease intrinsic motivation** despite good intentions. Patient demonstrates **compliance** (agreeing to try) rather than **genuine commitment**. Closing statement ("I'll give it a shot for now, but I still feel like I'm gonna struggle with sticking to it long-term") indicates low confidence and unresolved ambivalence. Without internal motivation, genuine partnership, and resolution of ambivalence, **relapse is likely**. Patient leaves session with same doubts and fears unaddressed.

**Estimated Likelihood of Sustained Behavior Change:** Low to Moderate (30-40%)

**Current MI Competency Level:** Pre-MI or non-MI. Session resembles traditional directive counseling/psychoeducation rather than motivational interviewing.

## Priority Recommendations for Improvement

1. **Shift from Expert to Collaborative Partner** – Fundamentally reframe role; co-create solutions with patient rather than prescribing them
2. **Evoke Rather Than Install Motivation** – Ask open-ended questions drawing out patient's own reasons, values, and resources
3. **Address Ambivalence Using Double-Sided Reflections** – Normalize hesitation; use reflections acknowledging both change talk and sustain talk
4. **Amplify and Reinforce Change Talk** – Pause and deepen patient's own language in favor of change; use reflections and follow-up questions
5. **Explore Sustain Talk Rather Than Prescribe Around It** – Use curiosity to understand doubts before problem-solving
6. **Use Scaling Questions to Evoke** – Follow scaling questions with evocative queries ("What would move it to 5?") rather than prescriptive advice
7. **Affirm Autonomy Explicitly** – Regularly affirm patient's right to choose and ownership of change process
8. **Deepen Empathy Through Emotional Exploration** – When patient expresses worry or hesitation, pause and explore feeling with curiosity
9. **Minimize Prescriptive Language** – Replace "should," "might want to," "would probably help" with collaborative language
10. **Explore and Affirm Patient Strengths** – Ask about past attempts, what worked, what was learned; build self-efficacy through strength-spotting

**Recommended Training:** Intensive MI training including didactic instruction, observation of expert demonstrations, role-play practice with feedback, and supervised clinical practice with MI-trained supervisors.
