## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Summary:**

This session represents **partially evidence-based CBT** with strong therapeutic relationship-building and sound OCD-focused interventions, but incomplete case conceptualization and treatment planning. The therapist demonstrates excellent foundational CBT skills but shows significant gaps in addressing comorbidity and formalizing treatment tools.

**Most Critical Issues Requiring Immediate Attention:**

1. **Failure to address comorbid depression** – Patient presented with "chronic depression" as primary complaint; session addressed only OCD. This represents significant clinical gap affecting treatment outcomes and patient safety.

2. **Incomplete agenda management** – Two agenda items identified; only one addressed substantively. No explicit prioritization or time allocation strategy.

3. **Lack of formalized CBT tools** – Cognitive work was sound but informal. No thought records, worksheets, or systematic monitoring provided.

4. **Insufficient suicide risk assessment** – Given patient's history of suicide attempt and current depression, more thorough assessment and safety planning needed.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**

- Comprehensive case conceptualization for comorbid OCD-MDD
- Formalization of CBT tools and systematic monitoring procedures
- Explicit teaching of treatment rationale and cognitive model
- Suicide risk assessment and safety planning protocols
- Integration of behavioral activation with exposure/response prevention

**Therapist Strengths to Leverage:**

- Excellent empathic understanding and therapeutic relationship
- Skilled use of Socratic questioning and guided discovery
- Appropriate behavioral experiment design
- Collaborative, patient-centered approach

With targeted supervision addressing the identified gaps, this therapist's already-strong interpersonal and discovery skills can be enhanced to provide more comprehensive, evidence-based treatment.
