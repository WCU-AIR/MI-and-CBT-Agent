## Overall Session Summary

**Overall MI Competency Score: 1.6/5**

**Session Assessment:**

This session demonstrates fundamental violations of Motivational Interviewing spirit and technique. The psychiatrist adopts an expert-driven, directive stance that positions Alexei as a passive recipient of advice rather than an equal partner in exploring change. Core MI competencies—evocation, autonomy support, collaboration, and partnership—are largely absent. While basic counseling skills (warmth, engagement, topic focus) are present, this is not Motivational Interviewing; it is supportive counseling with prescriptive advice.

**Critical Findings:**

- **Prescriptive language dominates** throughout session, emphasizing compliance over choice
- **Autonomy concerns explicitly expressed** by client ("I just don't want to feel like I'm being told what to do a lot") and ignored by clinician
- **Unilateral problem-solving** where clinician identifies problem, assesses risk, and prescribes solutions without collaborative input
- **Client's perspective dismissed** when Alexei expresses that he hasn't experienced problems or doesn't see it as urgent
- **Compliance-based motivation** likely outcome rather than autonomous motivation predictive of sustained change

**Likelihood of Evoking Sustained Motivation for Change:**

**Low.** While Alexei complies with recommendations, compliance-based motivation is less predictive of sustained behavior change than autonomous motivation. Psychiatrist missed critical opportunities to strengthen intrinsic motivation by exploring Alexei's values, evoking his own reasons for change, and supporting his autonomy. Alexei's explicit autonomy concern suggests he experienced session as controlling, which may increase psychological reactance and decrease likelihood of follow-through.

**Recommendations for Development:**

This clinician requires intensive Motivational Interviewing training focused on:
1. **Core MI spirit** (partnership, acceptance, compassion, evocation)
2. **Fundamental techniques** (open questions, reflective listening, evoking change talk, supporting autonomy)
3. **Shifting from prescriptive to evocative stance** (highest priority)
4. **Responding authentically to autonomy concerns**
5. **Strategic amplification of change talk**
6. **Exploration and softening of sustain talk**

Role-play practice with feedback and MI-specific supervision from certified trainer strongly recommended.
