## SUPERVISOR NOTES

**Session Quality Assessment:** Unacceptable

**Competency Level:** Not currently competent to deliver CBT or evidence-based psychiatric care to patients with bipolar disorder, particularly those with poor insight and dangerous behaviors.

**Immediate Interventions Required:**
1. Conduct comprehensive psychiatric assessment (manic symptoms, safety, substance use)
2. Reality-test delusional belief about HIV "cure" using Socratic questioning
3. Address medication refusal directly with psychoeducation
4. Implement behavioral interventions for sleep deprivation
5. Establish clear collaborative agenda and treatment goals
6. Apply specific CBT techniques (cognitive restructuring, behavioral experiments)

8. Establish regular monitoring and follow-up

**Recommendation:** Immediate supervision required. Senior clinician should be present at next session or patient should be referred to more experienced psychiatrist. Do not allow unsupervised continuation of care with this patient in current clinical state.
