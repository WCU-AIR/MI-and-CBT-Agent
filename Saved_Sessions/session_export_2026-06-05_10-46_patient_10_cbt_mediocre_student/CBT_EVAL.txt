## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION

**Priority 1 (CRITICAL):** Develop explicit case conceptualization integrating ADHD, SLD, MDD, panic, and acculturation stress. Identify primary treatment targets and communicate formulation to patient collaboratively.

**Priority 2:** Conduct thorough assessment of current ADHD symptoms and functional impact. Determine whether medication evaluation warranted.

**Priority 3:** Assess learning disorder impact and ensure appropriate academic accommodations in place.

**Priority 4:** Implement systematic cognitive restructuring using guided discovery and structured thought records, not therapist-generated alternatives.

**Priority 5:** Assess and address panic symptoms with specific anxiety management techniques if indicated.

**Priority 6:** Assess depressive symptoms and implement behavioral activation if depression is significant treatment target.

**Priority 7:** Establish clear, collaborative agendas with specific, measurable session goals and explicit prioritization.

**Priority 8:** Elicit feedback regularly (minimum 3 times per session) to ensure understanding and identify barriers.

**Priority 9:** Assign specific, detailed homework with clear rationale, verification of understanding, barrier assessment, and follow-up review.

**Priority 10:** Develop cultural competence and explore identity, acculturation, and discrimination concerns explicitly.

**Priority 11:** Implement genuine collaboration and shared decision-making in all treatment planning.
