## Overall Clinical Summary

**Overall MI Competency Score: 1.1/5**

**Assessment Level:** Pre-MI (not practicing Motivational Interviewing)

### Critical Findings:

This session represents a **fundamental failure of Motivational Interviewing practice** across all nine competency domains. The psychiatrist demonstrates virtually no MI competence and operates at a pre-MI level using traditional directive counseling delivered in a coercive manner.

**Session Impact:** Actively counterproductive. Client entered with genuine openness to change, acknowledged the problem, expressed desire to feel better, and offered concrete steps. By session end, client has been transformed into actively resistant: *"being told what to do like that? It's hard to feel like I actually want to make these changes."* The psychiatrist has violated the fundamental principle that autonomy support increases intrinsic motivation while coercion decreases it and generates reactance.

### Most Critical Deficits:
- ☐ Authoritarian stance from opening; explicit assertion of expert authority
- ☐ Repeated coercive language ("You have to," "You must," "This is non-negotiable")
- ☐ Complete absence of reflective listening (zero reflective statements in entire session)
- ☐ Dismissal of client's change talk as "not good enough"
- ☐ Failure to evoke client's own motivation, values, or ideas
- ☐ Active generation of psychological reactance through threats and demands
- ☐ Invalidation of client's lived experience and legitimate barriers
- ☐ Explicit rejection of collaboration ("I'm not here to negotiate")
- ☐ Threat-based motivation rather than intrinsic motivation

### Immediate Recommendations:

1. **Seek formal MI training** with certified MI trainer (2-day workshop minimum)
2. **Participate in ongoing supervision** with session review and coding for MI adherence
3. **Fundamentally reframe approach** to client autonomy as essential to behavior change
4. **Learn and practice reflective listening** as foundation of every interaction
5. **Eliminate all coercive language** and replace with collaborative alternatives
6. **Develop genuine curiosity** about client's perspective, barriers, and resources
7. **Do not continue practicing MI** until demonstrating competence through supervision

### Likelihood of Evoking Client Motivation for Change:
**Significantly reduced.** This session has actively undermined client's intrinsic motivation and generated explicit resistance. Continuing this approach will harm therapeutic relationship and reduce likelihood of sustained behavior change.
