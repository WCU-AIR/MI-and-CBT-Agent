## CRITICAL CLINICAL CONCERNS

**URGENT ISSUES REQUIRING IMMEDIATE ATTENTION:**

1. **Benzodiazepine Use Disorder Not Addressed:** Patient escalating alprazolam from 0.5 mg to 5 mg/day with tolerance and withdrawal symptoms. This is a medical emergency being completely ignored. Requires immediate comprehensive assessment, medical evaluation, and collaborative tapering plan.

2. **Reinforcement of Maladaptive Dependency:** Therapist's non-directive approach ("if you want to talk about something else, that's fine too") reinforces patient's excessive need for external direction rather than building independence. Contraindicated for dependent personality disorder.

3. **Inadequate Clinical Conceptualization:** Two major diagnoses requiring different intervention strategies not identified or prioritized. Treatment approach is generic anxiety management, not personality-focused or substance-use focused.

4. **Unaddressed Mood and Relationship Disturbance:** Patient's sadness, crying spells, and relationship difficulties completely unaddressed despite being central to presenting complaint.
