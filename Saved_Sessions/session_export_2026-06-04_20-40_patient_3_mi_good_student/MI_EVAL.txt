## Areas for Improvement

• **One missed opportunity to gently explore sustain talk about alcohol**: When Aisha states "I don't think that's a problem for me," psychiatrist accepts boundary but could have gently explored her reasoning with a soft, curious follow-up question without pressure.

• **Limited explicit discussion of potential barriers or obstacles**: While psychiatrist asks what would make change easier, there is minimal exploration of what might derail the plan or how Aisha would handle setbacks. Anticipating obstacles strengthens commitment.

• **No explicit discussion of connections between smoking and alcohol use**: Given both substances are present and Aisha is minimizing her drinking, gentle exploration of whether reducing smoking might affect drinking patterns could have provided integrated understanding.
