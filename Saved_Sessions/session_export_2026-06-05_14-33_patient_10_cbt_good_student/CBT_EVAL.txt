## CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

**Immediate Priorities:**

1. **Expand case conceptualization** to address ADHD, learning disorder, and panic as core treatment targets alongside anxiety
2. **Assess and address depressive symptoms** with behavioral activation strategies
3. **Explore cultural identity and acculturation stress** as relevant to patient's mental health
4. **Establish objective measurement** of progress using symptom tracking and validated outcome measures
5. **Increase homework specificity** with explicit contingency planning and measurement instructions

**Supervision Focus Areas:**

- Comprehensive assessment and formulation of comorbid presentations
- ADHD-specific interventions and their integration with anxiety treatment
- Learning disorder accommodations and their role in reducing academic anxiety
- Panic disorder CBT techniques (interoceptive exposure, psychoeducation)
- Behavioral activation for depression
- Cultural competence and contextual assessment
- Measurement-based care practices
