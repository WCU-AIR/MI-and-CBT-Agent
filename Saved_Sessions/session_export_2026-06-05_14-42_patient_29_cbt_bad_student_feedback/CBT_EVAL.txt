## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL CONFERENCE

**Session Quality Assessment:**

This therapist is functioning at a **pre-CBT level**. While general counseling skills are present (warmth, empathy, non-hostility), CBT-specific competencies are absent. The session demonstrates supportive counseling only, which is insufficient for this patient population.

**Most Critical Changes Required (Priority Order):**

1. **Establish coherent CBT conceptualization and treatment strategy** before next session
2. **Eliminate inappropriate self-disclosure and maintain professional boundaries**
3. **Implement structured agenda-setting and session planning**
4. **Replace directives with guided discovery and Socratic questioning**
5. **Apply specific CBT techniques:** cognitive restructuring, behavioral experiments, thought records, skills training
6. **Assign concrete, relevant homework with clear rationale and follow-up**
7. **Implement systematic feedback-seeking throughout sessions**
8. **Address core pathology:** antisocial cognitions, lack of remorse, medication non-adherence, impulse control

**Whether Session Qualifies as Evidence-Based CBT:**

**No.** This session does not meet criteria for evidence-based CBT. It is supportive counseling with generic advice. While supportive counseling has a role, it is insufficient for a 12-year-old with severe conduct disorder, limited prosocial emotions, comorbid ADHD, and criminal history showing no remorse.

**Supervision Recommendation:**

Intensive weekly supervision with CBT expert focus. Consider co-facilitation or observation of experienced CBT therapist. Recommend formal CBT training or certification program for this therapist.
