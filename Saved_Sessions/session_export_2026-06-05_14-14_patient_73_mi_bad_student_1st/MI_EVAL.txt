## **Overall Session Score: 1.0/5**

### **Summary Assessment**

This session represents a **complete failure to practice Motivational Interviewing**. Every core MI competency is absent or systematically violated. The psychiatrist operates from an authoritarian, coercive stance that is fundamentally contrary to MI principles and philosophy.

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- Zero reflective listening throughout entire session
- Systematic dismissal of client autonomy through coercive language and pressure
- Agenda imposition disconnected from client's stated goals and readiness
- Complete failure to evoke client's intrinsic motivation
- Repeated confrontation and argument instead of collaboration
- Misalignment with client's presenting concern and confidence level
- Violation of MI spirit and core philosophy

**Impact on Therapeutic Relationship and Client Motivation:**
This approach is likely to severely damage the therapeutic relationship, decrease intrinsic motivation, increase resistance and reactance, and result in treatment dropout. The patient entered with high intrinsic motivation (10/10 confidence in exercise) that the psychiatrist's coercive, dismissive approach is actively undermining.

**Recommendation:** Immediate, intensive formal MI training and regular supervision with an MI-trained supervisor are required before this clinician should continue practicing MI. This session should be used as a teaching example of what NOT to do in MI practice.
