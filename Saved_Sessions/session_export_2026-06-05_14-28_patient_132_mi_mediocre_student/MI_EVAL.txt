### Collaboration

Language pattern reveals lack of genuine collaboration: "Maybe you should...," "You might want to...," "It would probably help if...," "Have you tried...?" Goals set by psychiatrist, not collaboratively developed. No genuine power-sharing; psychiatrist controls agenda, solutions, and direction throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Critically deficient. Psychiatrist repeatedly pressures Wiremu toward specific behaviors and minimizes right to choose. Language reinforces obligation and removes choice: "You might want to...," "You should...," "It would probably help if...," "Maybe you should also think about..." True autonomy support absent. When Wiremu expresses legitimate concerns about losing independence and freedom, psychiatrist does not validate or explore as real tensions to be navigated but reframes: "Remember that getting help doesn't mean losing independence—it's about getting stronger." ## Overall MI Competency Assessment

**Overall Session Score: 1.4/5**

**Summary:** This session demonstrates **poor motivational interviewing practice** fundamentally inconsistent with MI principles and spirit. While psychiatrist maintains clear focus on target behaviors and Wiremu remains engaged, session characterized by expert-driven, prescriptive approach violating core MI principles of collaboration, autonomy support, evocation, and partnership.

**Critical Deficiencies:**
- ☐ Collaboration (1/5) — Absent; expert-driven throughout
- ☐ Autonomy Support (1/5) — Absent; prescriptive and controlling
- ☐ Evocation (1/5) — Absent; tells rather than asks
- ☐ Partnership (1/5) — Absent; unilateral decision-making
- ☐ Acceptance (2/5) — Minimal; conditional regard
- ☐ Empathy (2/5) — Minimal; limited reflective listening
- ☐ Cultivating Change Talk (2/5) — Present but not amplified
- ☐ Softening Sustain Talk (2/5) — Attempted but counterproductive
- ☐ Direction (3/5) — Present but imposed

**Current MI Competency Level:** Basic counseling skills demonstrated (listening, questioning, structure) but **true motivational interviewing competence absent**. This is directive counseling with supportive elements, not MI.

**Impact on Client Motivation:** Session likely to **reduce** Wiremu's intrinsic motivation long-term despite initial compliance. Prescriptive approach activates psychological reactance; autonomy undermined; extrinsic motivation emphasized over intrinsic; ambivalence unresolved; self-efficacy undermined. Wiremu at risk of returning to old patterns when external pressure removed or obstacles arise.

**Recommendation:** Psychiatrist requires formal MI training and clinical supervision to develop genuine MI competence and significantly improve outcomes for clients like Wiremu.
