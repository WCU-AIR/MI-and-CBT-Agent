## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Quality Assessment:** This session represents a fundamental failure to provide evidence-based CBT or appropriate psychiatric care for a patient with psychotic disorder due to Huntington's disease and mild neurocognitive disorder.

**Key Clinical Errors:**
- Misdiagnosis of presenting problem (treated psychosis as anxiety)
- Inappropriate intervention selection (cognitive restructuring for hallucinations)
- Absence of safety assessment (paranoid delusions with threats)
- No medical/psychiatric history review
- Excessive self-disclosure and boundary violations
- Repeated ineffective suggestions despite explicit patient feedback
- No accommodation for neurocognitive deficits

**Competency Gaps:**
- Insufficient knowledge of psychotic disorders
- Insufficient knowledge of neurocognitive disorders and Huntington's disease
- Inability to conduct proper psychiatric assessment
- Inability to apply CBT techniques appropriately
- Lack of understanding regarding when CBT is/isn't appropriate
- Poor session structure and agenda-setting skills

**Recommended Immediate Actions:**
1. Supervisor should review complete case with therapist
2. Therapist should not see this patient for next session without supervision plan
3. Comprehensive psychiatric and medical assessment needed before next clinical session
4. Referrals to psychiatry and neurology should be prioritized
5. Therapist should pursue additional training in psychotic disorders and neurocognitive disorders
6. Consider whether therapist has appropriate competence level for this patient population
