## CRITICAL CLINICAL SUMMARY

**Patient Presentation:** Mark Hill, acute Bipolar I Disorder, manic episode, severe, with mood-congruent psychotic features (grandiose delusions with religious content, racing thoughts, decreased need for sleep, psychomotor agitation, recent police involvement, recent hospitalization).

**Session Approach:** Standard CBT cognitive restructuring applied to psychotic delusions.

**Primary Clinical Error:** Therapist treated acute mania with psychosis as if it were a primary anxiety or mood disorder amenable to cognitive restructuring, rather than recognizing it as a psychiatric emergency requiring antipsychotic medication as first-line treatment.

**Evidence-Based Treatment for This Presentation:**
1. Antipsychotic medication (e.g., risperidone, olanzapine, quetiapine)
2. Mood stabilizer (e.g., lithium, valproate)
3. Psychiatric hospitalization if outpatient management inadequate
4. Psychoeducation about bipolar disorder
5. Safety assessment and planning
6. CBT for residual symptoms (after acute stabilization on medication)

**What This Session Provided:**
- Cognitive restructuring of delusions (contraindicated)
- Written reminder to manage thoughts (insufficient for psychosis)
- No psychiatric assessment or referral
- No medication discussion
- No safety planning
- No psychoeducation about bipolar disorder

**Recommendation:** Therapist must immediately refer Mark to psychiatry and seek supervision on bipolar disorder and appropriate role of CBT in conjunction with medication.
