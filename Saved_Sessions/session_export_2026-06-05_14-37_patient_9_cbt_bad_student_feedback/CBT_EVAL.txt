## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Appropriateness:** This session is fundamentally inappropriate for the patient's diagnosis. Standard CBT is contraindicated as primary intervention for APD without significant modifications focused on behavioral accountability and harm reduction. Therapist demonstrates no awareness of this diagnostic reality.

**Most Critical Deficiencies:**
1. **Diagnostic Misunderstanding:** Treats as stress management case rather than personality pathology
2. **Failure to Address Actual Problem:** Allows externalization of blame instead of establishing accountability
3. **Absence of Accountability Framework:** No consequences, contingencies, or clear behavioral expectations
4. **Excessive Self-Disclosure:** Multiple personal anecdotes undermine professional distance and model externalization
5. **No Systematic CBT Application:** Generic advice rather than structured technique implementation
6. **No Homework:** Patient leaves with no between-session task or practice mechanism

**Recommended Supervision Focus:** Diagnostic assessment of personality disorders, treatment modifications for APD, behavioral accountability strategies, appropriate use of self-disclosure, systematic CBT technique application, and reassessment of treatment modality appropriateness.
