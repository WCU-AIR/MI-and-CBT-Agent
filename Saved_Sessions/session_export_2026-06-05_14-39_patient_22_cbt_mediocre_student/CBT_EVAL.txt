## SUPERVISOR NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Immediate Priority (Before Next Session):
1. **Conduct suicide risk assessment and safety planning** – This is non-negotiable and urgent.
2. **Seek immediate supervision from BDD/OCD specialist** – Current approach is contraindicated.
3. **Complete training in Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP)** – This is the evidence-based treatment for BDD.
4. **Reconceptualize case as BDD with delusional beliefs** – Not standard anxiety requiring reassurance.

### Key Clinical Errors to Address:
- Reassurance-based interventions reinforce compulsive cycles and strengthen delusional beliefs
- Delusional beliefs require behavioral experiments and evidence-gathering, not reassurance
- ERP (not cognitive restructuring alone) is gold-standard for BDD
- Suicidal ideation requires immediate safety assessment and planning
- Compulsive behaviors (checking, picking, muscle-building) are central to disorder and must be targeted directly
