## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Session Competency Summary:**
This session demonstrates solid foundational CBT skills with particular strengths in collaboration, guided discovery, feedback elicitation, and interpersonal effectiveness. The therapist skillfully guided the patient through cognitive restructuring and developed specific, feasible coping strategies. Therapeutic alliance is strong.

**Critical Safety Concern:**
The most significant deficiency is the **absence of medical integration**. The patient presents with moderate-to-severe alcohol withdrawal (tremor, diaphoresis, tachycardia, elevated BP, reduced concentration). Psychological coping strategies cannot substitute for pharmacological management. **Immediate action required:** Confirm patient is receiving appropriate medical management (benzodiazepines, thiamine, magnesium, medical monitoring) before next session. If not, refer to medical staff or emergency department.

**Key Areas for Development:**
1. Integrate medical management into all treatment planning for substance use disorders
2. Develop comprehensive relapse prevention frameworks
3. Provide written homework materials and tracking mechanisms
4. Address secondary cognitions (shame, guilt) and contextual factors (marital relationships)
5. Expand range of CBT techniques (thought records, behavioral scheduling, urge surfing, problem-solving)
6. Discuss medication and peer support options for alcohol use disorder

**Recommendation:**
Schedule supervision focused on: (1) medical integration in addiction treatment; (2) comprehensive relapse prevention planning; (3) treatment of co-occurring shame/guilt in addiction; (4) couples/marital factors in alcohol use disorder treatment.
