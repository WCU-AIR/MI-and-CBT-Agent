## Overall Session Score: 1.4/5

---

### Summary Assessment

**Fundamental Issue:** This session represents a **departure from Motivational Interviewing principles and spirit**. The psychiatrist operates from an authoritarian, expert-driven stance antithetical to MI. Rather than evoking Wiremu's intrinsic motivation, the clinician imposes external motivation through prescription, threat, and coercion.

**Key Concerns:**
- Wiremu's powerful intrinsic motivation ("I'll do anything to get my kids back") is undermined rather than built upon
- Compliance appears based on external pressure and fear, not genuine intrinsic motivation
- Externally motivated change is fragile and likely to collapse when external pressure is removed
- Dismissal of difficulty with urges as "willpower" problem is potentially harmful, communicating shame and decreasing self-efficacy
- Risk factors for relapse are increased through this approach

**Critical Recommendation:** Psychiatrist must fundamentally shift approach from prescription to evocation, from threat to intrinsic motivation, from expert role to partnership, from judgment to acceptance. Formal training in Motivational Interviewing principles and spirit is essential for sustainable therapeutic outcomes with this client.
