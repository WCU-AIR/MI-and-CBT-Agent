## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Strengths
- Warm, non-hostile demeanor
- Willingness to listen and allow patient adequate space to express concerns
- Genuine attempts to be encouraging and supportive
- Friendly, consistent tone throughout session

### Critical Deficiencies

**1. Failure to Address Suicidal Ideation (URGENT)**
- Patient expressed passive suicidal ideation: "It should have been me"
- Therapist never directly assessed, explored, or developed safety plan
- Represents clinical and ethical failure in high-risk patient

**2. Absence of CBT Structure and Technique**
- No agenda, no guided discovery, no cognitive restructuring, no behavioral activation
- No thought records, sleep protocols, functional analysis, or homework
- Session was generic supportive counseling, not evidence-based CBT

**3. Misunderstanding of Depression Severity**
- Therapist treated moderate MDD with suicidal ideation as normal stress
- Equated patient's condition with therapist's exam stress
- Suggested "thinking positive" despite patient repeatedly communicating ineffectiveness

**4. Inappropriate Self-Disclosure**
- Therapist repeatedly centered own experience rather than focusing on patient
- Created false equivalence between therapist's stress and patient's MDD
- Undermined therapeutic alliance and patient confidence in therapist expertise

**5. Lack of Collaboration**
- Therapist operated in expert-advice mode
- Patient positioned as passive recipient rather than active partner
- No shared decision-making or collaborative conceptualization

**6. Repetitive, Non-Progressive Interventions**
- Therapist suggested "think positive" at least 4 times
- Each time patient communicated it wasn't working
- Therapist did not adjust or pivot to different approach

**7. Failure to Focus on Key Maintaining Factors**
- Did not systematically address guilt rumination, anhedonia, alcohol misuse, or insomnia
- Focused on peripheral wellness advice instead of central symptoms
- No exploration of thoughts and behaviors driving depression

**8. No Treatment Strategy or Conceptualization**
- Therapist had no apparent understanding of what causes this patient's depression
- No logical plan for how interventions connect to symptom improvement
- Patient left without understanding what treatment entails

### Recommendations for Immediate Implementation
1. Directly assess suicidal ideation and develop safety plan in next session
2. Establish structured agenda and collaborative treatment plan
3. Seek regular supervision from CBT-trained supervisor
4. Enroll in formal CBT training program (ABCT certification or equivalent)
5. Apply specific CBT techniques: cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, thought records, sleep protocol
6. Assign specific, rationale-based homework with patient commitment
7. Eliminate inappropriate self-disclosure; focus entirely on patient's experience
8. Use Socratic questioning instead of direct advice
9. Develop coherent treatment conceptualization and share with patient
