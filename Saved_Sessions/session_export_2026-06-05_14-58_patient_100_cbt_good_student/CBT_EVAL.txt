## SUPERVISOR NOTES FOR CLINICAL REVIEW

**Strengths to Reinforce:**
- Exemplary collaborative approach and guided discovery—continue modeling this with other patients
- Strong therapeutic relationship and de-shaming language—particularly effective for shame-based presentations
- Systematic feedback elicitation and responsive adjustment—excellent practice
- Specific, measurable homework with clear rationale—gold-standard practice

**Areas for Focused Development:**
- **Priority 1:** Develop skill in identifying and prioritizing multiple cognitions within a session. Practice asking: "Of these thoughts you've mentioned, which one causes you the most distress? Which one do you think we should focus on first?" Ensure all core maintaining factors are addressed rather than focusing narrowly on one thought.
- **Priority 2:** Conduct comprehensive trigger and functional analysis before developing interventions. Practice systematic questioning about antecedents, behaviors, and consequences across multiple situations.
- **Priority 3:** Introduce behavioral experiments and exposure-based techniques to complement cognitive restructuring. Study exposure-based approaches for body-focused repetitive behaviors.
- **Priority 4:** Develop explicit relapse prevention strategies given patient's history of relapse. Study relapse prevention models for trichotillomania.

**Recommended Supervision Focus:**
- Review session recording with focus on moment when second cognition ("Everyone must see how bad it looks") was mentioned. Discuss why it was not pursued and how to prioritize multiple cognitions.
- Role-play comprehensive trigger analysis and functional analysis.
- Study and practice behavioral experiments for testing hopelessness thoughts.
- Develop comprehensive treatment plan for this patient addressing all identified gaps.
