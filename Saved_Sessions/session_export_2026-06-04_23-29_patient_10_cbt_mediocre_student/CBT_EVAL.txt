## CLINICAL SUMMARY FOR SUPERVISOR REVIEW

### Critical Safety Concern
**URGENT:** Patient stated, "Sometimes I even think maybe I should just quit," which suggests passive suicidal ideation or hopelessness. Therapist did not explore, assess, or address this statement. Immediate suicide risk assessment required in next session using structured tool (e.g., C-SSRS). Document findings clearly. Consult with psychiatrist regarding safety planning if indicated.

### Key Deficiencies Requiring Immediate Attention
1. **Agenda-setting:** Establish collaborative, structured agenda with problem prioritization and time allocation
2. **Feedback elicitation:** Routinely check understanding and elicit reactions throughout sessions
3. **Case conceptualization:** Develop coherent formulation integrating ADHD, SLD, depression, panic, and psychosocial stressors
4. **Guided discovery:** Replace advice-giving with Socratic questioning
5. **Structured interventions:** Move from generic suggestions to specific, measurable, evidence-based techniques
6. **Homework:** Assign specific, written, monitored assignments with clear rationale and commitment-building

- Non-judgmental, validating tone
- Patient engagement and rapport
- Recognition of multiple problem domains

### Recommended Supervision Focus
- CBT case formulation and treatment planning
- Safety assessment and risk management
- Guided discovery and Socratic questioning

- Homework assignment and monitoring
- Cultural competence in working with diverse clients (Hispanic/Latino, LGBTQ+, first-generation college students)
