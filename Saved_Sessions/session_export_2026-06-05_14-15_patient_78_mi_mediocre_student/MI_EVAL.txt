## Summary of Clinical Evaluation

### Key Strengths
- Maintains consistent focus on target behavior (weight loss and snacking)
- Offers practical, evidence-based suggestions (food tracking, snack substitution)
- Demonstrates basic warmth and non-judgmental tone
- Briefly acknowledges Liyana's efforts ("working out a lot," "trying to eat better")
- Attempts to normalize change process ("change takes time")

### Critical Deficiencies
- **Fundamental MI spirit violation:** Expert-driven, directive approach rather than collaborative and evocative
- **Minimal empathy and validation:** Liyana's emotional distress (hopelessness, shame, isolation, fatigue) is largely unaddressed
- **Poor sustain talk management:** Substantial hopelessness and low confidence are overridden rather than skillfully explored
- **Lack of evocation:** Advice-giving replaces drawing out client's own motivation, ideas, and resources
- **Missed emotional content:** Liyana's mention of feeling alone at home is not explored—significant missed opportunity
- **Low confidence unaddressed:** Liyana rates confidence at 4/10; psychiatrist does not explore or strengthen this
- **Compliance vs. commitment:** Liyana's agreement to food tracking appears to be compliance rather than autonomous choice

### Impact on Client Engagement
This session is likely to undermine Liyana's intrinsic motivation and increase disengagement risk. Her agreement to track food appears reluctant ("I guess I could try, but I'm still not sure it'll change anything"). The psychiatrist's directive stance and failure to validate emotional experience may reinforce hopelessness and shame. Without addressing emotional foundations (isolation, hopelessness, shame), behavioral interventions are unlikely to be sustained. Liyana will likely abandon the task within days.

### Primary Recommendation
Fundamentally shift from expert-driven to collaborative, partnership-based stance. Develop genuine empathy for emotional experience. Use evocative questions rather than leading questions. Amplify and deepen Liyana's change talk. Explore hopelessness and low confidence with curiosity rather than overriding them. Address isolation and lack of support as potential foundational barriers. Collaborate on problem-solving rather than prescribe solutions. With focused practice on core MI competencies, this clinician can develop skills necessary for effective motivational interviewing.
