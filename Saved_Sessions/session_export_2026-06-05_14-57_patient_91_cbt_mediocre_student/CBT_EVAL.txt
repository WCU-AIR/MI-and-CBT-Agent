### Highest Priority Issues:

1. **Complete Neglect of Hypersomnolence Disorder (Most Severe)**
   - Patient mentioned excessive sleep, multiple naps, sleep inertia, and daytime sleepiness in every session
   - Therapist never explored, assessed, or addressed these symptoms
   - No medical evaluation, sleep study review, or sleep-related interventions proposed
   - Represents fundamental failure in comprehensive assessment and treatment planning

2. **Absent Agenda-Setting and Session Structure**
   - Identical vague opening repeated three times without modification
   - No prioritization between two major diagnoses
   - No collaborative goal-setting or time management
   - Violates foundational CBT principles

3. **No Homework Review or Follow-Up**
   - By session three, previous homework not reviewed
   - Patient reported "no progress" and "discouragement" without therapist inquiry
   - Critical failure in CBT protocol and missed opportunity to understand treatment barriers

4. **Failure to Respond to Treatment Non-Response**
   - Patient explicitly reported lack of progress by session three
   - Therapist did not adjust treatment plan or conduct reassessment
   - Repeated same vague opening without acknowledgment of patient's struggle

### Immediate Actions Required:

- Conduct comprehensive assessment of both diagnoses
- Establish structured, collaborative agenda-setting
- Review and address hypersomnolence systematically
- Implement homework review protocol in all future sessions
- Provide systematic, complete application of CBT techniques
- Respond to treatment non-response with reassessment and plan adjustment
- Seek clinical supervision and additional CBT training
