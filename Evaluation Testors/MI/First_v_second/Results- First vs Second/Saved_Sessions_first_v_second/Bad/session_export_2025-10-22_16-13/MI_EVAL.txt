# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts a harsh, judgmental tone from the outset, repeatedly dismissing the patient’s concerns as “making excuses.” There is no evidence of respect or warmth, undermining rapport and trust.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
No reflective listening or validation of the patient’s feelings is present. The psychiatrist ignores the patient’s ambivalence and emotional experience, instead confronting and invalidating his perspective.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist repeatedly imposes directives and refuses to collaborate or support autonomy, contradicting core MI principles of evocation and partnership.

### Partnership (5-point Likert)

- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The session is dominated by an expert-driven, authoritarian approach with no negotiation or shared decision-making. The patient’s suggestions are dismissed outright.
