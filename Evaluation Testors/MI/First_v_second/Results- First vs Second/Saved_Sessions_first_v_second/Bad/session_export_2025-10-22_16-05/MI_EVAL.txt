# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist’s tone is predominantly judgmental and directive, frequently dismissing the patient’s fears and ambivalence. Commands like “just do it” and “stop making excuses” undermine acceptance and create a non-supportive environment.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal reflective listening or emotional attunement is present. The psychiatrist ignores or dismisses the patient’s expressions of fear, overwhelm, and vulnerability, missing opportunities to validate and empathize.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is authoritarian and directive, lacking collaboration or evocation. The psychiatrist pressures the patient to comply, dismissing her autonomy and failing to support choice.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist assumes an expert role, issuing commands and overriding the patient’s input. There is no evidence of shared decision-making or genuine partnership.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language

- [ ] 3  
- [x] 2  
- [ ] 1 (none)
