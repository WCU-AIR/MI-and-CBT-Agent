# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts a judgmental and authoritarian tone from the outset, repeatedly blaming the patient and dismissing their perspective. There is no evidence of warmth or unconditional positive regard, which undermines rapport and acceptance.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal effort to understand or reflect the patient’s feelings or ambivalence. The psychiatrist ignores patient cues and responds with blunt commands and rhetorical questions, showing no reflective listening or validation.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is dominated by an authoritarian, directive style with ultimatums and demands. There is no collaboration, evocation of patient motivation, or support for autonomy.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist controls the session entirely, issuing commands without inviting patient input or shared decision-making. The power differential is maximized rather than minimized.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
No effective evocation or reinforcement of change talk. Patient tentative statements about trying or considering change are ignored or dismissed.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [x] 1 (reinforces status quo) → 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is met with confrontation and pressure, escalating resistance rather than softening it. No use of double-sided reflections or normalization.

### Focusing (5-point Likert)
