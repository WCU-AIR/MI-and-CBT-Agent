# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist repeatedly dismisses the patient’s concerns and labels his reasons as “excuses.” There is no evidence of respect or validation of the patient’s perspective, resulting in a confrontational and critical tone throughout.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal to no empathic responses or reflective listening were observed. Patient’s feelings of being busy and overwhelmed were ignored or challenged rather than understood or validated.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts an authoritarian, directive style with repeated commands and ultimatums, showing no collaboration or support for patient autonomy.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist controls the agenda and imposes a plan without patient input, explicitly stating “I’m setting this plan for you—no questions asked,” demonstrating a lack of partnership.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk was minimal and tentative; the psychiatrist failed to evoke or reinforce it, instead overriding patient statements with commands and ultimatums.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [x] 1 (reinforces status quo) → 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk was met with confrontation and labeling as “excuses,” reinforcing resistance rather than softening or redirecting it.

---

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Uses open questions  
- [ ] Reflects more than asks  
- [ ] Avoids roadblocks  
**Comments:**  
Engagement was poor due to confrontational style. Questions were mostly closed or leading, and no reflective listening was evident.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
Criteria:  
- [x] Confirms agreed goal  
- [ ] Redirects drifting conversation  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintained focus on smoking cessation and exercise but did so rigidly and without collaboration.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Explores values, hopes, past successes  
- [ ] Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
No evocation of patient’s own motivations or reasons for change; the psychiatrist imposed reasons and plans.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Strengthens commitment language  
- [ ] Elicits concrete steps  
**Comments:**  
Planning was imposed without patient input or collaboration; no joint development or consolidation of commitment occurred.

---
