# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts a harsh, directive, and judgmental tone throughout the session, repeatedly labeling the patient’s statements as “excuses” and dismissing their perspective. There is no evidence of unconditional positive regard or respect for the patient’s autonomy.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
No reflective listening or empathy is demonstrated. The psychiatrist ignores the patient’s expressed feelings and concerns, instead confronting and pressuring the patient without validation or understanding.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist consistently uses an authoritarian, expert-driven approach, denying the patient’s autonomy and insisting on immediate compliance without collaboration or evocation of the patient’s own motivations.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
There is no partnership; the psychiatrist dominates the conversation with commands and directives, ignoring the patient’s input and lived experience.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist does not evoke or reinforce any change talk. Patient’s minimal spontaneous change talk is dismissed or ignored.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance

### Engaging (5-point Likert)

**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist uses mostly closed, leading questions and issues commands rather than engaging the patient. No reflective listening is evident, and the confrontational style undermines rapport.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)

- [x] Confirms agreed goal

**Comments:**

### Evoking (5-point Likert)

**Comments:**
