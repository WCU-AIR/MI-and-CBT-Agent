# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist consistently adopts a judgmental and disapproving tone, repeatedly blaming the patient for “making excuses” and “feeling sorry for yourself.” There is no evidence of unconditional positive regard or warmth.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: little effort to understand  
Comments:  
There is a clear lack of reflective listening or attempts to understand the patient’s perspective. The patient’s expressions of frustration and resignation are dismissed or ignored rather than validated.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: authoritarian stance  
Comments:  
The session is dominated by an authoritarian, directive style with no collaboration or evocation. The psychiatrist insists on compliance and uses threatening language, showing no support for autonomy.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: expert-driven  
Comments:  
No partnership is established; the psychiatrist commands and pressures the patient without inviting input or shared decision-making, maintaining a strong power differential.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
□ 1 (none)  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist does not evoke or reinforce any change talk. Patient’s tentative statements about quitting are ignored or met with skepticism and pressure.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
□ 1 (reinforces status quo)  
Comments:  
Sustain talk is confronted harshly and labeled as “excuses” or “whatever attitude,” increasing resistance rather than softening or reframing it.
