### Empathy (5-point Likert)

- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand

Minimal effort to understand or reflect the patient’s feelings and barriers. The psychiatrist dismisses emotional content and responds with commands rather than empathy or validation.
