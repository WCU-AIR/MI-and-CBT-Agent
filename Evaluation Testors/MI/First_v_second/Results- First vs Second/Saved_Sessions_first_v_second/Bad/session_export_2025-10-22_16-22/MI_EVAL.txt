# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☐ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
☑ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist is consistently judgmental and disapproving, using language that blames the patient and dismisses their struggles (e.g., “Hard is no excuse,” “That’s exactly the kind of thinking that’s holding you back”). There is no evidence of unconditional positive regard.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☐ High: active reflective listening  
☑ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
No reflective listening or empathy is demonstrated. The psychiatrist ignores or dismisses the patient’s expressions of difficulty and ambivalence, failing to validate or explore the patient’s feelings.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☐ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
☑ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is highly authoritarian and directive, with commands and warnings dominating. There is no collaboration or support for patient autonomy; the psychiatrist assumes an expert-driven role.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☐ High: minimizes power differential  
☑ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist dominates the interaction, issuing directives without inviting patient input or shared decision-making. The power differential is unmitigated.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 1 (none) → ☐ 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is minimal and tentative from the patient and is neither skillfully evoked nor reinforced by the psychiatrist. The psychiatrist pushes for compliance rather than exploring motivations.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
☑ 1 (reinforces status quo) → ☐ 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is met with confrontation and admonishment rather than softening or strategic reflection. The psychiatrist invalidates the patient’s ambivalence, increasing resistance.
