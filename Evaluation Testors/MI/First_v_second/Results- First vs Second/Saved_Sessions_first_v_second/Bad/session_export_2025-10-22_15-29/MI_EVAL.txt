## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
□ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
☑ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist repeatedly dismisses the patient’s perspective, labeling her reasons as “excuses” and “rationalizations.” There is no warmth or support; instead, the approach is harsh and critical, increasing defensiveness.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
□ High: active reflective listening  
☑ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
No reflective listening or validation is demonstrated. The psychiatrist ignores the patient’s expressions of stress and ambivalence, failing to convey understanding or emotional attunement.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
□ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
☑ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts a controlling, directive style, issuing ultimatums and ignoring patient autonomy. There is no collaboration or evocation of the patient’s own motivations.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
□ High: minimizes power differential  
☑ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist dominates the interaction, issuing commands without inviting patient input or sharing power. The dynamic is coercive rather than collaborative.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance
