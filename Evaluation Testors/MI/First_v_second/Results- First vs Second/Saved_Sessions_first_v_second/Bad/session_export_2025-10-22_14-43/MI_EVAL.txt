## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)

- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
No reflective listening or validation of the patient’s feelings is present. The psychiatrist ignores the patient’s ambivalence and responds with confrontation and dismissal.

### Spirit (5-point Likert)

- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is dominated by coercion and directive commands. The psychiatrist disregards autonomy and collaboration, imposing change rather than evoking it.
