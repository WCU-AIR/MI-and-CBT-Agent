# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☐ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
☑ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist’s approach is predominantly judgmental and directive, using admonishing language such as “You have to take this seriously” and “There’s no excuse for not stopping now.” There is no validation or respect for the patient’s perspective, resulting in defensiveness.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☐ High: active reflective listening  
☑ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
No reflective listening or paraphrasing is evident. The psychiatrist ignores the patient’s feelings and rationalizations, repeatedly interrupting with warnings and commands, which fails to convey understanding or empathy.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☐ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
☑ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is highly authoritarian and directive. The psychiatrist issues commands and challenges the patient confrontationally, with no support for autonomy or collaboration, contrary to MI spirit.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☐ High: minimizes power differential  
☑ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist dominates the conversation, repeatedly telling the patient what to do without inviting input or shared decision-making. The patient is excluded from collaborative dialogue.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 1 (none) → ☐ 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is not evoked or reinforced. The psychiatrist confronts sustain talk and asks questions that provoke defensiveness rather than eliciting the patient’s own motivations for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)
