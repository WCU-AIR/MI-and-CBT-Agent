# Summary

This evaluation reveals a fundamental lack of adherence to motivational interviewing principles. The psychiatrist’s style is highly directive, confrontational, and judgmental, with no evidence of empathy, acceptance, or collaboration. The psychiatrist fails to evoke or reinforce change talk and does not engage in reflective listening or affirmations. To improve, the psychiatrist must adopt a respectful, empathic, and collaborative approach that supports autonomy and skillfully elicits the patient’s own motivations for change.
