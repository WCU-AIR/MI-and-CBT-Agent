# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist’s approach is predominantly judgmental and directive, repeatedly pressuring the patient to quit smoking without acknowledging or validating her fears and ambivalence. There is minimal warmth or acceptance, which likely undermines rapport and engagement.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal effort is made to understand or reflect the patient’s perspective. The psychiatrist does not explore or validate the patient’s fears and struggles, responding instead with commands and challenges that show little emotional attunement.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is highly authoritarian and directive, lacking collaboration or support for autonomy. The psychiatrist repeatedly demands compliance and immediate action, ignoring the MI principles of partnership and evocation.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
There is no evidence of partnership; the psychiatrist dominates the interaction with commands and pressure, positioning the patient as passive and noncompliant rather than an active participant.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk expressed by the patient is not evoked or reinforced. The psychiatrist repeatedly challenges the patient without exploring or amplifying her tentative desire or willingness to change.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [x] 1 (reinforces status quo) → 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is met with confrontation and dismissal rather than softening or reframing. The psychiatrist’s responses likely increase resistance instead of reducing it.

### Engaging (5-point Likert)

**Comments:**

### Focusing (5-point Likert)

### Planning (5-point Likert)
