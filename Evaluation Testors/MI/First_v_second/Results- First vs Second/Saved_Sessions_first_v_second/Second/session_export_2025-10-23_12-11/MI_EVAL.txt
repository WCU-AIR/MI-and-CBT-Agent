# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist’s approach is largely judgmental and directive, repeatedly dismissing the patient’s perspective and resistance. There is minimal warmth or validation, contributing to patient defensiveness.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal effort to understand or reflect the patient’s feelings and ambivalence. The psychiatrist ignores cues of frustration and does not validate the patient’s stress or challenges.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is dominated by an authoritarian, directive style with frequent commands and pressure. There is no evocation of the patient’s own motivations or support for autonomy.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist imposes a rigid agenda, minimizing patient input and autonomy. The interaction lacks collaboration or shared decision-making.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is minimal and subtle; the psychiatrist fails to reflect or explore it, instead focusing on confrontation and demands.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [x] 1 (reinforces status quo) → 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk and resistance are met with confrontation and insistence, reinforcing defensiveness rather than softening or redirecting it.

**Comments:**

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)
