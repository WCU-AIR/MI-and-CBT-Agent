# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts a harsh, directive style with repeated commands and ultimatums, showing no warmth or respect. Patient’s ambivalence and concerns are dismissed, undermining acceptance.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal to no reflective listening or validation of patient feelings. Patient’s fears about social rejection and ambivalence are ignored; responses are confrontational rather than empathetic.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist uses an expert-driven, authoritarian approach with ultimatums and pressure, denying patient autonomy and collaboration. No evocation of patient’s own motivations.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Communication is one-directional and controlling. Patient input is overridden; no shared decision-making or negotiation evident.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language

- [x] 2 (minimal, inconsistent)  
- [ ] 3  
- [ ] 4  
- [ ] 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Patient expresses some change talk spontaneously, but psychiatrist does not reinforce or amplify it. Focus remains on commands rather than eliciting motivation.

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Uses open questions  
- [ ] Reflects more than asks  
- [ ] Avoids roadblocks  
**Comments:**  
Engagement is poor; patient appears uncomfortable and defensive. Questions are mostly closed and leading; no reflective listening or affirmations.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
Criteria:  
- [x] Confirms agreed goal  
- [ ] Redirects drifting conversation  
**Comments:**  
Focus remains on alcohol use and its impact on soccer and scholarships, but in a rigid, directive manner without patient collaboration.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Explores values, hopes, past successes  
- [ ] Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
No skillful evocation of patient’s motivations or values. Patient’s change talk is not explored or amplified.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Strengthens commitment language  
- [ ] Elicits concrete steps  
**Comments:**  
Planning is imposed rather than collaboratively developed. No negotiation or patient input; plan is dictated with ultimatums.
