# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist repeatedly uses confrontational and judgmental language, labeling patient resistance as “excuses” and insisting on immediate quitting without respect for the patient’s perspective. There is no warmth or support evident.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
No reflective listening or validation of the patient’s feelings or ambivalence. The psychiatrist ignores subtle patient cues about complexity and difficulty, missing opportunities to demonstrate understanding.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The interaction is highly directive and controlling, with repeated commands and no invitation for patient input or autonomy. The spirit of MI is absent.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist dominates the conversation, repeatedly asking “why haven’t you done that?” and dictating what the patient must do, without collaboration or shared decision-making.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → [ ] 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk from the patient is minimal and superficial; the psychiatrist does not respond to or reinforce these statements, instead continuing with directives and warnings.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [x] 1 (reinforces status quo) → [ ] 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is met with confrontation and dismissal, labeled as “excuses,” which likely increases patient resistance rather than softening it.

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Uses open questions  
- [ ] Reflects more than asks  
- [x] Avoids roadblocks (not met)  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist asks mostly closed, leading questions and fails to reflect or explore patient responses. The confrontational style likely hinders engagement.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Confirms agreed goal  
- [x] Redirects drifting conversation (rigidly)  
**Comments:**  
Focus is maintained on smoking cessation but in a rigid, directive manner without patient agreement or collaboration.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change

- [ ] Explores values, hopes, past successes  
- [ ] Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
No effective evocation of patient motivation; the psychiatrist does not explore or affirm any reasons for change expressed by the patient.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps

- [ ] Strengthens commitment language  
- [ ] Elicits concrete steps  
**Comments:**  
No collaborative planning occurs; the psychiatrist demands immediate action without co-creating a plan or eliciting patient commitment.
