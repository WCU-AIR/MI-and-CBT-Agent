## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
□ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
☑ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist’s tone is frequently directive and confrontational, with statements like “You really need to quit smoking” and “You have to be strict about it,” showing little acceptance or respect for the patient’s perspective. There is minimal validation or warmth.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
□ High: active reflective listening  
☑ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal empathic responses or reflections are present. The psychiatrist does not acknowledge or explore the patient’s feelings or ambivalence, instead pushing a rigid agenda without demonstrating understanding.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
□ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
☑ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is dominated by an authoritarian, prescriptive style. Collaboration, evocation, and autonomy support are absent. The psychiatrist repeatedly pressures the patient to comply with strict rules.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
□ High: minimizes power differential  
☑ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist does not establish a collaborative relationship. The interaction is expert-driven, with commands and directives overriding the patient’s autonomy and input.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)
