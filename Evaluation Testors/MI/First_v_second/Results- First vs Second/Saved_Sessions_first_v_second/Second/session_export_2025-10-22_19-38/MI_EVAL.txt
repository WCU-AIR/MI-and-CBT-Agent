## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist’s approach is harsh and judgmental throughout, repeatedly telling the patient to “stop making excuses” and “take this seriously,” which conveys disapproval rather than acceptance.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
There is minimal to no reflective listening or empathic statements. The psychiatrist dismisses the patient’s concerns and ambivalence, showing little understanding of their perspective.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is dominated by an authoritarian, directive style with no collaboration or evocation. The psychiatrist pressures the patient to quit immediately without supporting autonomy.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner

- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist controls the conversation, issues commands, and overrides patient input, showing no partnership or shared decision-making.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)
