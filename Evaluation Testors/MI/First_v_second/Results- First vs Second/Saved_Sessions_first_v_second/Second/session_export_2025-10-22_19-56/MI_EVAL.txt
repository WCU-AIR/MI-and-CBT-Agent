## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
□ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
☑ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist’s approach is predominantly judgmental and directive. Patient’s expressions of stress and ambivalence are met with dismissive language such as “stop making excuses” and “you have to,” showing little unconditional positive regard or respect for the patient’s perspective.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
□ High: active reflective listening  
☑ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal empathic listening is demonstrated. The psychiatrist does not reflect or validate the patient’s feelings or struggles, instead focusing on pushing directives. There are no reflective statements that show understanding of the patient’s emotional experience.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
□ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
☑ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is dominated by an authoritarian, directive style with repeated commands and little collaboration or evocation. The psychiatrist does not support autonomy or explore the patient’s own motivations, which is contrary to the MI spirit.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
□ High: minimizes power differential  
☑ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist assumes an expert role, telling the patient what to do without inviting collaboration or shared decision-making. The patient’s autonomy and expertise in her own life are minimized.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
☑ 1 (none) → □ 5 (consistent, deep evocation)

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)
