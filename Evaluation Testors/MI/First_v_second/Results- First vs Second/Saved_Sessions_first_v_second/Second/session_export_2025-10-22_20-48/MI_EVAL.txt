# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts a consistently judgmental and confrontational tone, repeatedly dismissing the patient’s perspective and labeling his responses as “excuses.” There is no evidence of respect or support, resulting in a harsh and unsupportive atmosphere.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal to no empathy is demonstrated. The psychiatrist fails to reflect or validate the patient’s feelings or social context, instead interrupting with directives and challenges that ignore the patient’s ambivalence and struggle.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is dominated by an authoritarian, directive style with no collaboration or evocation. The psychiatrist imposes an agenda and repeatedly commands compliance, disregarding the patient’s autonomy.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
There is no partnership; the psychiatrist controls the conversation, dismisses the patient’s input, and uses blaming questions. The power differential is stark and unmitigated.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is minimal and tentative from the patient and is ignored or overridden by the psychiatrist’s repeated commands and accusations. No strategic efforts are made to evoke or reinforce change talk.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [x] 1 (reinforces status quo) → 5 (consistently pivots to change)
