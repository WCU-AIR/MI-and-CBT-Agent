## Global Ratings (Counselor Behaviors)

### 1. Acceptance  
**Score: 2/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist’s tone throughout the session is largely judgmental and directive rather than accepting. Phrases such as “You really should consider removing it,” “You have to take this seriously,” and “You really need to stop making excuses” communicate disapproval and pressure rather than unconditional positive regard. The psychiatrist repeatedly challenges Marisol’s autonomy and choices without validating her perspective or feelings. There is no evidence of warmth, empathy, or normalization of Marisol’s stance. The patient’s resistance is met with confrontation rather than acceptance, which likely exacerbates defensiveness.  

**Summary:**  
The psychiatrist demonstrates limited acceptance, often adopting a critical and authoritative stance that undermines rapport and client openness.

---

### 2. Empathy  
**Score: 2/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist shows minimal effort to understand Marisol’s perspective beyond the surface level. **Summary:**  
Empathy is limited; the psychiatrist fails to demonstrate accurate understanding or convey that understanding to the patient.

**Score: 1/5**

**Summary:**
