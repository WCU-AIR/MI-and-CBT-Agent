## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts a judgmental and directive tone throughout, dismissing the patient’s feelings and concerns. There is no warmth or respect, and the patient’s vulnerability is met with impatience and reprimands.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal effort to understand or reflect the patient’s perspective. Emotional cues of fear and overwhelm are ignored; no reflective statements or validation are present.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist is highly directive and controlling, issuing ultimatums and demands without collaboration or respect for autonomy.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
No evidence of partnership; the psychiatrist dominates the conversation, overriding the patient’s input and not inviting collaboration.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)
