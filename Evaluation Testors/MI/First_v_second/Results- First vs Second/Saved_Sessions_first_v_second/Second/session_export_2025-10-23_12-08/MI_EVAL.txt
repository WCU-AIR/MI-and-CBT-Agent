## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
☐ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
☑ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist repeatedly invalidates Maria’s emotional experience with judgmental language such as “you really should quit thinking about suicide” and “stop feeling sorry for yourself.” There is no evidence of unconditional positive regard or warmth, creating a dismissive and critical atmosphere.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
☐ High: active reflective listening  
☑ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal empathic listening is demonstrated. The psychiatrist ignores Maria’s expressions of pain and hopelessness, responding instead with commands and criticisms, failing to reflect or validate her feelings.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
☐ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
☑ Low: authoritarian stance

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
☐ High: minimizes power differential  
☑ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**
