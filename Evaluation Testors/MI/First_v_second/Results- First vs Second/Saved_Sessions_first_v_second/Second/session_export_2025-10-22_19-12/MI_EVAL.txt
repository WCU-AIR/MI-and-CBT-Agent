# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist’s approach is predominantly judgmental and directive, repeatedly challenging the patient’s ambivalence and labeling their statements as “excuses.” There is no evidence of warmth or validation of the patient’s perspective.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal effort to understand or reflect the patient’s feelings or perspective. The psychiatrist ignores the patient’s expressed ambivalence and reluctance, failing to validate or explore these emotions.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is highly directive and controlling, with repeated commands to quit immediately and no support for patient autonomy or collaboration.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist dominates the interaction, issuing directives without invitation or negotiation, showing no evidence of shared decision-making.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)
