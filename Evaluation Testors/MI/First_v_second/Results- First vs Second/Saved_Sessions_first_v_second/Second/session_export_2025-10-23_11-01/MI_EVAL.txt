## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist repeatedly dismisses the patient’s perspective and labels his resistance as “excuses,” showing a lack of respect and acceptance. The approach is confrontational and disapproving, undermining rapport.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
No reflective listening or validation of the patient’s feelings is evident. The psychiatrist ignores emotional cues and relational concerns, resulting in a cold and mechanical interaction.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is highly directive and confrontational, with no collaboration or support for autonomy. The psychiatrist pushes a rigid agenda without inviting patient input.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner

- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist dominates the interaction, issuing commands and demands without negotiation or shared decision-making, ignoring the patient’s autonomy.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)
