## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist’s approach is predominantly judgmental and directive, frequently issuing commands and expressing disapproval. There is minimal evidence of unconditional positive regard or warmth, which undermines rapport and patient engagement.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: little effort to understand  
Comments:  
Limited reflective listening is present. The psychiatrist dismisses the patient’s expressions of exhaustion and distress with challenges rather than empathy, failing to validate or explore the patient’s emotional experience.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: authoritarian stance  
Comments:  
The session is highly authoritarian and directive, lacking collaboration, evocation, or support for autonomy. The psychiatrist imposes an agenda and uses confrontational language, opposing the core spirit of MI.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: expert-driven  
Comments:  
There is no evidence of a collaborative relationship. The psychiatrist dominates the interaction with commands and blame, positioning the patient as passive and responsible for failure.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
□ 2 (minimal evocation)  
Comments:

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
□ 1 (reinforces status quo)

Sustain talk is met with direct challenges and demands, which likely increases resistance. The psychiatrist does not use strategies to soften or pivot away from sustain talk.
