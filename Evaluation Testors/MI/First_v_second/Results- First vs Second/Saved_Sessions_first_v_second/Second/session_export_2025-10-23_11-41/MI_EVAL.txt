# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist is consistently judgmental and directive, dismissing the patient’s concerns and labeling her hesitations as “excuses.” There is no evidence of respect or unconditional positive regard.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal to no reflective listening or acknowledgment of the patient’s feelings. The psychiatrist interrupts and challenges rather than seeks to understand the patient’s ambivalence and emotional experience.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts an authoritarian, expert-driven style, repeatedly telling the patient what to do and dismissing her autonomy and perspective.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
No partnership is established; the psychiatrist dominates the conversation and ignores the patient’s input, overriding her attempts to express concerns.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist does not evoke or reinforce change talk. Patient’s spontaneous change talk is ignored or challenged, limiting motivation development.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [x] 1 (reinforces status quo) → 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is met with confrontation and accusations of “making excuses,” reinforcing resistance rather than softening it.

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Uses open questions  
- [ ] Reflects more than asks  
- [ ] Avoids roadblocks  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist uses mostly closed, leading questions and issues commands. There are no reflections and frequent roadblocks that damage rapport.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
Criteria:  
- [x] Confirms agreed goal  
- [ ] Redirects drifting conversation  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a narrow focus on diet and exercise but ignores the patient’s broader health concerns and values.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Explores values, hopes, past successes  
- [ ] Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
No efforts to evoke the patient’s motivations or affirm autonomy; instead, the psychiatrist pushes directives.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Strengthens commitment language  
- [ ] Elicits concrete steps  
**Comments:**  
No collaborative planning or elicitation of patient-driven next steps; planning is imposed rather than co-created.
