# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist demonstrates a predominantly judgmental and directive stance, frequently labeling the patient’s reasons as “excuses” and showing little genuine acceptance or respect for the patient’s experience. The approach lacks warmth and unconditional positive regard.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: little effort to understand  
Comments:  
There is minimal reflective listening or validation of the patient’s feelings. The psychiatrist dismisses the patient’s fears and social context, responding with challenges rather than empathy, which likely inhibits rapport.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: authoritarian stance  
Comments:  
The session lacks collaboration, evocation, and autonomy support. The psychiatrist imposes an agenda with ultimatums and demands, undermining patient autonomy and failing to evoke the patient’s own motivations.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: expert-driven  
Comments:  
The interaction is dominated by directives and ultimatums without shared decision-making. The psychiatrist positions themselves as the authority, minimizing the patient’s role as an equal partner.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
□ 2 (some change talk present but poorly cultivated)  
Comments:  
The patient expresses desire and tentative commitment to change, but the psychiatrist rarely reinforces or deepens these statements, missing opportunities to amplify motivation.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
□ 1 (reinforces status quo)  
Comments:  
Sustain talk is met with confrontation and dismissal, labeled as “excuses.” There are no attempts to normalize ambivalence or use reflective strategies to soften resistance.

---
