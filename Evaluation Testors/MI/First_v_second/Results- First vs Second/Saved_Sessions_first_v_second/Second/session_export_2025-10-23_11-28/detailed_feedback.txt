## Recommendations

1. **Adopt a Collaborative Stance:**  
   Shift from directive commands to partnership. Invite the patient’s perspective and preferences.

2. **Use Open-Ended Questions:**  
   Encourage elaboration and exploration rather than yes/no answers.

3. **Practice Reflective Listening:**  
   Reflect the patient’s statements and emotions to demonstrate understanding and build rapport.

4. **Affirm Patient Strengths and Efforts:**  
   Recognize and reinforce the patient’s willingness and past successes.

5. **Evoke Change Talk:**  
   Explore the patient’s own reasons, desires, and confidence for change rather than imposing directives.

6. **Soften Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections and validate ambivalence to reduce resistance.

7. **Support Autonomy:**  
   Emphasize the patient’s right to choose and control the pace of change.

8. **Collaborative Planning:**  
   Work together to develop realistic, manageable steps toward behavior change.

9. **Summarize Key Points:**  
   Use summaries to consolidate motivation and clarify next steps.
