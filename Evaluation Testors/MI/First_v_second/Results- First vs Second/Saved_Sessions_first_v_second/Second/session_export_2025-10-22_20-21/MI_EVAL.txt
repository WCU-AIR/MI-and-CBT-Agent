## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)

- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective

- [x] Low: little effort to understand

### Spirit (5-point Likert)
