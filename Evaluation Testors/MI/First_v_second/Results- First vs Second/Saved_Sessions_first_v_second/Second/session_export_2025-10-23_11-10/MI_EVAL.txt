# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
□ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
☑ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist’s approach is predominantly judgmental and directive. Patient concerns and ambivalence are dismissed as “excuses,” and there is little warmth or validation. This limits patient comfort and openness.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
□ High: active reflective listening  
☑ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Limited reflective listening or acknowledgment of patient feelings. The psychiatrist responds with blunt commands and dismissive remarks, ignoring patient frustration and hesitation.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
□ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
☑ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is highly authoritarian and directive, lacking collaboration or support for patient autonomy. The psychiatrist insists on immediate quitting without exploring patient motivations or readiness.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
□ High: minimizes power differential  
☑ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains an expert-driven stance, repeatedly telling the patient what to do without genuine invitation to collaborate or negotiate goals.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
□ 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
☑ 2 (minimal cultivation)  
**Comments:**  
Patient expresses tentative change talk, but the psychiatrist does not skillfully evoke or reinforce it. Focus remains on commands and urgency, which may provoke resistance.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
□ 1 (reinforces status quo) → 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
☑ 1 (reinforces sustain talk)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is met with confrontation and dismissal. Patient concerns are labeled as “excuses,” increasing resistance rather than softening sustain talk.

### Engaging (5-point Likert)

☑ Low engagement due to confrontational style

### Focusing (5-point Likert)

Without these changes, the risk of patient resistance and disengagement remains high.
