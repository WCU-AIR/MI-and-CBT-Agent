## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist consistently adopts a judgmental and critical stance, repeatedly labeling the patient’s behavior as excuses or failures. There is no evidence of warmth or unconditional positive regard; patient feelings are dismissed throughout.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal to no empathy is demonstrated. The psychiatrist ignores or overrides the patient’s emotional expressions, failing to reflect or validate feelings of frustration and ambivalence.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is dominated by an authoritarian, expert-driven approach with repeated ultimatums and demands. There is no collaboration or support for patient autonomy.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)

- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist controls the conversation, dismisses patient input, and issues commands without inviting collaboration or shared decision-making.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)

### Focusing (5-point Likert)
