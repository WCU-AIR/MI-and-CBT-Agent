# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and respect for the patient’s concerns and feelings. Validates worries without judgment and honors autonomy throughout the session.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking are evident, capturing ambivalence and frustration well. Some reflections could be deeper to better acknowledge emotional impact such as guardedness and impatience.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
Maintains a collaborative, autonomy-supportive stance, inviting input and respecting control. Could more actively evoke intrinsic motivations and personal values to deepen engagement.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Excellent partnership demonstrated by inviting input, asking permission before sharing information, and emphasizing shared decision-making.
