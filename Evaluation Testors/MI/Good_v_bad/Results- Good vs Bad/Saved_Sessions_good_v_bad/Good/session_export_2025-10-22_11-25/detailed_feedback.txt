**Score: 4/5**

**Summary:**

### 4. Cultivating Change Talk  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist effectively elicits and reinforces change talk throughout the session. They reflect Derrick’s statements about wanting to avoid another DUI, setting aside money for a cab, and reaching out to a cousin for support. **Summary:**  
Frequent and effective evocation of change talk, but could more explicitly elicit commitment and confidence.

### 5. Softening Sustain Talk  
**Score: 3/5**

For example, the psychiatrist could have more explicitly normalized Derrick’s feelings or gently challenged minimizations by exploring consequences or discrepancies. The sustain talk is not reinforced, but the handling is somewhat passive and lacks skillful softening.

**Summary:**  
Sustain talk is acknowledged but not actively softened or redirected; more strategic handling needed.

### 6. Partnership

**Summary:**  
Strong collaboration and client-centeredness, but partnership could be deepened by explicit co-creation and power sharing.
