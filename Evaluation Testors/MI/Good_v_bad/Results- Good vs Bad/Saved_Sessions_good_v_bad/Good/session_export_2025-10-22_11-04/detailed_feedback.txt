### 3. Spirit of MI  
**Score: 5/5**  

**Explanation:**

### 4. Cultivating Change Talk  
**Score: 5/5**  

**Explanation:**

**Summary:**

### 5. Softening Sustain Talk  
**Score: 4/5**

For example, the patient’s worries about side effects could have been reframed alongside their motivation to quit to more explicitly soften resistance.

**Summary:**  
Sustain talk is handled respectfully and neutrally, but opportunities exist to more actively soften and redirect it toward change.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Enhance Softening of Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections and strategic reframing to gently reduce the impact of sustain talk, especially around medication side effects and stress triggers.

2. **Strengthen Planning:**  
   Collaboratively develop a detailed, concrete plan with the patient, including specific steps, timelines, and supports. Check for patient buy-in and confidence in the plan.

3. **Use Explicit Summaries:**  
   Provide clear, structured summaries that pull together key change talk and reinforce motivation before transitioning to planning or information sharing.

4. **Continue Affirming Strengths:**  
   Maintain personalized affirmations that highlight the patient’s past successes and resilience to build self-efficacy.

5. **Maintain Autonomy Support:**  
   Continue to seek permission before advice and emphasize the patient’s control over decisions and pace of change.
