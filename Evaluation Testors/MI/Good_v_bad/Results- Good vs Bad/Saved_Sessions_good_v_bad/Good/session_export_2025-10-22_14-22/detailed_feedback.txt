## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflections:**  
   Use more complex reflections that capture underlying emotions, ambivalence, and values. Avoid repetitive phrasing and tailor reflections to Marcus’s unique experience.

2. **Amplify Change Talk:**  
   When change talk emerges, reflect, reinforce, and strategically evoke more. Explicitly elicit commitment and confidence language to strengthen motivation.

3. **Skillfully Soften Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections and validate sustain talk while gently guiding toward change. Normalize ambivalence and acknowledge the difficulty of resisting peer pressure.

4. **Strengthen Affirmations:**  
   Offer specific, genuine affirmations recognizing Marcus’s strengths, past efforts, and values. Avoid generic praise.

5. **Enhance Planning and Summarizing:**  
   Explicitly summarize change talk and reinforce commitment to next steps. Collaboratively develop a concrete, actionable plan and check for client buy-in.

6. **Increase Evocation and Collaboration:**  
   More actively draw out Marcus’s own motivations, values, and ideas for change. Share power by explicitly inviting Marcus to co-create the agenda and plan.

7. **Seek Permission Before Advice:**  
   Before offering suggestions or information, ask permission to respect autonomy and enhance collaboration.

8. **Avoid Formulaic Responses:**  
   Personalize responses and avoid repetitive or scripted phrasing. Be present and responsive to the unique flow of the conversation.
