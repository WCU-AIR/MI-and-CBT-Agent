## Global Ratings (Counselor Behaviors)

### 1. Acceptance  
**Score: 5/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and respect toward Lucía throughout the session. From the outset, the psychiatrist acknowledges Lucía’s frustration without judgment (“feeling tired of the usual advice… sounds frustrating”) and validates her experience and perspective. The tone remains warm, supportive, and nonjudgmental, even when Lucía expresses resistance or sustain talk (“I’m just kinda tired of hearing the same thing”). The psychiatrist affirms Lucía’s autonomy and choices without pressure or disapproval. This level of acceptance is sustained and genuine, meeting the highest standard.

**Summary:**  
The psychiatrist excels in communicating acceptance, creating a safe and respectful environment that encourages openness.

---

### 2. Empathy  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing Lucía’s ambivalence and emotional state. For example, reflections such as “smoking has woven itself into the fabric of your daily life” and “there’s this uncertainty about what might take its place” show nuanced understanding. The psychiatrist also validates the complexity of Lucía’s feelings (“That complexity you’re describing… makes a lot of sense”). However, while reflections are accurate and empathetic, they tend to be somewhat surface-level and descriptive rather than deeply evocative of emotional nuance. There is room to deepen emotional attunement, for example by exploring Lucía’s frustration or resignation more explicitly.

**Summary:**  
Good empathy and reflective listening, but could be enhanced by deeper emotional exploration and more complex reflections.

---

### 3. Spirit of MI  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance, inviting Lucía’s input and emphasizing autonomy (“What would make this time feel more useful or different for you?”). The conversation avoids directive or authoritative language and supports Lucía’s control over the process. The psychiatrist evokes Lucía’s own motivations and values, such as when exploring her past resilience and connection to her daughter. However, the session occasionally feels somewhat structured and formulaic, lacking a fully fluid, evocative style that dynamically draws out Lucía’s intrinsic motivations. The psychiatrist could more explicitly reinforce Lucía’s agency and co-create the agenda or plan.

**Summary:**  
Strong collaboration and autonomy support, but the spirit could be more evocative and less formulaic.

---

### 4. Cultivating Change Talk  
**Score: 3/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist elicits some change talk, particularly when exploring Lucía’s readiness to change (e.g., “If you were to imagine a number from 0 to 10… where might you place yourself?”) and when discussing her past successes. Lucía offers subtle change talk (“common sense somewhere in there,” “Maybe I’d feel more stressed?”). However, the psychiatrist misses opportunities to amplify or deepen this change talk. For example, when Lucía expresses tentative willingness (“Maybe... I could try to notice when I’m lighting up just out of habit”), the psychiatrist reflects supportively but does not strategically reinforce or evoke further commitment or confidence language. The evocation of change talk is present but limited in frequency and depth.

**Summary:**  
Some evocation of change talk, but lacks consistent reinforcement and amplification to strengthen motivation.

---

### 5. Softening Sustain Talk  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist skillfully acknowledges sustain talk without confrontation or judgment. Lucía’s resistance (“I’m just kinda tired of hearing the same thing,” “Let’s just see what you’ve got”) is met with understanding and reflection rather than challenge. The psychiatrist uses reflective statements that validate ambivalence and normalize the difficulty of change (“That complexity you’re describing… makes a lot of sense”). However, while sustain talk is acknowledged and not reinforced, the psychiatrist could more actively use double-sided reflections or strategic reframing to gently shift sustain talk toward change.

**Summary:**  
Effective acknowledgment and neutralization of sustain talk, with room for more strategic softening techniques.

---

### 6. Partnership  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist consistently maintains a collaborative stance, inviting Lucía’s perspective and avoiding an expert-driven approach. Language such as “What do you imagine might be a small, realistic step you could take?” and “What do you think might help you remember…” demonstrates partnership. However, the collaboration is somewhat implicit rather than explicit; the psychiatrist does not overtly share power or explicitly negotiate the agenda or next steps with Lucía. More explicit co-creation of the plan and acknowledgment of Lucía’s expertise in her own life would strengthen partnership.

**Summary:**  
Strong collaboration and client-centeredness, but partnership could be deepened by explicit power-sharing and co-creation.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflections:**  
   Use more complex reflections that capture underlying emotions, ambivalence, and values, not just content.

2. **Amplify Change Talk:**  
   When change talk emerges, reflect it back with emphasis, ask for elaboration, and evoke commitment and confidence language.

3. **Strengthen Planning:**  
   Collaboratively develop a clear, concrete plan with explicit client agreement and follow-up steps.

4. **Enhance Partnership:**  
   Explicitly negotiate agenda and next steps with the client, emphasizing their expertise and control.

5. **Use Strategic Summaries:**  
   Provide explicit summaries that pull together change talk, reinforce motivation, and clarify plans.

6. **Softening Sustain Talk:**  
   Employ double-sided reflections and gentle reframing to acknowledge sustain talk while guiding toward change.

7. **Increase Emotional Attunement:**  
   Explore and reflect the client’s emotional experience more deeply to build connection and evoke motivation.
