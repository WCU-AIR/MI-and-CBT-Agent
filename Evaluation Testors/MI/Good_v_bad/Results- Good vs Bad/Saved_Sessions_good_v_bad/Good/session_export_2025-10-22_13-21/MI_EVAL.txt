# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and respect, validating the client’s ambivalence and social context without judgment. The warm, supportive tone fosters a safe environment for open exploration.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Good reflective listening and perspective-taking are evident, capturing both functional and emotional aspects of vaping. However, reflections tend to be somewhat surface-level and could deepen emotional nuance and underlying fears.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance, supports autonomy, and skillfully evokes client motivations. Some phrasing is formulaic, and partnership could be more personalized and dynamic to fully embody MI spirit.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Consistently invites client input and follows client lead, maintaining a collaborative tone. Partnership could be strengthened by more explicit co-creation of agenda and plans.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 4 (consistent, deep evocation)  
⬜ 1 (none) → ⬜ 5 (exceptional)  
**Comments:**  
Effectively elicits and reinforces multiple types of change talk (desire, ability, reasons, commitment). Links past successes to current change efforts. Could more frequently and explicitly evoke commitment and taking steps language earlier.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
⬜ 1 (reinforces status quo) → ☑ 3 (acknowledges but limited softening) → ⬜ 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is acknowledged and reflected neutrally but not actively softened or redirected. Use of double-sided reflections or gentle reframing to pivot toward change is limited.

---

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
☑ Uses open questions  
☑ Reflects more than asks  
☑ Avoids roadblocks  
**Comments:**  
Rapport is well established and maintained with warm, respectful tone. Open questions effectively invite elaboration and exploration.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
☑ Confirms agreed goal  
☑ Redirects drifting conversation  
**Comments:**  
Clear focus on vaping behavior related to basketball and stress management is maintained without being directive.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
☑ Explores values, hopes, past successes  
☑ Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is effectively evoked and reinforced, including linking to past successes. Could deepen evocation of commitment and taking steps language.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
☑ Strengthens commitment language  
☑ Elicits concrete steps  
**Comments:**  
Helps client develop a concrete plan with social support and goal setting. Plan could be more explicitly consolidated with clear commitment and contingency strategies.
