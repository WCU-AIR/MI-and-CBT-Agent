## Summary of Feedback

- Strong partnership and autonomy support empower Ethan to explore his own motivations.

### Areas for Improvement

---

## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflections:**  
   Use more complex reflections that capture underlying emotions, values, and ambivalence rather than just content. Avoid repetitive phrasing to maintain engagement.

2. **Amplify Change Talk:**  
   When change talk emerges, reflect it back with emphasis and explore its emotional significance. Link motivational statements explicitly to Ethan’s values and goals.

3. **Strategically Soften Sustain Talk:**  
   Employ double-sided reflections that acknowledge sustain talk while gently highlighting reasons for change. Use reframing to reduce resistance.

4. **Enhance Planning and Summarizing:**  
   Provide explicit summaries that consolidate change talk and reinforce commitment. Collaboratively develop a concrete, actionable plan with clear next steps.

5. **Increase Spontaneity and Personalization:**  
   Move beyond formulaic responses to tailor reflections and questions uniquely to Ethan’s experience and language.

6. **Explore Emotional Nuance:**  
   Delve deeper into Ethan’s feelings about peer pressure, social belonging, and fears related to change to evoke stronger motivation.
