### Areas for Improvement  
- Reflections are often surface-level; more complex, emotionally nuanced reflections are needed.

- Sustain talk is acknowledged but not skillfully softened or redirected using MI techniques like double-sided reflections.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflections:**  
   Use more complex reflections that capture underlying emotions, ambivalence, and values rather than just paraphrasing content.

2. **Amplify Change Talk:**  
   When change talk emerges, reflect and reinforce it explicitly. Ask evocative questions to deepen motivation and explore commitment and confidence.

3. **Skillfully Soften Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections and gentle reframing to acknowledge sustain talk while guiding toward change.

4. **Enhance Planning and Summarizing:**  
   Explicitly summarize key points, pull together change talk, and collaboratively develop concrete next steps or monitoring plans.

5. **Increase Evocation and Collaboration:**  
   More actively draw out client’s own reasons for change and explore ambivalence without pushing or directing.

6. **Avoid Formulaic Responses:**  
   Personalize responses to the client’s unique expressions and avoid repetitive or scripted phrasing.

7. **Deepen Emotional Attunement:**  
   Explore subtle emotional cues and ambivalence behind dismissive or resistant statements to build deeper rapport and engagement.
