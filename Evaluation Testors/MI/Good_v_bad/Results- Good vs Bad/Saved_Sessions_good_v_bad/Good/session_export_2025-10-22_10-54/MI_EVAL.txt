# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, creating a safe, nonjudgmental environment. The client’s ambivalence and concerns are met with warmth and respect throughout the session.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Reflections accurately capture the client’s ambivalence and emotional experience, acknowledging feelings of isolation and tension. However, reflections remain somewhat surface-level without deeply exploring underlying emotions or values.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance, inviting client input and supporting autonomy. The session avoids directive language and evokes client ideas, though deeper evocation of intrinsic motivation could enhance the MI spirit.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Strong partnership is evident with shared agenda setting and collaborative planning. The psychiatrist respects the client’s expertise about his own life and decisions.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 4 (consistent, moderate evocation)  
⬜ 1 (none) → ⬜ 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is effectively elicited and reinforced, including desire, ability, reasons, and tentative commitment. Some opportunities to deepen and amplify commitment language were missed.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
⬜ 1 (reinforces status quo) → ☑ 3 (acknowledges but limited pivoting) → ⬜ 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is acknowledged but not actively softened or redirected. The psychiatrist uses neutral reflections rather than strategic techniques to reduce resistance or resolve ambivalence.

---

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
☑ Uses open questions  
☑ Reflects more than asks  
☑ Avoids roadblocks  
**Comments:**  
Rapport is well established and maintained. The psychiatrist uses open questions and reflections effectively to encourage client sharing and exploration.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
☑ Confirms agreed goal  
☑ Redirects drifting conversation  
**Comments:**  
The session maintains clear focus on the client’s drinking behavior and ambivalence, consistently returning to relevant topics and exploring them in depth.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
☑ Explores values, hopes, past successes  
☑ Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist effectively evokes reasons for change and past successes, supporting autonomy. However, evocation could be deeper and more strategic, especially around intrinsic motivation and commitment.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
☑ Strengthens commitment language  
☑ Elicits concrete steps  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist guides the client toward developing a concrete plan involving drink limits and friend support. The plan is tentative and could be more fully consolidated with explicit commitment and follow-up.
