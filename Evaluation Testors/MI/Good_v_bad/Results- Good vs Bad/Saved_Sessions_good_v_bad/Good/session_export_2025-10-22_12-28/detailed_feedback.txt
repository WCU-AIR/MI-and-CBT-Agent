2. **Incorporate Explicit Summaries:**  
   Near session close, provide a structured summary that pulls together key change talk, ambivalence, and the agreed-upon plan to reinforce motivation and commitment.

3. **Continue Using Complex Reflections:**  
   Maintain the high level of emotional attunement and metaphorical reflections to deepen engagement.

4. **Maintain Strong Autonomy Support:**  
   Continue emphasizing patient choice and pace, especially when discussing next steps.
