## Global Ratings (Counselor Behaviors)

### 1. Acceptance  
**Score: 5/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and respect toward the patient throughout the session. From the outset, the psychiatrist invites the patient to set the agenda (“What would you like to focus on…?”), validating her priorities and concerns. The tone remains warm, supportive, and nonjudgmental, especially when acknowledging the patient’s struggles with stress and past failures (“That’s a lot to carry, and it makes sense…”). There is no evidence of labeling or judgment; instead, the psychiatrist normalizes the patient’s experience and validates her feelings. This level of acceptance is sustained consistently across the session.

**Summary:**  
The psychiatrist excels in creating a safe, respectful environment that fosters openness and trust.

---

### 2. Empathy  
**Score: 5/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist demonstrates highly accurate and nuanced understanding of the patient’s perspective. Complex reflections capture both the content and emotional undertones of the patient’s experience (e.g., “stress really pulls you into a place where your energy dips and comfort takes priority, almost like a protective pause”). The psychiatrist skillfully reflects ambivalence and emotional nuance, validating the patient’s doubts and hopes without minimizing either. The reflections are not merely surface-level paraphrases but show deep perspective-taking and emotional attunement. The patient’s thoughtful acknowledgments (“Yeah, that makes a lot of sense,” “I never thought about it that way”) indicate the reflections resonate well.

**Summary:**  
Empathy is demonstrated at a high level, with consistent, accurate, and emotionally rich reflections.

---

### 3. Spirit of MI  
**Score: 5/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist embodies the spirit of MI by maintaining a fully collaborative stance, evoking the patient’s own motivations, and supporting autonomy throughout. The session begins by inviting the patient to set the agenda, reinforcing partnership. The psychiatrist avoids directive or authoritarian language, instead offering information only with permission (“Would it be okay if I shared…?”). Autonomy is emphasized repeatedly (“It’s like you’re creating a kind of ‘low-pressure zone’ for change,” “It’s really insightful that you want to give it a go…”). The psychiatrist skillfully evokes the patient’s own reasons for change and confidence levels, and supports self-efficacy without pressure. The collaborative exploration of small, realistic steps and social support reflects a genuine partnership.

**Summary:**  
The session exemplifies strong MI spirit with consistent collaboration, evocation, and autonomy support.

---

### 4. Cultivating Change Talk  
**Score: 5/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist actively and skillfully cultivates change talk throughout the session. Open-ended questions invite the patient to articulate her desires, reasons, and ability to change (“What kind of small steps…?”, “What do you think makes those small steps feel more hopeful?”). The psychiatrist reflects and amplifies change talk effectively, reinforcing the patient’s emerging hope and realistic goals (“That sounds like a really thoughtful and compassionate approach…”). The scaling question about confidence is well-timed and followed by exploration of facilitators and barriers, deepening commitment language. The psychiatrist also elicits specific, actionable next steps and social support, reinforcing the patient’s agency and motivation.

**Summary:**  
Change talk is consistently and skillfully evoked, reflected, and reinforced, supporting movement toward change.

---

### 5. Softening Sustain Talk  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist acknowledges sustain talk and ambivalence without judgment and gently reframes it. For example, the patient’s doubts about sticking with changes under stress are validated (“It’s really insightful that you want to give it a go and see how it feels, even with those worries present”). However, while sustain talk is acknowledged and normalized, there is less evidence of explicit double-sided reflections or strategic redirection that might more actively soften sustain talk’s impact. The approach is mostly neutral to gently supportive rather than actively minimizing sustain talk.

**Summary:**  
Sustain talk is handled respectfully and without reinforcement, but could be softened more actively with strategic reflections.

---

### 6. Partnership  
**Score: 5/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a fully collaborative, client-centered partnership throughout. The patient is invited to set the agenda, and the psychiatrist consistently checks in for permission before sharing information. The language used emphasizes joint exploration and respects the patient’s expertise and autonomy (“I’d love to hear what you think about it,” “What might be one small, specific step you’d feel willing to try?”). The psychiatrist avoids expert-driven or directive approaches, instead co-creating the plan with the patient. This minimizes power differentials and fosters equality.

**Summary:**  
Partnership is exemplary, with clear evidence of shared power and mutual respect.

## Recommendations

1. **Enhance Softening of Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections that acknowledge sustain talk while gently steering toward change (e.g., “On one hand, you worry about sticking with it when stressed, and on the other, you’re hopeful that small steps can work.”).

2. **Increase Specific Affirmations:**  
   Offer more frequent and detailed affirmations that highlight the patient’s resilience, insight, and past successes to bolster confidence.

3. **Incorporate Explicit Summaries:**  
   End sessions with clear, structured summaries that integrate change talk, reinforce commitment, and clarify next steps to strengthen motivation.

4. **Maintain Current Strengths:**  
   Continue the excellent use of open questions, complex reflections, autonomy support, and collaborative planning.
