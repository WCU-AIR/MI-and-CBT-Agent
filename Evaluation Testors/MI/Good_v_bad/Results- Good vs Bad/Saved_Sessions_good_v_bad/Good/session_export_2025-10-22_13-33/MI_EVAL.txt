# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout the session, validating the patient’s perspective and avoiding criticism. However, the acceptance feels somewhat procedural and lacks deeper warmth or explicit unconditional positive regard.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
⬜ High: active reflective listening  
☑ Moderate: basic reflective listening with limited depth  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Reflections demonstrate understanding of patient content but remain surface-level, mostly paraphrasing without exploring emotional nuances or ambivalence. Empathy is present but inconsistent and lacks depth.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
⬜ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
☑ Moderate: polite collaboration with limited evocation  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist supports autonomy and invites input, maintaining a collaborative stance. However, evocation of patient motivations and values is minimal, and the session feels somewhat formulaic rather than deeply co-constructed.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Consistently invites patient permission and input, demonstrating respect for autonomy. Partnership is polite and collaborative but remains somewhat superficial without explicit agenda negotiation or deeper co-creation.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
⬜ 1 (none)  
⬜ 2 (minimal)  
☑ 3 (some attempts)  
⬜ 4 (consistent)  
⬜ 5 (deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Some questions about readiness and reasons for change are asked, but change talk remains weak and is not strategically reinforced or amplified. Missed opportunities to deepen motivation and highlight strengths.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
⬜ 1 (reinforces status quo)  
☑ 3 (acknowledges sustain talk neutrally)  
⬜ 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is acknowledged and validated without confrontation, but responses are mostly neutral reflections rather than active softening or reframing to gently guide toward change.

---

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
☑ Uses open questions  
☑ Reflects more than asks (moderate)  
☑ Avoids roadblocks  
**Comments:**  
Basic rapport is established with polite tone and open questions. Engagement is adequate but lacks emotional attunement or deeper personal connection.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
☑ Confirms agreed goal  
⬜ Redirects drifting conversation  
**Comments:**  
Focus is maintained on smoking and drinking but without clear guidance toward a specific change goal or agenda.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
⬜ Explores values, hopes, past successes (limited)  
☑ Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
Minimal evocation of motivation; some exploration of readiness and past successes but not actively drawn out or amplified.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
⬜ Strengthens commitment language (absent)  
⬜ Elicits concrete steps (absent)  
**Comments:**  
No concrete planning or commitment elicited; session ends without clear next steps.
