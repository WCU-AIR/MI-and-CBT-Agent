**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**

The evocation is present but shallow and inconsistent.

**Summary:**

### 5. Softening Sustain Talk  
**Score: 3/5**

For example, a reflection like “On one hand, you want to fit in, and on the other, you want to protect yourself” could have been used more consistently to soften sustain talk. The handling of sustain talk is uneven and lacks skillful redirection.

**Summary:**  
Acknowledges sustain talk but does not consistently soften or redirect it effectively.

### 6. Partnership  
**Score: 4/5**
