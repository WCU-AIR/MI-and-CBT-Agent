Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

1. Global Ratings (Counselor)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and invites the client to set the agenda, supporting autonomy. Affirmations of strengths are present. However, warmth and explicit normalization of ambivalence could be deeper.

Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
Comments:  
Good reflective listening and perspective-taking capture ambivalence and duality of experience. Reflections integrate content and feeling but sometimes feel formulaic and miss deeper emotional nuances like shame or fear.

Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
Comments:  
Strong collaboration and autonomy support with client-led agenda and evocation of motivations. Approach is somewhat formulaic and could be more evocative and co-creative in drawing out deeper values and hopes.

Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
Comments:  
Maintains collaborative stance and invites client input frequently. Partnership is somewhat superficial and could be enhanced by more explicit negotiation of agenda and shared decision-making language.

Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 4 (consistent, deep evocation)  
⬜ 1 (none) → ⬜ 2 ⬜ 3 ⬜ 5  
Comments:  
Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk including desire, ability, reasons, and need. Uses scaling and explores past successes. Could more consistently amplify commitment and confidence language.

Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
⬜ 1 (reinforces status quo) ⬜ 2 ⬜ 3  
☑ 3 (neutral handling) ⬜ 4 ⬜ 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
Comments:  
Sustain talk is acknowledged without judgment but handled neutrally. Lacks strategic use of double-sided reflections or reframing to soften or redirect sustain talk toward change.

☑ Avoids roadblocks

☐ Redirects drifting conversation

Evoking (5-point Likert)

Criteria:  
☑ Explores values, hopes, past successes  
☑ Affirms autonomy  
Comments:  
Effectively draws out reasons, desires, and abilities for change. Uses scaling and past successes well. Could deepen evocation of commitment and confidence.

Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
Criteria:  
☐ Strengthens commitment language  
☑ Elicits concrete steps  
Comments:  
Discusses manageable first steps and strategies but lacks explicit consolidation of commitment or structured plan.
