## Summary of Feedback

### Strengths  
- The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, supportive, and nonjudgmental stance.  
- Uses open questions and reflective listening to engage the patient.  
- Consistently seeks permission before sharing information and supports autonomy.  
- Elicits some change talk and encourages patient reflection on goals and strategies.

### Areas for Improvement  
- Reflections lack emotional depth and complexity; more nuanced empathy is needed.  
- Change talk is not consistently amplified or strategically reinforced.  
- Sustain talk is accepted neutrally rather than skillfully softened or redirected.  
- Partnership is somewhat one-sided; more co-creation and explicit sharing of power are needed.  
- Planning and summarizing are weak; no clear consolidation of commitment or next steps.  
- The session feels somewhat formulaic and scripted rather than dynamic and evocative.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflective Listening:**  
   Use more complex reflections that capture underlying emotions, ambivalence, and values. Reflect feelings and meaning, not just content.

2. **Amplify Change Talk:**  
   When change talk arises, reflect and reinforce it to deepen motivation. Explicitly elicit commitment and confidence language.

3. **Skillfully Soften Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections and gentle redirection to acknowledge sustain talk while guiding toward change.

4. **Enhance Partnership:**  
   Share power more explicitly by co-creating agenda and plans. Acknowledge the patient’s expertise and invite collaboration.

5. **Strengthen Planning and Summarizing:**  
   Develop concrete, actionable plans collaboratively and summarize key points to reinforce motivation and commitment.

6. **Personalize and Evoke More Deeply:**  
   Avoid formulaic phrasing; tailor responses to the patient’s unique experience and evoke their own reasons and values for change.

7. **Increase Emotional Attunement:**  
   Attend more closely to subtle emotional cues and validate the patient’s internal experience to build deeper rapport.

---

## Overall Score: **3/5**

**Summary:**  
This diagnostic session demonstrates a solid foundation in motivational interviewing principles, with respectful engagement, autonomy support, and some evocation of change talk. However, it falls short of excellence due to formulaic and surface-level reflections, limited amplification of motivation, weak handling of sustain talk, and minimal planning or summarizing. To improve, the psychiatrist should deepen empathic attunement, skillfully manage ambivalence, and more actively co-create the process with the patient.
