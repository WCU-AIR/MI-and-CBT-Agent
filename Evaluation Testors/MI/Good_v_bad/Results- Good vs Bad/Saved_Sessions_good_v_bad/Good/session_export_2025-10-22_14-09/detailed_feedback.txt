   Use double-sided reflections and gentle redirection to acknowledge sustain talk while guiding toward change.

4. **Strengthen Affirmations:**  
   Offer specific, genuine affirmations recognizing Amira’s strengths, past efforts, and resilience.

5. **Enhance Planning and Summarizing:**  
   Explicitly summarize key points, pull together change talk, and collaboratively develop a concrete, actionable plan with client buy-in.

6. **Increase Evocation and Collaboration:**  
   More actively draw out Amira’s own motivations, values, and ideas. Share power by explicitly inviting co-creation of agenda and plan.

7. **Avoid Formulaic Responses:**  
   Personalize responses and avoid repetitive phrasing. Be present and responsive to the unique flow of the conversation.
