# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, inviting the patient’s agenda and affirming reflections without judgment. The tone remains warm and validating throughout, fostering a safe environment for disclosure.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Good reflective listening and perspective-taking are evident, with accurate reflections of ambivalence and values. However, reflections tend to be somewhat surface-level and formulaic, lacking deeper emotional nuance or exploration of underlying fears.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and supports autonomy well, inviting patient input and emphasizing personal choice. The session avoids directive language but could be more explicitly evocative and co-creative in agenda-setting and planning.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Strong collaboration is maintained with respectful invitations for patient perspective and choice. However, the psychiatrist occasionally leads with structured questions and summaries, limiting full co-creation and shared decision-making.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 4 (consistent, good evocation)  
⬜ 1 (none) → ⬜ 5 (exceptional, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is skillfully elicited and reinforced, with patient articulating desire, reasons, ability, and commitment. Some opportunities to deepen and amplify commitment and confidence language were missed.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
⬜ 1 (reinforces status quo) → ☑ 3 (acknowledges sustain talk but limited softening)  
⬜ 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is acknowledged and reflected neutrally but not actively softened or reframed. The psychiatrist could use double-sided reflections or normalization to better reduce resistance and ambivalence.

---

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
☑ Uses open questions  
☑ Reflects more than asks  
☑ Avoids roadblocks  
**Comments:**  
Rapport is well established and maintained. Open-ended questions effectively invite elaboration and reflection. The patient appears comfortable and increasingly confident.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
☑ Confirms agreed goal  
☑ Redirects drifting conversation  
**Comments:**  
The session maintains clear focus on the patient’s goals related to alcohol use, soccer, and scholarships throughout.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
☑ Explores values, hopes, past successes  
☑ Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist effectively draws out motivations and strategies, linking them to values and past successes. Evocation of commitment and confidence could be deepened.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
☑ Strengthens commitment language  
☑ Elicits concrete steps  
**Comments:**  
Concrete next steps and strategies are discussed, though the plan could be more collaboratively developed and consolidated with stronger patient buy-in.

---

## 3. Counselor Behaviors (Behavior Counts – MISC/MITI)

### Open Questions (5-point Likert)  
☑ Draw out narrative, values  
⬜ Overuse of closed questions  
**Comments:**  
Open-ended questions are used consistently to encourage patient reflection and elaboration.

---

### Reflections (5-point Likert)

⬜ Few or no reflections  
**Comments:**  
Reflections are frequent and generally accurate but tend to be simple and surface-level rather than complex or amplified.

---

### Summarizing (5-point Likert)  
☑ Pulls together change talk  
⬜ Omits or distorts client language  
**Comments:**  
Summaries reinforce motivation and key points but are somewhat formulaic and could better integrate change talk and commitment language.

---

### Affirmations (5-point Likert)  
☑ Recognizes strengths, autonomy  
⬜ Judgmental, minimizing

Affirmations are genuine and recognize patient insight and strengths, though they could be more specific and varied.

---

### Persuade with Permission (5-point Likert)
