## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflections:**  
   Use more complex reflections that capture underlying emotions, values, and ambivalence. Reflect not just content but emotional experience and meaning.

2. **Amplify Change Talk:**  
   When change talk emerges, reflect, reinforce, and strategically evoke more, including explicit elicitation of commitment and confidence language.

3. **Skillfully Soften Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections and validate sustain talk while gently guiding toward change. Normalize ambivalence and acknowledge the difficulty of change.

4. **Strengthen Affirmations:**  
   Offer specific, genuine affirmations recognizing Lukas’s strengths, past efforts, and resilience. Avoid generic praise.

5. **Enhance Planning and Summarizing:**  
   Explicitly summarize change talk and consolidate commitment to next steps. Collaboratively develop a concrete, actionable plan and check for client buy-in.

6. **Use Persuade with Permission:**  
   Before offering suggestions or information, explicitly ask permission to maintain autonomy and collaboration.

7. **Increase Evocation and Collaboration:**  
   More actively draw out Lukas’s own motivations, values, and ideas for change. Share power by explicitly inviting co-creation of agenda and plan.

8. **Avoid Formulaic Responses:**  
   Personalize responses and avoid repetitive or scripted phrasing. Be present and responsive to the unique flow of the conversation.
