### 2. Empathy  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking. Reflections accurately capture Leila’s ambivalence, motivations, and barriers (e.g., “You clearly recognize how much exercise lifts your energy and mood,” “That mix of hope and challenge must feel pretty heavy at times”). **Summary:**  
Good understanding and validation of Leila’s perspective, but reflections could be deepened to better capture emotional complexity.

### 3. Spirit of MI  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**

However, the session occasionally feels somewhat formulaic and lacks a fully evocative style that might more actively draw out Leila’s deeper values or emotional drivers. The partnership is strong but could be enhanced by more explicit co-creation of agenda and plan.

**Summary:**  
Strong collaboration and autonomy support, but could be more evocative and dynamic in drawing out Leila’s intrinsic motivations and values.

### 4. Cultivating Change Talk  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist effectively elicits and reinforces change talk throughout the session. Leila offers multiple statements of desire, ability, reasons, and need for change (“I want to feel stronger and more energetic,” “I feel confident I can do this walk”). However, the psychiatrist misses some opportunities to explicitly elicit commitment language or explore Leila’s confidence and readiness in more detail beyond the two scaling questions. More strategic evocation of commitment and taking steps language would strengthen this domain.

**Summary:**  
Consistent evocation and reinforcement of change talk, but could more explicitly elicit and deepen commitment language.

### 5. Softening Sustain Talk  
**Score: 3/5**  

**Explanation:**  
Sustain talk is present in Leila’s discussion of barriers (distractions, weather, sales pressure), and the psychiatrist acknowledges these without judgment. However, the responses to sustain talk are mostly neutral reflections or summaries rather than active softening or strategic redirection. For example, the psychiatrist notes obstacles but does not use double-sided reflections or reframe sustain talk to reduce its impact (“Yes, distractions are tough, and at the same time you’re finding ways to work around them”). There is little evidence of explicitly normalizing ambivalence or gently guiding Leila toward resolving sustain talk. This area could be improved by more skillful handling of sustain talk to minimize its influence.

**Summary:**  
Acknowledges sustain talk respectfully but lacks consistent, skillful softening or redirection to reduce its impact.

### 6. Partnership  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**

However, the partnership is somewhat implicit rather than explicit; the psychiatrist does not overtly share power or negotiate the agenda in a way that fully equalizes the relationship. The session could benefit from more explicit co-creation of goals and plans, and more frequent invitations for Leila to lead the conversation or set the agenda.

**Summary:**  
Strong collaboration and client-centeredness, but partnership could be deepened by more explicit sharing of power and co-creation.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflections:**  
   Use more complex and amplified reflections that capture emotional nuance, ambivalence, and underlying values. Reflect feelings and meaning, not just content.

2. **Skillfully Soften Sustain Talk:**  
   Employ double-sided reflections and reframe sustain talk to reduce its impact and normalize ambivalence. Gently guide the client toward resolving sustain talk.

3. **Enhance Partnership:**  
   Explicitly invite the client to co-create the agenda, goals, and plan. Share power more overtly to strengthen collaboration.

4. **Strengthen Planning and Summarizing:**  
   Provide explicit summaries that consolidate change talk and reinforce commitment. Develop concrete, actionable plans with contingency strategies.

5. **Increase Evocation of Commitment:**  
   More actively elicit and reinforce commitment and taking steps language. Explore confidence and readiness in greater depth.

6. **Avoid Formulaic Responses:**  
   Personalize reflections and affirmations to the client’s unique experience. Avoid repetitive or generic phrasing.

## Overall Score: **4/5**

**Summary:**  
This session demonstrates strong foundational motivational interviewing skills, with excellent acceptance, autonomy support, and elicitation of change talk. However, to reach a higher level of MI proficiency, the psychiatrist should deepen reflections, skillfully soften sustain talk, explicitly co-create partnership, and strengthen planning and summarizing. These improvements will enhance emotional attunement, client engagement, and the likelihood of sustained behavior change.
