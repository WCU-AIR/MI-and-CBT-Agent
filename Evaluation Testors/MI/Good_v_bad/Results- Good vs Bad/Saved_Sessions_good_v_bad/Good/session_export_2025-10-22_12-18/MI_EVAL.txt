Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

1. Global Ratings (Counselor)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful and supportive tone, inviting the patient’s agenda and affirming resilience and thoughtful approaches. However, acceptance is somewhat surface-level and could be warmer with deeper validation of ambivalence.

Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
Comments:  
Reflections accurately capture content and some emotional undertones, showing understanding of ambivalence and mild surprise. Yet, reflections remain somewhat surface-level without deeper emotional exploration.

Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist demonstrates collaboration and autonomy support, inviting patient input and pacing. The style is somewhat formulaic and could be more evocative and co-creative to draw out deeper motivations.

Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
Comments:  
The session reflects a collaborative stance with patient input on focus and next steps. Partnership is present but somewhat superficial; more explicit co-creation and power-sharing would strengthen this.

Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 3 (some evocation, inconsistent amplification)  
Comments:  
Some change talk is elicited around tracking drinking and noticing effects, but the psychiatrist misses opportunities to amplify and reinforce it consistently or elicit stronger commitment language.

Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
☑ 3 (acknowledges sustain talk but neutral responses)  
Comments:  
Sustain talk is acknowledged through neutral reflections but not skillfully softened or redirected. There is little use of double-sided reflections or normalization to gently pivot toward change.

2. Core MI Processes
------------------------------------------------------------------
Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
Criteria:  
☑ Uses open questions  
☑ Reflects more than asks  
☑ Avoids roadblocks  
Comments:  
Good rapport and respectful tone engage the patient well. Open questions invite elaboration, though some questions are formulaic. Reflections predominate but lack emotional depth.

Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
Criteria:  
☑ Confirms agreed goal  
☑ Redirects drifting conversation  
Comments:  
Focus is maintained on ankle and drinking issues with clear guidance. Agenda-setting is present but could be more explicit and sharpened with summaries.

Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
Criteria:  
☑ Explores values, hopes, past successes  
☑ Affirms autonomy  
Comments:  
Some evocation of ambivalence and reasons for change occurs, especially regarding drinking. However, evocation is inconsistent and lacks depth in exploring values or emotional drivers.

Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
Criteria:  
☑ Strengthens commitment language  
☑ Elicits concrete steps  
Comments:  
A tentative plan to track drinking and talk with a roommate is discussed but not concretely developed or consolidated. No explicit check for commitment or follow-up plan.

Reflections (5-point Likert)
