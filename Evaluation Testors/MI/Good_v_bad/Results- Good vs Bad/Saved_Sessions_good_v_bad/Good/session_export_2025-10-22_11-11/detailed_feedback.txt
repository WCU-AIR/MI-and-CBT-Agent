   Use double-sided reflections and normalization to acknowledge sustain talk while gently guiding toward change.

   Explicitly summarize key points, pull together change talk, and collaboratively develop a concrete, actionable plan with clear commitment.

5. **Increase Evocation of Intrinsic Motivation:**  
   Explore Jordan’s deeper values, hopes, and personal reasons for change beyond immediate circumstances.

6. **Personalize and Vary Language:**  
   Avoid repetitive phrases; tailor responses to Jordan’s unique expressions to maintain engagement and demonstrate genuine presence.
