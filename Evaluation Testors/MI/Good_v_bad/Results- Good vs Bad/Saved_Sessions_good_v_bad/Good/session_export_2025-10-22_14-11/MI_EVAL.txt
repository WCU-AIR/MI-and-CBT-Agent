# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [x] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [ ] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist demonstrates consistent unconditional positive regard, warmly validating the patient’s experiences and efforts without judgment. Affirmations and normalization of ambivalence create a safe, supportive environment.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [x] High: active reflective listening  
- [ ] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Empathy is shown through nuanced, accurate reflections capturing both content and emotional undertones, such as hope, worry, and self-perception shifts. The psychiatrist’s reflections foster trust and engagement.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [x] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [ ] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session strongly embodies MI spirit by inviting patient input, evoking motivations, and supporting autonomy. The psychiatrist avoids directive language and respects the patient’s pace and readiness.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [x] High: minimizes power differential  
- [ ] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a clear partnership stance, co-creating agenda and plans with the patient, using inclusive language and supporting patient expertise and choice.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [ ] 1 (none)  
- [ ] 2  
- [ ] 3  
- [ ] 4  
- [x] 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is actively and skillfully cultivated through open questions and reflections that amplify the patient’s reasons, abilities, and desires for change. The psychiatrist consistently reinforces and expands change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)
