Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

1. Global Ratings (Counselor)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
□ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and respect throughout the session, creating a safe and supportive environment for the client to share vulnerabilities without judgment.

Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
□ Low: little effort to understand  
Comments:  
Good reflective listening and perspective-taking are evident, capturing both physical sensations and emotional experience. Reflections are accurate but somewhat surface-level and occasionally formulaic, with room for deeper emotional nuance.

Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
□ Low: authoritarian stance  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance, supports autonomy, and avoids directive behavior. However, the interaction sometimes feels formulaic and could more fully evoke the client’s unique motivations and values.

Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
□ Low: expert-driven  
Comments:  
Collaboration is strong with frequent invitations for client input. Partnership could be enhanced by more explicit co-creation of agenda and plans to deepen shared power.

Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
□ 1 (none) → ☑ 4 (good evocation, some missed opportunities) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
Comments:  
Change talk is effectively elicited and reinforced, including desire, reasons, ability, and commitment. Some opportunities to deepen and strategically amplify change talk were missed.

Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
□ 1 (reinforces status quo) → ☑ 3 (acknowledges but limited softening) → 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
Comments:  
Sustain talk is acknowledged and normalized but not actively softened or redirected. More strategic use of double-sided reflections or reframing could reduce sustain talk’s impact.
