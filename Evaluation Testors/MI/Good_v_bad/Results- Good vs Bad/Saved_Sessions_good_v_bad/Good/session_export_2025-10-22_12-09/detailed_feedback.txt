### 5. Softening Sustain Talk  
**Score: 3/5**  

**Explanation:**

For example, reflections are mostly neutral or descriptive rather than using double-sided reflections or strategic reframing to gently shift sustain talk toward change. The psychiatrist validates the difficulty but does not explicitly normalize ambivalence or use techniques to minimize the impact of sustain talk. This results in sustain talk lingering without being skillfully softened.

**Summary:**  
Sustain talk is acknowledged but not consistently softened or redirected; more strategic handling is needed.

### 6. Partnership  
**Score: 4/5**

The session feels collaborative but could be more explicitly egalitarian.

**Summary:**  
Strong collaboration and client-centeredness, but partnership could be deepened by more explicit co-creation and power sharing.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflections:**  
   Use more complex reflections that capture underlying emotions, values, and ambivalence to deepen client engagement.

2. **Skillfully Soften Sustain Talk:**  
   Employ double-sided reflections and gentle reframing to acknowledge sustain talk while guiding toward change.

3. **Amplify Commitment and Confidence:**  
   When clients express tentative commitment or low confidence, explore what would increase these and reinforce strengths.

4. **Enhance Partnership:**  
   Explicitly invite the client to co-create the agenda and plan, sharing power and fostering equality.

5. **Use Structured Summaries:**  
   Summarize change talk and plans explicitly to consolidate motivation and clarify next steps.

6. **Personalize Responses:**  
   Avoid formulaic phrasing; tailor reflections and questions to the client’s unique context and emotional experience.
