# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist demonstrates unconditional positive regard throughout the session, consistently validating the patient’s feelings of exhaustion and hopelessness without judgment. The tone is warm and supportive, affirming the patient’s strength in sharing difficult emotions.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Empathy is shown at a high level with nuanced reflections that capture both the content and emotional subtext of the patient’s experience. The psychiatrist accurately understands and conveys the patient’s distress, ambivalence, and subtle hope.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains collaboration and supports autonomy, inviting the patient’s perspective and gently evoking motivation. Some elements feel somewhat formulaic, such as scaling questions, which slightly reduce the natural flow of the MI spirit.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The session is collaborative with frequent invitations for patient input. However, the agenda and direction are largely guided by the psychiatrist rather than fully co-created, limiting deeper partnership.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
☑ 4 (consistent, deep evocation)  
⬜ 1 (none)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is skillfully evoked and nurtured despite the patient’s ambivalence and low readiness. The psychiatrist highlights small hopes and past coping efforts but could more actively amplify and explore commitment and confidence language.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
☑ 4 (consistently pivots to change)  
⬜ 1 (reinforces status quo)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is acknowledged and validated without confrontation. The psychiatrist gently redirects focus toward change but could use more double-sided reflections to strategically soften sustain talk.
