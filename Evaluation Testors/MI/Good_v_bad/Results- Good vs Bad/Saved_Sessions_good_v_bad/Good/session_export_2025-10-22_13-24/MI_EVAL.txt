# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, validating the patient’s feelings and frustrations. Acceptance is evident but somewhat formulaic, lacking deeper warmth or explicit normalization of resistance.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Good reflective listening and perspective-taking are demonstrated, capturing ambivalence and frustration. However, reflections are mostly surface-level and occasionally repetitive, missing deeper emotional exploration.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist supports autonomy and collaboration, inviting patient input and respecting pace. The style is somewhat formulaic and could be more evocative to actively draw out intrinsic motivation.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Consistent invitation of patient perspective and preferences fosters partnership. Power sharing is present but could be strengthened by explicitly acknowledging patient expertise and co-creating agenda and plans.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
⬜ 1 (none)  
⬜ 2  
⬜ 3  
☑ 4  
⬜ 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Some change talk is elicited, including tentative willingness and practical strategies. However, the psychiatrist misses opportunities to amplify and reinforce this talk to build stronger motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
⬜ 1 (reinforces status quo)  
⬜ 2  
⬜ 3  
☑ 4
