---

## Recommendations

1. **Enhance Softening of Sustain Talk:**  
   Incorporate more double-sided reflections that acknowledge sustain talk while gently highlighting change talk (e.g., “On one hand, the anxiety feels overwhelming, and on the other, you have this strong hope and past success that remind you you can handle it”). This can help reduce the impact of sustain talk and resolve ambivalence more effectively.

2. **Use Explicit Summaries:**  
   At key points, especially near session end, provide clear, structured summaries that pull together Hana’s change talk, strengths, and plans. This reinforces motivation and commitment.

3. **Continue Affirming Strengths and Resilience:**  
   Maintain specific affirmations that highlight Hana’s courage and insight, reinforcing her self-efficacy.

4. **Maintain Collaborative Planning:**  
   Continue to co-create manageable, concrete steps with Hana, ensuring plans feel doable and aligned with her values.
