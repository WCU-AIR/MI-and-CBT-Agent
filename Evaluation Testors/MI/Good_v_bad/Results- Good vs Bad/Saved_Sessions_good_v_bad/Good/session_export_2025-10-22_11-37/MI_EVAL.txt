# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [x] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [ ] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard, validating the patient’s concerns and ambivalence without judgment. The tone remains warm and supportive throughout, creating a safe space for open sharing.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [x] High: active reflective listening  
- [ ] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Reflections capture both content and emotional nuance, showing deep understanding of the patient’s experience and ambivalence. The psychiatrist’s responses validate feelings and concerns with accuracy and warmth.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [x] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [ ] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance and supports autonomy, inviting patient input and avoiding directive language. Some information sharing is somewhat didactic, though permission is sought before providing it.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [x] High: minimizes power differential  
- [ ] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist fosters collaboration and respects the patient as expert in their own experience. Permission is sought before advice, though agenda-setting and plan co-creation could be more explicit.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [ ] 1 (none)  
- [ ] 2  
- [ ] 3  
- [x] 4  
- [ ] 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is effectively elicited and reinforced, including desire, ability, reasons, and tentative commitment. Opportunities to deepen commitment and confidence language more strategically were somewhat missed.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [ ] 1 (reinforces status quo)  
- [ ] 2  
- [ ] 3  
- [x] 4  
- [ ] 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is acknowledged and softened with double-sided reflections and validation. More explicit normalization and reframing could further reduce resistance.

---

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
Criteria:  
- [x] Uses open questions  
- [x] Reflects more than asks  
- [x] Avoids roadblocks  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist builds strong rapport using open questions and reflective listening, maintaining a warm and accepting tone throughout.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Confirms agreed goal  
- [ ] Redirects drifting conversation  
**Comments:**  
Focus is maintained on smoking and stress management, but explicit negotiation of agenda and goal could be stronger.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
Criteria:  
- [x] Explores values, hopes, past successes  
- [x] Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist effectively draws out motivations and ambivalence, exploring values and past experiences. More strategic elicitation of commitment and confidence would enhance evocation.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Strengthens commitment language  
- [x] Elicits concrete steps  
**Comments:**  
Some discussion of manageable next steps (trying vaping) occurs, but the plan is not fully consolidated or collaboratively developed with clear commitment.
