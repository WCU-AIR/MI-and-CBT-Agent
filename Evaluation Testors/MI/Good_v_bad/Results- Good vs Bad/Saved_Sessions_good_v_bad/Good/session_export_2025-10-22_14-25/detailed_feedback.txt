## Recommendations

1. **Enhance Softening of Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections that acknowledge both sides of ambivalence explicitly (e.g., “On one hand, drinking helps you connect with friends, and on the other, it’s causing injuries and regret…”). This can gently shift sustain talk toward change without confrontation.

2. **Increase Explicit Summarizing:**  
   Provide clear, structured summaries that pull together change talk, client strengths, and agreed-upon plans to reinforce motivation and commitment.

3. **Continue Strategic Evocation of Confidence and Commitment:**  
   Explore potential barriers in more depth and elicit client strategies for overcoming them, reinforcing self-efficacy.

4. **Maintain Strong Partnership and Autonomy Support:**  
   Continue to invite client input and permission, ensuring the client remains the expert in their own change process.
