---

## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflections:**  
   Use more complex reflections that capture underlying emotions, ambivalence, and values. Reflect feelings and meaning, not just content.

2. **Amplify Change Talk:**  
   When change talk arises, reflect and reinforce it more strongly. Elicit commitment and confidence language explicitly.

3. **Softening Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections and strategic reframing to gently shift sustain talk toward change. Normalize ambivalence and validate difficulty.

4. **Enhance Summarizing:**  
   Provide explicit, structured summaries that integrate change talk and reinforce motivation and commitment.

5. **Increase Personalization and Evocation:**  
   Avoid formulaic responses; tailor reflections and questions to the client’s unique experience and emotional state.

6. **Explicit Permission for Advice:**  
   Always ask permission before offering suggestions or information to maintain collaboration and respect autonomy.

7. **Consolidate Planning:**  
   Collaboratively develop a clear, actionable plan with client buy-in and confidence-building strategies.
