# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, acknowledging the patient’s frustration and perspective without moralizing. However, acceptance feels somewhat procedural and lacks deeper warmth or explicit normalization of the patient’s feelings.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
⬜ High: active reflective listening  
☑ Moderate: basic reflections, some understanding  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Reflections capture content and some feelings but remain surface-level paraphrases. There is limited exploration of underlying emotions such as frustration or defensiveness, resulting in a somewhat shallow empathic connection.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
⬜ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
☑ Moderate: collaborative but limited evocation  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist supports autonomy and collaboration, inviting input and avoiding confrontation. However, the session focuses mainly on compliance and procedural navigation, with minimal evocation of intrinsic motivation or ambivalence.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Consistently invites patient’s input and preferences, treating him as an active participant. Partnership is somewhat superficial, focusing on procedural compliance rather than co-creating a meaningful agenda or plan.
