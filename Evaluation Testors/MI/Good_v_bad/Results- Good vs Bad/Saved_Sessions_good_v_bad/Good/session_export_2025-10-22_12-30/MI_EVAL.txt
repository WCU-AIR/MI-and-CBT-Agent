# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [x] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [ ] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful and supportive tone throughout, validating the patient’s experience without judgment. However, acceptance feels somewhat formulaic and lacks deeper warmth or explicit normalization of ambivalence.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [x] High: active reflective listening  
- [ ] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Good reflective listening and perspective-taking are evident, with accurate reflections of both content and emotional undertones. Reflections tend to be somewhat repetitive and surface-level, missing opportunities for deeper emotional attunement.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Moderate  
- [ ] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains collaboration and supports autonomy but the style is somewhat formulaic and lacks full evocation of the patient’s motivations and values. There is limited depth in drawing out change talk or reinforcing agency.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Moderate  
- [ ] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**

### Planning (5-point Likert)
