## Recommendations

1. **Amplify Change Talk:**  
   - When change talk emerges, reflect and reinforce it more explicitly.  
   - Ask questions to elicit commitment (“What would make you feel ready to try?”) and confidence (“How confident do you feel about this step?”).

2. **Use Explicit Summaries:**  
   - Summarize key change talk and plans clearly to consolidate motivation and clarify next steps.  
   - Use summaries to highlight client strengths and progress.

3. **Probe Confidence and Commitment:**  
   - Explore client confidence and readiness more directly to identify and address barriers.  
   - Use scaling questions or confidence rulers to deepen this exploration.

4. **Maintain Strengths:**  
   - Continue the warm, empathic, and collaborative approach.  
   - Keep validating ambivalence and softening sustain talk skillfully.
