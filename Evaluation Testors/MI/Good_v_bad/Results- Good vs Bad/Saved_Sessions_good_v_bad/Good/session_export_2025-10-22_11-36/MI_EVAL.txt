# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [x] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [ ] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful and nonjudgmental tone throughout, validating the patient’s perspective and experience. Statements like “Thanks for sharing that” and “I appreciate how grounded you seem” show support. However, the acceptance feels somewhat procedural and lacks deeper warmth or explicit unconditional positive regard.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [x] High: active reflective listening  
- [ ] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Reflections demonstrate understanding of the patient’s content and perspective but remain surface-level and factual. Emotional nuances and underlying feelings such as defensiveness or social pressure are not deeply explored or validated, resulting in somewhat mechanical empathy.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [x] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [ ] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains collaboration and supports autonomy by inviting patient input and avoiding directive advice. However, evocation of motivation is limited and the session feels formulaic rather than dynamically engaging the patient’s own motivations or values.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [x] High: minimizes power differential  
- [ ] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Consistently invites patient’s perspective and choices, demonstrating collaboration. Yet, the partnership is somewhat superficial and lacks explicit negotiation or co-creation of agenda and plans, limiting full equality.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [ ] 1 (none)  
- [ ] 2  
- [ ] 3  
- [x] 4  
- [ ] 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Some change talk is elicited, including patient’s recognition of limits and tentative plans to stay aware. However, these are not consistently amplified or reinforced, and commitment language remains weak and tentative.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [ ] 1 (reinforces status quo)  
- [x] 2  
- [ ] 3  
- [ ] 4  
- [ ] 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is frequent and acknowledged but not actively softened or redirected. The psychiatrist mostly reflects sustain talk neutrally without using double-sided reflections or reframing to gently shift toward change.

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
Criteria:  
- [x] Uses open questions  
- [ ] Reflects more than asks  
- [x] Avoids roadblocks  
**Comments:**  
Rapport is established with respectful tone and open questions that invite elaboration. However, reflections are fewer and tend to paraphrase rather than deepen understanding, limiting engagement depth.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
Criteria:  
- [x] Confirms agreed goal  
- [x] Redirects drifting conversation  
**Comments:**  
The conversation remains focused on drinking behavior and ambivalence about change. The psychiatrist maintains topic focus but does not actively guide toward a specific change goal.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
Criteria:  
- [x] Explores values, hopes, past successes  
- [x] Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
Some exploration of patient’s values and tentative plans occurs, and autonomy is supported. However, evocation is shallow and lacks depth or strategic reinforcement of motivation.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
Criteria:  
- [ ] Strengthens commitment language  
- [ ] Elicits concrete steps  
**Comments:**  
Planning is minimal and vague. While the patient identifies a general approach (talking with a friend), no concrete plan is developed or consolidated by the psychiatrist.
