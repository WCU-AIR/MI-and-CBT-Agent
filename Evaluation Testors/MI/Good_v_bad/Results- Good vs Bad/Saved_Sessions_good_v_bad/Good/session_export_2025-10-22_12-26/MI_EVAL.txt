# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful and nonjudgmental tone throughout, validating the patient’s concerns and skepticism. Acceptance is evident but somewhat formulaic, lacking deeper warmth or explicit unconditional positive regard that might encourage more openness.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Reflections capture patient concerns and some emotional undertones, such as valuing strength and fear of side effects. However, empathy remains surface-level without deeper emotional attunement or exploration of underlying fears and ambivalence.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist supports autonomy and collaboration, inviting patient input and avoiding persuasion. Yet, the session feels somewhat formulaic with limited evocation of deeper motivations or values, and partnership is not fully co-created.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
⬜ High: minimizes power differential  
☑ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Collaboration is present but somewhat one-sided; the psychiatrist guides the agenda without explicitly sharing power or co-creating the plan. Patient expertise and preferences are acknowledged but not fully integrated into decision-making.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 3 (moderate presence)  
⬜ 1 (none) → ⬜ 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is elicited through questions about past successes and confidence but is not consistently reinforced or amplified. Missed opportunities to deepen motivation or elicit stronger commitment language.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
☑ 3 (moderate softening)  
⬜ 1 (reinforces status quo) → ⬜ 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is acknowledged neutrally but not actively softened or redirected. The psychiatrist reflects concerns without using strategies like double-sided reflections or normalization to reduce resistance.

---

## 2. Core MI Processes

### Engaging (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Build rapport, acceptance, empathy  
☑ Uses open questions  
☑ Reflects more than asks  
☑ Avoids roadblocks  
**Comments:**  
Rapport is established with respectful tone and acceptance. Open questions invite elaboration, though some feel formulaic. Engagement could be deepened with more personalized connection and emotional attunement.

---

### Focusing (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Maintain direction toward change goal  
☑ Confirms agreed goal  
☑ Redirects drifting conversation  
**Comments:**  
Focus is maintained on cholesterol management and medication ambivalence. However, the conversation lacks a clearly negotiated agenda or explicit guidance toward a concrete change goal.

---

### Evoking (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Draw out client’s reasons for change  
☑ Explores values, hopes, past successes  
☑ Affirms autonomy  
**Comments:**  
Some evocation of reasons for change and past successes occurs, with affirmation of autonomy. Yet, evocation lacks depth and frequency, and stronger exploration of values and commitment is missed.

---

### Planning (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Consolidates commitment, next steps  
☑ Strengthens commitment language (tentatively)  
☑ Elicits concrete steps (tentative)  
**Comments:**  
Next steps such as scheduling follow-up and making a list for the doctor are discussed but remain tentative. The plan lacks consolidation and explicit commitment, with ambivalence unresolved.
