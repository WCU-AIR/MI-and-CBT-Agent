## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Commitment and Confidence Elicitation:**  
   - When asking about confidence, follow up with questions like “What would it take to move from a 5 to a 7?” or “Can you tell me about a time you succeeded that gives you hope?”  
   - Explicitly elicit and reinforce commitment language to strengthen motivation.

2. **Use Double-Sided Reflections:**  
   - Reflect both sustain and change talk in the same statement to acknowledge ambivalence while gently guiding toward change (e.g., “On one hand you feel unsure if you deserve this, and on the other hand you really want to keep moving forward.”).

3. **Provide Explicit Summaries:**  
   - Summarize key change talk and plans periodically to reinforce motivation and consolidate next steps.

4. **Continue Affirming Strengths and Insight:**  
   - Maintain specific affirmations that highlight Elias’s courage, insight, and progress.

5. **Maintain Collaborative Planning:**  
   - Continue to break down plans into manageable steps and check for Elias’s buy-in.
