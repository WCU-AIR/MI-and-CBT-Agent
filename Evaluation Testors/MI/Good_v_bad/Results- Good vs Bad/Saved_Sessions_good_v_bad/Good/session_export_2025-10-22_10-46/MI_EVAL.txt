# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful and supportive tone throughout, affirming Laura’s efforts and experiences without judgment. Statements like “That sense of responsibility and consistency really shows a lot of strength” demonstrate positive regard. However, acceptance could be warmer and more individualized to fully convey unconditional positive regard.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
☑ Moderate: active reflective listening present but somewhat superficial  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Reflections accurately paraphrase Laura’s statements but tend to be surface-level and lack emotional depth. The psychiatrist misses opportunities to explore Laura’s ambivalence and emotional nuances more deeply, resulting in inconsistent empathy.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
☑ Moderate: collaboration, evocation, autonomy support present but limited  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist supports autonomy and uses open questions, maintaining a collaborative stance. However, evocation of intrinsic motivation is limited, and the session feels somewhat formulaic rather than deeply empowering or evocative.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
☑ High: works as equal partner with minimized power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist consistently invites Laura’s input and avoids an expert-driven approach. Language is inclusive and collaborative, though partnership could be deepened with more explicit co-creation of agenda and goals.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
☑ Moderate: some elicitation of change talk but inconsistent amplification  
⬜ Low: none or minimal  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is elicited around avoiding poor grades and future goals but is tentative and not consistently reinforced or deepened. Opportunities to amplify commitment and confidence language are missed.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
⬜ Low: sustain talk largely unaddressed or reinforced  
☑ Moderate: minimal softening or pivoting  
⬜ High: consistently pivots to change  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk about low motivation and preference for status quo is acknowledged but not actively softened or redirected. The psychiatrist does not use double-sided reflections or reframing to manage ambivalence effectively.

# Summary

This evaluation reflects a competent but developing use of motivational interviewing. The psychiatrist shows respect, supports autonomy, and uses open questions and reflections. However, the session is limited by formulaic responses, superficial reflections, insufficient handling of sustain talk, and weak planning. To enhance MI adherence, the psychiatrist should deepen emotional attunement, skillfully manage ambivalence, amplify change talk, personalize affirmations, and collaboratively develop concrete plans.
