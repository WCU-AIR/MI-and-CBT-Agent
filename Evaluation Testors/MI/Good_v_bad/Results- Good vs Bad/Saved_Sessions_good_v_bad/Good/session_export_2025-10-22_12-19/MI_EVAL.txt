# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, explicitly acknowledging the patient’s guardedness and boundaries. However, acceptance feels somewhat procedural and lacks warmth or deeper positive regard that might soften defensiveness.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Reflections show effort to understand the patient’s perspective but remain mostly factual and surface-level. Emotional nuance and deeper exploration of underlying feelings such as fear or vulnerability are limited.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist supports autonomy and invites input, demonstrating collaboration. However, evocation of patient motivation is limited and the approach feels somewhat formulaic rather than fluidly responsive.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Patient input is invited and autonomy respected, but partnership is superficial and lacks explicit co-creation or acknowledgment of patient expertise. Interaction resembles a polite interview more than a fully equal partnership.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 1 (none) → ⬜ 2 ⬜ 3 ⬜ 4 ⬜ 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
No change talk is evoked or present. The patient remains resistant and minimally communicative. The psychiatrist misses opportunities to gently evoke even small expressions of hope or curiosity.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
☑ 2 (acknowledges but neutral) → ⬜ 3 ⬜ 4 ⬜ 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is acknowledged respectfully without reinforcement, but responses are mostly neutral reflections rather than strategic softening or redirection toward ambivalence or openness.
