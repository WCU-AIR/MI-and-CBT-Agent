## Global Ratings (Counselor Behaviors)

### 1. Acceptance  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a generally respectful and nonjudgmental tone throughout the session. They acknowledge Julian’s guardedness and ambivalence without criticism, using affirming language such as “I appreciate how you’re thinking through what might help” and “That’s a really honest and important insight.” The psychiatrist avoids labeling or judgment and validates Julian’s feelings of overwhelm and uncertainty. However, the acceptance is somewhat surface-level and formulaic. There is limited explicit expression of unconditional positive regard or normalization of Julian’s ambivalence and anxiety. For example, the psychiatrist could have more explicitly normalized Julian’s hesitation and guardedness as understandable responses to a difficult situation.  

**Summary:**  
Respectful and supportive, but lacks deeper warmth and explicit normalization that would elevate acceptance to the highest level.

### 2. Empathy  
**Score: 4/5**

Good understanding and validation of Julian’s perspective, but could deepen emotional attunement and explore underlying feelings more fully.

**Summary:**

   Offer specific, genuine affirmations recognizing Julian’s strengths, past successes, and practical insights. Avoid generic praise.

5. **Enhance Planning and Summarizing:**  
   Collaboratively develop a concrete, actionable plan with Julian. Use explicit summaries to consolidate change talk and clarify next steps.

6. **Increase Emotional Attunement and Evocation:**  
   Explore Julian’s feelings and values more deeply to evoke intrinsic motivation beyond external consequences. Personalize responses to avoid formulaic language.

7. **Maintain Strong Partnership:**  
   Continue to seek permission and support autonomy, while encouraging Julian’s active participation in decision-making.
