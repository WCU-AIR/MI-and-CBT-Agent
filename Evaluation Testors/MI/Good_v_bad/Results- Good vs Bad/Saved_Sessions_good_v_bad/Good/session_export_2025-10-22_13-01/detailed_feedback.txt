---

## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflections:**  
   Use more complex reflections that capture underlying emotions, values, and ambivalence. Avoid repetitive phrasing and strive to move the conversation forward emotionally and motivationally.

2. **Amplify Change Talk:**  
   When change talk emerges, reflect and reinforce it explicitly. Ask evocative questions to deepen desire, ability, reasons, and need for change. Elicit commitment and confidence language.

3. **Skillfully Soften Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk while gently guiding toward change. Normalize ambivalence and validate the difficulty of change.

4. **Strengthen Affirmations:**  
   Offer specific, genuine affirmations recognizing Dylan’s insight, social awareness, and tentative willingness to change. Avoid generic praise.

5. **Enhance Planning and Summarizing:**  
   Explicitly summarize change talk and collaboratively develop a concrete, actionable plan. Check for client buy-in and confidence.

6. **Increase Evocation and Collaboration:**  
   More actively draw out Dylan’s own motivations, values, and ideas for change. Share power by explicitly inviting Dylan to co-create the agenda and plan.

7. **Avoid Formulaic Responses:**  
   Personalize responses to Dylan’s unique situation and style. Be present and responsive to the flow of the conversation rather than relying on scripted or repetitive phrases.
