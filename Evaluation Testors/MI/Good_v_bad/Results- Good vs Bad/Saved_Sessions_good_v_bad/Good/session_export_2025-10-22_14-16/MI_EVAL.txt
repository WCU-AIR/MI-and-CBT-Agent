Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

1. Global Ratings (Counselor)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and respect throughout the session. Warm, nonjudgmental tone and affirmations create a safe space for client disclosure.

Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
Comments:  
Good reflective listening with accurate reflections of client ambivalence and emotions. Some reflections are somewhat surface-level and formulaic; deeper emotional nuance could be explored.

Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
Comments:  
Strong collaborative stance supporting client autonomy and evoking motivation. Occasionally the style feels structured and formulaic rather than fully fluid and evocative.

Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
Comments:  
Excellent partnership demonstrated by frequent invitations for client input and co-creation of goals and plans. Language is inclusive and empowering.

Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
☑ 4 (consistent, deep evocation)  
⬜ 1 (none) → ⬜ 5 (exceptional)  
Comments:  
Change talk is effectively elicited and reinforced throughout, with client articulating desire, ability, reasons, and commitment. Some missed opportunities to amplify change talk more deeply and earlier.

Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
⬜ 1 (reinforces status quo) → ☑ 3 (acknowledges but limited softening) → ⬜ 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
Comments:  
Sustain talk is acknowledged respectfully but not skillfully softened or redirected. More use of double-sided reflections or reframing could reduce sustain talk’s impact.

☑ Confirms agreed goal

Comments:

Evoking (5-point Likert)

☑ Affirms autonomy

Affirmations (5-point Likert)
