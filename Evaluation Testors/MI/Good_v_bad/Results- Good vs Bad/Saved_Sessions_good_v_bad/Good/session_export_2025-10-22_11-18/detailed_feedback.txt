## Global Ratings (Counselor Behaviors)

### 1. Acceptance  
**Score: 5/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and respect toward Elira throughout the session. **Summary:**  
The psychiatrist excels in acceptance, creating a safe and supportive environment that encourages honest disclosure.

### 2. Empathy  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing Elira’s emotional experience and ambivalence. Reflections such as “That heaviness you carry seems to weigh on you a lot,” and “That doubt and fear… sounds really heavy, like a protective shield” show an understanding of the emotional nuance behind Elira’s avoidance. **Summary:**  
Strong empathy is evident, but reflections could be more varied and delve deeper into emotional complexity.

### 3. Spirit of MI  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance, inviting Elira’s input and supporting her autonomy throughout. The session avoids directive or authoritative language, instead emphasizing Elira’s own insights and choices (“What do you think would help move that number a little higher?” “How do you feel about trying that in the next few days?”). The psychiatrist evokes Elira’s motivations and strengths, and respects her pace and hesitations. However, the session occasionally feels somewhat structured and formulaic, with repeated use of scaling questions and standard MI phrases. While collaboration is strong, the evocation of deeper personal values or more explicit partnership in agenda-setting and planning could be enhanced to reach a fully evocative MI spirit.

**Summary:**  
Good collaboration and autonomy support, but the session could be more fluid and evocative to fully embody MI spirit.

---

### 4. Cultivating Change Talk  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist effectively elicits and reinforces change talk throughout the session. Examples include exploring Elira’s desire for less stress, her recognition of helpful supports, and her tentative commitment to texting a friend. The psychiatrist reflects and amplifies these statements, such as “It shows you’re already drawing on the support that helps you feel understood,” and “You’re clearly thoughtful about how to set yourself up for success.” The use of scaling questions to assess readiness and confidence is appropriate and helps quantify motivation. However, there are missed opportunities to deepen change talk by asking more evocative questions about Elira’s values or the personal meaning of change, or by explicitly eliciting commitment language beyond tentative plans.

**Summary:**  
Change talk is well cultivated and reinforced, but could be deepened with more evocative questions and stronger elicitation of commitment.

---

### 5. Softening Sustain Talk  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist skillfully acknowledges sustain talk without judgment and gently redirects toward change. For example, Elira’s doubts and fears about failure and avoidance are validated (“It makes sense that avoiding feels safer”), and the psychiatrist reframes these as protective but limiting. The psychiatrist does not confront or challenge sustain talk but rather normalizes ambivalence and highlights Elira’s curiosity and strengths. This approach reduces resistance and keeps the conversation moving toward change. However, the session could benefit from more explicit use of double-sided reflections that juxtapose sustain and change talk to further soften sustain talk’s impact.

**Summary:**  
Effective softening of sustain talk with validation and gentle redirection, though more strategic use of double-sided reflections would enhance this skill.

### 6. Partnership  
**Score: 5/5**  

**Explanation:**

Elira is invited to set the agenda (“what would you most like to focus on?”), and her ideas and preferences guide the conversation. This equal partnership fosters empowerment and respects Elira’s expertise in her own experience.

**Summary:**  
Excellent partnership with clear minimization of power differentials and genuine collaboration.
