# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
☑ High: consistently respectful and supportive  
⬜ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful and supportive tone throughout, validating the patient’s experience and struggles without judgment. Acceptance is evident in affirmations and empathetic reflections. However, deeper expressions of unconditional positive regard and normalization of difficulty could enhance acceptance further.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
☑ High: active reflective listening  
⬜ Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Good reflective listening and perspective-taking are demonstrated, with accurate reflections of the patient’s ambivalence and emotional state. Some reflections capture the patient’s feelings well, though emotional nuance could be deepened by explicitly naming underlying fears and discouragement.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
☑ High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
⬜ Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist shows collaboration and autonomy support, inviting the patient’s ideas and avoiding directive advice. The spirit of MI is present but somewhat formulaic; more active evocation of deeper motivations and explicit reinforcement of autonomy would strengthen the MI spirit.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
☑ High: minimizes power differential  
⬜ Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist works collaboratively, frequently inviting the patient’s input and respecting autonomy. Partnership is evident but could be enhanced by more explicit co-creation of agenda and plan, further minimizing power differentials.
