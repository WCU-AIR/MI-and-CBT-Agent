### 4. Cultivating Change Talk  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**

**Summary:**

## Recommendations

1. **Explicitly Elicit Commitment and Confidence:**  
   - Use direct questions to invite the patient to verbalize commitment and confidence (e.g., “On a scale of 0 to 10, how ready are you to put the alcohol away at home?” “What would help you feel more confident about taking that step?”).

2. **Use Structured Summaries:**  
   - Summarize key change talk and agreed-upon steps explicitly to reinforce motivation and clarify the plan.

3. **Consolidate Planning:**  
   - Collaboratively develop a clear, actionable plan with the patient, checking for readiness and willingness to commit.

4. **Continue Emphasizing Autonomy:**  
   - Maintain respect for the patient’s control while gently guiding toward change.

5. **Maintain Complex Reflections and Affirmations:**  
   - Continue using nuanced reflections and specific affirmations to deepen engagement.
