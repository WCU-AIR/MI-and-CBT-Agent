## Global Ratings (Counselor Behaviors)

### 1. Acceptance  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a generally respectful and supportive tone throughout the session. ### 2. Empathy  
**Score: 4/5**

**Summary:**  
Good reflective listening and perspective-taking, but reflections could be deeper and more emotionally rich.

### 5. Softening Sustain Talk  
**Score: 3/5**  

**Explanation:**

The handling of sustain talk is adequate but lacks skillful softening.
