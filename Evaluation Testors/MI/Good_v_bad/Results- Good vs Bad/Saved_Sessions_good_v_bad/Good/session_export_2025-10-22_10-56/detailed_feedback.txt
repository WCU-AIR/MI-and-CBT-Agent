## Recommendations

1. **Explicit Permission-Seeking:**  
   Before sharing information or suggestions (e.g., about drinking guidelines or strategies), ask permission to enhance collaboration and respect for autonomy.

2. **Strategic Softening of Sustain Talk:**  
   Use double-sided reflections that acknowledge sustain talk while gently guiding toward change (e.g., “On the one hand, you enjoy the comfort wine brings; on the other, you’re concerned about your mood…”).

3. **Structured Summarizing:**  
   Incorporate explicit summaries that pull together key change talk, ambivalence, and plans to reinforce motivation and commitment.

4. **Maintain Strengths:**  
   Continue the warm, empathic, and collaborative style that effectively engages and empowers clients.
