## Recommendations

1. **Elicit Commitment Earlier:**  
   Proactively ask for commitment and confidence ratings earlier in the session to solidify motivation.

2. **Use Double-Sided Reflections:**  
   When sustain talk arises, reflect both sides of ambivalence to soften resistance and validate complexity.

3. **Enhance Affirmations:**  
   Offer more frequent, specific affirmations that highlight patient strengths, resilience, and past successes.

4. **Explicit Summarizing:**  
   Summarize key change talk and next steps explicitly to reinforce motivation and clarify the plan.

5. **Strengthen Planning:**  
   Collaboratively develop a concrete plan with clear next steps, timelines, and accountability supports.

6. **Maintain Strong Partnership:**  
   Continue to emphasize autonomy and collaboration, inviting patient input at every stage.
