# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [x] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [ ] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and respect. The patient’s concerns are validated without judgment, creating a warm and supportive atmosphere throughout the session.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [x] High: active reflective listening  
- [ ] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Good reflective listening and perspective-taking are evident, with accurate reflections of the patient’s feelings and ambivalence. However, reflections tend to be somewhat surface-level and formulaic, lacking deeper emotional exploration.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [x] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [ ] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a collaborative stance, supports autonomy, and skillfully evokes patient ideas. Some responses feel formulaic, limiting natural flow and deeper evocation of values and motivations.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [x] High: minimizes power differential  
- [ ] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
Strong partnership is demonstrated by inviting patient input, co-creating plans, and minimizing power differentials. The patient is empowered throughout the session.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [ ] 1 (none)  
- [ ] 2  
- [ ] 3  
- [x] 4  
- [ ] 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is effectively elicited and reinforced, including desire, ability, reasons, and commitment. Opportunities to deepen change talk by linking to personal values were present but not fully utilized.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [ ] 1 (reinforces status quo)

### Planning (5-point Likert)

- [x] Strengthens commitment language
