# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [x] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [ ] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist maintains a respectful and supportive tone throughout, acknowledging the patient’s resistance without judgment. Acceptance is evident but somewhat formulaic and could be deepened with more explicit normalization and warmth.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [x] High: active reflective listening  
- [ ] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Good reflective listening and perspective-taking are demonstrated, capturing ambivalence and relational motivations. However, reflections are somewhat surface-level and repetitive, lacking deeper emotional nuance.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [x] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [ ] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist supports autonomy and collaboration, inviting the patient’s perspective and pacing. The spirit is somewhat formulaic and could be more evocative and explicitly co-creative.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [x] High: minimizes power differential  
- [ ] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
A collaborative stance is maintained with frequent invitations for patient input. Partnership is present but somewhat superficial, lacking explicit co-creation of agenda or plan.
