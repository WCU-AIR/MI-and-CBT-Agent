### 5. Softening Sustain Talk  
**Score: 4/5**  

**Explanation:**  
Sustain talk is minimal in this session, as the patient is largely consistent and motivated. However, when the patient expresses challenges or ambivalence (e.g., “putting it into practice feels harder,” “habit—it’s hard to break”), the psychiatrist acknowledges these without reinforcing them. The psychiatrist uses reflective listening to validate the difficulty and normalizes the struggle (“You’re aware that this pattern isn’t sustainable, which shows a lot of insight”). While sustain talk is not explicitly reframed or double-sided reflections used, the psychiatrist skillfully avoids pushing or confronting, allowing the patient to explore ambivalence safely.

**Summary:**  
Sustain talk is handled well through validation and nonjudgmental reflection, though more explicit softening techniques could be employed.
