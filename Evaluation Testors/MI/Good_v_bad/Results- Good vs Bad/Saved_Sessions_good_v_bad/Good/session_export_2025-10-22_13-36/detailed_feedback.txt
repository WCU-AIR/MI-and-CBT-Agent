## Recommendations

1. **Deepen Reflections:**  
   Use more complex reflections that capture underlying emotions, values, and ambivalence. Reflect feelings such as fear, shame, or frustration more explicitly.

2. **Skillfully Soften Sustain Talk:**  
   Employ double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk while gently guiding toward change. Normalize ambivalence and validate the difficulty of change.

3. **Enhance Partnership:**  
   Explicitly invite Yasmin to co-create the agenda, goals, and plans. Share power by asking how she wants to proceed and what feels most helpful.

4. **Amplify Change Talk:**  
   When change talk emerges, reflect and reinforce it more strongly. Explicitly elicit commitment and confidence language to consolidate motivation.

5. **Strengthen Planning and Summarizing:**  
   Provide clear, structured summaries that pull together change talk and reinforce motivation. Collaboratively develop concrete, achievable plans with Yasmin.

6. **Avoid Formulaic Language:**  
   Personalize reflections and affirmations to Yasmin’s unique experience. Vary phrasing to maintain engagement and authenticity.

7. **Explicitly Reinforce Autonomy:**  
   More clearly emphasize Yasmin’s right to choose pace and direction, reinforcing her control over the process.
