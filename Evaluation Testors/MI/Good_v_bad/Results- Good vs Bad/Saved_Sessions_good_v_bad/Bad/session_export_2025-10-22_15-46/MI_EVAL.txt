# Summary

The psychiatrist’s approach is consistently authoritarian, confrontational, and lacking in empathy or acceptance. There is no evidence of reflective listening, affirmations, or collaborative planning. Change talk is minimally present but not cultivated, and sustain talk is harshly confronted rather than softened. The session fails to embody the spirit of motivational interviewing, resulting in low engagement and likely increased patient resistance. To improve, the psychiatrist must adopt a warm, accepting stance; use reflective listening; support autonomy; and collaboratively evoke and reinforce the patient’s own motivations for change.
