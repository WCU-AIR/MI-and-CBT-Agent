# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist repeatedly dismisses and judges the patient’s efforts and feelings, using language such as “You have to be stricter” and “Stop feeling sorry for yourself.” There is no evidence of warmth or support, undermining rapport and acceptance.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Minimal to no reflective listening is present. The psychiatrist ignores or invalidates the patient’s emotional expressions, responding with confrontational commands rather than empathy or validation.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts a controlling, directive approach, repeatedly demanding compliance and ignoring the patient’s autonomy or perspective. There is no collaboration or evocation of the patient’s motivations.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist imposes a rigid agenda without negotiation or shared decision-making, positioning self as the expert and the patient as subordinate.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is minimal and tentative from the patient and is not nurtured or amplified by the psychiatrist. No open-ended questions or reflections are used to evoke or strengthen change talk.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance  
- [x] 1 (reinforces status quo) → 5 (consistently pivots to change)  
**Comments:**  
Sustain talk is met with confrontation and invalidation, reinforcing resistance rather than softening it. The psychiatrist labels patient concerns as excuses and feeling sorry for oneself.

### Focusing (5-point Likert)
