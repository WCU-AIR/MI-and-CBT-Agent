# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist’s tone is largely judgmental and directive, repeatedly dismissing the patient’s fatigue as “excuses” and “slacking off.” There is no evidence of warmth, empathy, or validation of the patient’s experience, undermining unconditional positive regard.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
Empathy is essentially absent. The psychiatrist ignores the patient’s expressed exhaustion and struggles, failing to reflect or validate her feelings. Patient’s questions about normalcy are dismissed, contributing to frustration.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is rigid and controlling, with repeated commands and demands for compliance. There is no collaboration or support for autonomy, positioning the patient as subordinate rather than an active agent.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist dominates the session, ignoring patient input and concerns. There is no shared decision-making or negotiation, and the patient’s questions are dismissed, reflecting a significant power imbalance.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
Change talk is not effectively evoked or reinforced. Patient’s tentative willingness to try walking or meal prepping is met with demands rather than encouragement or exploration.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance
