# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist is consistently judgmental and dismissive, labeling the patient’s fears as “excuses” and “overcomplicating.” There is no warmth or support; the patient’s vulnerability is met with criticism rather than acceptance.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
No reflective listening or validation of the patient’s feelings occurs. The psychiatrist interrupts and challenges the patient’s concerns without acknowledging or exploring them.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts an authoritarian, directive approach, repeatedly insisting on immediate compliance and denying patient autonomy. There is no collaboration or evocation of the patient’s own motivations.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The interaction is dominated by the psychiatrist’s agenda with no invitation for patient input or shared decision-making. Power is wielded coercively, undermining partnership.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)

### Focusing (5-point Likert)

### Evoking (5-point Likert)

- [ ] Affirms autonomy  
- [x] None of the above  
**Comments:**

### Planning (5-point Likert)

- [ ] Strengthens commitment language  
- [ ] Elicits concrete steps  
- [x] None of the above  
**Comments:**
