This evaluation reveals a session that is highly directive, confrontational, and dismissive of the patient’s perspective. The psychiatrist fails to demonstrate acceptance, empathy, or partnership, and does not employ core MI skills such as open questions, reflections, or affirmations. To improve, the psychiatrist must adopt a respectful, empathetic, and collaborative approach consistent with motivational interviewing principles.
