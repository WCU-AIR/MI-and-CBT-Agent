## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving

The psychiatrist adopts a harsh, judgmental tone throughout, repeatedly accusing the patient of “making excuses” and dismissing their efforts. There is no evidence of respect or support, which undermines rapport and trust.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
No reflective listening or validation of the patient’s feelings is demonstrated. The psychiatrist ignores the patient’s fears and ambivalence, responding instead with commands and accusations.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)

**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist uses an authoritarian, coercive approach, refusing to negotiate or support autonomy. Collaboration and evocation are absent.

### Partnership (5-point Likert)
