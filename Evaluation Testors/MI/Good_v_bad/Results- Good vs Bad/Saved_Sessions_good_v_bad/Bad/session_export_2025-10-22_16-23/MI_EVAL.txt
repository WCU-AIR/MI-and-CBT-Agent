# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts a harsh, judgmental tone throughout, repeatedly labeling the patient’s concerns as “excuses” and “weakness.” There is no evidence of unconditional positive regard or acceptance of the patient’s pain and ambivalence.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist fails to reflect or validate the patient’s feelings. Patient’s expressions of pain and struggle are minimized or dismissed rather than acknowledged, showing a near-complete absence of empathy.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The session is dominated by directives and ultimatums. The psychiatrist ignores patient autonomy and does not collaborate or evoke the patient’s own motivations, adopting a controlling, prescriptive style.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
No partnership is established; the psychiatrist repeatedly overrides the patient’s expressed readiness and choices, maintaining an authoritarian expert role.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language

**Comments:**

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)
