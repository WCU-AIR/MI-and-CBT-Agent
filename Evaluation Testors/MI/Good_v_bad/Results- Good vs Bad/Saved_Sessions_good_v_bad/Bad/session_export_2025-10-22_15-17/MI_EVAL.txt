### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: judgmental, disapproving  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist consistently uses harsh, judgmental language, labeling the patient as “lazy” and “weak.” There is no evidence of unconditional positive regard or respect for the patient’s feelings or struggles. This approach damages rapport and trust.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: little effort to understand  
Comments:  
The psychiatrist fails to demonstrate empathy or reflective listening. The patient’s feelings of overwhelm and ambivalence are ignored or dismissed, with no validation or understanding conveyed.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: authoritarian stance  
Comments:

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
□ Low: expert-driven  
Comments:  
There is no partnership or shared decision-making. The psychiatrist controls the conversation, shutting down the patient’s attempts to express her perspective, reinforcing a strong power differential.

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
□ 2 (minimal, inconsistent)

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)
