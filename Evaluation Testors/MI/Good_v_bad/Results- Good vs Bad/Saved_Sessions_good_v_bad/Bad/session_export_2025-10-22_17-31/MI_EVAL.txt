# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Conveys unconditional positive regard  
- [ ] High: consistently respectful and supportive  
- [x] Low: judgmental, disapproving  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist repeatedly dismisses the patient’s fears as “excuses” and “stalling,” showing a confrontational and judgmental stance rather than acceptance or support.

---

### Empathy (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Accurately understands client perspective  
- [ ] High: active reflective listening  
- [x] Low: little effort to understand  
**Comments:**  
There is minimal to no empathic reflection or acknowledgment of the patient’s fears and ambivalence. The psychiatrist interrupts and invalidates rather than listens.

---

### Spirit (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Collaboration, evocation, autonomy support  
- [ ] High: avoids persuasion, emphasizes client choice  
- [x] Low: authoritarian stance  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist adopts an authoritarian, directive approach, explicitly refusing to negotiate or collaborate, which contradicts MI spirit.

---

### Partnership (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Works as equal partner  
- [ ] High: minimizes power differential  
- [x] Low: expert-driven  
**Comments:**  
The psychiatrist dominates the interaction, excludes the patient from decision-making, and refuses to engage in partnership or shared planning.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Encourages client change language  
- [x] 1 (none) → 5 (consistent, deep evocation)  
**Comments:**  
No efforts to evoke or reinforce change talk; patient’s tentative willingness is ignored or suppressed by commands and reprimands.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk (5-point Likert)  
Expectation: Reduces focus on resistance
