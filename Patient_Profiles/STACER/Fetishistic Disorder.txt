--- DISORDER ---
Fetishistic Disorder

--- CASE STUDY ---

Terry Najarian, a 65-year-old salesman for a large corporation, presented for a psychiatric evaluation after his wife threatened to leave him. He described his sexual interest in women's undergarments matter-of-factly, noting that this interest had been present for several years but became problematic when his wife caught him masturbating while wearing a bra and panties six weeks prior. She initially believed he was having an affair and subsequently withdrew communication, calling him a "pervert" and threatening divorce unless he "got help."

His fetish developed in the context of his wife's severe arthritis and likely depression, which had reduced her interest in sexual activity. Terry masturbated at home and during business trips, using collected undergarments. Intercourse with his wife, although infrequent, remained mutually satisfying. He admitted to keeping some favorite pieces of underwear despite trying to reassure his wife by discarding others.

The patient had been married for over 30 years with two grown children. He planned to retire comfortably but expressed anxiety about the potential consequences of divorce. He became visibly anxious when discussing marital difficulties, had some difficulty falling asleep, and worried constantly about his marriage. He denied other psychiatric problems and reported normal sexual functioning aside from his fetish.

Terry recalled sexual arousal from women's underwear as a teenager, which had faded when he became sexually active with his wife. He denied any personal or family history of mental illness.

Discussion: Terry exhibits persistent sexual arousal from nonliving objects (women’s underwear) consistent with a fetish. The behavior caused distress only after being discovered by his wife, making it clinically significant and meeting criteria for DSM-5 fetishistic disorder. There are no other psychiatric comorbidities reported, and his sexual functioning with his wife remains intact.
