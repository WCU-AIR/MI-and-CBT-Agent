--- DISORDER ---
Sexual Sadism Disorder; Alcohol Use Disorder; Generalized Anxiety Disorder (provisional)

--- CASE STUDY ---

Raven Lundquist (she/her, preferring Raven) was a 24-year-old graduate student in philosophy who presented for psychiatric evaluation at her brother's insistence. The siblings lived together, and her brother threatened to withhold her allowance from a small trust if she did not get "some help."

Raven explained that her brother was concerned she was "sadomasochistic." She reported being in a relationship with Lilly for about a year; both identified as queer. Raven described attending bondage parties where she engaged in "mistress-slave scenes," being whipped and humiliated by Lilly, while she generally preferred to be in control in private.

Raven sometimes drugged sexual partners without their knowledge, including Lilly and previous partners, finding the sense of control sexually exciting. Only one prior partner had complained after discovering photos on Raven's camera.

Regarding alcohol, Raven denied illicit drugs and tobacco but admitted to frequent binge drinking. She often missed early morning classes due to hangovers and had verbal conflicts with her brother and Lilly after drinking.

Childhood history included abandonment by her mother, multiple foster placements, and adoption by a well-meaning but intrusive woman who died the year prior, leaving a trust. Raven had memory gaps and suspected possible sexual molestation. Medical history included scoliosis treated with a Harrington rod and extended hospitalization.

Raven reported sexual attraction to both men and women, preferring women for submissive roles. She experienced calmness during sexual control or intoxication but was otherwise anxious. She had been previously diagnosed with generalized anxiety disorder and used prescribed benzodiazepines, which she also used to sedate sexual partners. She denied ever having clinical depression, phobias, panic attacks, obsessions, compulsions, delusions, or hallucinations. Cognitive function was intact.

Discussion: Raven’s masochistic activities do not cause distress or functional impairment, so she does not meet criteria for sexual masochism disorder. However, she meets DSM-5 criteria for sexual sadism disorder because she acted on arousal from the suffering of nonconsenting partners, which caused harm. She also meets criteria for alcohol use disorder due to repeated inability to control intake, binge drinking, and interpersonal conflicts. Generalized anxiety disorder remains provisional.
