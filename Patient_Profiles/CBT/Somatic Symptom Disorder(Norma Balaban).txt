--- DISORDER ---
Somatic Symptom Disorder

--- CASE STUDY ---

Norma Balaban was a 37-year-old married woman referred by her primary care physician for evaluation of depression and multiple somatic symptoms. She worked as an administrative assistant to a university dean. She had been generally healthy aside from obesity and binge eating, with prior gastric bypass surgery 6 years earlier.

She reported a range of physical complaints over the past year, including nocturnal leg spasms, daytime leg aches, sleep difficulties leading to "brain fog," cold sensations in extremities and face, pulsating eye sensations, urinary difficulties, menstrual irregularity, right gluteal pain radiating to the right thigh, neck stiffness, and thoracic back spasms.

She had undergone extensive medical evaluations including visits to rheumatologists, neurologists, and neuro-ophthalmologists. Tests including EEGs, EMG, brain and spinal MRIs, lumbar punctures, and laboratory exams were essentially normal. She had started fluoxetine and gabapentin with partial improvement.

Ms. Balaban spent significant time documenting her symptoms, linking them to vital signs, and researching online. She acknowledged depressed mood, some anhedonia, occasional suicidal thoughts, and premenstrual depressive symptoms over the past year. She had a history of postpartum depression 6 years prior. Family history included cancer, depression, and hypertension. She lived with her husband and two daughters (ages 10 and 6).

On mental status exam, she was alert, casually but neatly dressed, cooperative, with depressed mood and psychomotor retardation. Thought process and content were normal, with no perceptual abnormalities. Cognitive function appeared intact.

Discussion: Ms. Balaban exhibits excessive thoughts, feelings, and behaviors related to her somatic symptoms for at least 6 months, consistent with DSM-5 criteria for somatic symptom disorder (SSD). Her depressive symptoms and prior psychiatric history are notable, but the primary diagnosis focuses on her disproportionate response to somatic complaints.
