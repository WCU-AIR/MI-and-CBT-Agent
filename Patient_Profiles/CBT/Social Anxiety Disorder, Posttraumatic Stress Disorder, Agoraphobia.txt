--- DISORDER ---
Social Anxiety Disorder, Severe; Posttraumatic Stress Disorder, Moderate; Agoraphobia, Severe

--- CASE STUDY ---

Nadine, a 15-year-old girl, was brought for psychiatric evaluation due to long-standing shyness and escalating anxiety. She reported feeling constantly tense for several years, often accompanied by dizziness and crying. Nadine was unable to speak in most social situations outside her home or school classes and refused to leave the house alone. She experienced extreme anxiety interacting with peers, adults, and even in simple public tasks such as ordering at a restaurant.

Nadine had been teased and bullied since kindergarten, with bullying intensifying in middle school and taking on a racial/cultural dimension. She experienced physical assault and repeated verbal threats, including encouragement toward suicide. Despite transferring to a specialty arts high school to escape the bullies, her social anxiety worsened, leading to severe agoraphobia. She began to spend entire weekends at home and developed nightly nightmares about past bullying. Suicidal ideation and self-injury emerged in the month prior to evaluation.

Her family environment contributed to her distress. Nadine described her mother as loud, excitable, and aggressive, and her father as socially shy and often absent due to work. Nadine’s avoidance of her mother’s perceived volatility may have reinforced her inability to assert herself and cope with social challenges.

On examination, Nadine was anxious, guarded, and visibly distressed when discussing social situations. She reported suicidal thoughts and recent self-injury. Cognition was intact, but insight was limited regarding the relationship between her anxiety, trauma, and coping strategies.

Discussion: Nadine demonstrates severe social anxiety disorder, with marked and persistent fear of social interactions due to embarrassment and perceived incompetence. Her anxiety has generalized into severe agoraphobia, manifesting as avoidance of nearly all public spaces. She also meets criteria for moderate PTSD, including intrusion (nightmares), avoidance, negative alterations in cognition and mood, and hyperarousal, stemming from prolonged bullying trauma. Environmental and familial factors, including her mother’s dysregulated attachment style and past bullying experiences, have exacerbated her condition. No medical or substance-related causes were identified.
