# Psychiatry Clinical Evaluation – Core of Psychiatry

## 1. Interview Process

| Item                 | Expectation                        | Criterion                                                                                                                                | Comments |
|----------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Rapport              | Establishes relationship          | □ Introduces self<br>□ Explains interview<br>□ Respectful<br>□ Open, explorative beginning                                                |          |
| Rapport              | Develops and sustains rapport     | □ Remains respectful and non-judgmental<br>□ Genuine interest displayed by verbal and non-verbal responses<br>□ Acknowledges patient’s distress with empathic responses |          |
| Control of process    | Maintains control of the interview| □ Interrupts politely when required<br>□ Redirects when required<br>□ Facilitates organization of disorganized patients                  |          |
| Cultural sensitivity  | Demonstrates cultural sensitivity | □ Engages patient in a culturally safe manner                                                                                            |          |
| Ends the interview    | Smoothly closes the interview     | □ Attends to timing<br>□ Provides a pertinent closing statement                                                                           |          |

## 2. Interview Technique

| Item                     | Expectation                        | Criterion                                                                                                                               | Comments |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Information gathering    | Maintains an open, explorative process | □ Non-verbal behavior encourages patient to tell their story<br>□ Listens attentively<br>□ Note taking does not distract from the interview |          |
| Uses a facilitative questioning style |                      | □ Questioning follows a logical but flexible sequence<br>□ Asks clear questions in plain language<br>□ Avoids leading questions<br>□ Avoids stacked (multiple) questions<br>□ Moves effectively between open and closed questions<br>□ Facilitates expression of emotions |          |
| Pursues important information |                         | □ Appropriately responds to informational and affective cues<br>□ Pursues symptom detail<br>□ Asks for clarification                  |          |
| Maintains flow           | Maintains flow                   | □ Supportively confronts inconsistencies<br>□ Appropriately deals with unusual, difficult, or distressing content<br>□ Comfortably allows silence to facilitate further expression<br>□ Reframes when required<br>□ Summarizes when appropriate |          |

## 3. Interview Content

| Item                                    | Expectation                          | Criterion                                                                                                                               | Comments |
|----------------------------------------|------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Elicits a complete, relevant and accurate history | Identifies the person               | □ Obtains complete demographic information                                                                                            |          |
|                                        | Identifies the presenting complaint(s) or problem(s) and its/their history (History of Presenting Complaint) | □ Obtains data on presenting complaint(s) or problem(s)<br>□ Assesses pre-morbid state<br>□ Assesses stressors related to presenting illness<br>□ Assesses previous illness episodes if relevant, and determines similarities with/differences from this episode<br>□ Identifies treatment interventions and response for this episode |          |
| Screens for symptoms relevant to the differential diagnosis and identification of co-morbid symptoms |                                    | □ Reviews primary criteria of other relevant diagnoses<br>□ Reviews substance use and abuse<br>□ Assesses impact of substance use on person and others<br>□ If appropriate, assesses motivation to change current substance use |          |
| Ensures safety                         |                                    | □ Completes an appropriate risk assessment (self-harm, aggression, self-care, and competency)<br>□ Reviews current medication(s), dosage(s) and response<br>□ Reviews use of over-the-counter products<br>□ Elicits a complete, relevant and accurate history of side effects<br>□ Defines allergy status |          |
| Identifies relevant past history       |                                    | □ Reviews past medical history including family history of medical disorders<br>□ Reviews past psychiatric history<br>□ Reviews family psychiatric history<br>□ Reviews forensic history |          |
| Identifies the developmental and psycho-social history |                                  | □ Reviews and assesses:<br>– Family history and dynamics<br>– Gestational and perinatal history<br>– Childhood and adolescent development<br>– Occupational history and current functioning<br>– Relationship history<br>– Past and current history of abuse<br>– Current supports<br>– Relevant cultural identities, migration history, and associated trauma and stressors<br>– Spirituality<br>– Identifies social and cultural supports including family, kin networks, and communities<br>– Identifies social and cultural stressors and systemic inequities<br>– Explores patient’s explanatory model of illness |          |
| Conducts a formal Mental State Examination as indicated |                                    | □ Appropriately adapts the Mental Status Examination to be culturally competent<br>□ Assesses mood symptoms<br>□ Assesses anxiety symptoms<br>□ Assesses psychotic symptoms<br>□ Assesses insight and judgment<br>□ Assesses cognition if relevant<br>□ Considers intellectual function if relevant |          |
