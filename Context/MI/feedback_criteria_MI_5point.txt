Motivational Interviewing Feedback Criteria (5-Point Likert Scale)

Global Ratings (Counselor Behaviors)
------------------------------------
1. Acceptance: Extent to which the counselor communicates unconditional positive regard for the client.
   1 = Judgmental, disapproving, labeling
   2 = Inconsistent respect, occasional judgment
   3 = Neutral stance, basic respect
   4 = Generally respectful and supportive
   5 = Consistently respectful, warm, supportive

2. Empathy: Extent to which the counselor accurately understands and conveys the client’s perspective.
   1 = Little effort to understand, minimal reflective listening
   2 = Limited perspective-taking, mostly factual focus
   3 = Adequate but inconsistent effort to understand
   4 = Good reflective listening and perspective-taking
   5 = Highly accurate, consistent understanding and reflections

3. Spirit of MI: Overall demonstration of collaboration, evocation, and autonomy support.
   1 = Authoritarian stance, persuasion, directive
   2 = Some collaboration but frequent persuasion
   3 = Moderate partnership, occasional evocation
   4 = Strong partnership, draws out client’s own reasons, supports autonomy
   5 = Fully collaborative, evocative, autonomy-supportive throughout

4. Cultivating Change Talk: Counselor attention to, and evocation of, client language in favor of change.
   1 = No effort to evoke change talk
   2 = Rare or weak efforts
   3 = Occasional evocation, limited depth
   4 = Frequent evocation of change talk
   5 = Consistent, skillful evocation of change talk

5. Softening Sustain Talk: Counselor responses that reduce or shift away from sustain (status quo) talk.
   1 = Reinforces sustain talk
   2 = Rarely shifts away from sustain talk
   3 = Sometimes redirects but unevenly
   4 = Often redirects effectively
   5 = Consistently redirects, minimizes sustain talk impact

6. Partnership: Quality of collaboration, minimizing power differential, working as equals.
   1 = Authoritative, expert-driven stance
   2 = Limited collaboration, mostly directive
   3 = Partial collaboration, inconsistent partnership
   4 = Strong collaborative stance most of the time
   5 = Fully equal, client-centered partnership

Behavior Counts (Counselor)
---------------------------
For each, note observed frequency and quality, then provide an overall 5-point score.

- Open Questions: Uses questions that invite elaboration rather than yes/no answers.
- Reflections: Uses more reflections than questions; favors complex reflections.
- Affirmations: Recognizes client strengths, efforts, and values.
- Persuade with Permission: Provides information/advice only with explicit client permission.
- Emphasize Autonomy: Explicitly supports client choice and control.

Behavior Counts (Client Speech)
-------------------------------
- Change Talk: Desire, Ability, Reasons, Need, Commitment, Taking Steps
- Sustain Talk: Language favoring status quo
- Neutral Talk: Content unrelated to change

Evaluation Guidance
-------------------
- All domains are rated on a 1–5 Likert scale.
- Ratings should reflect overall quality and consistency of behavior.
- Provide qualitative feedback with examples of effective and ineffective moments.
- Focus on both counselor skillfulness and client speech as indicators of MI process quality.
