# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

## 1. Global Ratings (Counselor)

### Acceptance
**Expectation:** Conveys unconditional positive regard<br>
□ High: consistently respectful and supportive<br>
□ Low: judgmental, disapproving<br>
**Comments:** ___________________________________________

### Empathy
**Expectation:** Accurately understands client perspective<br>
□ High: active reflective listening<br>
□ Low: little effort to understand<br>
**Comments:** ___________________________________________

### Direction
**Expectation:** Maintains clear focus on target behavior or change goal.<br>
□ High – keeps the session on-track and purpose-driven while remaining collaborative.<br>
□ Low – allows session to drift without consistent focus.<br>
**Comments:** ___________________________________________

### Collaboration
**Expectation:** Works as an equal partner; shares power and decision-making.<br>
□ High – minimizes power differential; invites client input and joint problem-solving.<br>
□ Low – expert-driven, directive, or dismissive of client input.<br>
**Comments:** ___________________________________________

### Autonomy Support
**Expectation:** Emphasizes client choice and self-determination.<br>
□ High – affirms freedom to choose; avoids coercion; validates client control.<br>
□ Low – uses imperatives or pressure; limits perceived choice.<br>
**Comments:** ___________________________________________

### Evocation
**Expectation:** Draws out the client’s own reasons and motivations for change.<br>
□ High – consistently elicits, explores, and reinforces change talk.<br>
□ Low – provides reasons or advice without exploring client’s view.<br>
**Comments:** ___________________________________________

### Cultivating Change Talk
**Expectation:** Encourages client change language<br>
□ High – consistent, deep evocation of change talk<br>
□ Low – little to no elicitation of change talk<br>
**Comments:** ___________________________________________

### Softening Sustain Talk
**Expectation:** Reduces focus on resistance<br>
□ High – consistently pivots away from sustain talk toward change<br>
□ Low – reinforces or dwells on status quo language<br>
**Comments:** ___________________________________________

### Partnership
**Expectation:** working with the client as an equal partner<br>
□ High – actively fosters shared decision-making<br>
□ Low – maintains imbalance or hierarchy in the interaction<br>
**Comments:** __________________________________________
