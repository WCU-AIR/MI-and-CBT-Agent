Psychiatric Interview Evaluation Criteria

1. Rapport Building

Item: Establishes relationship
Criteria:
- Introduces self
- Explains interview purpose
- Is respectful
- Begins with an open, explorative approach

Item: Develops and sustains rapport
Criteria:
- Remains respectful and non-judgmental
- Displays genuine interest (verbal and non-verbal)
- Acknowledges distress with empathic responses

Item: Maintains control of the interview
Criteria:
- Interrupts politely when needed
- Redirects as needed
- Organizes disorganized patients

Item: Demonstrates cultural sensitivity
Criteria:
- Engages in a culturally safe manner

Item: Ends the interview smoothly
Criteria:
- Manages timing
- Provides a pertinent closing statement

2. Interview Technique

Item: Information gathering with open exploration
Criteria:
- Uses non-verbal cues to encourage storytelling
- Listens attentively
- Note-taking is non-distracting

Item: Facilitative questioning style
Criteria:
- Follows logical, flexible sequence
- Uses clear, plain language
- Avoids leading questions
- Avoids stacked (multiple) questions
- Balances open and closed questions
- Facilitates emotional expression

Item: Pursues important information
Criteria:
- Responds to emotional/informational cues
- Explores symptom details
- Seeks clarification

Item: Maintains flow
Criteria:
- Supportively confronts inconsistencies
- Manages difficult/distressing content
- Uses silence appropriately
- Reframes when needed
- Summarizes when appropriate

3. Interview Content

Item: Identifies the person
Criteria:
- Obtains complete demographic info

Item: History of Presenting Complaint (HPC)
Criteria:
- Gathers data on presenting complaint
- Assesses pre-morbid functioning
- Assesses related stressors
- Reviews prior illness episodes and compares
- Identifies past treatments and outcomes

Item: Screens for differential diagnosis and comorbid symptoms
Criteria:
- Reviews diagnostic criteria
- Screens substance use/abuse
- Evaluates impact of substance use
- Assesses readiness to change use (if applicable)

Item: Ensures safety
Criteria:
- Conducts risk assessment (self-harm, aggression, self-care, competency)
- Reviews current medications and effects
- Reviews OTC product use
- Assesses side effects
- Clarifies allergy status

Item: Reviews past history
Criteria:
- Medical history incl. family medical disorders
- Psychiatric history
- Family psychiatric history
- Forensic history

Item: Identifies developmental and psychosocial history
Criteria:
- Family history/dynamics
- Gestational/perinatal history
- Childhood/adolescence
- Occupational history & current function
- Relationship history
- Abuse history (past & current)
- Social supports
- Cultural identities, migration, related trauma
- Spirituality
- Family/community/kin supports
- Social/cultural stressors and systemic inequities
- Patient’s own explanatory model of illness

Item: Mental State Examination (MSE)
Criteria:
- Culturally adapted MSE
- Mood
- Anxiety
- Psychotic symptoms
- Insight and judgment
- Cognition (if relevant)
- Intellectual function (if relevant)
