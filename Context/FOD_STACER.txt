# Psychiatry Clinical Evaluation – Foundations of Psychiatry

## 1. Interview Process

| Item                 | Expectation                        | Criterion                                                                                                                                | Comments |
|----------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Rapport              | Establishes relationship          | □ Introduces self<br>□ Explains interview<br>□ Respectful<br>□ Open, explorative beginning                                                |          |
| Rapport              | Develops and sustains rapport     | □ Remains respectful and non-judgmental<br>□ Genuine interest displayed by verbal and nonverbal responses<br>□ Acknowledges patient’s distress with empathic responses |          |
| Control of process    | Maintains control of the interview| □ Interrupts politely when required<br>□ Attempts to redirect when required                                                              |          |
| Cultural sensitivity  | Demonstrates cultural sensitivity | □ Engages patient in a culturally safe manner                                                                                            |          |
| Ends the interview    | Smoothly closes the interview     | □ Attends to timing<br>□ Provides a pertinent closing statement                                                                           |          |

## 2. Interview Technique

| Item                  | Expectation                        | Criterion                                                                                                                               | Comments |
|-----------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Information gathering | Maintains an open, explorative process | □ Non-verbal behaviour encourages patient to tell their story<br>□ Listens attentively<br>□ Note taking does not distract from the interview |          |
| Information gathering | Uses a facilitative questioning style | □ Questioning follows a logical sequence<br>□ Asks clear questions in plain language<br>□ Avoids leading questions<br>□ Avoids stacked (multiple) questions<br>□ Attempts to move between open and closed questions<br>□ Facilitates expression of emotions |          |
| Information gathering | Pursues important information     | □ Appropriately responds to informational and affective cues<br>□ Attempts to pursue and clarify symptom detail                        |          |
| Maintains Flow        | Maintains Flow                    | □ Reframes when required<br>□ Summarizes when appropriate                                                                               |          |

## 3. Interview Content

| Item                                    | Expectation                          | Criterion                                                                                                                               | Comments |
|----------------------------------------|------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Elicits a complete, relevant and accurate history | Identifies the person               | □ Obtains demographic information                                                                                                     |          |
|                                        | Identifies the presenting complaint(s) or problem(s) and its/their history (History of Presenting Complaint) | □ Obtains data on presenting complaint(s) or problem(s)<br>□ Assesses pre-morbid state<br>□ Assesses stressors related to presenting illness<br>□ Inquires about previous illness episodes<br>□ Inquires about past and present treatment interventions and response |          |
| Elicits a complete, relevant and accurate history | Screens for symptoms relevant to the differential diagnosis and identification of comorbid symptoms | □ Screens for symptoms of relevant co-morbid illnesses, including mood, psychotic, and anxiety symptoms<br>□ Inquires about substance use |          |
| Ensures safety                         |                                    | □ Completes an appropriate risk assessment (suicidal and homicidal ideation)<br>□ Reviews current medication(s), dosage(s) and response<br>□ Reviews use of over-the-counter products<br>□ Assesses side-effects<br>□ Inquires about allergies |          |
| Elicits a complete, relevant and accurate history | Identifies relevant past history     | □ Reviews past medical history including family history of medical disorders<br>□ Reviews past psychiatric history<br>□ Reviews family psychiatric history |          |
| Identifies the developmental and psycho-social history |                                  | □ Reviews family history<br>□ Reviews birth history if relevant<br>□ Inquires about childhood and adolescent development<br>□ Reviews occupational history and current functioning<br>□ Inquires about relationship status<br>□ Inquires about abuse history<br>□ Assesses current supports<br>□ Inquires about other relevant social or cultural factors |          |
| Conducts a formal Mental State Examination as indicated |                                    | □ Assesses mood symptoms<br>□ Assesses anxiety symptoms<br>□ Assesses psychotic symptoms<br>□ Assesses insight<br>□ Assesses cognition if relevant<br>□ Considers intellectual function if relevant |          |
