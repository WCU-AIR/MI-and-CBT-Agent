## 3. Interview Content

| Item                                                      | Expectation                                                                                                   | Criterion                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Comments |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Elicits a complete, relevant and accurate history         | Identifies the person                                                                                         | □ Obtains complete demographic information                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |          |
|                                                           | Identifies the presenting complaint(s) or problem(s) and its/their history (History of Presenting Complaint)  | □ Obtains data on presenting complaint(s) or problem(s)<br>□ Assesses pre-morbid state<br>□ Assesses stressors related to presenting illness<br>□ Assesses previous illness episodes if relevant, and determines similarities with/differences from this episode<br>□ Identifies treatment interventions and response for this episode                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |          |
|                                                           | Screens for symptoms relevant to the differential diagnosis and identification of co-morbid symptoms          | □ Reviews primary criteria of other relevant diagnoses<br>□ Reviews substance use and abuse<br>□ Assesses impact of substance use on person and others<br>□ If appropriate, assesses motivation to change current substance use                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |          |
|                                                           | Ensures safety                                                                                                | □ Completes an appropriate risk assessment (self-harm, aggression, self-care, and competency)<br>□ Reviews current medication(s), dosage(s) and response<br>□ Reviews use of over-the-counter products<br>□ Elicits a complete, relevant and accurate history of side effects<br>□ Defines allergy status                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |          |
|                                                           | Identifies relevant past history                                                                              | □ Reviews past medical history including family history of medical disorders<br>□ Reviews past psychiatric history<br>□ Reviews family psychiatric history<br>□ Reviews forensic history                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |          |
|                                                           | Identifies the developmental and psycho-social history                                                        | □ Reviews and assesses:<br>– Family history and dynamics<br>– Gestational and perinatal history<br>– Childhood and adolescent development<br>– Occupational history and current functioning<br>– Relationship history<br>– Past and current history of abuse<br>– Current supports<br>– Relevant cultural identities, migration history, and associated trauma and stressors<br>– Spirituality<br>– Identifies social and cultural supports including family, kin networks, and communities<br>– Identifies social and cultural stressors and systemic inequities<br>– Explores patient’s explanatory model of illness |          |
| Conducts a formal Mental State Examination as indicated   |   Conducts a formal Mental State Examination as indicated                                                     | □ Appropriately adapts the Mental Status Examination to be culturally competent<br>□ Assesses mood symptoms<br>□ Assesses anxiety symptoms<br>□ Assesses psychotic symptoms<br>□ Assesses insight and judgment<br>□ Assesses cognition if relevant<br>□ Considers intellectual function if relevant                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |          |

## 4. Case Presentation

| Item                            | Expectation                                                                                                                                 | Criterion                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Comments |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Defines limitations of the data | Identifies issues in the information gathering process                                                                                      | □ Reports on the reliability of the patient (with examples)<br>□ Reports on the accessibility of the patient (with examples)<br>□ Identifies deficits in the interview and their potential effect on the data collection                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |          |
| Presentation skills             | Provides a coherent, accurate summary of the case                                                                                           | □ Uses descriptive terms correctly (e.g., delusions)<br>□ Presents case in an orderly, concise, systematic manner that is sufficiently detailed<br>□ Accurately reports the Mental State Examination<br>□ Accurately reports the risk assessment (self-harm, aggression, self-care, competency)<br>□ Accurately reports the Mental Status Examination<br>□ Identifies relevant comorbidities                                                                                     |          |
| Synthesizing skills             | Synthesizes all the clinical information into a diagnosis, differential diagnosis and case formulation                                      | □ Presentation emphasizes the necessary information to support and defend the preferred diagnosis and differential<br>□ Provides a working diagnosis including co-morbidities supported by evidence from the interview<br>□ Provides a differential diagnosis supported by evidence from interview<br>□ Discusses co-morbidities and interplay between diagnosis<br>□ Provides a realistic prognosis<br>□ Describes barriers to compliance or optimal treatment for this patient |          |
| Synthesizing skills             | Provides an accurate and coherent formulation covering the rudimentary bio-psycho-social factors influencing the patient and their disorder | Identifies contributing:<br>□ Biological factors<br>□ Psychological factors<br>□ Social factors<br>□ Cultural factors<br>□ Provides a sophisticated and accurate account of the interplay between these components that enhances the understanding of the patient<br>□ Identifies prominent internal conflicts and/or cognitive distortions that influence the patient’s presentation                                                                                            |          |
